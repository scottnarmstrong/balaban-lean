Summary. P4 treats the small-field part of one renormalization step k -> k+1 on the whole lattice Z^d (the "small field region is the whole lattice"; the generalization to small-field subdomains is said to be easy and is deferred, p. 675-676, p. 682). It (i) restates the inductive hypotheses of P1 in sharper form as (H.1)-(H.7) (pp. 676-679), (ii) states Theorem 1 and Theorem 2 (the step maps the space of densities with hypotheses (H.1)-(H.7) into the same space with k+1 and changed parameters), (iii) reduces the transformation T^(k) to a "fluctuation integral" (2.41) around the unique minimal configuration, (iv) defines the new contributions to the effective action and to the generating functional (2.51)-(2.57), (v) rescales (S^(k)), (vi) introduces the renormalization operation (change of coefficients (4.1)), and (vii) in Sect. 5 writes the renormalization conditions (H.5) as a system of 3 equations for the 5 constants (zeta, b, c, d, e), supplements it with 2 free choices, and solves it by a contraction argument. Sect. 6 describes the last step (k_1) where v_k first exceeds 7/8 L^-2 and the final integration without further renormalization.

## 1. Setting and notation

Lattices (as in P1). T_eta: torus of unit... "the lattice T_eta" with eta = L^-k, lattice spacing eta; T_1^(k) is the unit lattice of the "new" variable at scale k (spins psi_k on T^(k)); T_L^(k+1) is the lattice of blocks (cubes B_{k+1}(z) of side L). Scale parameters L (block ratio), M = L^m the size of "large cubes". Spin configurations take values in R^N; background (external) field h in neighbourhood of the unit sphere S^{N-1}; g is the source for the generating functional. Q_k: averaging operator from T_eta to the coarser unit lattice (psi ~ Q_k phi); Q = Q_{k+1}, Q^* adjoint; delta^eta, Delta^eta the discrete derivative and Laplacian on T_eta.

Main action (1.1) p. 676:
$$A_k(\psi,\phi;h,a,\lambda,v)=\tfrac12 a\|\psi-Q_k\phi\|_1^2+\tfrac12\|\partial^\eta\phi\|^2+\tfrac{\lambda}{8}\||\phi|^2-1\|^2+\tfrac v2\|\phi-h\|^2,$$
with eta = L^-k, 1/2 < a < 3/2, lambda >= 1, 0 < v <= 1. psi lives on T_1^(k), phi on T_eta. phi_k(psi;h,a_k,lambda_k,v_k) is the minimal configuration of the variational problem (P1 Sect. 2, constructed in P2). Equivalent formulation with Lagrange multiplier alpha_k (2.5): J_k(theta,psi,phi,alpha) with -(1/(2 lambda))||alpha||^2 + <alpha,|phi|^2-1>/2, inf over psi, phi, sup over alpha (2.6).

Density (H.1): rho_k(psi_k) = chi_k exp[A_k + F_k(psi_k,h,g)], A_k = -beta_k A_k(psi_k,phi_k;h,a_k,lambda_k,v_k) + E_k(psi_k,h) - E_k |T_1^(k)| (the printed symbol clash E_k function/constant is as in the text). Sizes: epsilon_k = alpha_{0,k} eta^alpha with alpha_{0,k} = alpha_0(1+sum_{n=k}^infty n^-2), alpha = (d-2)/2 - gamma, gamma in (0, min{(d-2)/2, 1/2}). chi_k = characteristic function of the space Xi_k(1, beta_k^{-1/2} p(beta_k)) (so psi_k restricted to small field), p(beta)=A_0 (log beta)^{p_0}, p_0 even integer, A_0 large. E_k real analytic on Xi_k(1,epsilon_k); F_k analytic on Xi_k(1,epsilon_k) x {g: ||g||_{l^1}<1}.

Localization structure. D_j = D_j(T): localization domains (polymer-like connected sets of large cubes; sizes m_mu < x_mu < M_mu, M_mu - m_mu < 2 L_mu xi), d_j(X) = tree length; Xi_j^c(B_j(X);1,epsilon_j) complex analyticity domain from P3 Sect. 4 (neighbourhood of X only, strictly containing the P1 domain). Exponential tree decay exp(-kappa d_j(X)).

Complex scale parameters: a_j = a_k (1-L^{-2k})/(1-L^{-2j}), lambda_j = lambda_k (L^j eta)^2, v_j = v_k (L^j eta)^2 (H.2). Running coefficients (H.6): beta_k = beta L^{k(d-2)} gamma_k, a_k = a (1-L^-2)/(1-L^{-2k}) gamma_k^{-1} (version A) or a_k = a(1-L^{-2})/(1-L^{-2k}); lambda_k = lambda L^{2k}, v_k = v L^{2k} delta_k, with gamma_k^{±1}, delta_k^{±1} < exp(c_8 sum_{j<=k} L^{-2 gamma j}).  (The formula in the PDF is typeset as "beta_k = beta L^{k(d-2)} gamma_k, a_k = a(1-L^-2)/(1-L^-2k)", or alternatively "a_k = a (1-L^-2)/(1-L^{-2k}) gamma_k^{-1}, lambda_k=lambda L^{2k} gamma_k, v_k = v L^{2k} delta_k" "depending on renormalization procedures". UNCERTAIN on exact placement of gamma_k in the two alternative versions at p. 679.)

Characteristic functions/fluctuation variables: chi^(k) = product of (2.2) chi(|theta - Q psi| < 2 beta_k^{-1/2} p(beta_k)) and a restriction on fluctuation variables (2.39)-(2.40) with p_1(beta)=A_1 (log beta)^{p_1}, p_1 even > 2.

## 2. Main results

The inductive hypotheses (pp. 676-679; "the changes ... make them more precise and restrictive, so they do not affect the considerations of [1]"):

(H.1) (p. 676) positive coefficients beta_k, a_k in (1/2,3/2), lambda_k >= 1, 0<v_k<=1, functions E_k, F_k and constants E_k, alpha_0, gamma with the density form above; E_k real analytic on Xi_k(1, epsilon_k); F_k real analytic on Xi_k(1,epsilon_k) x {g: ||g||_{l^1}<1}.

(H.2) (p. 677) E_k(psi_k,h) = sum_{j=1}^k E_k^(j)(psi_k^(j)(psi_k,h),h), with E_k^(j)(psi_j,h)=sum_{y in T^(j)} E_k^(j)(y;psi_j,h); analytic on Xi_j(1,epsilon_j), epsilon_j = alpha_{0,k} xi^alpha, xi = L^-j; a_j, lambda_j, v_j linked to a_k, lambda_k, v_k as above.

(H.3) (p. 677) O(N) invariance E^(j)(y;R psi,Rh)=E^(j)(y;psi,h) and covariance under Euclidean symmetries r of T^(j).

(H.4) (p. 677-678) localization expansion E^(j)(y;psi_j,h) = sum_{X in D_j: y in X} E^(j)(y,X;psi_j,h); each term depends only on psi_j|X, extends analytically to Xi_j^c(B_j(X);1,epsilon_j), bound |E^(j)(y,X;psi+psi',h+h')| < E_0 exp(-kappa d_j(X)); kappa arbitrarily large depending on M; same symmetries. Comment (p. 678): the new analyticity space is larger than P1's (H.5), so analyticity in (H.1) follows from (H.4).

Effective potential (1.2) V_k^(j)(psi,h)= lim E_k^(j)(y;psi,h) = lim |T|^{-1} E_k^(j)(psi,h) (constant configuration), 2-point function G_k^(j,infty)(x-x') = second derivative at (e_1,e_1), (1.3).

(H.5) (p. 678) renormalization conditions at (e_1,e_1), e_1=(1,0,...,0):
V_k^(j)(e_1,e_1)=0; (d/dh)V·e_1 = 0 and (d/dpsi)V·e_1 = sum_y' (d/dpsi_j(y')) E^(j)(y;e_1,e_1)·e_1 = 0 (the second in the form printed); and sum_x tr[G^(j,infty)(x)(e_2⊗e_2)] |x|^2 = -Delta_p tr[G^(j,infty)(p)(e_2⊗e_2)]|_{p=0} = -d (d^2/dp_mu^2) G_{k;2,2}^(j,infty)(0)=0 for mu=1..d. So: vanishing vacuum energy, vanishing first derivatives in the "radial" direction at the sphere point, vanishing second-moment of the transversal two-point function (no "mass-like" and no "wave function" correction beyond the Gaussian one). NOTE: H.5 is stated for each j <= k but, by (H.2)-(H.4), only the last j=k+1 term (new) must be renormalized (p. 678: "we will be concerned only with the last one").

(H.6) (p. 679) beta_k = beta L^{k(d-2)} gamma_k, a_k = a (1-L^-2)/(1-L^{-2k}) [times gamma_k^{-1} in one variant], lambda_k = lambda L^{2k} [gamma_k], v_k = v L^{2k} delta_k, gamma_k^{±1}, delta_k^{±1} < exp(c_8 sum_{j<=k} L^{-2 gamma j}), c_8 arbitrarily small for beta large. (The PDF shows "lambda_k = lambda L^{2k}" in the first variant and "lambda L^{2k} gamma_k" in the second; see UNCERTAIN note in Sect. 1.)

(H.7) (p. 679) F_k(psi_k,h,g) = <g,phi_k>_1 + sum_{j=1}^k F_k^(j)(psi_k^(j)(psi_k,h),h,g), F^(j)(psi_j,h,g)=<g, M^(j)(.;psi_j,h,g)>_1, M^(j)(x;.) analytic on Xi_j(1,epsilon_j) x {||g||_{l^1}<1}, O(N)-equivariant ( M(x;R psi, Rh, Rg) = R M ), Euclidean covariant, localization M^(j)(x;.) = sum_{X in D_j: x in X} M^(j)(x,X;.) with bound |M^(j)(x,X;psi+psi',h+h',g)| < c_9 xi^{(d-2)/2+gamma-alpha_1} exp(-kappa d_j(X)), c_9, alpha_1 positive, kappa as in (H.4).

- Dependence on P2 (analytic implicit function theorem with explicit bounds uniform in lattice size; infinite-dimensional, Banach-space-valued analytic maps) and P3 (exponentially-decaying operators and their localization expansions) is heavy. Mathlib has analytic implicit function theorem in Banach spaces (as of last knowledge, partial; guess), but the uniform estimates are research-level.
- The fluctuation integral (2.41) as a formal object (Gaussian measure on a infinite lattice with covariance C^(k)) needs thermodynamic limit infrastructure; recommend finite torus with the "wind around" restriction (H.4) as the paper does, and treat the torus-independence statements as a separate layer.
- The deferred localization expansions (Props 2.1, 2.2, 4.1, 4.2) are the hardest and are not in this paper; a faithful formalization of P4 will need them or must take them as frozen interface statements. The statements of (H.1)-(H.7), Theorems 1-2 and Props 2.1-2.2, 4.1-4.2, 5.1 would be natural freeze candidates.
- The tricky point for a formal statement: (H.2) defines E_k as sum over j of terms evaluated at minimal configurations psi_k^(j)(psi_k,h) of P1; this recursive structure (analyticity spaces depending on k) needs a clean type-level design.
