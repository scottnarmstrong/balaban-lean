# The physical collar and the regional mass-switch wall

## Result

P7's converted action is a sum of regional actions with half weight on crossing bonds. A zero-mass versus complete-background comparison at the same physical data therefore has an exact stationary quadratic chord **plus a crossing-bond flux**. The latter is of order $\beta_jm_j|\partial\bar\phi_j^{m_j}|$ per wall bond, and can have a nonzero limit when $\beta_jm_j$ stays fixed. It cannot be absorbed into the $O(\beta_jm_j^2)$ chord. A bounded wall cost can be paid in the large-field one-step exponent once its incidence in the *new* region is proved; a small wall cost needs the actual small-field gradient bound on each counted bond. The existing `ActionConvInput.wall_bound` concerns P6 coefficient conversion within one mass branch and does not provide this mass-switch estimate.

The product P5 (2.47) $\times$ P6 (3.2), written as P7 (2.6)–(2.7), retains the exterior spin variables as parameters of the original density. P7 (2.10)–(2.12) changes coefficients without renormalizing these spins. Consequently the original window background reads the trace of the **inherited original field** on its Dirichlet collar, while the later reference background of P7 (2.40) reads the composed trace (2.40) below. These traces are not identified by (2.13) or by the window-block estimate (2.48). The sources and landed Lean code do not prove a common-collar identity, a collar estimate small enough for P3 Proposition 2.1, or a source-level counterexample satisfying every P7 cutoff. The corrected response and interface below make these missing premises explicit. In particular, a complete proof of scheme A's $R$ switch is still open.

## 1. Exact localized action identity and wall estimate

Work in one rescaled scale-$j$ lattice, with $m=m_j$, $\beta=\beta_j$, real unit $h$, one generating set $G$, and a fixed physical datum $D$ on $I=\Omega_1(G)$ and its one-bond collar $C_G$. Let $\phi^0,\phi^m$ be the small constrained solutions of the massless and complete equations for **that same datum**, and put $w=\phi^0-\phi^m$, extended by zero on $C_G$. The action evaluated at both fields is the *physical complete action* $A_m$; changing the mass in one of the two action evaluations would define a different comparison. Write $\bar\phi^m$ for its extension by $D.\Psi$ on the collar. For any P7 region $Y\subset I$, with star bond weights $1$ inside and $1/2$ across $\partial Y$, the quadratic expansion, constraint $|\phi^0|=|\phi^m|=1$, complete Euler–Lagrange equation, and regional Green formula give

$$A_{m,Y}(D,\phi^0)-A_{m,Y}(D,\phi^m)
=\frac12\langle w,(H_m+\alpha^m)w\rangle_{Y,*}
 +S_Y(w,\bar\phi^m),\qquad
S_Y=\sum_{b\in\operatorname{st}(Y)}\left\langle\frac{w(b_-)+w(b_+)}2,\partial_b^{\rm out}\bar\phi^m\right\rangle.\tag{1.1}$$

Here the quadratic notation includes the regional block, bond, mass and multiplier terms with their actual coefficients; it is the remainder obtained by expanding the displayed regional action, not a positivity assertion for an arbitrary subregion. To verify (1.1), expand the quadratic action at $\phi^m$, use $|\phi^0|^2-|\phi^m|^2=0$ to replace the linear multiplier term by $-\langle w,\alpha^mw\rangle/2$, and apply $\langle\partial w,\partial\bar\phi^m\rangle_{Y,*}=\langle w,-\Delta\bar\phi^m\rangle_Y+S_Y$. This is `Region.bondPair_green` in `ActionConvGreen.lean`, with `starPair` defined in `ActionConvDef.lean`. The identity holds for complex bilinear fields as algebra, although the real positivity used below does not. Even for $Y=I$, $w=0$ on $C_G$ leaves a half-bond flux from its interior endpoints. `INTERFACE-A.md` §1 gives a one-site example with common collar for which $\beta S_Y$ tends to a positive constant as $m\downarrow0$, $\beta m=c$; hence deleting $S_Y$ is false.

Here is an exact finite wall estimate. Assign each crossing bond $b$ a physical/rescaled gradient bound $r_b\ge|\partial_b\bar\phi^m|$ and endpoint response bounds $R_x\ge|w(x)|$ (take $R_x=0$ on a common exterior collar). Then Cauchy–Schwarz bond by bond gives

$$\boxed{\ |\beta S_Y|\le \beta\sum_{b\in\operatorname{st}(Y)}r_b\frac{R_{b_-}+R_{b_+}}2.\ }\tag{1.2}$$

This is the localized form to use in a wall decomposition; `ActionConvReduce.lean:wall_sum_le` proves its finite-sum mechanism. If a same-datum mass-response theorem gives $R_x\le K_mB_hm\,P_m(x)$, then the right side is at most $K_mB_h\beta m\sum_b r_b[P_m(b_-)+P_m(b_+)]/2$. For the uniform response $P_m\le1$, $K_m=2K_2$ as normalized in `INTERFACE-A.md`, it is at most $2K_2B_h\beta m\sum_b r_b$ (and half this bound for a wall against the common exterior collar). If the local Hessian remainder obeys $|\langle w,(H_m+\alpha^m)w\rangle_{Y,*}|\le C_H|Y|_j(K_mB_hm)^2$, the complete bound is

$$|\beta\Delta A_{m,Y}|\le\tfrac12C_HK_m^2B_h^2\beta m^2|Y|_j
 +K_mB_h\beta m\sum_{b\in\operatorname{st}(Y)}r_b.\tag{1.3}$$

The response is a **new mass-source application**, not an existing consequence of `layered_response`: that theorem and `lemma41_uncond` compare data at fixed $\nu$ and fixed $h$. `WeightedProp51S` does allow a general source $f_1$, but to use it one must prove that the difference of the two Euler–Lagrange equations is its `IsPerturbed` system with source $m(\phi^m-h)$, and verify `Prop51Hyp`, `OpSetting`, the defect-zero condition, the small multiplier and the weighted source bound. For a uniform weight the expected input is $|\phi^m-h|\le2B_h$ and $m$ below its smallness threshold, yielding $R_x\le2K_2B_hm$ in the BG normalization. For a localized profile $P_m$, the weighted source must be established at every site; a localized *datum* perturbation by itself says nothing about this distributed mass source. Thus (1.3) is a proved algebraic reduction to named, still unverified mass-response and Hessian hypotheses.

There are two distinct available gradient bounds. On a real P2 data domain with its (R2) threshold at most one, $|D.\Psi(c)|<\sqrt2$ on the collar, while $|\phi^m(x)|=1$ inside; hence on every interior-to-collar bond $r_b<1+\sqrt2<3$, and on an interior bond $r_b\le2$. This gives a fixed wall cost after the physical datum has been shown to lie in that domain. On the support where P7 Lemma 2.2 actually applies, printed p. 508 (2.41) gives $|\partial^\eta\phi_k^0|<3K_1K_6\delta_k$ on $U_k$, and (2.42) gives $<9\delta_k/8$ on $Z'''{}^c\cap U_k$; for its analytic extension the corresponding $\delta_k$ is replaced by $2\varepsilon_k$ (p. 508). These bounds concern the **reference massless** field and only those specified sites. A complete-background wall bound needs the mass derivative response as well: on a bond covered by (2.41), $r_b\le3K_1K_6\delta_k+|\partial_b w|$ after putting both gradients in the same $\eta$ units. `WeightedProp51S` provides a scaled derivative component under the mass-source premises; it does not extend (2.41) to all P7 walls. For a wall outside this support, use the real bound $3$ or prove another regularity lemma. For complex operator bounds, the real bound $3$ must be replaced by a bound from the specified pinned chart and its complex data domain.

For $j\le k_0$, D-IV.11 gives $\beta_jm_j\le C_0=e^{2\epsilon}$. Thus each **rescaled wall bond** in (1.3) costs at most $3K_mB_hC_0$ in the real domain, or $K_mB_hC_0(3K_1K_6\delta_j+|\partial w|)$ on a verified regular wall. To turn this into the one-step bound one needs the geometric charge

$$\sum_{Y,j}\sum_{b\in\operatorname{st}(Y)}r_{j,b}
 \le\bigl(I_0+I_1\log\beta_k\bigr)|X_{Z'}|_{k+1},\tag{1.4}$$

with fixed $I_0,I_1$ and all converted action and cutoff walls included, or a stronger uniform $I_1=0$ estimate. The raw fact that each $j$-site has at most $2d$ incident bonds bounds one region at one scale; it does **not** prove (1.4) across all $j$ or assign old/exterior walls to new volume. Under (1.4), the wall part of (S2) is paid by enlarging $c_2$ by $K_mB_hC_0I_0$ and $c_1$ by $K_mB_hC_0I_1$ in Definition II.5.11. The small chord is paid there only after its analogous new-volume/localization count. The constants $I_0,I_1$ must be chosen before row 14 of LF-RECONSTRUCTION §I.1.3; $c_1,c_2$ are then chosen in row 14, and $\lambda_*,\beta$ after them in row 16. A bound larger than $O(\log\beta_k)$ per new cube cannot simply be hidden in these two fixed coefficients. The present `StepDecomp.sum_le` and `ActionConvInput.wall_bound` are conditional P6 conversion bookkeeping, not a proof of (1.4) for the new mass switch.

## 2. Which field is on the physical collar?

Let $G=\mathbb B_{k_1,k}$ of P7 (2.13), $G'=\mathbb B_k(U_k)$, $I=\Omega_1(G)$, and $C_G=\{c\notin I:c\sim x\text{ for some }x\in I\}$. Before the P7 window rearrangement, P5 p. 512 (2.47) is the prior-step operator; P6 p. 16 (3.2) adds the regional main action and integrates the specified $\psi_k,\psi'_k$ variables, leaving the other arguments of the kernel as parameters. P5 p. 513, after (2.49), explicitly states that dependence on $\psi_k$ outside $\Omega_{k+1}$ is unchanged and is that of the original action. P7 p. 499 (2.6) displays the product of these operators; (2.7) composes its Gaussian transformations and moves its cutoffs and actions. P7 p. 500 (2.8) records the resulting integration variables, and the paragraph after (2.10) says that the spin variables are not renormalized. Therefore forming the window integral freezes every spin outside its integration set at its value in the original density. It does not set a newly exposed layer-zero site equal to a convenient $\psi_0$ coordinate or to the later reference field.

The physical collar datum is the value read by the original density after its exterior variables have been frozen. In the notation of P7 (2.46), the **required trace identity** is

$$\Psi^{\rm ph}(c)=\left[Q^*(G,\mathbb B_k)\psi\right](c),\qquad c\in C_G,\tag{2.1}$$

provided the $Q^*$ lift is defined at this released collar site. This is the explicit kernel-level prescription, not a proved sitewise formula: the link between that lift and the datum of every original boundary bond is CB-1. Here $\psi$ is the multiscale configuration appearing in each original action of (2.6) before its integration. Evaluating (2.1) requires the actual P2/P7 $Q^*$ kernel on the parent piece supplying $c$: $P_0=\mathbb B_{k_1}(W_{k_1})$ in $Z_{k_1}$, $P_1=\mathbb B_k$ in $Z\setminus Z_{k_1}$, or $P_2=\mathbb B_k(U_k)$ in $\widetilde U_k\setminus Z$. On a common surviving fine site it reads $\psi_0(c)$; on a common parent layer-$p$ block it reads its inherited spin $\psi_p(b_p(c))$. On a newly exposed site where the $Q^*$ kernel interpolates, it reads that kernel's conditional average of the parent spins/fine spins. This is an explicit functional prescription from the original operator, but **not a closed sitewise formula for all glued collar sites**: P7 (2.13) identifies the three interior layer pieces, not a partition of $C_G$ or an overlap-value rule, and P2 p. 633 (4.9) states $Q$ on new/common blocks rather than the needed $Q^*$ trace on every released collar site. Giving the apparent formula $\psi_{n_i(c)}(b_{n_i(c)}(c))$ on all three pieces would assume the missing kernel identity. P5 p. 488 (1.4) defines a single-scale $Q^*$ lift; combined with P2's block convention, it allows a collar to read a parent $k$-block spin. Thus the blanket choice $\psi_0(c)$ needs a separate bridge. This is CB-1 of `COLLAR-3.md`, not settled by the operator rearrangement.

The later reference datum is definite. P7 p. 508 (2.40) and P2 p. 636 (4.19)–(4.21), equivalently `Background/Composition.lean:composeField_of_collar`, give

$$\Psi^0(c)=\bar\phi_k^0(c)=
\begin{cases}\phi_k^0(c),&c\in\Omega_1(G'),\\
\widetilde\psi_k^0(b_k(c)),&c\notin\Omega_1(G').\end{cases}\tag{2.2}$$

Here $\phi_k^0=\phi_k(G';\widetilde\psi_k^0)$. Formula (2.2) is a different construction from (2.1). P7 p. 509 proves (2.47)–(2.48) by taking a point in a *window block*, writing its inherited value as $\psi_j(y)$, and comparing it with (2.40). It proves no equality on $C_G$. The cutoffs in $X_{\rm win}$ test selected old/new blocks, scale-$k$ and scale-$k_1$ regions, and fluctuations. As recorded in `COLLAR-3.md` §2, no printed support inclusion assigns **every** collar site to a tested block and gives its difference from (2.2) at the $\widehat W_k\delta_{k_1}$ scale. The coarse bound $|\Psi^{\rm ph}-\Psi^0|<4$ follows from two real (R2) data domains, if the physical datum is proved to be in that domain; it exceeds the small perturbation threshold $\delta_w$ of `WeightedProp51S`. Thus neither a common-collar proof nor a usable collar estimate follows from P5 (2.47), P6 (3.2), P7 (2.1)–(2.7), (2.13), or the printed window tests. The abstract collar-spin variation in `COLLAR-2.md` §3 refutes inference from (2.48) alone, but is not a full P7-admissible counterexample. This is the precise unresolved CB-1/CB-2/CB-3 status.

For the **mass-only** edge of scheme A, use the same $D^{\rm ph}$ in both variational problems; the collar equality then holds by definition and (1.1) applies. For the **physical-to-reference** edge used in P7's window expansion, use (2.1) and (2.2); equality must be proved from the original kernel or its difference must be estimated. These two edges must not be conflated. An exact square decomposition is

$$A_{m,Y}(D^{\rm ph},\phi^0(D^{\rm ph}))-A_{m,Y}(D^0,\phi^m(D^0))
=\underbrace{A_{m,Y}(D^{\rm ph},\phi^0(D^{\rm ph}))-A_{m,Y}(D^{\rm ph},\phi^m(D^{\rm ph}))}_{\text{chord plus wall (1.1)}}
+\underbrace{A_{m,Y}(D^{\rm ph},\phi^m(D^{\rm ph}))-A_{m,Y}(D^0,\phi^m(D^0))}_{\text{data change, including collar}}.\tag{2.3}$$

All cutoffs, inherited terms, measures and normalization are outside (2.3) and need their own operator comparison.

## 3. Correct response statement $(2.49')$ and the interface clause

Set $A\subset I$ to the sites where the window-block estimate is proved; $T=I\setminus A$, and $S=T\cup\{x\in I:x\sim c\text{ for some }c\in C_G\}$. Let $\tau(n)=C_{23}\widehat W_k\delta_{\max(k_1,\min(n,k))}$, $B\ge\sup_{T\cup C_G}|\Psi^{\rm ph}-\Psi^0|$, and $\gamma=\operatorname{gammaW}(d)$. Define

$$P(x)=\sup_{z\in A}\tau(n(z))e^{-\gamma d_G(x,z)}+B\sup_{s\in S}e^{-\gamma d_G(x,s)}.\tag{3.1}$$

**Conditional corrected P7 $(2.49')$.** Suppose both *actual* data objects on $I\cup C_G$ are in the required P2 background domains, the full difference obeys $|\Delta\Psi(z)|\le\tau(n(z))$ on $A$ and $\le B$ on $T\cup C_G$, and all `WeightedProp51S` premises (`Prop51Hyp`, `OpSetting`, zero base defect, smallness including $B\le\delta_w$, and the perturbed equation) hold. Then each of $|\delta\phi|$, $\ell|\partial\delta\phi|$, $\ell^2|\Delta\delta\phi|$, and $\ell^2|\delta\alpha|$ is bounded by

$$\mathcal R(x)\le K_wc_F(d,L)P(x).\tag{2.49c}$$

At $x\in A$ of layer $j$, if the adjacent-layer profile satisfies `LayerProfile` with constants $A_0,R$ and $R\le e^{\gamma(\varrho-1)}$, this becomes

$$\mathcal R(x)\le K_wc_F(d,L)\left[A_0R\,C_{23}\widehat W_k\delta_j+B e^{-\gamma d_G(x,S)}\right].\tag{2.49d}$$

The proof is `COLLAR-3.md` §4: `DeltaPhiResp.lean:pertSource_local` bounds the source at each site by its own data difference and adjacent collar differences; the positive envelope $P+\eta$ defines an admissible Agmon weight by the triangle inequality; `WeightedProp51S` gives (2.49c), and `DeltaPhiWeight.lean:ratio_decay_le` gives (2.49d). Letting $\eta\downarrow0$ finishes. `lemma41_uncond` treats a supported fixed-mass data perturbation, and `layered_response` is the special case $B\le\tau(1)$; neither proves the premise $B\le\delta_w$. If CB-0 gives common collar, set $B=0$ only when the lower-layer bad set $T$ is also empty; otherwise retain its amplitude separately. If CB-2 gives $B\le C_\partial\widehat W_k\delta_{k_1}$ and the missing lower layers are covered, set $C_*=\max(C_{23},C_\partial)$ and apply `layered_response` with the *full* `DataChangeBound`. Recovering P7's exponential in the distance to $(\Omega_k^c)^\sim$ requires a further geometric comparison of that set with $S$, especially at $\operatorname{st}(Z)$; it is not in the landed theorem. P7 p. 511 (2.58)–(2.60) uses the response on every layer of $X_{\rm win}$ and both named surfaces, so a bound only far from the collar does not close its proof.

**Conditional scheme-A regional interface.** For each local region $Y$ and identical physical datum, combine (1.3) with its inherited-energy and observable differences. With the constants and segment-domain hypotheses of `INTERFACE-A.md` §2, the resulting local exponent satisfies the precise *input form*

$$|J_{k,Y}(g)|\le\tfrac12C_HK_m^2B_h^2\beta_km_k^2|Y|_k
 +K_mB_h\beta_km_k\sum_{b\in\operatorname{st}(Y)}r_{k,b}
 +C_EE_0\frac{m_k}{\varepsilon_k}\,v_{k,Y}
 +C_Fc_9\xi_k^{\rho_F}\frac{m_k}{\varepsilon_k}\|g\|_1v_{k,Y}
 +K_mm_k\|g\|_1v_{k,Y}+|J^{\rm dat}_{k,Y}|+|J^{\rm cut,meas}_{k,Y}|.\tag{3.2}$$

Here $v_{k,Y}$ is the verified support volume in new $k+1$ cubes; the third and fourth coefficients require *localized* $E,F$ comparisons, not just the whole-torus estimates of `INTERFACE-A.md` (2.3)–(2.4); $J^{\rm dat}$ is the second edge of (2.3) if the P7 reference is used; and $J^{\rm cut,meas}$ contains cutoff, reference-measure and denominator changes. Formula (3.2) is an exact bookkeeping reduction plus conditional estimates, **not** a proved bound on the complete $R$ operator. If the entire right side sums to $(q_0+q_1\log\beta_k)|X_{Z'}|_{k+1}$ with constants fixed before row 14, add $q_0$ to $c_2$, $q_1$ to $c_1$, and enlarge (S0)/(S5) bounds as their verified comparison requires. Lemma II.5.12 then retains its scalar budget argument with the later $\lambda_*$; (S2) still requires a uniform majorant for *all old variables* and the Gaussian/cutoff integration of Definition II.5.7. No claim of an $e^{-\kappa_Dd(Y)}$ chord or of the $2\kappa-1$ exponent follows from (3.2). The real fixed-unit $h$ frozen-root statement needs these real estimates and analyticity in the observable source $g$; it does not acquire a mass-uniform transverse complex-$h$ domain.
