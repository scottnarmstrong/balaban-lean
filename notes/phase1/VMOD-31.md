## 2. The cover and the fixed-support count

**REFUTED for $K\ge2$: a proper nonempty top-only domain.** If $F.\Omega_j=T$ for every $1\le j\le K$, $F.\Omega_{K+1}=\varnothing$, and $T$ is a proper nonempty subset of the connected torus, choose adjacent $x\notin T$, $y\in T$. At $j=K-1$, `SupSep F m₁` (`P6PairCover.lean:67`) demands $\operatorname{supDist}(x,y)>4L^{K+m_1}$, although it is $1$. The generating-set separation also fails for a positive separation parameter. Merely taking “the region near $W_k$ and the halo” as $T$ is therefore invalid. At $K=1$ that particular separation clause is vacuous, but the uniform construction below avoids a separate case.

**PROVED mathematical construction; Lean declaration OPEN.** Define $F^{\rm top}$ on the **whole cover torus** by $F^{\rm top}.\Omega_j=\mathrm{univ}$ for $1\le j\le K$ and $F^{\rm top}.\Omega_{K+1}=\varnothing$ (set irrelevant indices to $\varnothing$). Give it any separation parameter $\varrho_F>0$, chosen with $d\le\varrho_FL^2$ for the generic degree theorem. `GeneratingSet` nestedness, block union, and separation (`Multiscale/GeneratingSet.lean:60-76`) are immediate; separation and `SupSep` are vacuous. `CubeAligned` (`Localization/CubeAligned.lean:128`) follows from $m_1\ge2$ and $L^{m_1}\mid N_1L^{m_1}$. Its only nonempty layer is $K$; every index is a $b$-cell base. Both $H^-\prec F^{\rm top}$ and $H^\partial\prec F^{\rm top}$ hold by inclusion into `univ`, with no old-halo comparison. There may be cover indices far outside either operator's $\Omega_1$; `CoverGeom` asks for its partition and bounds only on the operator domain, so these indices are allowed.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the same numerical premises as `p6_coverGeom_old/boundary`; Lean wrappers OPEN.** Apply `p6_coverGeom_of_pair` to $(F^{\rm top},H^-)$ and $(F^{\rm top},H^\partial)$ to obtain `p6_coverGeom_top_old` and `p6_coverGeom_top_boundary`, each with $Cx=\texttt{Cchi}\ d\ L$. Apply `Dec2.coverBase_mixed` with $N_1>5$ to obtain `p6_coverBase_top_old/boundary` with the same $K_c=\texttt{mixedKc}\ d\ L$; `Dec2.footLocalGeom_coverOf` then supplies the generic foot support field. No new DG or NC proof is required. The two operators need only their own `OpSetting` and the common cover geometry; this also covers a physical Dirichlet wall through a cover box.

**PROVED conditional geometry (Lean wrapper OPEN).** For *every* top cell $U$, including one wholly in a new hole, every cell Moore-adjacent to $U$ lies in $F^{\rm top}.\Omega_K=\mathrm{univ}$. The landed general `Localization/P6ActiveHalo.lean:151` theorem `root_patch_count`, with $L^{m_0}\mid N_1$, therefore gives
$$\#\{P\in\operatorname{cubeIdx}F^{\rm top}:\operatorname{MooreTouch}(U,\texttt{Pset}\ m_1\ P)\}\le b_{\rm root}:=3^dA'.\tag{2.1}$$
The general `Localization/MooreDegree.lean:271` theorem `mooreGraph_degree_le` gives degree $b_g:=3(L+4)^d$ when $d\le\varrho_FL^2$; this is automatically arrangeable because the separation field of $F^{\rm top}$ is vacuous. `LedgerPacking.lean:111` `card_idx_in_cells_le` remains available and bounds $A'$ indices per top cell. Hence the flat-wall family of $L^{k(d-2)}$ layer-1 patches from VMOD-26 (4.1a) is absent: $F^{\rm top}$ has **no** layer-1 patches when $K>1$. Even on a hole-side face, a charged top source cell has at most $3^dA'$ incident patches, independent of $k$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `OPEN-P6-SOURCE-SIZE-BRIDGE`, `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE`, and `P6_COEF_ROOTED_XB` for the actual P6 coefficient.** Let $B$ be the chosen old source cover and $A_X$ the active top cells. VMOD-25 (1.1) proves mathematically $|B|\le3^dL^d(1+\Delta_X)$ and at most $c_B(1+\Delta_X)$ top cells in $B$, $c_B=3^dL^{2d}$; its Lean overlap wrapper remains open. Under the named source-size bridge, $|A_X|\le c_A(1+\Delta_X)$, $c_A=L^d[3(2L-1)]^d$. Put $R=A_X\cup\{\hbox{top cells in }B\}$, $c_R=c_A+c_B$, $D_*=\max\{b_g+1,b_{\rm root}\}$, $z=D_*^2e^{-\kappa_2}<1$, $h=-\log(1-z)$, $H=e^{hc_R}$, and $q=hc_R$. The first inequality follows by dropping the fixed-support restriction; the second uses $|R|\le c_R(1+\Delta_X)$. **PROVED mathematical construction; Lean declaration OPEN.** For fixed floor depth $r_0\ge0$ (called $n_0$ in the task, distinct from the torus parameter `n0`), put $J=\max(1,K-r_0)$ and let $F^{\rm floor}.\Omega_j=\mathrm{univ}$ for $1\le j\le J$, $F^{\rm floor}.\Omega_j=F^\sharp.\Omega_j$ for $J<j\le K$, and $\Omega_{K+1}=\varnothing$.
