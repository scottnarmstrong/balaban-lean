**OPEN-P6-TOP-COMPOSITE (continued).** On the common parameter vector $\sigma_s$, obtain $(\phi_b(s),\alpha_b(s))$ from `Dec2.decBgField`/`decBgMult C_t H_b` (`Decoupled2Background.lean:66,74`), form `flEdat H_b H_m (φ_b,α_b)` and the old fluctuation solution using `Dec2.decG C_t H_m` (`Decoupled2Solve.lean:46`) and `Dec2.DecFixed`. `PairedLocalAgree.pairedRaw` (`PairedLocalAgree.lean:260`) is the landed **two-field raw-data composition** for these selectors. Write $\Psi_s$ for the resulting composed first spin on $B(\Lambda_{k+1})$, with the old $A_k$ spins in $X\cap\Omega_{k+1}^c$, $h$, and $g$ held as external arguments. The planned scalar is
$$\mathcal F_{A,X,\mathcal B}(u,s)=\texttt{rep.B}\ A\ X\bigl(\text{old multilevel spin with its first spin replaced by }\Psi_s,\;\text{old }A_k\text{ spin},\;h,\;g\bigr).\tag{1.1}$$

**OPEN-P6-COEF-ROOTED-XB, exact target.** For each admissible complex datum $u$, $J\subset\iota_X$, let $a_{A,X,\mathcal B,J}(u)=\texttt{Localization.coef}(\mathcal F_{A,X,\mathcal B}(u,\cdot))J$ (`I5Param.lean`/`AbstractExpansion.lean`). Give $\iota_X$ the graph $P\sim Q$ iff `LedgerCounter.MooreTouch (Pset m₁ P) (Pset m₁ Q)`. Then
$$a_{A,X,\mathcal B,J}(u)\ne0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\forall\text{ components }J_0\text{ of }J,\ \exists P\in J_0,\ U\in R_X:
\operatorname{MooreTouch}(\texttt{Pset}\ m_1\ P,U).\tag{1.2}$$
For $J=\varnothing$ the conclusion is vacuous. The quantifiers are for **both** pair operators, every valid history/source cover, and every datum on the common strict H7 polydisc. (1.2) is stronger than merely saying every incidence-graph component touches $U_X$; the Moore projection has its own proof obligation.

## 2. Zero-parameter factorization and where the proof stops

**PROVED conditional matrix factorization.** For $J\subset\iota_X$, put $\sigma_P=0$ on $\iota_X\setminus J$, $\sigma_P=1$ on nonparameter seed patches, and allow arbitrary values on $J$ in the permitted disc. By `DecCover.sProd_ne_zero_iff` (`Decoupled2Geometry.lean:110`), a live centre $z$ contributes to either $G_s^{H_b}$ or $G_s^{H_m}$ only if every parameter patch in $C_t.Qset(z)$ outside $U_X$ lies in $J$; this is `Dec2.EligC` (`Decoupled2FpGeometry.lean:153`). For each operator separately, take a site set $Y$ that is a union of components of its site–centre–parameter incidence graph. Then every active centre's whole operator footprint is in $Y$ or misses $Y$ (`IsIncUnion.dichotomy`, `Decoupled2FpGeometry.lean:188`). The block projection argument inside `Gs_local_fp` (`Decoupled2FpLocal.lean:32`) yields, for fixed admissible $\alpha$ and invertible $1-\mathcal K_s$,
$$P_Y^{H}G_s^{H}(\alpha)(1-P_Y^{H})=(1-P_Y^{H})G_s^{H}(\alpha)P_Y^{H}=0,
\qquad H\in\{H_b,H_m\}.\tag{2.1}$$
The same conclusion follows by applying `Gs_local_fp` to sources agreeing on $Y$ and then its complement. **Not a product formula for the old boundary functional:** (2.1) is a block decomposition of each Green operator. At the old wall the two projections use $Y\cap H_b.\Omega_1$ and $Y\cap H_m.\Omega_1$; no inclusion $H_m\prec H_b$ is used. `Dec2.dg5_local` needs `HatConn` and the older `CoverLocalGeom` would require every full-torus patch to lie in $H.\Omega_1$, generally false. The relevant landed route is `FootLocal`, `dg5'_inner_fp` (`Decoupled2FpLocal.lean:277`), `RowsFl.RowF_SYF_subset_Y` (`RowsFl.lean:88`), and `PairedLocalAgree.paired_fields_agree` (`PairedLocalAgree.lean:152`).

**PROVED finite-graph construction, Lean wrapper OPEN-TOP-JOINT-INCIDENCE.** Make one finite graph with common site and parameter vertices, separately labelled live centres for $H_b,H_m$, all edges of their `incGraph`s, and an edge from each selected parameter $P\in J$ to each site of its $b$-cell `cubeP P`. Let $Y_J$ and $D_J$ be the site and parameter projections of the components reachable from the seed $U_X$. By closure under edges, $(Y_J,D_J)$ satisfies `IsIncUnion` for **both** operators; by the extra edges, every $P\in D_J$ has its entire patch in $Y_J$. This discharges the *graph shape* and *cell* hypotheses of `PairedLocalAgree.paired_locality` (`PairedLocalAgree.lean:299`), but not its selector, data-domain, and affine read-dependency hypotheses. Using one graph is necessary because the two physical walls give different incidence graphs.

**PROVED top-grid projection, conditional on OPEN-TOP-FOOTPRINT-HAT; Lean wrapper OPEN-TOP-MOORE-PROJECTION.** For a top centre $z$, $C_t.Qset(z)$ consists of **all** aligned $b$-cells meeting the axis-aligned `hatBox z` (`DecCover.lean:35,59`), hence is Moore-connected on the torus. If $t\in\texttt{footprint}\ H\ a\nu\ z\subset\texttt{hatBox}\ z$, its $b$-cell belongs to $Qset(z)$. Since $z$ is eligible, every $Qset(z)$ cell outside $U_X$ is selected in $J$. A path inside $Qset(z)$ from an incident parameter patch to the cell of $t$ therefore runs through selected Moore-neighbouring patches until it reaches $U_X$, if it reaches the seed at all. Adjacent site vertices have identical or Moore-neighbouring $b$-cells. Projecting a path in the joint incidence graph from $U_X$ to a parameter $P$ gives a Moore path from $P$ to a cell of $U_X$ through selected patches, allowing a return to $U_X$ between components. Thus every reached Moore component meets a root in $R_X$. The needed `footprint\subset hatBox` is plausible directly from `dirichletOperator` rows (same $H$-block or nearest neighbour, $n_H\le K$, and `hatRad-intRad=3L^2L^K`); **it is not a landed conclusion**: `Vmod7Local.lean:312,440` takes it as a premise. The torus-grid connectivity and its precise `MooreTouch` translation are also not landed. No claim about an actual coefficient follows from this geometry alone.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `OPEN-TOP-FOOTPRINT-HAT` AND `OPEN-TOP-MOORE-PROJECTION`: component factorization.** For a Moore component $J_0$ of $J$ having no patch Moore-adjacent to a cell of $R_X$, every eligible centre whose $Qset$ meets $J_0$ has all of its parameter patches in $J_0$: its connected $Qset$ contains only selected patches unless it meets $U_X$, which would root $J_0$. Its footprint lies over those same patches. Nearest-neighbour site edges cannot leave the corresponding joint-graph component without making a Moore adjacency between two selected patches or meeting $U_X$. Hence the joint graph splits into the seed component, the components of $J$ missing the seed, and the remaining site components. Formula (2.1) applies to each split for **both** $H_b$ and $H_m$, using their respective intersections with $\Omega_1$ at the wall. This is the precise zero-parameter mixed Green factorization; the later nonlinear field and covariance-square-root factorization still require the named selector/locality hypotheses.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `OPEN-P6-TOP-READ-SPLIT`, `OPEN-P6-TOP-COV-SQRT-LOCAL`, `OPEN-P6-TOP-SELECTORS`, `OPEN-TOP-JOINT-INCIDENCE`, and `OPEN-TOP-MOORE-PROJECTION`.** If every $s$-dependent read in (1.1), including the mixed covariance square root, is at a site of $U_X$ or on its proven row/collar dependency set, the paired locality theorem makes $\mathcal F(u,s)$ independent of parameters in a detached joint-graph component when other free parameters vanish. ## 4. The physical output-domain obstruction

**PROVED source check.** P6 (4.20), (4.24)–(4.25), printed pp. 30–31, requires terms localized on $Y$ in the **new** region class and analytic on its localized generating set. For the old boundary term, P6 p. 34 explicitly invokes P3 (4.44)–(4.50) and concludes $Y\in\mathcal D_K(\mathrm{mod}\,\Omega_K^c)$ with $X\subset Y$. P3 (4.44), printed p. 78, assigns each marked auxiliary cube in physical layer $j$ to boundedly many $\pi_j$ cubes **inside that layer**; this is why each component is a domain of the new generating set. It then builds $\mathbb B_K(Y)$ for the analytic space. The checked PDF page makes that layer-by-layer assignment explicit. In Lean, `Localization.PolymerGeometry.Dcubes/Dsites` (:278-291) requires a $\pi'_j$ cube containing `cellOf q` inside `G.layer q.1`; `gm1` (:433) and `localization_expansion` (`I5Expansion.lean:107`) require $c(i)\in\texttt{cubeIdx}\ G$ for that **same output generating set**.

**REFUTED as an automatic transfer.** With $G=F^{\rm top}$, the direct landed output labels are `polymerDomains Ftop m₀ U_X` and $Y=\texttt{Ysites}\ F^{\rm top}\ m_0\ c\ U_X\ J$: connected unions of top-scale $D(P)$ cells in the full torus (`PolymerGeometry.lean:280,421`; `I5Expansion.lean:65`). They are **not automatically** `Fluctuation.DjMod m₀ K Ωnew Y`, nor `LargeField.bdryDoms (OmegaCube A.H K) Z`: take a proper connected new hole larger than one local $D(P)$, a top patch near its boundary, and a seed outside it; $Y$ can meet a proper subset of that hole, violating `DjMod`'s every-hole-component all-or-none clause (`SizeModDefs.lean:84`). Adding the old $X$ and completing every touched new hole might repair membership, but a bounded multiplicity, the modular size transfer, localized H7 data, and $X\subset Y$ then need a **new** `OPEN-P6-TOP-OUTPUT-DOMAIN/GM2` theorem. A top parameter cube lying in a lower physical layer is not an index of that layer of the physical new generating set, so the P3 (4.44) assignment and the landed `Dsites`/`gm1` theorem cannot be reused. The next step's landed `D7Bdry` input class is `bdryDoms` (`Def7Hist.lean:285-299`; `Def7.lean:368`), so direct $F^{\rm top}$ labels do not meet its binder. This is a real source dependence even if (1.2) is eventually proved.
