## 2. The fixed-$Y$ generating set and the source replacement

**PROVED.** For a finite union $Y$ of aligned $K$-blocks, instantiate `LargeField/Background/MinimalGenSet.lean` with $q=j=K$, $M_1=L^{m_1}$, $N_1$ as here, and $X=Y$. Then `minimalGenSet (le_refl K) Y` has $\Omega_K=\operatorname{cubeInterior}_K(Y)=Y$ by `minLayer_self` and block union, $\Omega_n=\operatorname{nearStep}_n(\Omega_{n+1})$ for $n<K$ by `minLayer_of_lt`, and separation $\rho_Y=5LM_1=5L^{m_1+1}$. `nearStep` adds the $n$-cubes at index sup-distance at most $5L$. This is P3 (4.4)–(4.6) in the full-level case; P3 printed pp. 78–79, PDF `sources/BF02506385.pdf` pp. 46–47, prescribes additional intersections with original lower layers in the general mixed-level case. `GeneratingSet.single` and `SmallField.Def6.levelGS` have whole-torus top domains. `GeneratingSet.addTop` can give an arbitrary prescribed top block union $A$ only after proving $A\subset\Omega_k$ and distance $>\rho L^k$ from $\Omega_k^c$; `p6Hold/p6Fsharp` discharge these for their specific `stepW/p6Halo`, not for arbitrary $Y$.

**REFUTED (landed engine applies for free).** `minimalGenSet` has $\rho_Y=5LL^{m_1}$, but `BgSetting.hr` requires $r_0L^{m_1}\le\rho_Y-1$ and `hr₀` requires $r_0\ge4dL^3$. These imply $4dL^3L^{m_1}\le5LL^{m_1}-1$, impossible for $d,L\ge3$. The same `Gp[Y]` also fails `IsLevel Gp K`, required by `HatEngineLift.liftC`, `HatEngineLocalEngine.i5_functional_hat`, `InteractionHatV/E/F/Nu` and parts of the physical bounds. A wider `nearStep` constructor with separation at least the source $\rho$ (or a separate local hypothesis that avoids `BgSetting.hr`) and mixed-level hat lift/energy statements are **OPEN**. Merely changing the separation type of `minimalGenSet` cannot prove a false inequality.

**PROVED (what P3 actually says).** P3 (4.44)–(4.47) forms $Y$ by enlarging the components of $Y_1$, replaces the generating set *inside each pre-resummation term* by $G_p[Y]$, and then sums over $C$ with fixed $Y$. It states equality of its background function when the two generating sets agree on $Y_1\cap\Omega_K$ and cut parameters agree inside $Y_1$ and vanish on the relevant outside parameter cubes. No raw-data zero extension appears. For this hatted step the proposed definition is therefore $F_C^Y(D_Y,\hat\psi_Y)=\operatorname{coef}_C(F^{G_p[Y]}(D_Y,\hat\psi_Y,s))$, using the **same physical formula**, local cover, and `extParams` convention, with the top-unit coordinates indexed by `unitPts Gp[Y]` (identified with `blocksOf Y`). Its target identity is
$$F_C^Y(D|_{G_p[Y]},\hat\psi|_{\operatorname{blocksOf}Y})=F_C^{G_p}(D,\hat\psi)\quad\text{for every globally admissible }D,\hat\psi.\tag{R}$$
Here $D|_{G_p[Y]}$ needs a restriction lemma into `BgData Gp[Y]` and the local admissible domain; equality of raw restrictions at source sites is insufficient to establish admissibility. The hat restriction is well typed because `DecoupledCovUnit.unitPts` uses only the top layer. The source establishes its background counterpart; (R) for $V,E,F,\nu/\mathscr M$ is **OPEN**.
