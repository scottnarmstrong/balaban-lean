### Why strict H7 cannot supply the Wiener part

Let $N\ge1$, $\omega=e^{2\pi i/N}$ and $R>1$. On the $2N$-variable closed radius-$R$ polydisc put
$$F_N(s,t)=R^{-2}N^{-3/2}\sum_{i,j=0}^{N-1}\omega^{ij}s_it_j.\tag{1.7}$$
Finite Fourier orthogonality gives $\sum_i|\sum_j\omega^{ij}t_j|^2=N\sum_j|t_j|^2$; Cauchy–Schwarz with $\sum_i|s_i|^2,\sum_j|t_j|^2\le NR^2$ gives $\sup|F_N|\le1$. For the AE coefficient `Localization.coef` of the multilinear polynomial, the coefficient of the set $\{s_i,t_j\}$ is $R^{-2}N^{-3/2}\omega^{ij}$ and all other nonconstant support coefficients vanish. Hence, for every fixed $\eta$,
$$\sum_C e^{\eta|C|}|a_C(F_N)|=R^{-2}e^{2\eta}\sqrt N\longrightarrow\infty.\tag{1.8}$$
This is a **PROVED finite analytic counterexample** to any torus/parameter-uniform deduction of (1.4) from a strict H7-type supremum and the individual `abstract_expansion_on` bound. It also satisfies a vacuous `LocalityAEOn` if its data set is one point, `Ag` is equality and every `Yc C` is the whole carrier; so AE's formal locality premise alone does not rescue the sum. It is not an actual P6 action: the P6 cover may impose extra sparsity or a tree norm. Such a theorem is the missing content of `OPEN-P6-SITE-MIXED-RESPONSE`. Calling that content a Cauchy estimate would be false. The marked-read and pure-step site bounds are independent additional clauses; neither follows from (1.8) or from pointwise H7 control.
