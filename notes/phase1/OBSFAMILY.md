## T1. Target theorem

**PROVED (index and normalization).** `StepComplexOut.lean:M0C` is a function of `g`, site `y`, spin `i`, and contains `((S.betaU)^(1/2):ℂ)` times the interpolation integral. `StepComplexThm31.lean:thm31bC_of_input_M0` identifies that integral with a localized coefficient sum for a **fixed complex step** and source; `Thm31CoeffB.lean:obsCoeff` gives the canonical coefficient formula, and `thm31b_coeff_M0` proves its value for the real step. The new physical complex-step theorem must identify its coefficients with this same canonical formula, then use `((Sθ.betaU)^(-1/2):ℂ) * Sθ.M0C C g y i`, where `Sθ := S.withDatum (renThetaDatum S e D ξ) hθh`. This explicitly distinguishes the datum of the original step `S.D'`, the variable class datum `D`, and the *theta datum* `Dθ := renThetaDatum S e D ξ`. `RenormThetaDatum.lean:renThetaDatum_h` proves `Dθ.h=D.h`, so the fibre proof `hθh` exists; `renThetaDatum_zero` makes `Sθ = S.withDatum D hDh` at `ξ=0`.

**OPEN — precise theorem shape.** For `R : StepTowerRenSol κmin`, an admissible `k,N₁,ν,β,p₁`, `S := R.toStepTower.step k hk N₁ ν β p₁`, `C := zeroClass …`, a class radius `0<e≤R.t`, and `r₀=R.r₀`, assume `ThetaChartMargin 3 3 2 e εstep r₀` and `εstep≤R.t` (W482's useful case `e=R.t/2`). Set `G:=S.G'`, `Src:=Fld 3 3 N₁ R.m₁ (k+1) 2`, `Uq : Set Src := {g | ∑ y, ∑ i, ‖g y i‖ < q}`, `Dom D := XiCOneReal G S.a S.ν e D ∧ ∀ x i, D.h x i=(S.h₀ i:ℂ)`, and `Q := expDom G Dom r₀ ×ˢ Uq`. The intended conclusion, after `0<q≤1` and a uniform scale threshold, is one family `termM` **chosen before** `D,ξ,g`:

```lean
∃ termM : (p : G.Ω 1 × Fin 2) →
    Finset (Torus 3 (N₁ * 3^R.m₁)) →
    ((RawData 3 3 (N₁ * 3^R.m₁) (k+1) 2 × (Fin 3 → ℂ)) × Src) → ℂ,
  (∀ p Y, Y ∈ YsM p →
    AnalyticOnNhd ℂ (termM p Y) Q) ∧
  (∀ p Y, Y ∈ YsM p → ∀ v∈Q, ∀ v'∈Q,
    v.1.2 = v'.1.2 → RawAgree Y v.1.1 v'.1.1 →
    (∀ y∈Y, v.2 y = v'.2 y) → termM p Y v = termM p Y v') ∧
  (∀ p Y, Y ∈ YsM p → ∀ v∈Q,
    ‖termM p Y v‖ ≤ BM0 * Real.exp (-κb * dY Y)) ∧
  ∀ p D, Dom D → ∀ ξ, ‖ξ‖ < r₀ → ∀ g∈Uq,
    let Dθ := renThetaDatum S e D ξ
    let Sθ := S.withDatum Dθ (by simpa [renThetaDatum_h] using (And.right ‹Dom D›))
    ((Sθ.betaU ^ (-(1/2 : ℝ)) : ℝ) : ℂ) * Sθ.M0C C g p.1 p.2 =
      ∑ Y ∈ YsM p, termM p Y ((rawOf D, ξ), g)
```

Here `YsM p` is the site-root `renYs` cover at `p`, `dY=renDY`, and `κb≤2R.κdec` is the chosen output rate. The displayed binder names and `by` cast are specification notation: the implementation should use a named `hDh : ∀ x i, D.h x i=(S.h₀ i:ℂ)` and `hθh` to avoid elaborating a proof inside `let`. The term type may use the project’s exact `G`-indexed `RawData`/site aliases. Add support/anchor `termM p Y=0` when the site root is absent, and a zero-chart identity for the same chosen coefficients at `ξ=0`. **Do not** replace the right side by an arbitrary analytic extension: it must equal the physical `M0C` of `Sθ` at every fibre datum and every allowed `ξ`. The `IsLocExp` part of W486's `RenormExpInputOn` follows by fixing `g`; source locality is an additional clause needed for H7/Root-F. For the entire unit ball choose `q=1` and `Uq=l1Ball`; `q<1` is sufficient only for the correlation-only version.

## T4. Root boundary

**PROVED.** `Frozen/Roots/LowTemperatureCorrelations.lean:lowTemperature_correlations` states limits and bounds of the Gibbs one-point and truncated two-point functions for unit real `h`, and has **no source-ball binder**. `Correlations/Identity.lean:onePoint_eq_of_clauses` uses the first source derivative at `g=0` in direction `sourceDelta x i`; `twoPointTruncated_eq_of_clauses` uses the mixed second derivative at `g=0` in directions `sourceDelta x i` and `sourceDelta y k` (`Model/Derivatives.lean`). A uniform Cauchy bound on any fixed ball `0<q<1`, together with a local analytic identity there and the needed volume/scale estimates, is enough for those derivatives. This alone does not establish the remaining thermodynamic/ν limits and covariance estimates of Root-C.

**PROVED / OPEN.** `Frozen/Roots/LogPartitionMultiscale.lean:logPartition_multiscale` requires each `T` analytic on the **full open unit** `ℓ¹` source ball and the representation identity for every `‖g‖₁<1`; its coefficient bound is only imposed on `‖g‖₁≤1/2`. A proof on one fixed `q<1` cannot discharge that statement. W465 allows `q=1`, but the future marked coefficient construction must preserve that full open domain with one coherent analytic family; alternatively prove compatible families for every `q<1` and glue them on overlaps. Uniform numerical bounds up to `q=1` and compatible choices are additional work, not consequences of fixed-source `ObsMarks`.
