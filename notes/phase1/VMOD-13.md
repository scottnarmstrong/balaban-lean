\operatorname{Dec2.HatConn}(C,F),\\
\Omega_1(H)\subset\Omega_1(F),\qquad

\end{gathered}\tag{1.1}$$

The conclusion is an `IsIncUnion` for the *operator* $H$, plus $P\in A_Y\Rightarrow P\in\mathcal C$ and $\operatorname{cubeP}(P)\subset Y$. **`RowInHat` for both operators: proved conditional on the explicit layer comparison.** Let $q=(m,z)\in C.\operatorname{cubeIdx}$, $x\in\operatorname{intBox}(q)\cap\Omega_1(H)$, and assume
$$n_H(x)\le m+1.\tag{1.2}$$
An off-diagonal nonzero entry of $H_D(\alpha)(x,y)$ is either a nearest-neighbor bond or a site $y$ in the same complete $H$ block as $x$; the diagonal case is $y=x$. To obtain (1.2), the P6 domain comparison $n_H\le n_F$ of `VMOD-11.md` (1.5)–(1.7) puts any hypothetical $x$ with $n_H(x)\ge m+2$ in $F.\Omega_{m+2}$. ### 1.1 A physical-wall obstruction to `HatConn`

`Dec2.HatConn C F` is the assertion $\operatorname{ConnIn}(\operatorname{hatBox}(q)\cap\Omega_1(F))$ for **every** $q\in C.\operatorname{cubeIdx}$ (`Decoupled2Geometry.lean:90`). It is not a consequence of a generating-set separation, a halo *inside* the old domain, `CoverLocal`, or mixed NC3. The following is an exact set-level counterexample to those proposed implications.

Work at $k=1$ on a sufficiently large aligned torus and in the first two coordinates of $d\ge3$. Write $r=[R_0]+1$ and choose two nonempty rectangular sets $S_1,S_2$ of $\pi_1$ cells with first-two-coordinate index ranges $[0,t]$ and $[t+4r+1,2t+4r+1]$, respectively; take equal overlapping ranges in the remaining coordinates. Set $S=S_1\cup S_2$ and $W=S^{\approx2}$, using the cyclic sup-neighborhood of radius $2r$ from `LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` I.2 and I.3.1. Then the first two coordinate ranges of the two pieces of $W$ end at $t+2r$ and start at $t+2r+1$. They touch only diagonally and are distinct nearest-neighbor components. Let $U=S^\approx$, and construct $A$ by the prescribed one-$\pi_2$-cell closure and halo. The row-7 bound $r\ge4L+21$ and `VMOD-11.md` (1.3)–(1.4) put $A$ strictly inside $W$, far from the diagonal meeting corner. Thus $F$ formed from $\Omega_1(F)=W$ and $\Omega_2(F)=A$ has the proposed separation and auxiliary-interface margin. A layer-one cover center $q$ in the final $M_1L$ cell of the first piece at the corner is outside $A$. Its hat box reaches the first $M_1L$ cell of the second piece: `hatRad` is $(5/2)M_1L+3L^3$, whereas the two cell bases differ by only $M_1L$ in each of the first two coordinates. The two nonempty intersections of its hat with $W$ cannot be joined by nearest-neighbor sites of $W$. Hence `HatConn C F` fails.

This constructs a valid **minimal-set/halo geometry** from the indicated $S=Z_1^c$ and its exact $\approx$ neighborhoods. It does **not** by itself prove that this $S$ is the output of a full admissible `RegionHistory`: `History/RegionHistory.lean` also constrains $Z_1$ through `FlowSteps`, the chosen record and possible removal. The assertion that a record realizes these two surviving small-field boxes is **OPEN-P6-WALL-REALIZATION**. In particular, the R6 opening has a large collar before $Z_1^{\rm pre}$ is formed (`LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` I.3.1); this may exclude the pictured wall without removal, and must be checked rather than discarded. The landed `Region.Inclusions.thick2_Zpre_compl_disjoint` (`LargeField/Region/Inclusions.lean:291`) concerns $Z^{\rm pre}$, whereas $W_k$ uses the post-removal $Z_k$ when $k\le k_0$. Neither that declaration nor another landed history theorem inspected here establishes all-history `HatConn` for $F$. Therefore the virtual-cover route is not established for all P6 histories, and the question of its failure for an actual history cannot honestly be closed by citing separation alone. This matters already at the allowed $k=k_0=1$ case (`LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` I.1.2 admits $k_0=0$ and $k_0\ge1$).

There is a corresponding obstruction to simply asserting G-site–G-cell. Take the wall geometry above, let $X_1$ contain a cover cell on the first side and miss the second, and let $\mathcal C$ include every charged $P\in C.Qset(q)$ (add a chain of cells on the first side if the anchor is farther away). Then $q$ is `Dec2.EligC` by its definition (`Decoupled2FpGeometry.lean:153`) and is live for $H_-$; `intBox q` meets both physical components, so `Dec2.mem_footprint_self` (`:105`) puts sites of both in its footprint. The NN component $Y$ anchored on the first side excludes the second. Thus $q\in B_{H_-}(Y)$ but $T_{H_-}(q)\nsubseteq Y$: **G-foot is false for this component**. This proves that the four graph conditions of `VMOD-12.md` cannot be derived from the separation/halo properties alone. The selected P6 $X_{1,b}$ and full history realization must be checked before asserting this as a counterexample to the *exact* P6 theorem; that check is part of OPEN-P6-WALL-REALIZATION. If it succeeds, the correct repair is to use incidence-graph components as localization supports and pay for their extra connections in GM2, or to impose an additional local wall-regularity condition proved for the admissible histories. Repeating a direct proof of G-foot for the NN component would be impossible in that instance.

For $k\ge2$ the $5L$ dilation by cells whose side is a multiple of $L$ may exclude this small diagonal configuration at layer one; `DecoupledFieldLocal.hatConn_of_layer_one` (`:65`) only reduces the check to layer-one centers **under** its unavailable strong `DGSetting.cover_sep`. It is not a proof for the minimal P6 set. The exact remaining assertion is **OPEN-P6-HATCONN**, indexed by the actual admissible history, not by an arbitrary generating set.

## 2. The full mixed H7 response

The **landed** `Fluctuation/WeightedProp51.lean:302`, `weightedProp51S_exists`, proves the corrected one-operator weighted Proposition 5.1. Its statement (`:193`) takes an ordinary perturbed Euler–Lagrange system for a *single* $G$, `Dec2.OpSetting G`, zero constraint defect, a source bounded in a weight `IsWeight G (gammaW d)`, and a small perturbed solution. It yields weighted bounds with a constant $K_w(d,L,N)$. `Fluctuation/FarDecayUncond.lean:131`, `lemma41_domain_S`, supplies its one-operator complex-domain application. Neither theorem has a common cover, a parameter $s$, a mixed physical pair, or a bound uniform in $s$. `Dec2.cubeBlockBounds_mixed` supplies the pointwise cut-block field of `CoverBase` only; it does not furnish the weighted inverse/source inequality for the full decoupled resolvent or an open H7 domain. The existing `CubeBlockHolder` theorem is likewise separate (`Localization/DecoupledCubeHolder.lean:88`). This is a type-level reason the requested bound does not follow from those two landed results alone.

Here is the exact useful **conditional implication**. Fix the finite common parameter index $J$, a complex H7 data set $S$, and $P_{\kappa_1}=\{s\in\mathbb C^J:|s_i|<e^{\kappa_1}\}$. For each $H\in\{H_-,H_\partial\}$ and every $(u,s)\in S\times P_{\kappa_1}$ assume:

1. The assembled background data and the old perturbed fluctuation data lie in the **strict** `InDomainC`/`StrictDataM` sets of the landed `Dec2.i4b_analytic` (`Decoupled2Background.lean:308`) and `Dec2.i4a_analytic` (`Decoupled2SolveAnalytic.lean:62`), with uniform positive slack on every closed Cauchy polydisc used by AE; all `DecSpecs`, `FamSpec`, `AnSpec`, numerical, and source hypotheses of those declarations hold for the same $C,a,\nu,K_c,C_x$.
2. The two data changes are supported outside their specified collars, their weighted decoupled sources are at most $C_{\rm src}r_{H,k}$, and a **mixed decoupled weighted response theorem** gives, on the complete read set $R_b$ of each action cube, for value, first differences, second differences and multiplier,
   $$\|w_H(u,s)\|_{*,R_b}\le C_{\rm resp}r_{H,k}e^{-\gamma_wD_k},\qquad
   \gamma_w=\operatorname{gammaW}(d),\quad D_k=M([\hat R_k]-1)-2M_1.\tag{2.1}$$
   The response theorem must include the old fluctuation and the affine composition, as well as both background comparisons. It must retain the same $s$ in all systems.
3. The complex H7 regularity inequalities hold for every block, bond, surface and mass term read by the actual P6 cube action, with first-variation coefficient at most $C_A r_{H,k}$ and quadratic coefficient at most $C_Q$; the action read set lies in the region on which (2.1) holds. At $k\le k_0$ use the $\nu$-free variational equation and retain the linear mass term, with $\nu_{k+1,u}|\phi-h|\le C_{\rm mass}r_{H,k}$ on the fixed-$h$ H7 set. At $k>k_0$ use the complete equation. At $k=k_0$ apply the $\nu$-free estimate before the separate convention switch. `FLUCTUATION.md` §3.6 (3.13a) proves the fixed-$h$ mass arithmetic **conditional on** H7-to-BG inclusion; it does not prove that inclusion.

Let $n_b$ bound the number of scalar action summands in one cube and assume $D_k\ge0$. Expanding each quadratic summand exactly and using (2.1) gives, uniformly in $(u,s)$,
$$\begin{aligned}
|F_b(u,s)|&\le n_b\beta_{k+1,u}\left(C_A r_{H,k}\,C_{\rm resp}r_{H,k}e^{-\gamma_wD_k}
  +C_Q C_{\rm resp}^2r_{H,k}^2e^{-2\gamma_wD_k}\right)\\
&\le C_F'\beta_{k+1,u}r_{H,k}^2e^{-\gamma_wD_k},\qquad
C_F'=n_b(C_A C_{\rm resp}+C_Q C_{\rm resp}^2).\tag{2.2}
\end{aligned}$$
The second inequality uses $D_k\ge0$. The $\nu$-free linear mass coefficient is included in $C_A$, with the separate $C_{\rm mass}$ bound; the surface term is included in the same first variation. This is the general algebra behind `Fluctuation/CubeActionBound.lean:108`, `cubeDiff_bound_of_far`, and `:245`, `step3_arith`. For the *undecoupled complete* action their explicit bound is $\frac12|Q_b|(d+3)N(2rT+T^2)$ and, if $T\le c r e^{-\gamma_wD_k}$, one may take $C_F'=\frac12|Q_b|(d+3)N(2c+c^2)$. This formula is **not** an already proved mixed P6 constant: the old and boundary actions, $\nu$-free mass, and common-cover $s$ response require their own $C_A,C_Q,C_{\rm resp}$.

Under item 1, the landed analytic maps and the finite-dimensional affine data maps make $F_b$ holomorphic on an open neighborhood of $S\times P_{\kappa_1}$; `Theorem42Input.DecoupledCubeResponse.analytic` (`Fluctuation/Theorem42Input.lean:55`) demands precisely `AnalyticOnNhd` there. A pointwise H7 inclusion without uniform strict slack does not supply an open neighborhood of the *closed* Cauchy polydisc, so it cannot be used for AE. The common index and cube-indexed agreement issues of `VMOD-12.md` §2.1 remain separate from this analytic proof.

The missing assertions are therefore **OPEN-H7-BG-MIXED** (strict, common open domain and H7-to-BG transport), **OPEN-DECOUPLED-WEIGHTED-RESPONSE** (a common-$s$ weighted source/inverse bound yielding (2.1), including the old fluctuation), and **OPEN-OLD-H7-DECAY** (the actual two P6 data comparisons, support distance $D_k$, and full action read-set regularity). Equation (2.2) is proved *conditional on these open assertions*. `WeightedProp51S` is a proved cited input to the corresponding undecoupled one-operator assertion, not a proof of the mixed assertion. The late exponential absorption is the separate conditional calculation of `FLUCTUATION.md` (3.14)/(Λ28′).
