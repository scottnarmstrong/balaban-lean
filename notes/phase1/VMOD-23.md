## 2. Packing in one actual cover, and its limit

Fix $F=\mathrm{p6FsharpC}$ (landed `Localization/P6PairOp.lean:p6FsharpC`) and its one index set $I=\operatorname{cubeIdx}F$ (landed `Localization/Cover.lean:cubeIdx`, `mem_cubeIdx`). Put $A'=(L^{m_0})^d$ and $c(P)=\operatorname{cellOfCube}(m_0,m_1,P)$. `GeneratingSet.layer_disjoint` says that if $(j,y),(j',y)\in I$, then $j=j'$: both memberships put the *same point* $y$ into disjoint layers. Thus the $K$ indices $(j,A_0)$ of `VMOD-19.md` (1.1) **cannot simultaneously be indices of this actual cover**. The earlier finite-record counterexample is valid for its abstract SN–AG–GRID record, but is not an actual-cover counterexample. **Status: PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the cited PROVED `layer_disjoint` and `mem_cubeIdx`.**

There is a stronger, exact packing fact that does not require the preceding injectivity. Assume the theorem's divisibility premise $L^{m_0}\mid N_1$. Let $R$ be a **specified finite set of layer cells** $(n,b)$, $N_{\rm cells}=|R|$, and $V_R=\{P\in I:c(P)\in R\}$. Landed `GraphGmCount.card_deriv_in_cell` applies to $I$: `mem_cubeIdx` supplies $P.1\le K$ and `CubeBase`. The fibers $\{P\in I:c(P)=r\}$, $r\in R$, partition $V_R$ and each has cardinality at most $A'$. Summing these fiber cardinalities proves the first inequality below. The size-$n$ subsets of a set of cardinality $|V_R|$ are counted by $\binom{|V_R|}{n}$, and binomial coefficients are monotone in the upper argument. Hence
$$|V_R|\le A'N_{\rm cells},\qquad
\#\{C\subset V_R:|C|=n\}=\binom{|V_R|}{n}\le\binom{A'N_{\rm cells}}n.\tag{2.1}$$
For any family $\mathscr F\subset\mathcal P(V_R)$ and $t=e^{-\kappa_2}$,
$$\sum_{C\in\mathscr F}e^{-\kappa_2|C|}
\le(1+t)^{|V_R|}\le(1+t)^{A'N_{\rm cells}}
\le e^{A'tN_{\rm cells}}.\tag{2.2}$$
To prove (2.2), enlarge $\mathscr F$ to the whole powerset of $V_R$, use the binomial formula $\sum_{C\subset V_R}t^{|C|}=(1+t)^{|V_R|}$, and then $1+t\le e^t$. The exponentials have nonnegative terms; $\kappa_2\ge0$ is convenient but not needed for this finite identity. **Status: PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the cited PROVED `card_deriv_in_cell`**; the remaining steps are finite-set counting and the binomial formula. This is the requested actual-cover packing bound, with the displayed exact $A'$.

The sentence “$N_{\rm cells}\le C(1+D)$ at fixed site support” is **a separate assertion**. Neither `layer_disjoint` nor (2.1) proves it. `SizeModCore.cellCount_le_of_admissible` bounds the number of **active top $\pi_K$ cells** in $S=Y\cap\Omega_{k+1}$ by $c_G(1+D(S))$, $c_G=[3(2L-1)]^d$, for the chosen-cover site size. The set $R$ in (2.1) contains **layer-labeled cells at the derivative's own scale**. The cited G1 bound supplies no injection of those cells into the active top cells, no bounded-multiplicity map to them, and no control of derivative cells inside an uncharged completed hole or the separate action read collar. A single top cell can contain many cells of a lower layer in the generic `GeneratingSet`/`Cover` API; disjointness of the layers at one point does not bound their total count by one top cell. Whether the *specific* $F^\sharp$, actual P6 coefficients, and valid histories rule out all such extra candidates is **OPEN-P6-CANDIDATE-REGION**. I have no actual-P6 counterexample to it and make no claim that the needed estimate is false.

Here is the exact conditional fixed-support theorem, including the cost of replacing the paper's ownership count by (2.2). For each old source $X$ and site support $S$, suppose all contributing effective $C$ lie in one $V_{R(X,S)}$, with
$$|R(X,S)|\le c_0+c_1D(S),\qquad c_0,c_1\ge0,\tag{2.3}$$
uniformly in history, scale and torus. Let $q=A'c_1e^{-\kappa_2}$ and $H=e^{A'c_0e^{-\kappa_2}}$. If every individual coefficient obeys
$$\|a_{X,C}\|_{H7}\le E_b e^{-\kappa_0\Delta_X-2\rho D(S)-\kappa_2|C|},\tag{2.4}$$
then triangle inequality and (2.2) give
$$\left\|\sum_{C:S_C=S}a_{X,C}\right\|_{H7}
\le E_b H e^{-\kappa_0\Delta_X-(2\rho-q)D(S)}.\tag{2.5}$$
If the old-source estimate at fixed $S$ is $\sum_{X\in\mathscr X(S)}e^{-\kappa_0\Delta_X}\le K_{\rm src}(1+D(S))$, in the sense of P6 (1.5), the complete old-boundary contribution satisfies
$$\|B_{\rm old}^+(S)\|_{H7}\le
E_bK_{\rm src}H e^{-(2\rho-q-1)D(S)},\qquad
K_{\rm src}=K_0(3L^2)^d.\tag{2.6}$$
Here $1+D\le e^D$. For $E_b=\beta_k^{-2}$, $\beta_k^{1/2}\ge K_{\rm src}H$ gives the paper's $\beta_k^{-3/2}$ prefactor. To retain the *exact* paper rate $2\kappa-1$ through this weaker count, require $\rho\ge\kappa+q/2$. A sufficient two-budget arithmetic premise for a site transfer $D\le\Delta_X/L+dL|C|$ is
$$\kappa-1\ge\kappa_0+2\rho/L,\qquad \kappa_1\ge4\rho dL,
\qquad\kappa_2=\kappa_1/2-1.\tag{2.7}$$
It converts $e^{-(\kappa-1)\Delta_X-(\kappa_1-1)|C|}$ into (2.4). Equations (2.3)–(2.7) are **PROVED as an implication** by exponent arithmetic and finite sums; their application to P6 is **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OPEN-P6-CANDIDATE-REGION**, the open individual P6 analytic/geometric witness, and the matching old-source count/size bridge. If (2.3) holds, a separate *assumed global Wiener coefficient norm* is unnecessary for this fixed-support value bound. The produced D7 clause still records the resulting aggregate fixed-support bound, and through-hole/read-pin estimates need their own argument.

The more economical route is the paper's stronger count. Landed `GraphGmCount.boundary_gm3_fixed_Y` assumes each derivative home cell is in the **new part of one fixed whole output** $Y_c\setminus B_c$, every new cell is owned by some selected derivative completion $D(P)$, $|D(P)|\le G=(2L+1)^d$, and at most $A'$ derivatives occupy a cell. With $\kappa_2/2\ge1$ and $\kappa_2/2\ge\log(GA')$, it proves
$$\sum_{C:\,Y(C)=Y}e^{-\kappa_2|C|}\le1.\tag{2.8}$$
Indeed $b=|Y_c\setminus B_c|\le G|C|$, while the common candidate set has size at most $A'b$; splitting the weight in half gives
$e^{-\kappa_2|C|}\le e^{-(\kappa_2/2)b/G}e^{-(\kappa_2/2)|C|}$, and the second factor sums to at most $\exp(A'b e^{-\kappa_2/2})\le e^{b/G}$. `GraphGmExponent.boundary_gm3_fixed_Y_rate` packages the sufficient $\kappa_1\ge6$ and $\kappa_1\ge4d\log((2L+1)L^{m_0})+2$. **Status: PROVED CONDITIONAL ON these cited PROVED declarations and their explicit ownership/location premises.** For a site support that forgets the layer ledger, neither the fixed whole-output identity nor ownership follows from (2.1); a filled modular hole can enter the whole output without a derivative owner. That is why (2.8) cannot simply be invoked for the site fiber.
