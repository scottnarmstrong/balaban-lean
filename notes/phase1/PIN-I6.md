# PIN-I6/PROP: the pinned class does not inherit the anisotropic I-6 estimate

## Verdict and exact scope

The requested $C_IE_0\sigma^{3-\alpha_1}(\varepsilon_k/\varepsilon_j)^2$ termwise estimate is **false** if `SourceForm` means factorization through $b=\nu_jh$, continuity at $\nu_j=0$, bounded analyticity on the pinned physical $h$ chart, and the axial/profile germs of `Q13-ANSWER.md` §3 without a quantitative second transverse-$b$ jet condition. The counterexample below obeys the four renormalization conditions, is $O(N)$ invariant, is one-site local, and has the required bounded pinned and axial/profile germs. Its failure occurs for constant spins, before any background composition or polymer summation. In particular the landed `IsSFFamily.renormalization_aniso(_scaled)` cannot be obtained by restricting its domain to the pin.

A corrected **sufficient** theorem is stated below in the actual `TermFamily`, `Itw`, `Dlt`, `Ttw`, `SumConds`, `avgFamilyA`, and `DataHypAniso` objects of the landed files. It separates a reference-jet certificate for each old term from half-margin transport and new-term estimates. The algebraic and summation conclusion has a complete proof by the named landed lemmas. Obtaining that certificate from the physical fluctuation construction remains an analytic task; this note does not turn it into a consequence of pinned analyticity. It also gives a stronger optional bridge, bounded analyticity of the *old term* on `TubeAniso`, for which the landed theorem applies verbatim. Q13's one-block examples prohibit assuming that stronger bridge for the unmodified physical switch or raw mass-free activity.

## 1. What the landed theorem actually consumes

`RenormAnisoCtx.lean:46–75` defines `PremisesTermAniso T i₀ i₁ ν r E₀ κ A K₀ Dom`. Its analytic and bound fields hold on `TubeAniso i₀ ν r X`, whose reference polydisc has spin and transverse-$b$ radius $r$, and longitudinal-$b$ radius $r^2$ (`RenormAnisoDefs.lean:16–35,195–221`). Here $r=r_j=\texttt{anisoRadius}\asymp\varepsilon_j$, specifically
$$r_j=\varepsilon_j/K_r,\qquad K_r=(2\,\texttt{bgK1}(d,L,N)+2)\,4N(1+N\,\texttt{rotB}(N))\tag{1.1}$$
by `RenormAnisoBridge.lean:486–512` and `RenormAnisoBridgeInf.lean:194–197`. In contrast, on the pinned physical chart $h=h_*+z$, $\|z\|<r_h$, one has $b=\nu_jh$ and $|b_\perp|\lesssim\nu_jr_h$. When $\nu_j\ll r_j$ this chart contains no transverse-$b$ disc of radius $r_j$. Thus the premise `TubeAniso ⊆ pinned domain` of `PremisesTermAniso.ofDomain` (`RenormAnisoCtx.lean:113–145`) is false.

The exact failed proof lines are `CtxAniso.analytic_poly`/`hM` (`RenormAnisoCtx.lean:353–364`), which feed `aniso_taylor_diff` and the Hessian bounds in `TermOK.local_bound_aniso` (`RenormAnisoLocal.lean:28–57,65–160`), and `CtxAniso.constant_potential_aniso`, used at `RenormAnisoIrrel.lean:280–334`. The shape identities, Ward identity, tensor algebra, Euclidean cancellation, counting, and scale arithmetic do not require a wide source disc *once replacement jet bounds have been provided*. A bounded axial germ only covers $b=Vh_*$; a transverse spin germ at $b=\nu_jh_*$ only controls spin derivatives there. Neither bounds the second derivative in a transverse-$b$ direction. Holomorphy in $g$ is independent of this issue.

For clarity, the landed `Itw` is (`RenormTermwiseDefs.lean:168–190`)
$$I_X(\psi,b)=T_X(\psi,b)-T_X^{\rm ref}(\psi,b)-A_X(\psi)-\Phi_X(\psi),\tag{1.2}$$
where the first $T_X$ here is `T.term X (pack X ψ b)`, $T_X^{\rm ref}$ is Lean `Ttw` (value and first $u,v$ jets), $A_X$ is `Ax`, and $\Phi_X$ is `Phitw`. In the rest of this note `f_X$ denotes `T.term X`, avoiding the two meanings of $T_X$.

## 2. One-site counterexample, including all four renormalization conditions

Take $N\ge2$, $e=e_1$, the centred one-site family $X=\{0\}$ in the abstract landed `TermFamily` interface, and positive physical mass $m=\nu_j$. For a packed spin $p$ and packed source $b$, set
$$f_m(p,b)=\frac{c}{m}\bigl[(p\cdot p)(b\cdot b)-(p\cdot b)^2\bigr],\qquad m>0,\tag{2.1}$$
with $c>0$ fixed small enough for the desired $E_0$ pinned bound. In physical coordinates $b=mh$ this is $cm[(p\cdot p)(h\cdot h)-(p\cdot h)^2]$; at $m=0$ define it to be zero. This physical expression is jointly polynomial in $(m,p,h)$, so continuity at zero is no obstacle. It is invariant under simultaneous complex orthogonal rotations, independent of position, and entire in $(p,b)$ for each $m>0$. On any fixed pinned chart with bounded $p,h$ it has norm at most $C_{\rm pin}cm$, hence at most $E_0$ after fixing $c$ (the regime has $m\le1$). It vanishes identically on the aligned axial germ $p=Ue,b=Ve$ and is bounded on the transverse spin profile germ $b=me$ with its stipulated fixed spin radius. Its `SourceForm` is the displayed polynomial in $b$. To realize the same example with Def-6's geometric polymers, assign this one-site dependence to any fixed admissible $\pi'_j$ polymer containing its centre, multiply $c$ by its fixed $e^{-\kappa d_j(X)}$ factor, and average its finite point-group orbit. The extra spin coordinates are ignored. The estimates below acquire only that fixed factor and the moment remains zero because both spin derivatives are at the centre.

At the reference $(p,b)=(e,me)$, $f_m=0$, $D_{b\cdot e}f_m=0$, and $D_{p\cdot e}f_m=0$. Hence `SumConds.ren_val`, `.ren_b`, and `.ren_psi` hold for the singleton family. The spin Hessian is supported at the same lattice site in both arguments, so the displacement square in `SumConds.moment` is zero. Thus the fourth condition holds regardless of that Hessian's value. Every translate supplies the same centred one-site term; its Euclidean covariance and the torus summation identity are automatic. The test does not rely on a missing cancellation.

Let $\sigma=s=L^{j-k}$, $m=\sigma^2$ (or $m=c_*\sigma^2$ with fixed $0<c_*<1$), $\varepsilon=\varepsilon_k$, and let $\psi(x)=p_\theta=\cos\theta\,e+\sin\theta\,e_2$ be constant with $\theta=\varepsilon/2$, while $h=e$, $b=me$. For sufficiently small $\varepsilon$, `DataHypAniso` (`RenormAnisoCtx.lean:76–80`) holds on a domain containing these two identical constant configurations: `sq`, `adj`, and `sec` are zero, `bdd0=1`,
$$\|b-mp_\theta\|=m\|e-p_\theta\|\le m\theta\le\sigma^2\varepsilon,\quad
|vInv(\psi,b)-m|=m(1-\cos\theta)\le\sigma^2\varepsilon^2.\tag{2.2}$$
Take $K_2=0$. The pair is in the pinned chart, including the old birth spin disc, since $\theta=O(\varepsilon_k)\ll\varepsilon_j$. The numerical premises $m\le\sigma^2$, $0<\sigma\le1$, and $\varepsilon\le\Lambda r_j$ hold along sufficiently separated scales. Choosing an ideal mass sequence with $\nu_k=c_*\le1$ gives $\nu_j=c_*s^2$, so this is compatible with the later complete-background part of the flow, up to its stated upper mass threshold. It is not necessary to claim this is a generated RG term.

Here `Ttw=0` because its three reference jets vanish; `Ax=0` because the radial spin derivative vanishes; `Phitw=0` because the spin field is constant. Therefore
$$|\texttt{Itw}|=|f_m(p_\theta,me)|=cm\sin^2\theta\ge c_1\sigma^2\varepsilon_k^2.\tag{2.3}$$
The claimed I-6 upper bound would be $C E_0\sigma^{3-\alpha_1}(\varepsilon_k/\varepsilon_j)^2$. For a fixed birth level $j$ and $k-j\to\infty$, the quotient of (2.3) by that bound is bounded below by a positive multiple of $\varepsilon_j^2\sigma^{-(1-\alpha_1)}$, which diverges because $0<\alpha_1<1$. This disproves a uniform $C$ for the pinned-only class, even after the four sum conditions. The precise missing control is visible from
$$D^2_{b_\perp}f_m(e,me)=2c/m,\tag{2.4}$$
whereas the landed polydisc bound yields $O(E_0/r_j^2)$ by `aniso_norm_D2_basis_le`. A fixed first-$b$ jet at the reference would not exclude (2.1), since that first jet is zero.

## 3. Corrected PIN-I6: a reference-jet certificate and its proof

The following is a sufficient theorem in landed objects. It is weaker than requiring a bounded extension of $f_X$ across the entire transverse-$b$ polydisc because it concerns only the actual physical interpolation and its reference jets. It is an **additional** analytic hypothesis on a pinned class until proved from the fluctuation construction.

Fix $1\le j\le k$, $r=r_j$, $\nu=\texttt{nuIdeal L k (ν k) j}$, $i_0\ne i_1$, and a centred `TermFamily T` obtained from the pinned birth terms by the same nonwinding limit and Reynolds average as `avgFamilyA`. Suppose its polymer geometry and counting fields are those of `PremisesTermAniso`, and `SumConds T i₀ i₁ ν` holds. For every `DataHypAniso Dom ν σ ε K₂ ψ b` coming from the pinned output domain, with the numerical premises of `termwise_irrelevant_aniso`, require the following **PinJet** facts for every $X\in T.\texttt{polys}$, writing $M_X=E_0e^{-\kappa T.\texttt{dist}X}$ and $t_X=1+T.\texttt{dist}X$:

1. `PinJet.germ`: $f_X$ is analytic near `refPt i₀ ν X`, and its simultaneous-rotation identity there gives `TermOK` and `ward_algebra`. Its reference tensors satisfy $|a_X|\le M_X/r$ and $|B_X|,|A_X|,|m_X|\le4M_X/r^2$ in the exact `tensA`, `tensB`, `tensAA`, `tensM` types of `RenormAnisoLocal.lean`. This is where a *second transverse-$b$* SourceForm estimate, not just factorization, is needed; spin Cauchy and the transverse spin profile give the spin tensor bounds.
2. `PinJet.small`: in the small-polymer case $(\texttt{cZA N Λ}+\texttt{cK1 N A})t_X\sigma\varepsilon\le r/2$, the two actual rotated configurations and their spin interpolation lie in the pinned birth chart with a half-radius margin. On that interpolation the second-order **Taylor difference**, with the same notation $z_c,\delta,Z,Z_0$ as `TermOK.local_bound_aniso`, obeys $2M_X(Z^3+Z_0^3)$, and $|D^2f_X(P_0)[z_c,\delta]|\le4M_XZ_0(D/r)$. These are precisely the two Cauchy consequences used at `RenormAnisoLocal.lean:65–96,143–160`; they may be supplied by physical source derivative bounds, without bounding $f_X$ at all transverse-$b$ points.
3. `PinJet.potential`: for the constant physical pair, the reference remainder obeys the *separate* estimate proved from the wide tube in `CtxAniso.constant_potential_aniso`:
$$|f_X(\operatorname{const}\psi(0),b)-\texttt{Ttw}_X(\psi,b)|\le12|X|M_X\,\texttt{cZA}(N,\Lambda)^2\sigma^4(\varepsilon/r)^2.\tag{3.1}$$
The axial germ supplies the value and first reference jets, but (3.1) also needs quantitative control of the angular/mixed second jet. The counterexample violates (3.1). A useful source-level sufficient condition is to bound the invariant-coordinate second derivatives of the constant potential on every connecting $(u,v,w)$ segment by the Cauchy values of the anisotropic disc, including $\partial_w$ and the mixed transverse-$b$ derivative. A bound only at the reference point, without a segment margin, is insufficient.
4. `PinJet.tail`: in the complementary large-polymer case, the two physical values are bounded by $M_X$ and the reference coefficients have the bounds in item 1. The crude `tailBnd` of `RenormTermwiseBound` then applies. The two actual pairs need membership in the pinned birth domain; no wide source disc is required for this case.

**Corrected theorem.** Under `PinJet`, the exact numeric hypotheses of `termwise_irrelevant_aniso` ($0<r\le1$, $0<\varepsilon\le\Lambda r$, $0<\sigma\le1$, $\sigma^2\varepsilon\le1/4$, $0\le\nu\le\sigma^2$, $0\le E_0$, $K_2\le K_{\rm sig}\sigma^{2-\alpha_1}\varepsilon$, $0<\alpha_1<1$) imply
$$|\texttt{Itw T i₀ i₁ ν X ψ b}|\le C_{\rm tw}E_0e^{-(\kappa-1)T.\texttt{dist}X}\sigma^{3-\alpha_1}(\varepsilon/r)^2.\tag{3.2}$$
With $r=\varepsilon_j/K_r$, the right side is $C_{\rm tw}K_r^2E_0e^{-(\kappa-1)d_j(X)}\sigma^{3-\alpha_1}(\varepsilon_k/\varepsilon_j)^2$. If `SumConds`, point-group invariance, and the same counting constant $K_0$ hold for the averaged family, `RenormClassTheorem.lean:135–163,165–200,320–381` gives
$$\texttt{energy T ψ b}-\texttt{Aterm T i₀ ν ψ}=\sum_X\texttt{Itw}_X,\quad
\sum_X|\texttt{Itw}_X|\le C_{\rm tw}K_r^2K_0E_0\sigma^{3-\alpha_1}(\varepsilon_k/\varepsilon_j)^2,\quad \sum_yA_y=0.\tag{3.3}$$
If $\sigma=C_0s$, $s=L^{j-k}$, and $\varepsilon_k/\varepsilon_j\le K_Rs^{(d-2)/2-\gamma}$, then (3.3) is at most $C_IE_0s^{d+\alpha_3}$ per centre, with $\alpha_3=1-2\gamma-\alpha_1>0$ and $C_I=C_{\rm tw}K_r^2K_0C_0^{3-\alpha_1}K_R^2$. Summing the number $s^{-d}$ of $j$-centres per current block and then the geometric series over $j$ gives $C_IE_0/(1-L^{-\alpha_3})$ per current block, plus the finitely many close birth scales treated by the crude class bound as in `SMALLFIELD.md` Theorem 2.1. This is the I-6 scale sum, conditional only on the displayed PinJet facts and the stated class geometry/cancellation.

**Proof.** In the small case replace the calls to `aniso_taylor_diff`, `aniso_norm_D2_le`, and the four tensor Cauchy bounds in `TermOK.local_bound_aniso` by PinJet items 1–2. Every equality from `TermOK.lin_pack`, `.quad_pack`, `ward_algebra`, and the spin/gradient estimates in `CtxAniso.small_case` is unchanged; its final seven-term bound is exactly `smallBndA`. In the large case use item 4 in the existing `CtxAniso.tail_case` proof; it gives `tailBnd`. `smallBndA_le` and `tailBndA_le` (`RenormAnisoIrrel.lean:108–218`) absorb both into $(C_s+C_t)M_X|X|^2t_X^6\sigma^{3-\alpha_1}(\varepsilon/r)^2$. The `itw_eq` algebra and item 3 add the constant-potential contribution, using $\sigma^4\le\sigma^{3-\alpha_1}$ (`RenormAnisoIrrel.lean:280–334`); hence the raw coefficient is $C_s+C_t+12\,\texttt{cZA}(N,\Lambda)^2$. `weight_absorb` removes $|X|^2t_X^6$ at the cost of one unit of $\kappa$ (`RenormAnisoIrrel.lean:384–423`), proving (3.2). Termwise summability follows from `tsum_norm_le_of_termwise`. The identity in (3.3) is `energy_sub_Aterm_avgA`: `SumConds.ren_val`, `.ren_b`, `.ren_psi` annihilate the summed `Ttw`, while `.moment` and point-group covariance annihilate the summed `Phitw`. `Aterm_torus_sum_avgA` is an antisymmetric reindexing, valid for any spin field. The scale exponent is $3-\alpha_1+(d-2)-2\gamma=d+1-2\gamma-\alpha_1$. Each step is an equality or inequality from the cited landed proof after replacing only its wide-domain Cauchy inputs by the listed certificate; no conclusion is taken as a hypothesis.

An alternative **strong old-term bridge** is to prove the literal `PremisesTermAniso` fields for an auxiliary bounded extension of each inherited $f_X$ to `TubeAniso` and its compatibility with the pinned physical germ. Then `PremisesTermAniso.ofDomain`, `termwise_irrelevant_aniso`, and `IsSFFamily.renormalization_aniso_scaled` prove (3.2)–(3.3) directly, with no new local proof. This is an honest sufficient assumption, but is stronger than Q13's physical pinned class and is false for (2.1) with a mass-uniform $E_0$ bound. It is also unavailable for Q13's unresummed switch activity.

## 4. PROP: transport and new coefficients

The irrelevance theorem is a statement about an already constructed family of old terms. To preserve the modified class, use separate **PinTransport** premises at every later step. For each inherited birth term $f_{j,X}$, the actual `composeField`/background map from the closed output spin disc of radius $r_s\varepsilon_{k+1}$ and its axial/profile germs must be analytic into the *interior* of its birth pinned domain, with at least half of every birth margin left. It must use the birth physical source $b_j=\nu_jh$ and birth background convention $\mu_j=\texttt{bgMassAt}(k_0,j,\nu_j)$; replacing either by the output background mass changes the term. The same statement is required uniformly in the coupling and RG parameters $\xi$. `MASS-LEVEL.md`'s level-$j$ D1 or its unit-real-witness top-level variant is part of the domain proof, not a consequence of `renormalization_aniso`.

For each new localized energy and observable coefficient, require analytic membership and the H4/H7 bounds on the output pinned chart, the reference-jet certificate (or another route to (3.2)), and compatible axial/profile germs. For an inherited term, analyticity of the pullback follows by composition; its numerical H4 bound follows from birth membership; its $\xi$ derivatives require the specified half-margin. For a new term, none of these bounds follows from I-6: they are PIN-FREE/AXIAL, PIN-COMPOSE/SWITCH and PIN-H7 in `Q13-ANSWER.md` §3, with physical mixed covers and the $x_1^2$ moment. The sums/averages require the nonwinding thermodynamic stabilization and point-group covariance of `RenormClassFamily.lean`; pinning $h_*$ must be transported to $Rh_*$ under rotations, not held fixed while rotating only the spin. These premises yield class preservation by the same finite sum, composition, and bound arguments as Def-6, but their analytic verification is still open. Thus the corrected PROP statement is conditional on explicit transport and new-coefficient estimates, rather than an unconditional theorem from pinned membership.
