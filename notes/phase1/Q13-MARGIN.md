# Q13 round 24: what the cutoff hierarchy actually gives

## Summary

**PROVED:** under the genuine early regime and the landed running-flow hypotheses, $\delta_j/\varepsilon_j\to0$ uniformly in $j$, and also $\sqrt{\nu_j}/\varepsilon_j\to0$ and $\nu_j/\varepsilon_j^2\to0$ as the initial $\beta$ grows. Thus every *same-site, same-solver* sharp `Reg` inequality has a numerical margin inside its corresponding complex `RegC` inequality. The mixed $3/4,5/4$ tests have the same margin when their own sharp indicators are present.

**REFUTED as stated:** `chiY=1` for the retained region does not imply a uniform `regionalStrictTube` or even membership in the full H7 domain. `chiY` reads only the current block field; H7 also reads old fields, a fluctuation field, and data on their own generating sets. The separate sharp factors P5 (1.24)–(1.25), (2.13), (2.42) and the history geometry provide some of those inputs. Even after they are supplied, the landed `prop51_mass_analytic_uniform` gives mass-independent contraction constants on an *open* domain, not a history-uniform radius of a complex mass disk. The exact remaining transfer estimates are stated below.

## 1. Landed hierarchy and its quantitative ratio

`LargeField/Analytic/Constants.lean:runLam,pZero,deltaRun,deltaRun_eq` give $\lambda_j=\log(a\beta_j)$ and $\delta_j=A_0\lambda_j^{p_0}e^{-\lambda_j/2}$; `Def7.lean:Coh.delta_cs` identifies this with `cs.delta j`. `SmallField/Def6.lean:Consts.alpha,Consts.eps`, `SmallField/Xi.lean:epsSeq`, and `Analytic/Constants.lean:epsRun,epsOneRun` give $\alpha=(d-2)/2-\gamma$, $\varepsilon_j=\alpha_0T_jL^{-j\alpha}$, $1\le T_j=1+\sum_{n\ge j}n^{-2}<3$, and $\varepsilon_{1,j}=(A_1/A_0)\varepsilon_j$ (`Def7.lean:epsOne`). `completeXiDom` is `Def6v2Bridge.lean:171`; `xiFieldDom` is `Def6v2Domain.lean:94`. They use `XiDom`/`XiC` at the complete solver mass and radius $\varepsilon_j$. H7 uses `DeltaC`, `DeltaPrimeC`, `FluctDomain` and $\|g\|_1<1$ (`Characteristic/Analytic.lean:55–180`).

`Analytic/Constants.lean:RunningHyp` gives $\beta_j=\beta L^{j(d-2)}\Gamma_j$ with $e^{-\epsilon}\le\Gamma_j\le e^\epsilon$. `SmallField/Def6v2Regime.lean:RunningHyp.beta_mul_nu_le` gives $\beta_j\nu_j\le E:=e^{2\epsilon}$ for $j\le k_0$ **when `KZeroGenuine` holds**; the conventional $k_0=0$ case with $\beta\nu>1$ has no such assertion and is separate. Put $\theta=(d-2)\log L$, $\lambda_0=\log(a\beta_0)$, where $\beta_0$ is the running value. Then $\lambda_j\le\lambda_0+j\theta+2\epsilon$ and
$$\frac{\delta_j}{\varepsilon_j}\le\frac{A_0e^{\epsilon/2}}{\alpha_0\sqrt{a\beta}}(\lambda_0+j\theta+2\epsilon)^{p_0}L^{-j\gamma}.\tag{1.1}$$
If $\lambda_0+2\epsilon\ge p_0(d-2)/\gamma$, the right factor decreases in real $j\ge0$, hence
$$\sup_{j\le k_0}\frac{\delta_j}{\varepsilon_j}\le\frac{A_0e^{\epsilon/2}}{\alpha_0\sqrt{a\beta}}(\lambda_0+2\epsilon)^{p_0}\longrightarrow0.\tag{1.2}$$
This is elementary from the displayed landed definitions; no separate Lean `delta_over_eps` theorem was found. Likewise $\nu_j/\varepsilon_j^2\le Ee^\epsilon(\alpha_0^2\beta)^{-1}L^{-2\gamma j}$ and $\sqrt{\nu_j}/\varepsilon_j\le\sqrt{Ee^\epsilon}(\alpha_0\sqrt\beta)^{-1}L^{-\gamma j}$. Thus $2\nu_j<\eta^2$ and $2\sqrt{\nu_j}<\varepsilon_j/2$ follow eventually for any fixed $\eta>0$, subject to the genuine regime.

The order is fixed geometry and P2/BG constants (ledger rows 0–8), $A_1,A_0$ with $A_0=4K_2B_5A_1$ (row 9), then the small-field $\alpha_0$ and the `XiC` upper bounds $3(4d+6)(2d+1)\alpha_0\le1$, $3(4d+8)(2d+1)\alpha_0\le c_1^{\rm BG}$ (row 11), then $\lambda_*$ and initial $\beta$ (row 16). LF-RECONSTRUCTION §§I.1.4, II.1.1–II.1.3 and III.O.3 record these rows. $(\Lambda17)$ makes $3dL\bar\delta$ small relative to the BG and reference-domain constants, $(\Lambda20)$ ensures $\lambda_j\ge12p_0$, $(\Lambda22)$ pays the composition input $C_{23}\hat W_k\delta_j\le c'_6$, and $(\Lambda25)(d)$ pays P7's cutoff-response errors. None of these rows asserts that `chiY` implies every H7 or `StrictData` test. P5 p. 498 defines $\delta_j$; P4 (H.1) defines $\varepsilon_j$; P5 (1.37) gives $\varepsilon_{1,j}$.

## 2. Exactly what the sharp predicates test

`Characteristic/Functions.lean:ChiKProp,chiK,chiNext` use `Reg` on the cube neighbourhood $\square^{\sim1}$; `chiNext_eq` is definitional. `Def7SmallField.lean:chiY` is their product over cubes in $Y$. `Characteristic/Reg.lean:Reg` includes `InUnionDomainR` and `RegIneq`: `BlockClose` on $T$, `GradClose` on $S$, and `FieldClose` on $S$ at dimensionless mass $t=\mu L^{2j}$. Therefore, at each **tested** site and for the **same** solver mass, the raw real bounds are exactly
$$|\Psi-Q\phi|<\delta_j,\quad L^j|\partial\bar\phi|,\ L^{2j}|\Delta\bar\phi|,\ L^{2j}|\alpha|<\delta_j,\quad \sqrt{\nu_j}|\phi-h_*|<\delta_j,\quad |h_*\cdot h_*-1|=0.\tag{2.1}$$
The last field test is void for mass zero. For $h_*$ and the real background both unit, $|\phi-h_*|\le2$ and $\sqrt{\nu_j}|\phi-h_*|\le2\sqrt{\nu_j}$; this improves the last bound without any cutoff. On the same site `DeltaC`/`RegC` has the first four thresholds $\varepsilon_j$ and thus slack $\varepsilon_j-\delta_j$; its field threshold has slack at least $\varepsilon_j-2\sqrt{\nu_j}$, and the unit-field square test has full slack $\varepsilon_j^2$ (`Characteristic/Analytic.lean:DeltaC`). **PROVED** by unfolding, conditional only on `RegC`'s separate complex-domain conjunct. The sharp predicate includes `InUnionDomainR`, not a quantitative complex-domain witness.

For a mixed transition, `Characteristic/Functions.lean:chiPrimeNew` uses `RegMixedIneq` and its own `InUnionDomainR`. When this particular sharp factor equals one, its far/near block deviations are $<3\delta_i/4$, $<5\delta_i/4$, its gradient, Laplacian, multiplier and positive-mass field tests are $<3\delta_i/4$. `Characteristic/Analytic.lean:DeltaPrimeC` uses $\varepsilon_i$ in place of $\delta_i$, so the corresponding numerical slacks are $3(\varepsilon_i-\delta_i)/4$ and $5(\varepsilon_i-\delta_i)/4$. **PROVED** by unfolding; membership of `DeltaDomain2` for every cube requires the history to place that mixed factor on every cube it quantifies over. `Quiet.lean:quietKernel` includes two such products and separate `chiK`/`chiNext` products, each on a *specified*, smaller cube set (lines 153–185). It is not a product of all the tests quantified by `H7Domain`.

`SmallField/Xi.lean:XiBounds` asks, on **every** site of its $G.\Omega_1$, scaled gradient $<\varepsilon_j$, Laplacian and multiplier $<\varepsilon_j$, block closeness $<\varepsilon_j$, adjacent **collar–collar bond** $<\varepsilon_j$, mass distance $<\varepsilon_j$, and source norm $<\varepsilon_j^2$. At sites and bonds actually covered by (2.1), the first four have slack $\varepsilon_j-\delta_j$. A collar–collar bond is not one of `GradClose`'s bonds based at a tested interior site; `Reg`'s `InUnionDomainR` supplies only some witness $\eta\le c_1^{\rm BG}$ for its R1 bound, not $C\delta_j$. Its prospective slack is $\varepsilon_j-C_{\rm collar}\delta_j$, with `cutoffCollarPropagation` **OPEN** for the actual inherited $G_j(P)$. Positive-mass R3 of `Background/Data.lean:InPsiTilde` is at most $2\nu_j$ for unit data direction and real unit $h_*$, and R4 is zero. Thus a *chosen* BG witness $\eta$ has R3 slack $\eta^2-2\nu_j$ and R4 slack $\eta^2$; the sharp existential `InUnionDomainR` does not choose $\eta$ with a fixed fraction of $c_1^{\rm BG}$ left over. These are the missing R1/R2 and collar margins of `cutoffDataRegularity`.

`XiCBounds` is a **difference** predicate: `grad`, `lap`, `mult`, `val`, `close`, `collarBond`, `hAbs`, `hDot` all have left side exactly zero at the real centre $\Psi''=h''=0$ with the same real/complex solution. Their respective numerical margins there are $\varepsilon_j$, $\varepsilon_j$, $\varepsilon_j$, $\varepsilon_j$, $\varepsilon_j$, $\varepsilon_j$, $\varepsilon_j$, $\varepsilon_j^2$ at $\sigma=1$ (the `hAbs` threshold is $\varepsilon_j$). **PROVED** directly from `XiCBounds` (`Xi.lean:120–158`), conditional on `XiAt` and `InUnionDomainC` for that real centre. Keeping half those margins on a complex trial disc requires an actual data-response bound; `psiTildeC_subset_xiC` (`XiD2.lean:166`) gives one if an `InPsiTildeC` witness with $\varepsilon+(2K_1+1)\delta\le\varepsilon_j$ is supplied. `StrictData` of `Background/ContractionAnalytic.lean:66` has eight tests: (P1) first differences of the **approximate base** $\phi_0$; (P2) its Laplacian; (P3) $|\phi_0|<3/2$; (P4a) scaled base multiplier $\alpha_0$; (P4b) scaled forcing $f_1$; (P4c) perturbation $\delta\Psi$; (P4d) sphere defect $g(\phi_0)$; and (P4e) scaled Laplacian of that defect. ## 3. The mass disk and an explicit failed implication

`Background/Prop51Mass.lean:prop51_mass_analytic_uniform` (line 266) gives one analytic solution on the union of sets `StrictData(δ₀,δ₁)(massShift ν q)` over real reference masses $0\le\nu L^{2q}\le1$. Its `c_6,\delta_*,K_2` are independent of that reference mass. It gives **no fixed open-ball radius** about a family of actual history data. `massShift` (line 149) changes the base multiplier by the complex mass difference; for the *physical same datum*, the forcing also changes by $\mu(\phi_0-h_*)$, and choosing `rawQ` anew changes the approximate base. A joint physical mass path must control all of these in `StrictData`. Thus neither $t_0$ nor $\nu_j\le t_0/2$ follows from the theorem alone. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `uniformBaseStrictMargin` and `sameDatumMassPath`:** if every strict test has slack $s_*>0$ at $t=0$ and changes by at most $C_mt$ in the normalized mass $t=\mu L^{2j}$, then $t_0=s_*/(2C_m)$ is a common disk and $\nu_j\le t_0/2$ follows once $\sup_{j\le k_0}\nu_j\le s_*/(4C_m)$; the latter follows from $\nu_j\le Ee^\epsilon\beta^{-1}L^{-j(d-2)}$ after $s_*,C_m$ are fixed. The inputs, not the final arithmetic, are **OPEN**.

There is a direct counterexample to the proposed implication from *retained sharp block predicates alone*. Take all block fields and $h_*$ constantly $e_1$, so `chiK=chiY=chiNext=1` at both masses (their background is $e_1$); on any nonempty H7 fluctuation support choose $\psi'_i=2\varepsilon_{1,i}e_2$ at one block. The block predicates do not read $\psi'_i$, but `FluctDomain` fails. **REFUTED:** `chiY=1 \Rightarrow H7Domain`. P5 (2.42) supplies the separate fluctuation characteristic $\chi^{(i)}$; after the physical $\beta_i^{-1/2}C_{\rm loc}^{1/2}$ conversion of P5 (2.20), (2.39), its radius is $B_5(A_1/A_0)\delta_i$ (LF-RECONSTRUCTION Lemma IV.1.4, p. 4466), which is $o(\varepsilon_{1,i})$ by (1.2). P5 (1.24)–(1.25) supplies `chiNew` for multiscale data and fluctuation fields; P5 (2.13) and P6 (2.1) supply the mixed and next-scale cutoffs. These are distinct factors, with history-dependent supports. P5 (1.36)–(1.37) asks for all of them on its H7 domain.
