## 3. Apparent conflicts and their resolution

**Collar rate: resolvable numerically, OPEN as a shared Lean tower.** `DETFIT.md` 's centered $3a$ hull has $B=18c_1=60750$ and rate $\lambda/(1+B)-c_1\log2$. Therefore the exact sufficient lower bound is

The mere landed statement $\lambda\ge\kappa'$ does not imply it: at $\lambda=\kappa'$, even $\lambda/(1+B)<\kappa_{\rm out}$. The *proof* of `tower_core_rates` fixes a finite $\kappa$ and does not state this new bound. No upper bound on $\kappa$ occurs in its conclusion: increasing $\kappa$ requires increasing $m_1$ to keep

$E_0$ or $\beta_0$. This gives a consistent *paper order*, **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** a strengthened `tower_core_rates_at_least`; it is not a landed witness.

The best explicit rate bound currently justified by the cited landed geometry plus the proposed centered-hull cell identity is $\lambda/(1+18c_1)-c_1\log2$. `HatLabelsGeom.thick_union` charges $18$ per attached box; `CubeSystem.card_le_dExt` supplies $|T|\le c_1(1+d_{\rm Ext}T)$; `SizeModG1.G1_cellCount` supplies at most $2^{c_1(1+d(H(T)))}$
