## B. Can the shell be misaligned?

**PROVED (landed requirement).** Background/Data.lean:InPsiTilde.r3 imposes $mL^{2\ell(s)}|1-u_s\cdot h|<\eta^2$ for **every** $s\in dataSites(G)$; dataSites includes the collar. History/ParentDomain.lean:parentPsi and liftSingle_Ψ_eq_parentPsi identify $u_s$ with the normalized parent datum at that site. The early theorem pays R3 with $2\nu_k<\eta^2$; this mass-only inequality is unavailable uniformly above $k_0$ (EarlyMassRatio.lean:lower_ratio_of_gt_kZero). Thus Q13-LATE's (RD), or an equivalent direct R3 estimate, is needed to apply the *landed global solver theorem* late.

**PROVED (source forcing term).** P2 (1.14), p. 610, has $(\alpha+\nu)\phi$ on the left and $\nu h$ on the right. If an almost-unit shell datum points to $v\perp h$, the normalized tangential mass forcing near $\phi\approx v$ has size $\nu$; in scale-$k$ units it is $\nu_k$. At an ordinary input near $\frac78L^{-2}$ this is fixed positive size for fixed $L$, while $\delta_k\sim\beta_k^{-1/2}p_0(\beta_k)\to0$. The assumption $\nu_k\le\frac78L^{-2}$ does not make the forcing $O(\delta_k)$.

**PROVED (model showing failure of uniform shell estimates).** In the scalar linearized boundary problem $(-\Delta+\nu_k)w=0$ on a half-space with unit-size misaligned boundary value, the decaying solution has boundary gradient of order $\sqrt{\nu_k}$ and a mass term of order $\nu_k$ near the boundary. This model does not prove nonexistence of the nonlinear constrained minimizer; it refutes an $O(\delta_k)$ bound at *all* shell sites without an alignment hypothesis as $\delta_k\downarrow0$.

**PROVED (where Lean spends R3).** Background/LocalSolution.lean:real_mass_bound consumes R2–R4 at one site; Approximate.lean:phi0_mass_real applies it for every $x\in G.\Omega_1$, including shell sites, and site_H_hyp likewise reads r3 there. Mass116.lean:bg_mass_bound uses phi0_mass_real through lemma53 to obtain $\sqrt m\,|\phi-h|(x)\le K_1\eta/\ell(x)$ for every $x\in G.\Omega_1$. Def5.lean:bg_real/theorem71 packages these all-site bounds and the minimizer.

**PROVED (other proof dependencies).** Contraction.lean bounds the mass part of the residual using BgHyp.massLe *after* the approximate field is small; its setup consumes the approximate-field estimates, so removing collar R3 would require a new weighted or interior contraction. Calculus.lean:Rvec_bound itself uses only $mL^{2k}\le1$ and star bounds, **not** r3; it needs no changed statement, but its callers would need new star bounds. CompositionDomain.lean:lemma62_core reads parent r3 on inherited data sites (including collar); Theorem73.lean uses bg_mass_bound. Merely deleting collar r3 from InPsiTilde leaves these proofs without premises.

**REFUTED (V2 as a one-line domain change).** A finite-volume minimizer may exist with arbitrary unit boundary values, but P2 (1.15)–(1.16) and the landed BgBounds are uniform on the whole solver region. Pair/sphere control alone cannot give their shell mass estimate; an interior-only theorem would need a new distance-weighted boundary-layer analysis and revised composition/locality statements. I found no such result in the specified source statements or landed Lean.

## The current cube factors and the verdict

**PROVED (two radii).** Def7SmallField.lean:genW uses $W_2(Y)$; RetainedShell.lean:W2_subset_nbhd and TopBlockTransport.lean:dataSite_cube_mem_nbhd place its datum cubes at distance at most $2\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor+3$ from $Y$. **REFUTED (the proposed direct V1 deduction).** Current chiY factors can control the inner one-cube shell, conditional on the averaging-block inclusion, but their definitions do not direction-test the outer part of $W_2(Y)$. TopBlockTransport.lean maps retained shell sites to the first family of ChiDomProp, which has only pair/sphere tests. A site whose cube is farther than three cubes from $Y$ is outside every current chiY stencil; sites at distance two or three may be read without being direction-tested. Existence of an admissible history with such a site and a nonzero post term remains OPEN; factor geometry alone cannot certify (RD) for all histories.

**REFUTED (use of the Q13-LATE constant-field witness as a full-history counterexample).** A constant $v\ne h$ on *all* blocks passes ChiDomProp's pair/sphere tests, but with nonempty $Y$ it must also pass late chiY direction tests; Q13-LATE did not establish this, and sufficiently misaligned constant data can fail those tests. The predicate-level witness against ChiDomProp remains valid. The relevant possible counterconfiguration equals $h$ on the chiY directional stencil and rotates only farther into a retained component; its full-history nonzero-kernel realization is still OPEN.

**REFUTED (large-field cost as automatic shell alignment).** Negating P5 (1.19) assigns a large-field mark and a cost of order $\exp[-c\beta_k\delta_k^2]$ **where that cube test is made**. It does not force a cube inside an already retained component to pass the test, nor does a cost imply pointwise alignment. P7's identity $R$ above $k_0$ supplies no new shell cutoff. The cost may control a complementary sector, but it cannot establish R3 for the parent datum there.

**OPEN — verdict V3 (source-to-Lean gap, not a proof that the paper's final theorem is false).** V1 lacks coverage of the outer retained shell; V2 loses the all-site mass estimates used by the current solver. The papers require boundary alignment for their stated background estimate yet do not display a factor or lemma deriving it on all data of P5's $W_2(Y)$ background. A different localized boundary-layer argument may be intended; its precise estimate is missing. No class or domain definition should be strengthened on the basis of Q13-LATE alone.

**OPEN (question for the author; five sentences).** P2 (1.11), (1.15)–(1.16) impose mass alignment on the collar data to obtain the complete background estimate, while P5 (1.4) uses the complete background on $\mathbb B_k((Z_k^c)^{\approx2})$ for $k>k_0$.
