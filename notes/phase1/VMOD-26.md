## 1. The papers' topology and size

**PROVED source reading.** P1 (2.26), p. 117, calls $\mathcal D_j$ the connected unions of **open** overlapping $\pi'_j$ cubes; its defining sentence says “connected unions of open cubes.” The cover cubes have side $LM\ell_j$ and bases on the $M\ell_j$ lattice, so adjacent cover cubes overlap in their interiors. P1 (2.27) defines $d_j(X)=\inf M^{-1}|\Gamma|$: $\Gamma$ is a **continuous tree graph contained in $X$** and meets **every** $\pi'_j$ cube contained in $X$. The quoted defining phrase is “continuous tree graph contained in $X$ and intersecting every cube.” Neither formula uses a tree on cube centers or face bonds.

**PROVED source reading.** P5 (1.33)–(1.35), p. 496, takes $X\in\mathcal D_k(\mathrm{mod}\,\Omega^c)$ when each *component* of $\Omega^c$ is either wholly in $X$ or disjoint from it. Formula (1.34) uses a “connected tree graph in the continuous space, contained in the domain $X$” and meeting every $\pi'_k$ cube that **intersects $X\cap\Omega$**, with length $M^{-1}|\Gamma|_\eta$. It does **not** choose a subcover. P5 (1.35) is the pinned entropy sum over $X\ni\square\subset\Omega$ with weight $e^{-d_k(X\mathrm{mod}\,\Omega^c)}$; (1.38)–(1.39), p. 497, put that size in the H7 boundary bound. P6 (4.32)–(4.34), p. 34, consumes exactly $d_k(X\mathrm{mod}\,\Omega_k^c)$ and asserts output decay in $d_{k+1}(Y\mathrm{mod}\,\Omega_{k+1}^c)$. P6 (4.11), p. 25, starts its cell-count proof with the tree of P1 (2.27), confirming the continuum convention in an actual estimate.

**PROVED topology conclusion.** Two open cover cubes whose closures meet only at a corner or a lower-dimensional face have empty intersection and, by themselves, form two open components. They can belong to the same $X\in\mathcal D_j$ only if further *overlapping open* cubes connect them. A zero-length point at the corner is outside that two-cube open domain and is not an admissible P1/P5 tree. This is why the VMOD-25 corner example refutes the **landed closed-cube** `LargeField.dCovMod`/fine-NN comparison but does **not** refute a comparison with the papers' open-domain functional. By contrast, P5's $\Omega^c$ is a closed complement; two of its pieces sharing a corner point *can* be one continuum component. The landed `LargeField.DmodOmega` and `Fluctuation.DjMod` choose NN components instead, an additional paper-to-Lean identification obligation. P6 p. 45 adds a different relation for its last resummation: two modular domains count as connected only when $Y_1\cap Y_2\cap\Omega_{k+1}\ne\varnothing$. Ordinary continuum connectedness and this active-overlap relation serve different steps; corner contact outside $\Omega_{k+1}$ satisfies neither active-overlap condition. P3 (4.47)–(4.48), printed pp. 79–80, uses source decay in $d_j(X)$ and tries to transfer it to output decay; the all-cover incidence of P1 (2.27) is the printed size being used, while the landed corrected GM2 uses a chosen cover. P1/P5/P6 are consistent on their stated topology. **OPEN/known separate gap:** P3 (4.48), p. 79, uses an all-cover cube in $X_1$ as though it contained an old chosen fine cube; the U-shaped counterexample in `CHECKS1-astra.md` §1 refutes that implication. It does not license changing the papers' definition silently.
