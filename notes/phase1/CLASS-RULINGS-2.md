# CLASS-RULINGS-2: units, switch, source, and restricted fields

## Ruling

Use one **physical** mass sequence $\nu_j$ in the action and source $b_j=\nu_jh$, and a separate **background** mass $\mu_k$, fixed from the a priori index $k_0$ before the step: $\mu_k=0$ for $k\le k_0$, $\mu_k=\nu_kL^{-2k}$ for $k>k_0$. In the fine-unit action the prefactor is $\beta_kL^{-k(d-2)}$. The full complex-$h$ domain currently used by `IsSF` and `IsObs` at $\mu_k=0$ must be replaced, for the physical Q13 route, by a bounded pinned field domain and the axial/profile germs in `Q13-ANSWER.md` §3. These changes are a proposal for the draft classes, not a claim that their preservation theorem has been proved.

The zero constant-spin observable response proved from the landed class is a domain artifact. At $\mu_k=0$ its class bounds a holomorphic function on every constant complex $h$, so Liouville makes it independent of $h$. Physical $\nu_j$ is generally positive in that regime, and P5 keeps its field term in the main action. The repair must preserve $\nu_jh$ in the terms and restrict the complex field to $|h|\le B_h$; source $g$ remains analytic on its stated $\ell^1$ ball. A fixed real unit $h$ in the frozen root lies in the repaired physical class. The switch and restricted irrelevance estimates remain conditional on the named Q13 inputs.

**6. P5's boundary correction, exactly identified.** P5 p. 489, (1.8), subtracts
$$\sum_{y\in Z_k^c\cap T^{(j)}}\sum_{x\in(Z_k^c)^\approx\cap T^{(j)}}\left\{\tfrac12v_1^{(j)}(y,x)[\psi_j^2(x)-\psi_j^2(y)]+v_2^{(j)}(y,x)\nu_jh\cdot[(\psi_j)_0(x)-(\psi_j)_0(y)]\right\}.$$

P1 p. 146, (3.106), at the reference $(\psi,b)=(e_1,ve_1)$, gives the local coefficients
$$v_1(x,y,X;1,v,0)=D_{\psi(x)}\mathcal E(y,X;e_1,ve_1)[e_1],\qquad v_2(x,y,X;1,v,0)=D_{b\cdot e_2}D_{\psi(x)}\mathcal E(y,X;e_1,ve_1)[e_2].\tag{P1 3.106}$$

P1 (3.106) also defines a transverse second-spin derivative $v_0$; P5 (1.8) uses $v_1,v_2$. An implementation must retain the whole-lattice origin of the coefficients and the restricted-domain antisymmetric sum. It vanishes after the full torus double sum but generally not on $Z_k^c$; P5 p. 490 explicitly says it cancels relevant and marginal boundary terms. `LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` Definition IV.3.1 (D1), currently at lines 2493–2496, and the corresponding Def-7 representation should add this definition, its branch condition, and the $v_1,v_2$ coefficient data. The Lean action must not silently replace $(\psi_j)_0$ by $\psi_j$.
