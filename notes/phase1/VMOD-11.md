### 1.1 Construction and domain proof

The finite-grid proof of `VMOD-2.md` §1.1 gives, with $r=[R_{k-1}]+1\ge4L+21$,
$$\operatorname{dist}_\infty(A,W^c)\ge(r-2L-2)M\ell_k,
\qquad B(x,LM\ell_k)\subset A\quad(x\in U). \tag{1.3}$$
Indeed, a coarse cell meeting $U$ is followed by one complete $\pi_K$ halo cell before $A^c$; closure and halo together move at most $2L+2$ old $\pi_k$ cells from $U$. Since $r>2L+2$, $A\subset W$. Thus the lower layers and their recursive separation are unchanged and the inserted interface obeys
$$\operatorname{dist}_1(W^c,A)\ge(r-2L-2)M\ell_k>5LM_1\ell_k. \tag{1.4}$$
The lower old domains are $M_1\ell_n$-cell unions, and $A$ is a $\pi_K$-cell union, so $F^\sharp$ is cube aligned. This is **proved conditional on the proved paper-level LFR recursive dilation and region-history facts** (`LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` II.2.2–II.2.5); the finite-set identification in Lean is open. The global separation one may claim uniformly for $F^\sharp$ is $\varrho_3=5LM_1$. The inequalities $\varrho\ge4dLM_1$ required by `DGSetting.cover_sep` and $\varrho\ge4dLM$ required by `BlockCount.blockCount_fam` fail as proposed applications to that parameter. Equation (1.4) only improves the new interface.

For the old pair, the domains give the exact pointwise relation
$$n_{H^-}(x)=\min(n_{F^\sharp}(x),k),\qquad
\Omega_1(H^-)=\Omega_1(F^\sharp). \tag{1.5}$$
For the boundary pair, LFR Definition II.2.4(iv) gives, for $n\le k$,
$$\Omega_n(H^\partial)=
(\Omega_n(H^-)\cap\Omega_{k+1}^c)\cup
(\Omega_n(U_{k+1})\cap\Omega_{k+1}),\quad
\Omega_K(H^\partial)=U_{k+1}\cap\Omega_{k+1}. \tag{1.6}$$
LFR Lemma I.3.5(f) says $\Omega_{k+1}\subset Z_k^c\subset A$. Outside $\Omega_{k+1}$ (1.6) agrees with the old lower domains; inside it the cover has layer $K$. Therefore
$$\Omega_1(H^\partial)\subset\Omega_1(F^\sharp),\qquad
n_{H^\partial}(x)\le n_{F^\sharp}(x). \tag{1.7}$$
Equations (1.5)–(1.7) are proved directly from the displayed definitions and cited history inclusion. `CoverPartition.lean coverOf_partition_of_subset` then proves, for **both** $H$, $\sum_q\chi_q(x)=1$ and $\operatorname{coverDenom}(F^\sharp,x)\ge4^{-d}$ on $\Omega_1(H)$, once the source-to-Lean inclusions and `CubeAligned` are supplied. Its `commonCover_partition` packages exactly this implication with the history set $\Omega_{k+1}$. The reverse inclusion $\Omega_1(H^-)\subset\Omega_1(H^\partial)$ is false in the empty-history example of `VMOD-5.md` §2.

### 1.2 The seven geometric fields, with constants

Here is the direct proof for either $H$, using (1.5) or (1.7), $M_1\ge4L^2$, and the cyclic sup-separation of the LFR minimal sets. The cover-layer comparison is the finite-grid fact that a box or bump based at scale $m$ meets only cover layers $m-1,m,m+1$: a two-scale jump requires at least $5LM_1\ell_n$ at the intervening interface, whereas the relevant box radius is at most $3M_1\ell_m$ and the bump radius $3M_1\ell_m/2$. This is the scale comparison proved in `VMOD-2.md` §1.4 and `VMOD-3.md` §2; the new interface uses (1.3). In particular $n_H(x)\le m+1$ for $x\in\operatorname{intBox}(q)\cap\Omega_1(H)$, and the same bound after an $H$-block displacement when $\chi_q(y)\ne0$.

1. **`blockAligned`.** `BoxGeometry.lean box_sameCell` makes $\operatorname{intBox}(q)$ a union of aligned cells of side $M_1\ell_m$. Every $H$-block meeting it has side at most $\ell_{m+1}$, which divides $M_1\ell_m$ under the row 5a divisibility assumption. Each such block is wholly inside or outside the box. This is `BlockAligned H (intBox q)`, including boxes that straddle the *physical Dirichlet wall* of $H^\partial$.
2. **`chi_unit`.** The denominator estimate just proved applies at $x$ and its nearest neighbors, even if a neighbor is outside the operator domain. The nonnegative bump is at most the sum of bumps, giving $0\le\chi_q\le1$ at the required stencil points.
3. **`sum_chi`.** On $\Omega_1(H)$ the denominator is positive, so the normalized bumps sum to one. This is also the exact conclusion of `coverOf_partition_of_subset`.
4. **`chi_smooth`.** The quotient proof of `Cover.lean chi_diff`, with the intrinsic sup-overlap count in place of its stronger global-separation wrapper, gives first and second differences at scale $n_F$ bounded by $cH1(d,L)/(M_1\ell_{n_F})$ and $cH2(d,L)/(M_1\ell_{n_F})^2$. Since $n_H\le n_F$, the `IsSmoothField H` norm is bounded with $C_x=Cchi(d,L)=cH1(d,L)+d,cH2(d,L)$. The direct quotient proof must still be ported to Lean; citing `chi_diff` with the unavailable $4dLM_1$ binder is invalid.
5. **`inner_near`.** `Cover.lean inBox_of_chi_ne_zero` gives the $3M_1\ell_m/2$ bump radius. A nearest-neighbor move costs one site; an $H$-block move costs at most $\ell_{m+1}=L\ell_m$. The inequality $M_1\ge4L^2$ places the resulting point and its stencil inside the $5M_1\ell_m/2$ interaction-box interior, as required by `Inner`.
6. **`card_box`.** Only $m\in\{n_F(x)-1,n_F(x),n_F(x)+1\}$ occur. At each scale at most $5^d$ aligned box centers contain $x$. Hence the field constant is exactly $3\cdot5^d$.
7. **`card_qset`.** A parameter-cell witness in a hat box lies within $13M_1\ell_m/4$ of its center. Sup-separation excludes scales differing by two or more. At each of the three possible scales the coordinate count is $(8L)^d$, giving $|\mathcal Q(q)|\le N_0(d,L)=3(8L)^d$.

These are complete finite-grid arguments **conditional on the cited LFR geometry and numerical row**. They are **not yet landed `CoverGeom` instances**: the LFR history and minimal-set facts, the direct `chi_diff` and overlap lemmas, and the two layer comparison lemmas still need Lean proofs. The constants $C_x$, $3\cdot5^d$ and $N_0$ do not depend on $k$, history, or the wall. If a direct Lean quotient proof yields a larger explicit $C_x$, every `DecSpecs`/`FamSpec` numerical row must be rechecked with that value rather than silently using `Cchi`.

## 2. What the landed fluctuation chain can and cannot yield

The structure in `Fluctuation/Theorem42Input.lean` fixes a finite action-cube set $\mathcal Q$, an open complex data set $S\subset U$, a finite common parameter index $\iota$ with centers $c(i)$, $\kappa_1$, $C_b$, an agreement relation $Ag$, actual action difference $\mathrm{act}(b,u)$, and a family $F(b,u,s)$ on $S\times\{|s_i|<e^{\kappa_1}\}$. Its fields, **with these exact quantifiers**, are
$$\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{at\_one}:&F(b,u,\mathbf1)=\mathrm{act}(b,u),\\
\mathrm{analytic}:&F_b\text{ holomorphic on }S\times\operatorname{polydisc}(0,e^{\kappa_1}),\\
\mathrm{bound}:&|F(b,u,s)|\le C_b\text{ there},\\
\mathrm{loc}:&F_b\text{ has }\operatorname{LocalityAEOn}(e^{\kappa_1},S,
Pset\circ c,\operatorname{compY}(c,X1(F^\sharp,m_0,j,\{b\})),Ag).
\end{array}\tag{2.1}$$
Here $b\in\mathcal Q$, $u\in S$ and $s$ is in the displayed polydisc in every row. The intended quantitative value is $C_b=C_F'\beta r_H^2e^{-\gamma_wD_k}$ (`Theorem42Input.lean` module note; `FLUCTUATION.md` §4.2 Steps 2–3), with an explicit proof of its comparison to the actual cube action required.

**Candidate actual family.** For each $s$ solve the decoupled background equation on $H^\partial$ and the decoupled fluctuation equation on $H^-$ using the *same* $C^\sharp$ and the same parameter coordinates. Compose the two data maps as in `LOCALIZATION.md` §4, variant (V2), then set $F(b,u,s)$ equal to the normalized P6 cube action difference of the two resulting backgrounds, including the old $\nu$-free or complete action according to the step. This is the required physical family; the `s`-independent inhabitant of `DecoupledCubeResponse.nonempty_of_undecoupled` does not prove its locality or weighted decay for the intended agreement relation.

* **At one is a conditional algebraic consequence.** `Decoupled2Background.i4b_one` and `Decoupled2One.i4a_one` identify each decoupled solution at $s=1$ with its corresponding ordinary solution under `DecSpecs`/`FamSpec`, `OneSpec`, admissible domains and uniqueness. `Decoupled2.Gs_one` is the operator identity behind this. Then substitute the solutions into the affine composition and the P6 cube action. `Fluctuation/Theorem42WireStep.lean composed_change` is a relevant composition identity; `Theorem42WireAct.lean realStepAct` names a one-parameter actual cube difference. A **new** theorem must identify that expression and its star/counterterm convention with the `act` assigned in (2.1) for each of P6's two comparisons, including the $k=k_0$ change of convention. None of the one-theorems proves that last action identity by itself. Name the remaining identification OPEN-ACTUAL-CUBE-AT-ONE.
* **Analyticity is conditional on a mixed H7 domain theorem.** `Decoupled2Background.i4b_analytic`, `Decoupled2SolveAnalytic.i4a_analytic`, and `CubeAction.cubeDiff_analytic` supply the three analytic pieces for a fixed operator pair under their hypotheses. The affine composition is analytic. Their application to the actual P6 data requires an **open common neighbourhood** of $S\times\operatorname{polydisc}(0,e^{\kappa_1})$ on which both assembled background data, the perturbed fluctuation data, and the composed data remain in their respective BG/H7 domains. This is OPEN-H7-BG-MIXED (`FLUCTUATION.md` §3.6). The landed `Theorem42WireAct.realStepAct_analytic` is an undecoupled $s=1$ theorem, not this joint assertion.
* **The uniform bound remains open.** `WeightedProp51.lean weightedProp51S_exists` supplies the corrected one-set weighted Proposition 5.1 input, and `FarDecayUncond.lean lemma41_domain_uncond`, `CubeActionBound.lean cubeDiff_step3_quadratic`, `Theorem42WireAct.realStepAct_bound` give undecoupled far-action controls under their explicit hypotheses. They do not bound the **mixed, decoupled** response uniformly on the complex $s$ polydisc. The needed two physical-domain source and response estimates, open-domain transport, and old-action quadratic expansion are OPEN-OLD-H7-DECAY / OPEN-DECOUPLED-WEIGHTED-RESPONSE. The fixed-$h$ mass arithmetic and late exponential absorption are proved conditionally in `FLUCTUATION.md` (3.13a)–(3.14); they do not supply this analytic response estimate.
* **Locality does not follow from the landed `i5_fluct`.** Individual `i4b_local_fp` and `i4a_local_fpS` provide locality on `Dec2.IsIncUnion` graph-component pairs once both `FootLocal` structures and the matching data-agreement hypotheses are available. A new theorem must turn the P6 polymer components used by `compY` and `LocalityAEOn` into simultaneous incidence unions for *both* physical operators and must transport agreement through the affine composition. The landed `I5FluctGeom.incUnion_of_nnComp` instead assumes `CoverLocal C Hp`, `HatConn C Hp`, **and** $\Omega_1(Hm)\subset\Omega_1(Hp)$. In the actual orientation $Hp=H^\partial$, $Hm=H^-$, the first and third premises are false in the empty history above. `I5FluctLocal.fan_local_fl` and `I5Fluct.i5_fluct` use this lemma. Thus they cannot be instantiated for P6 merely by giving them the newly available mixed `CoverBase.nc`. Name the needed paired footprint-to-`LocalityAEOn` result OPEN-TWO-OPERATOR-LOCALITY (or OPEN-I5-COMPOSITE-MIXED). Its theorem must state the concrete graph/component map and exact `Ag`, not assume the desired locality as a binder.

The two footprint locality theorems also carry numerical and data premises beyond `FootLocal`: $c\exp(N_0\kappa)\le M_1$ for I-4b or $c_\star\exp(N_0\kappa)\le M_1$ for I-4a, a `DecSpecs` or `FamSpec` witness, bounded decoupling parameters, their `extS` rules off and on the parameter set, the relevant `IsIncUnion`, and `InDomainC`/`BgHyp` or `DecHyp` plus `DataAgreeS` on `SYF`. The at-one step additionally uses `OneSpec` and the smallness inequalities; the analytic step uses its `AnSpec` and open-domain hypotheses. These binders must be verified with $K_c$ from VMOD-9, $C_x$ from §1.2, and the actual P6 H7 radii. Neither row 6 of (1.1) nor the halo establishes them automatically.

Consequently `DecoupledCubeResponse` for the actual step is **conditional on four named, independently checkable assertions**: OPEN-ACTUAL-CUBE-AT-ONE, OPEN-H7-BG-MIXED, OPEN-DECOUPLED-WEIGHTED-RESPONSE, and OPEN-TWO-OPERATOR-LOCALITY, in addition to the pair instances and numerical hypotheses. The mathematical mixed-wall NC3 theorem removes the cut-operator bound from that list once its Lean bridge lands; it does not prove any of these four assertions.

There is a further independent binder in `Theorem42.theorem42_of_input`:
$$\varrho_{F^\sharp}\ge4dL\,L^{m_0+m_1}=4dLM.\tag{2.2}$$
Its use in `cube_expansion_traces` is through the landed cover-count and trace geometry. The uniform $\varrho_{F^\sharp}=5LM_1$ from the minimal construction does not satisfy (2.2). The replacement is OPEN-BLOCKCOUNT-MINIMAL: prove the required $M$-scale count/trace estimate directly for the recursive minimal set, propagate its explicit constant, and restate the input theorem with that count. The local $M_1$-scale bounds $3\cdot5^d$ and $N_0$ do not discharge (2.2). `theorem42_of_input` also requires `CovSetup` for every action cube, disjointness of parameter cells from $X1$, injectivity of $c$, the numerical $\kappa$/$\kappa_1$ inequalities, and the `AgY`-to-`Ag` link. These require verification for the *chosen* P6 cover, not just a `DecoupledCubeResponse` witness.
