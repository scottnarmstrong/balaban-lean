# Charging a modular collar: the exact theorem and the missing interfaces

**12 October 2026.** Response to `followup_r17.txt`. The proposed repair has a valid **conditional** geometric theorem and a valid weighted resummation. The hypotheses needed to apply them to P6 are not consequences of the cited large-field results. In particular, the positive new-volume exponent counts the seed set $\mathcal N_{k+1}=\mathsf P_{k+1}\cup\mathsf Q_{k+1}\cup\mathsf R_{k+1}\cup\mathsf S_{k+1}$, whereas a collar of $\Omega_{k+1}^c$ or $Z_{k+1}$ may contain cubes generated by the shadow of $Z_k$ with no new seed. Also, P5 H7 and the landed `Def7.IsRep.bdry` require an **unweighted bound on each boundary coefficient**. A large-field kernel bound cannot by itself supply it. The precise repair would require a new coupled boundary/operator norm or a direct coefficient estimate.

Lean references of the form `File.declaration` below identify a declaration in the named `.lean` file; `File` is not asserted to be a Lean namespace. The declarations themselves were checked in the read-only landed tree.

## 1. Source definitions and the charge that is actually available

P5 (1.33)–(1.34), printed p. 496, use block-union NN domains and a continuous tree meeting **every** $\pi'_j$ block that intersects $Y\cap\Omega_j$. The tree length is not reduced inside a hole; see `VMOD-16.md` §1 and its PDF transcription. The landed `Fluctuation/SizeModDefs.lean` defines `DjMod`, but `ModAdmissible` and `dcovMod` use a **chosen** family of contained cover blocks. Therefore a bound for $d^{\rm cov}$ is not automatically a bound for P5's $d$.

Definition II.5.11 of `notes/phase1/LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` §II.5.6, matching P6 (3.9)–(3.11), printed pp. 19–20, specifies for a pre-$R$ component $Z'$:
$$
\kappa^{(k)}(Z')=a_kN(Z')-b_kO(Z'),\qquad
a_k=A_3(\log\beta_k)^{2p_0},\quad b_k=c_1\log\beta_k+c_2,
\tag{1.1}
$$
where $N(Z')=|\mathcal N_{k+1}\cap Z'|_\#$ and $O(Z')=|Z_k^c\cap Z'|_{k+1}$. The **positive** term counts new seeds, not all cubes of $Z'\setminus Z_k^\uparrow$ and not all cubes of $\Omega_{k+1}^c\setminus\Omega_k^c$. The negative term charges old-complement volume, including hull-added cubes. This is implemented by `LargeField/Budget/Exponents.lean` `BudgetConsts.kappaStep` and `RegionHistory.kappaPre`; `LargeField/Region/Step.lean` `Region.newCubes` is exactly the union of the four seed sets. Definition II.5.7 (S2) bounds the **integrated step kernel** by $e^{-\kappa^{(k)}}$; `LargeField/Operators/KerBound.lean` `KerBound` is the composed bound (1.26). Neither statement bounds an individual boundary coefficient.

There is a direct obstruction to identifying the requested count with $N$. Take a nonempty old region $Z_k$ and set $\mathsf P_{k+1}=\mathsf Q_{k+1}=\mathsf R_{k+1}=\mathsf S_{k+1}=\varnothing$. Then $N=0$. Yet the new shadow is built from $U'_k=(Z_k^{\sim(\rho'+1)})^\uparrow$, and `RegionStep.nbhd_Uprime_subset` puts the $(\rho_{k+1}+4)$-neighborhood of $U'_k$ in $(\Omega'_{k+1})^c\subset\Omega_{k+1}^c$ (`LargeField/Region/Inclusions.lean:142–152, 181–186`). On a sufficiently large torus, take $Z_k$ to be one bounded old component: that neighborhood has cells outside $Z_k^\uparrow$. Thus an output touching the new shadow can have a positive count of newly occupied hole or $Z$ cubes while $N=0$. This is a **proved set-function observation**; it does not assert that its collar cost exceeds the old source size. It shows that the proposed payment by $a_kN$ needs a separate charging map, including a treatment of old-shadow cubes.

The long $\mathsf P_2$ tube of `VMOD-16.md` §2 is the complementary test: it has $N\asymp T$, and any P1/P5 block-union output containing the fixed source has new size at least $T-52$. Thus a positive seed charge could pay this example if a bounded charge-to-collar map and a sufficient numerical surplus are proved. It does not prove those assertions for all admissible histories.

## 2. A corrected size-transfer theorem with explicit constants

Put $K=k+1$ and $G=(2L+1)^d$. The following is a theorem **for a specified witness record**; the record's existence for actual P6 histories is `OPEN-P6-CHARGED-COLLAR-GEOMETRY`.

**Theorem 2.1 (charged witness transfer).** Assume $d,L\ge3$, the scale and divisibility hypotheses of `GraphGmTree.modular_tree_of_witnesses`, and $X\in\mathcal D_k(\operatorname{mod}\Omega_k^c)$ with $X\cap\Omega_k\ne\varnothing$. Let $C$ be the retained derivative generators and let $E$ be a finite family of **distinct charged generators**, each assigned to a different physical cube counted by $n_{\rm new}(Y)$; thus $|E|\le n_{\rm new}(Y)$. Choose source blocks $Q_0$ and, for every $P\in C\sqcup E$, a nonempty family $D(P)$ of at most $G$ aligned layer cells, with a home cell containing $P$. Give the union $\mathcal G=\bigcup_{P\in C\sqcup E}D(P)$ a face-adjacent forest rooted at the source blocks, exactly as in the `hatt` premise of `modular_tree_of_witnesses`. Assume the source nesting `hSN`, active-source inclusion `hSB`, layer, alignment, home, cover and cardinal premises of that theorem for the enlarged generator family. Define, using the same `holeCl` convention as `Localization/GraphGmDefs.lean`,
$$
R=X\cup\bigcup Q_0\cup\bigcup_{g\in\mathcal G}g,
\qquad Y=\operatorname{holeCl}_{\Omega_K}(R).
\tag{2.1}
$$
Assume $Y\in\mathcal D_K(\operatorname{mod}\Omega_K^c)$. For the **P5 original-size** conclusion, require the source-nesting and path construction for every P5-admissible old tree, and assume that each resulting output tree meets every $\pi'_K$ block intersecting $Y\cap\Omega_K$; this is the all-block incidence premise `OPEN-P6-ORIGINAL-BLOCK-INCIDENCE`. Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_K^{\rm cov}(Y\operatorname{mod}\Omega_K^c)
&\le L^{-1}d_k^{\rm cov}(X\operatorname{mod}\Omega_k^c)
       +dG|C|+dG\,n_{\rm new}(Y),\\
d_K(Y\operatorname{mod}\Omega_K^c)
&\le L^{-1}d_k(X\operatorname{mod}\Omega_k^c)
       +dG|C|+dG\,n_{\rm new}(Y).
\tag{2.2}
\end{aligned}
$$
The second line uses the original-size version of source nesting: each source $Q\in Q_0$ contains an old active cover block met by **every** P5-admissible old tree. Thus the explicit constants for this certificate are $C_1=C_2=d(2L+1)^d$. If one seed requires at most $u$ generator records, $C_2=udG$.

*Proof.* For $d^{\rm cov}$ choose a minimizing old pair, which exists in the landed finite-torus model by `SizeModCore.exists_admissible_eq_dcovModE` and `exists_admissible_eq_dcovMod`. Apply the construction of `GraphGmTree.modular_tree_of_witnesses` to $C\sqcup E$: its proof retains the old tree, scales its cost by $L^{-1}$ (`bondCost_scaleW_succ`), joins each root and each of at most $G$ cells per generator inside $Y$, and bounds the added cost by $dG(|C|+|E|)$. Its `hKgen` and `hcov` give a chosen admissible output cover, so `SizeModCore.dcovMod_le_of_admissible` gives the first line. If the two sorts of generators occupy the same `ZCell`, take their union; its cardinal is at most $|C|+|E|$, so the same bound holds. For P5's continuous $d_k$, take an old admissible tree of cost at most $d_k(X)+\varepsilon$. The identical box paths are continuous paths inside $Y$; source nesting attaches them to the old tree, with the same normalized upper bounds. The all-block incidence premise makes the resulting tree P5-admissible, including cover blocks that intersect $Y\cap\Omega_K$ but are **not contained in $Y$**. Its cost is at most $(d_k(X)+\varepsilon)/L+dG(|C|+|E|)$. Let $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. Finally $|E|\le n_{\rm new}(Y)$. $\square$

The all-block incidence premise cannot be replaced by `hKgen`: that landed premise concerns only a selected family of blocks **contained** in $Y$. P5 (1.34) quantifies over every intersecting block. In a top-cell model it would suffice to prove that the constructed tree visits a site of every generated active top cell and that each remaining active output block contains an old active block met by the old tree. The former is visible in the proof of `GraphGmTree.modular_tree_of_witnesses` at lines 303–309 and 466–470; the latter is new. Theorem 2.1 does not assert that (2.1) is always a block-union domain. The thin-hole example shows why that membership and the charged completion are substantive premises.

For the requested **physical** count, define $n_{\rm hole}(Y)$ as the number of new $\pi_K$ cubes in hole components met by $Y$ plus the cubes of $Z_K\setminus Z_k^\uparrow$ that the completion uses, counted once. The precise choice of “touches” must be fixed at the $\pi_K$-cell level, for instance intersection with the one-cell neighborhood of $Y$. Theorem 2.1 applies with $n_{\rm new}=n_{\rm hole}$ only after each completion generator is injected into those cubes. The further comparison
$$n_{\rm hole}(Y)\le u\,|\mathcal N_K\cap Z'|_\#\tag{2.3}$$
is **false without a shadow term**, as the empty-record example above shows. A viable actual-history assertion must have the form $n_{\rm hole}(Y)\le uN(Z')+vS_k(Y)$ with an old-shadow account $S_k(Y)$, and must show how the old budget pays $vS_k(Y)$. No such assertion is landed. Merely redefining $n_{\rm new}$ as $|\mathcal N_K\cap Y|$ would not prove the injection required in Theorem 2.1.

## 3. What the large-field factor can pay

Here is the exact finite-sum implication. It separates the algebra, which is **proved**, from the geometric and analytic hypotheses, which are **OPEN** for P6.

**Theorem 3.1 (weighted fixed-output resummation).** Fix a history, a pre-$R$ component $Z'$, a P5 output $Y$, and write $D=d_K(Y\operatorname{mod}\Omega_K^c)$, $n=n_{\rm new}(Y)$, $N=N(Z')$, $O=O(Z')$. Suppose every coefficient attached to $(X,C)$ satisfies
$$|a_{X,C}|\le\beta_k^{-2}e^{-(\kappa-1)\Delta_X-(\kappa_1-1)|C|},\qquad
D\le\Delta_X/L+C_1|C|+C_2n,\tag{3.1}$$
on the same strict H7 domain for both P6 operators, with $\Delta_X$ the matching old size. Put $\kappa_2=\kappa_1/2-1$. Assume $L\ge3$, $\kappa_0\ge0$, $\kappa\ge3\kappa_0+3$, and $\kappa_1\ge4\kappa C_1$. Assume the number of old sources attached to $Y$ is at most $S(1+D)$, and that for each source every contributing derivative family is a subset of a common candidate set $V_Y$ with
$$|V_Y|\le A'C_G(1+D)+A'J n,\qquad C_G=(3(2L-1))^d.\tag{3.2}$$
Here $A'=(L^{m_0})^d$ is the number of derivative indices in one layer cell (`GraphGmCount.card_deriv_in_cell`); $J$ is an explicit number of extra candidate cells per charged physical cube. Put
$$q=A'C_Ge^{-\kappa_2},\qquad r=A'Je^{-\kappa_2},\qquad q<1.\tag{3.3}$$
Then the **unweighted** fixed-$Y$ sum satisfies
$$
\sum_{X,C:\,Y(X,C)=Y}|a_{X,C}|
\le \beta_k^{-2}\frac{Se^q}{1-q}
\exp\!\bigl(-(2\kappa-1)D+(2\kappa C_2+r)n\bigr).
\tag{3.4}
$$
If, additionally, $n\le uN$, the coefficient bound is uniform in every variable integrated by the step operator, and that operator has (S2) bound $e^{-a_kN+b_kO}$, the norm of the corresponding **linear** operator contribution is bounded by the same constant times
$$\exp\!\bigl(-(2\kappa-1)D-[a_k-u(2\kappa C_2+r)]N+b_kO\bigr).\tag{3.5}$$
Consequently the existing LF-2 gain $g_kN$ is retained under the **new numerical condition**
$$A_3(\log\beta_k)^{2p_0}-g_k\ \ge\ u(2\kappa C_2+r).\tag{3.6}$$
For a P6 (4.34)-type prefactor after this weighted operation, also require $\beta_k^{1/2}\ge Se^q/(1-q)$. A convenient sufficient count condition is $\kappa_1\ge2+2\log(2A'C_G)$, which makes $q\le\frac12$.

*Proof.* The size inequality and the first four numerical assumptions give, by the same arithmetic as landed `Vmod7Glue.exponent_arith_graph`, now retaining the collar term,
$$
(\kappa-1)\Delta_X+(\kappa_1-1)|C|
\ge\kappa_0\Delta_X+2\kappa D+\kappa_2|C|-2\kappa C_2n.
\tag{3.7}
$$
Drop $e^{-\kappa_0\Delta_X}\le1$. For each $X$, sum over the candidate subsets, enlarging the actual fiber:
$\sum_{C\subset V_Y}e^{-\kappa_2|C|}=(1+e^{-\kappa_2})^{|V_Y|}\le e^{q(1+D)+rn}$. Multiply by $S(1+D)$. Since $e^{(1-q)D}\ge1+(1-q)D$ for $D\ge0$, one has $(1+D)e^{qD}\le(1-q)^{-1}e^D$. This proves (3.4). Multiply by the (S2) factor and use $n\le uN$ to obtain (3.5); (3.6) leaves $g_kN$. $\square$

If the sharper ownership premises of landed `GraphGmCount.boundary_gm3_fixed_Y` hold for a **fixed undecorated $Y$**, its `boundary_gm3_fixed_Y_rate` replaces (3.2)–(3.3) by $\sum_Ce^{-\kappa_2|C|}\le1$, so $q=r=0$ in (3.4)–(3.6). This is the cheapest counting route. Yet a collar cell generated by $\mathsf P$ with $C=\varnothing$ is not owned by a derivative. Taking `Bc` to mean only the old source makes the landed ownership premise false. Enlarging `Bc` by the history-paid collar can restore ownership of the remaining cells, but then one must prove that every selected derivative has `cellOfCube P ∈ Yc\setminus Bc`; derivatives inside that collar violate this. Equation (3.2) is a way to pay their possible entropy, provided its candidate-location assertion is proved. `SizeModG1.G1_cellCount` supplies the active-cell part $C_G(1+D)$ for $d^{\rm cov}$, not the extra $Jn$ part or the P5 original size.

Theorem 3.1 concerns an operator times a sum of boundary coefficients. Its operator step is a norm estimate for a linear contribution; applying it to the exponentiated boundary action in (D1) would require an additional uniform exponential-integrability argument. It **does not** establish P6's displayed (4.33)–(4.34), printed p. 34, as an unweighted boundary expansion. P6 (4.34) bounds each fixed-$Y$ coefficient before applying the large-field operator. In the landed `LargeField/Def7.lean`, `IsRep.bdry` is a `D7Bdry` record whose `bound` field is $\|B(Y,\mathbb A)\|<B_0e^{-\kappa d^{\rm cov}_K(Y)}$ on H7, while `IsRep.opBound` and `IsRep.budget` are separate fields. Thus (D5), (D6)/(H6′), and LF-2 cannot absorb the positive factor in (3.4) **inside the existing `IsRep` clause**. The clause to change is **(D7)**, together with its (D1)/(D5) consumption: introduce a history-charged boundary norm or store a local credit allocation, then prove that the new representation class is preserved by the RG step and remains summable. That is `OPEN-P6-WEIGHTED-D7`. In particular (3.6) is stronger than the landed `BudgetSetup.gain_ge`, which proves only $g_k\le a_k$, and a new version of the budget induction must retain the indicated surplus. The `BudgetSetup.new_volume` inequality and `RegionHistory.lemmaB_recursive` pay the negative old-complement and combinatoric terms in (1.1); they do not provide a spare per-boundary-output charge.

## 4. Relation to the decorated and Moore routes

Charging a full block-union collar **could** remove the need for `VMOD-15.md`'s ledger $(X,S,\mathcal L)$ if `OPEN-P6-CHARGED-COLLAR-GEOMETRY`, the fixed-$Y$ count or candidate bound (3.2), the old-source count, the charge comparison with its shadow account, and `OPEN-P6-WEIGHTED-D7` are all proved. The output would then be an ordinary $Y\in\mathcal D_K(\operatorname{mod}\Omega_K^c)$ and the sums in (3.4) would already be at fixed undecorated $Y$. The ledger is presently a conditional alternative, not an extra datum forced by algebra.

The charged collar does **not** make `OPEN-P6-STAR-TO-MODULAR` disappear. It specifies a possible solution of that problem: take the rooted Moore/incidence support of `VMOD-14.md`, construct the NN block-union completion (2.1), and prove its generator/attachment and price record. The diagonal-wall history of `VMOD-14.md` still refutes an NN-component bridge for the physical decoupled fields. The Moore bridge for both $H_-=\mathbb B_k(W_k)$ and $H_\partial=G^\partial_{k+1}$, including eligible-hat connectivity or an incidence-component substitute, remains `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE`; the mixed strict H7 estimate remains `OPEN-P6-MIXED-H7`. Filling a collar does not prove either analytic assertion. NN components of $\Omega_K^c$ remain the modular holes throughout.
