## T2. Target interface and the paper's route

**PROVED as source statements.** FLUCTUATION Theorem 3.1(a) asks for energy coefficients on `Y=hatY(S)∈𝒟_{k+1}` with rate `κa=2κ−5/2−3κ0−c1` in `d^{cov}_{k+1}(Y mod Ωᶜ)`; (b) asks for observable coefficients on those domains with rate `κb=κa−κ0−1`, including locality, analyticity, symmetry and the stated small prefactor (`notes/phase1/FLUCTUATION.md:397-409`). The renormalized (g),(h) use rates at least `κb` (`:416-419,686-694`). P4 H.4 and H.7 require terms indexed by `X∈𝒟_j`, local on `X`, with `exp(−κ d_j(X))`; P5's next-step theorem says the *new* `j=k+1` terms have `3κ/2` in place of `κ` (`review/papers/P4-LTE-II-RG-equations.md:23-25,70,79`; `review/papers/P5-LTE-III-inductive-description.md:136-144`). The stronger new rate closes the induction at `κ`, subject to the other hypotheses.

**PROVED as manuscript arithmetic; OPEN as complete RG instantiation.** The paper obtains this margin by starting its localized interaction at `κU=2κ−1`, losing additive amounts for PE, entropy and a further resummation, and requiring `κ` sufficiently large: `κb≥3κ/2` iff `κ≥7+8κ0+2c1` (`FLUCTUATION.md:397-399,546-555,569-578,686-694`). The terms are already indexed by activity/localization domains `hatY(S)`; the text does not pass observable or energy cube-set coefficients through `detCollarHull` (`:540-555,569-573`). The factor `L` gained on rescaling is visible for a *fixed physical bond set*: `scaleW(L,m₀,m₁,j)=L^{-(m₀+m₁+j)}`, so its cost at `j+1` is `1/L` of its cost at `j` (`Localization/TreeLength.lean:528`). Optimizing over different covers and adding a hull need separate comparison lemmas; this fixed-bond identity does not cancel `1/60751` or `1/93,767,625` for newly generated terms. The cited induction closure instead uses the additive rate budget above. The current Lean `dExt` coefficients are an intermediate representation, not the manuscript's completed `(a),(b)` output.

## T3. Options and durable-code cost

**A. Raise the tower's `κdec`. REFUTED as a complete fix; OPEN as a redesign of the rate scheme.** An extra lower bound of roughly `κdec>94.75 million` makes the generic observable hull rate positive, but it still cannot reach `κout=1.5κdec` because its starting rate is exactly `κout`; the same objection is stronger after the `renYs` fibre map. A two-parameter `StepTower (κmin,κdecMin)` or a new lower-bound field would require changing `StepTower.κdec_eq`, its constructors (`StepTower`, `StepTowerTheta`, `StepTowerRen`, `StepTowerRenSol`), the fixed-numeral thresholds (`DetPackageFromTower`, `RenormDetFibreTower`, `DetCollarHullWitness`, `DetOutputTermsWitness`, `UniformChartMargins`, `ScaleActivityFinal`, etc.), and dependent rate proofs. `rg` finds 13 Lean files using `κdec_eq` and 31 using it or the pinned `100*(...)` numeral; that is a lower bound on the affected files, not a compilation estimate. It would make those pinned-equality constructions/proofs unnecessary or require replacing them, while leaving the observable collar and fibre losses unresolved. The core rate theorem only needs lower inequalities (`StepTower.lean:45-47,149-153`), so such a parameter is mathematically possible, but it should not be implemented solely for F1.

**B. Sharpen collar and fibre geometry. OPEN.** A smaller `c1` or a linear coefficient 19 improves the numerical sign, but cannot preserve an output rate equal to the input rate. A verified near-isometric or additive hull estimate plus a revised `κ'`/`κout` surplus could work; a fibre estimate with rate cost at most that surplus is separately needed for `renYs`. The work replaces `detCollarHull_sizeJ_le`, `detCollarHull_fibre`, `renDY_renFibreMap_le`, `card_renFibre_le`, their threshold lemmas, and the downstream uses of `hullRate`/`renDetA` (`DetCollarHull.lean:170-245`; `RenormDetFibreMap.lean:322-392`). Those current inequalities would become unnecessary for the sharpened lane, while the determinant may still use them. There is no source-level proof here of the required improved constants, so this is a research task rather than a short repair.

**C. Keep cube-set coefficients in `dExt`. REFUTED as the final output; useful as an intermediate.** The step consumers require `𝒟_{k+1}` domains and tree-size decay, not arbitrary finite cube sets (`FLUCTUATION.md:401-409`; P4 H.4/H.7 cited above). `renFibreMap` maps a polymer `X` meeting the root to `renYs`, and `renRegroup_norm_le` explicitly assumes polymer support and a `sizeJ` bound (`RenormDetFibreMap.lean:58-66,178-203,555-574`). For connected `T`, `sitesOf T` can serve as such an `X`, but a direct bridge must prove locality and adequate rate after the fibre regrouping; the current factor `A` defeats the observable budget. Choosing C would make the observable-specific `Thm31bPolymer.lean` regrouping unnecessary as an output route, while preserving the canonical `obsCoeff` and the determinant collar code. It does not remove F2 by itself.

**D. Prove canonical connected support and then build paper-domain coefficients directly. RECOMMENDED, with a separate fibre-design gate.** This is narrower than proving support for every abstract `ObsMarks`: the structure assumes only root and mark-cube membership of `hker` (`Fluctuation/Thm31b.lean:90-96`). The cluster substrate already proves `Gst=0` at `dOmega=⊤` (`Polymer/MarkedCubesVanish.lean:248-278`), and `gsumCoeff=0` there (`Fluctuation/RootActiveSize.lean:189-203`); `markIntTerm` is an integral of `Gst` (`Fluctuation/Thm31bFirst.lean:38-42`). For the physical kernel, `obsHker` comes from `obsRowLabel` of a `𝒟` domain (`Fluctuation/ObsMarksAssembly.lean:449-462`), and `sitesOf_obsRowLabel=Y` with finite `dOmega` (`Fluctuation/ObsRowLabels.lean:61-85`). The first-order convolution shares the mark cube with the kernel, so `finSet_union` supplies the connected-union step (`Polymer/MarkedCubesVanish.lean:73-87`). **OPEN:** package those facts into `obsCoeff ... T = 0` for `dOmega(sitesOf T)=⊤` for the canonical physical record, keeping the *same* family across datum/source parameters; then construct coefficients on the paper's `hatY(S)` domains with locality, analytic dependence and a proven rate budget. The generic `ObsMarks` interface alone cannot imply kernel connected support. This path retains `Gst`, `markIntTerm`, `gsumCoeff`, `obsCoeff`, `obsHker`, the PE and root-assembly code; it makes the non-polymer remainder and terminal observable `detCollarGrouped` application in `Thm31bPolymer.lean` unnecessary. The determinant collar/fibre code remains useful for its separate high-rate lane. If the observable must also be recast on `renYs`, the existing `renRegroup_norm_le` is a new obstruction, so specify a direct fibre-local domain construction or another additive-rate bound before writing that layer.

**Decision.** Start with D's canonical support theorem and a source-to-`hatY(S)` statement matching Theorem 3.1(a),(b), with an explicit rate ledger before implementation. Keep A and B as conditional alternatives only if the paper-domain construction fails for a precise geometric reason. Do not extend the observable collar-remainder theorem or change the tower numerics first: either would create code that this route does not consume. The author should rule on the intended output interface (`hatY(S)`/paper domains versus collar hulls, and whether the observable enters the `renYs` fibre), because that choice fixes which construction can satisfy the induction; the report gives the concrete trade-off for that ruling.

## T4. Text for Scott

The present observable estimate has the right decay on finite sets of block cubes, but passing those sets through the generic collar hull divides the rate by 60,751 and subtracts `3375 log 2`. At our fixed tower values the result is negative; increasing `κdec` would make it positive only at an enormous value and would still miss the required output rate, which rises with `κdec`. The determinant can afford these losses because its starting rate grows with the freely chosen `κ`; the observable cannot. I recommend proving that the *canonical* observable coefficients vanish on disconnected cube sets, using the existing cluster vanishing lemmas and the kernel's domain labels, and then assembling coefficients directly on the localization domains used in the paper. This keeps the marked expansion and its estimates, and avoids extending an observable collar construction that the induction cannot use. We need your ruling on that domain interface and on whether the observable must also pass through the renormalization fibre; that second map has its own large rate loss.
