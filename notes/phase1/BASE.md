# Round 35 — the physical base step and the zero class

**PROVED (scope).** This is a read-only audit of P3 §§1–2, P3 (4.41)–(4.47), P4 (1.1)–(1.9), (2.51)–(2.63), (3.1)–(3.2), the frozen model and Root-F, and the cited landed Lean. ## Index and initial density

**PROVED (paper index).** P4's input density is $\rho_k$ and its fluctuation integral produces $\rho_{k+1}$; P4 (2.54), (2.63) name the new energy terms before the rescaling (3.1)–(3.2). For the original model this is $\rho_0\xrightarrow{T^{(0)}}\rho_1$. P3 (1.1), (1.4) describe the generic $k\to k+1$ localization of inherited functions and the fluctuation covariance; at $k=0$ the inherited effective-term sum is empty, though new terms still arise.

**PROVED (Lean index).** Fluctuation/StepObjects.lean:StepSetting has $hk1:1\le k$, $G'$ a level-$k+1$ set, and stepG a level-$k$ set. LevelStepSetting.lean:hatStepK retains that binder; hatStepK_betaU gives $\beta L^{k(d-2)}=\beta 3^k$ for $d=L=3$. Thus its $k=1$ case is the paper's $1\to2$ transformation, with levels 1 and 2 and paper-unit multiplier $3\beta$, not the physical $0\to1$ transformation; hatStepK_one_nine identifies it with hatStep only at $k=1,N_1=9$.

**REFUTED (an index relabeling).** Recalling hatStepK's $k=1$ instance “step zero” does not turn its level-1 old action, level-2 solver, or $3\beta$ fluctuation scaling into the unit-lattice hard-spin integral. A genuine $k=0$ step object and bridge are missing from this small-field interface.

**PROVED (landed base records).** LargeField/Base/BaseSmallField.lean:BaseSmallFieldInput packages the $k=0$ remainder $\widetilde K^w_0$, boundary exponent $b_0$, cutoff transfer, locality, analytic/source and symmetry clauses as *inputs*. baseStepData_of_input gives BaseStepData and the Gaussian structure conditional on them; BaseStepData.S2 is a separate one-step bound. Inhabited/BaseStep.lean:baseStepData_nonempty uses the zero remainder and zero boundary exponent, so its kernel is zero and it is no physical first integral.

**PROVED (actual model).** Frozen/Model/Energy.lean defines $H_0(\phi)=\frac12\sum_{x,\mu}|\phi_{x+e_\mu}-\phi_x|^2+\frac\nu2\sum_x|\phi_x-h|^2$ on sphere-valued spins. Frozen/Model/PartitionFunction.lean defines $Z(g)=\int e^{-\beta H_0(\phi)+\langle g,\phi\rangle}d\sigma^{\otimes}$, with the sphere measure from AprioriMeasure. These definitions fix the hard constraint, mass normalization and unscaled source.

**PROVED (zero *terms*, precisely).** Fluctuation/StepIdentityInhabited.lean:zeroClass has $m_0=0$, Tm=zeroTerms and $E_k=0$. SmallField/Def6Inhabited.lean:zeroTerms makes every localized energy and extra observable term zero; EcompAt_zero and FcompAt_zero_eq show $\mathcal E=0$ while the base source $\sum g\cdot\phi$ remains. Thus “zero class” refers to absent **effective corrections**; it does not erase the main action or source.

**PROVED (the class predicate).** SmallField/Def6v2.lean:IsSF2 requires on real physical data $\rho_k=\chi_k\exp[-\beta_k^{\rm fine}A_k^{\nu}+\mathcal E_k-E_k|T_1^{(k)}|+\mathcal F_k]$, plus SFTermClauses and GsSpecStrong; IsSFFamily2 adds every torus, torus independence, renormalization conditions and coefficient bounds. LargeField/Def7v2Class.lean:IsRep2 further requires the history form D1Form2, its SF family D2SF2, pin, operator, budget, boundary, coherence and coefficient clauses. RepFam supplies the data; a RepFam witness alone is not an initial-density identification.

**PROVED (what is definitional or proved).** zeroClass's Tm and Ek are definitionally zero; StepIdentityInhabited proves the corresponding composed-energy equality and the retained linear source identity. SmallField/Def6v2Inhabited.lean:isSF2_rho2 proves IsSF2 for a *constructed* rho2 when its term and domain hypotheses are supplied. zeroClass is a StepClass record, not a density; here it is an abstract stand-in for the absence of old corrections. The StepActivityFinal theorem proves StepActivityBound for zeroClass at its specified positive-scale step, with $g=0$; it neither assumes nor proves that this class is the frozen-model $\rho_0$.

**OPEN (physical base identification).** Prove an exact equality between the frozen hard-spin integrand/measure and a $k=0$ base density and kernel: nearest-neighbour bonds including bonds crossing each first-step region, $\beta/2$ and $\beta\nu/2$, $|\phi|=1$ surface measure and chart Jacobian, $e^{\langle g,\phi\rangle}$, and the vacuum normalization. The current positive-scale expo2 uses a background action, a characteristic $\Xi$ cutoff, and $aIdeal$; it is not definitionally the unrestricted original density. No landed theorem gives the needed equality or an IsRep2 instance for that physical $\rho_0$.

## What the first output must contain

**PROVED (new energy and source in the papers).** Even when old $\mathcal E,\mathcal M$ vanish, P4 (2.51)–(2.54) produces the log determinant and fluctuation interaction, and a source contribution from the fluctuation expectation. P4 (2.63) includes the Gaussian/indicator normalizer in the vacuum constant. Setting old effective terms to zero determines neither the renormalized $\beta_1,a_1,\nu_1$ nor the field normalization $z_1$.

**PROVED (activity versus output density).** Inhabited/ScaleActivityFinal.lean:stepActivityBound_zeroClass_direct_physical_scale produces, for $d=L=3,N=2,k\ge1,3\mid N_1,N_1>5,0<\nu$ and $\nu 3^{2(k+1)}\le1$, an activity prefactor $C(\log\beta_k)^{3p}/\sqrt{\beta_k}$ and an allowed output rate. Its StepActivityBound conclusion is a real $g=0$ bound on labels; it contains no defined $\rho_{k+1}$, output IsSF2/IsRep2 proof, or $k=0$ specialization. Fluctuation/LevelLocalCover.lean:levelLocalCover supplies a genuine local cover at those positive scales.

**PROVED (conditional analytic engines).** Fluctuation/ParamActivityPhysical.lean:DirectTermsFamily and ParamActivitySubspace.lean:DirectTermsFamilyOn transport a supplied analytic local activity over an ambient or relative parameter domain; Inhabited/ParamActivitySubspaceWitness.lean:physical_paramMarkFamily_nonempty is a witness, not a physical first-step expansion. **PROVED (where the generator is first needed).** In P3 (4.41)–(4.45), $X$ is an *old* input localization domain and $Y$ is the resulting output polymer. P3 pp. 78–79 replaces the generating set inside every pre-resummation term by $\mathbb B_{k+1}(Y)$, using equality on the active reads, before proving (4.46)–(4.47) on $\Xi^c_{k+1}(\mathbb B_{k+1}(Y))$. At $k=0$ there are no inherited effective terms, but the **new** $E^{(1)}(y,X)$ and $M^{(1)}(x,X)$ still need generators when $\rho_1$ is formed; P3 gives the localization pattern, not their physical construction.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON BaseMarkedExpansion and BaseLocalReplace (two explicit choices).** Let $\mathcal L_{y,X}^{G}$ denote a *first-step* rooted cut-derivative sum, resummed to output polymer $X$ by the P3 (4.41)–(4.45) pattern, with all background and covariance formulas evaluated on generator $G$; put $F_G=\tfrac12\log\det C^{(0)}_G+S^{(1)}_G-\text{renormalization counterterms}$. Then $E^{L}_1(y,X;\psi_1,h)=\mathcal L_{y,X}^{\mathbb B_1(X)}[F_{\mathbb B_1(X)}](\psi_1,h)$ and $E^{G}_1(y,X;\psi_1,h)=\mathcal L_{y,X}^{\mathbb B_1}[F_{\mathbb B_1}](\psi_1,h)$; the analogous source functional gives $M^L_1,M^G_1$ and carries the base linear source separately. These are exact target formulas conditional on construction of $\mathcal L$, not landed definitions.

**OPEN (equality of the choices).** P3's replacement argument establishes equality of the cut formulas on common admissible physical data when their active reads agree. A Lean BaseLocalReplace theorem must identify these particular first-step formulas and show $E^L_1=E^G_1$ and $M^L_1=M^G_1$ there. The resulting physical densities would then be equal, but the class records have different generator fields and analytic domains; outside the common domain equality of analytic extensions is not automatic. Without that bridge, it is unjustified to say the two Lean class members differ *only* in declared domains.

## The high-mass branch $k_f=0$

**PROVED (reference Hessian).** FINAL.md §1 computes at the whole-torus saddle $h$ the tangent operator $K_\nu=-\Delta+\nu$ and full-space completion $A_0=K_\nu(I-P_h)+P_h$. For $\nu>7/(8L^2)$, it has a volume-independent massive gap and decaying inverse/square root (LF-BASE-KF0.md (B.2)–(B.3)). This is a reference operator, not the Hessian of an arbitrary conditional good region.

**PROVED (exact Root-F target).** Frozen/Roots/LogPartitionMultiscale.lean:logPartition_multiscale asks $\exists\delta>0\;\forall\kappa,c_9>0\;\exists L\ge2,M\ge1,\beta_0\;\forall\beta\ge\beta_0,\nu\in(0,1],|h|=1$ for a witness $k_f$ and terms. Its $k_f=0$ branch has $\sqrt\nu\le L$, $1/2\le L\sqrt\nu$, and only $j=1$ terms; $\nu>7/(8L^2)$ satisfies these inequalities. On all tori with $ML\mid\ell$, it requires $T_1(x,X;g)=0$ unless $x\in X$ and $X$ is a connected $ML$-block polymer; locality in $g|_X$, analyticity for $\|g\|_1<1$, the $c_9L^{-((d-2)/2+\delta)}e^{-\kappa|X|/(ML)^d}$ bound for $\|g\|_1\le1/2$, the exact principal-log identity for $Z(g)/Z(0)$, and nonwinding torus agreement.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON KF0OneScaleInput.** LF-BASE-KF0.md §B.4 gives an assembly: if local analytic $p_\ell(X;g)$ have full source-ball bound $B_\beta e^{-\kappa|X|/(ML)^d}$, exact torus agreement and $Z(g)/Z(0)=\exp(\langle g,h\rangle+\sum_X[p_X(g)-p_X(0)])$, then $T_1(x,X;g)_i=\int_0^1\partial_{g_i(x)}p_X(tg)\,dt$ supplies Root-F when $4\sqrt N B_\beta\le c_9L^{-((d-2)/2+\delta)}$. The frozen branch is therefore a one-scale local log theorem, not an ordinary block-spin step.

**OPEN (physical KF0OneScaleInput).** LF-BASE-KF0.md (B.1) shows the crossing-bond energy at a hole boundary; it is omitted by any product of independent good and bad region weights. The missing proof needs a conditional chart and exact sphere-measure identity, cut-local massive covariance/determinant, crossing-bond and source activities, a normalized heavy-object bound of the form (B.5), and exact torus transport. The raw Peierls gain (B.4) and the reference decay (B.3) do not supply these normalized activities. BaseSmallFieldInput's zero witness does not address this branch.
