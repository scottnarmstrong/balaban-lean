## 1. What the fixed-ledger count does and does not prove

Let $K=k+1$, $G$ be a cover-generating set, and $C$ a finite set of derivative parameters. For a fixed *layer-cell ledger* $\mathcal L$, landed `GraphGmExponent.boundary_gm3_fixed_Y_rate` gives
$$\sum_{C:\,\mathcal L_C=\mathcal L}e^{-\kappa_2|C|}\le1,\qquad
\kappa_2=\kappa_1/2-1,\tag{1.1}$$
under its explicit alignment, ownership and rate binders. Summing (1.1) over all ledgers with the same active site support $S$ multiplies the right side by the number of such ledgers; the theorem has no statement bounding that number. The abstract SN–AG–GRID record of `VMOD-18.md` allows $K$ singleton families with one old source of $\Delta_X=0$, the same $S$, and distinct ledgers, giving
$$\sum_{C:S_C=S}e^{-\kappa_2|C|}\ge K e^{-\kappa_2}.\tag{1.2}$$
The construction and its proof are in `VMOD-19.md` §1. It uses aligned parameter indices, not membership of every index in one actual `Cover.cubeIdx G`: `Multiscale.GeneratingSet.layer_disjoint` excludes those same-point indices in a single cover. Thus (1.2) **PROVES failure of the requested uniform estimate from the stated SN–AG–GRID and landed fixed-ledger premises**; it does not prove failure for every actual P6 cover. The extra actual-cover theorem needed to recover a geometric ledger bound is still **OPEN-P6-ACTUAL-LEDGER-PACKING**. In particular, citing `Cover.card_cubeActive_le` does not prove it: that theorem counts bumps active near one site, whereas $C$ can range over a large source or a completed hole.

There is also a purely analytic obstruction to deriving a uniform absolute coefficient sum from a strict H7 supremum. Let $N\ge1$, $\omega=e^{2\pi i/N}$, and
$$P_N(s,t)=N^{-3/2}\sum_{i,j=0}^{N-1}\omega^{ij}s_it_j.\tag{1.3}$$
For $|s_i|,|t_j|\le1$, Cauchy–Schwarz and the finite Fourier orthogonality identity give
$$|P_N(s,t)|\le N^{-3/2}\sqrt N
 \left(\sum_i\left|\sum_j\omega^{ij}t_j\right|^2\right)^{1/2}
 =N^{-1}\sqrt{N\sum_j|t_j|^2}\le1.\tag{1.4}$$
But the sum of absolute coefficients is $N^2N^{-3/2}=\sqrt N$. Replacing $(s,t)$ by $(s/r,t/r)$ makes the supremum at most one on the polydisc of radius $r>1$, while the degree-two coefficient mass weighted by $e^{2\eta}$ is $e^{2\eta}r^{-2}\sqrt N$. Thus **strict H7 boundedness plus individual Cauchy estimates do not imply a uniform Wiener/ledger sum**. The polynomial is an abstract analytic counterexample, not a proposed P6 action.

The aggregate norm therefore needs a second, operator-relative datum: a **weighted absolute coefficient mass**, not just a pointwise bound for each retained ledger. For an old site label $\ell$ and each physical system $H\in\{H_-,H_\partial\}$, write $a^H_{\ell,C}$ for its AE coefficient and $m^H_{\ell,C}$ for a common-H7 majorant. At the P6 interface require
$$\mathcal W^H_\eta(\ell):=
 \sum_C e^{\eta|C|}m^H_{\ell,C}\le c_Hm_-(\ell),\qquad c_H\ge0.\tag{1.5}$$
The sum is over **all** effective derivative records, hence all ledgers, before sorting by new support; if one derivative set creates several coefficients, each is a separate summand. A state class could store a uniform `StepWiener` field quantifying over every admissible next step; for the one-step theorem it is cheaper and exact to make (1.5) an operator response hypothesis. Calling the old fixed-support norm alone a preserved aggregate norm would be false under (1.2). Equation (1.5) is the required additional content of `OPEN-P6-SITE-MIXED-RESPONSE`; it is not a consequence of its previously stated strict H7 value bound.

The proposed `VMOD-19.md` read-pin also overcharges a completed hole. There $U_\ell=\widehat Y(S_\ell)\cup R_\ell$ contains an entire adjacent hole even when the coefficient reads no field there. For a long hole $F$, take order $|F|$ distinct nearby active supports $S_j$ of bounded modular size. Their completions all contain a fixed $t\in F$, so equal permitted support majorants make $\sum_{\ell:t\in U_\ell}m_\ell$ grow with $|F|$. This is the finite example in `VMOD-19.md` §3. The corrected class keeps **two sets**: $Y_\ell=\widehat Y(S_\ell)$ for geometry and $R_\ell$ for the *actual field/history read footprint*. `read_pin` sums labels with $t\in R_\ell$; a worker must not silently replace $R_\ell$ by $Y_\ell$. H7 may still be tested on a larger legal domain containing $Y_\ell\cup R_\ell$. Any new reads created by the response need the marked estimate (2.4) below.

## 2. Complete finite-sum transfer with explicit constants

Here is a self-contained conditional P6 statement. Fix $d\ge3$, $L\ge3$, $K=k+1$, a finite torus with the divisibility hypotheses of `SizeModEntropy.G2_S`, a post-R input history $A$, and its pre-R step output $A^+$. The input is the revised `D7BdrySite` with fine size (1.6), majorants $m_-(\ell)\ge0$, constants $(B_0,\kappa,K_{\rm read})$, and
$$\sum_{\ell:S_\ell=T}m_-(\ell)\le B_0e^{-\kappa D_A(T)},\qquad
\sum_{\ell:t\in R_\ell}m_-(\ell)\le B_0K_{\rm read}.\tag{2.1}$$
Both sums include all old source and ledger labels. Let $\rho>0$ be the desired output rate and put
$$\mu:=\kappa-2\rho/L\ge aEnt(d,L)+1,\quad
\rho\ge aEnt(d,L)+1,\quad
\eta\ge2\rho dL.\tag{2.2}$$
For **both** $H_-$ and $H_\partial$, `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE` supplies the actual coefficient-zero, source-index and SN–AG–GRID record with the following exact extra conditions: the old geometric source of a label with active support $T$ is $X=\widehat Y_A(T)$ (or an explicitly covered set with the **same** fine modular cost $D_A(T)$); the read collar is handled without enlarging that cost; every nonzero coefficient has $X\cap\widetilde\Omega_{A^+}\ne\varnothing$ and its new support satisfies $X\cap\widetilde\Omega_{A^+}\subset\widetilde S_{\ell,C}$ on the common fine torus; and Theorem 2.1 of `VMOD-18.md` applies with $\Delta_X=D_A(T)$. These conditions imply
$$D_{A^+}(S_{\ell,C})\le D_A(T)/L+dL|C|.\tag{2.3}$$
No source equality or coefficient-zero assertion is claimed to be landed; they are precisely part of `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE`.

`OPEN-P6-SITE-MIXED-RESPONSE` now means **all** of the following, uniformly over histories, scales, the strict common H7 neighborhood, and the closed Cauchy polydisc. It supplies (i) same-$s$ background and fluctuation response for both physical systems on the complete P6 action/read collar, including first and second differences and the $k=k_0$ $\nu$-free linear mass term, bounded by $C_{\rm resp}r_{H,k}e^{-\gamma_w[M([\widehat R_k]-1)-2M_1]}$ as in `VMOD-20.md` (4.1); (ii) local analytic coefficient majorants satisfying the **full Wiener inequality** (1.5), not only individual Cauchy bounds; (iii) an exact new read set $R_{\ell,C}$ and a marked-read inequality for newly read sites,
$$\sum_{\ell,C:\ t\in R_{\ell,C}\setminus R_\ell}m^H_{\ell,C}
 \le B_0c_HK_{{\rm add},H}\quad\text{for every fine site }t;\tag{2.4}$$
and (iv) the pure P6 step activities, with no old boundary label, having fixed-support sum $\le B_{\rm st}e^{-\rho D_{A^+}(S)}$ and read-pin sum $\le B_{\rm st}K_{\rm st}$. These are **OPEN analytic producer assertions** with explicit target norms. The marked-read part cannot be removed merely by (1.5): a site in a large new hole may lie in arbitrarily many new geometric completions. The pure-step constants are outputs of the analytic producer; no numerical value is inferred from P6's pointwise bound.

**Theorem 2.1 (one P6 step, finite-sum half).** Under (2.1)–(2.4), the block/cell compatibility of the post-R history, and the two named open producer assertions, the new majorants have a fixed-support bound at rate $\rho$ and a read-pin bound with
$$\begin{aligned}
C_{\rm cell}&:=L^d c_G K_0(d,L,\mu),\\
C_{\rm sup}&:=C_{\rm cell}\left(1+\frac1{e\rho}\right),\\
B_0^+&:=B_{\rm st}+B_0C_{\rm sup}(c_-+c_\partial),\\
B_0^+K_{\rm read}^+&:=B_{\rm st}K_{\rm st}
 +B_0\sum_{H\in\{H_-,H_\partial\}}c_H(K_{\rm read}+K_{{\rm add},H}).
\end{aligned}\tag{2.5}$$
When $B_0^+=0$ take $K_{\rm read}^+=0$; otherwise the last line defines it. If the two producer assertions also give the revised D7Site locality, reality, symmetry and strict H7 analyticity fields for every coefficient, finite regrouping gives the entire revised `D7BdrySite` at scale $K$. **Status: PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE` AND THE STRENGTHENED `OPEN-P6-SITE-MIXED-RESPONSE`.** This is a one-step theorem with an already given post-R input class; it does not assert `OPEN-R-SITE-NORM`.

*Proof.* Fix new active support $S$ and set $D_+=D_{A^+}(S)$. For every pair $(\ell,C)$ sorting into $S$, (2.3) gives
$$e^{2\rho D_+}m^H_{\ell,C}
 \le e^{(2\rho/L)D_A(T)}e^{2\rho dL|C|}m^H_{\ell,C},
 \qquad T=S_\ell.\tag{2.6}$$
Sum first over **all** $C$ for each $\ell$; (1.5) and $\eta\ge2\rho dL$ make this at most $c_Hm_-(\ell)e^{(2\rho/L)D_A(T)}$. A contributing $X=\widehat Y_A(T)$ has a nonempty active trace in the fine lift $\widetilde S$; post-R nesting (3.2) below makes that trace part of the fine lift $\widetilde T$. Therefore some old aligned cell $t$ lies in $T$ and inside a new cell of $S$, after the stated fine-lift identification. For each new cell there are $L^d$ old cells. Sum (2.1) over old supports containing one of these old cells, then apply landed `G2_S` at rate $\mu$:
$$\sum_{\ell,C:S_{\ell,C}=S}m^H_{\ell,C}
 \le B_0c_H L^d|S|_{\pi_K}K_0(d,L,\mu)e^{-2\rho D_+}.\tag{2.7}$$
Landed `SizeModCore.cellCount_le_of_admissible`, applied to the minimizing site witness, gives $|S|_{\pi_K}\le c_G(1+D_+)$. Since $D_+\ge0$ and $D_+e^{-\rho D_+}\le1/(e\rho)$,
$$(1+D_+)e^{-2\rho D_+}
 \le\left(1+\frac1{e\rho}\right)e^{-\rho D_+}.\tag{2.8}$$
Sum (2.7) over the two H systems and add the pure-step majorant. This is the fixed-support line of (2.5), with **every old label and derivative ledger already summed**. For read-pin, split $t\in R_{\ell,C}$ into $t\in R_\ell$ and $t\in R_{\ell,C}\setminus R_\ell$. By (1.5), the first sum is bounded by $c_H\sum_{\ell:t\in R_\ell}m_-(\ell)\le B_0c_HK_{\rm read}$. The second is (2.4). Add the pure-step read sum and the two H systems to obtain the last line of (2.5). All sums are finite on the torus; no interchange of an unproved infinite series occurs. $\square$

This theorem has **exactly two named open P6 producer inputs**, but (1.5) and (2.4) are substantial new analytic estimates inside the second one. It would be incorrect to use the older, weaker “strict H7 response” wording and claim this conclusion. The actual-cover geometric alternative `OPEN-P6-ACTUAL-LEDGER-PACKING` could replace (1.5) only after it is proved with the required old-source and marked-read bounds. The revised norm is therefore a real strengthening, not a relabeling of the old proof gap.

## 3. The post-R domain lemma and the SN cover

The landed `RegionHistory` stores `rem` and `Z_eq`; it does **not** define a global post-R active-domain accessor. `Def7Hist.OmegaCube H k` is the raw pre-R value `(H.step (k-1)).Omega` for $k\ge1$. `Region/Overwrite.removeOverwrite` and `flowOverwrite` define and prove a single-removal generating-set operation, but `OmegaCube` does not call them. Define the accessor in a new region/history helper, then use it in the new site representation:
```lean
namespace Balaban.LargeField.RegionHistory

def removedSet (H : RegionHistory S n R k0 K) (k : ℕ) :
    Set (Torus S.d (n k)) :=
  ⋃ c ∈ H.rem k, (c : Set (Torus S.d (n k)))

def omegaPost (H : RegionHistory S n R k0 K) (k : ℕ) :
    Set (Torus S.d (n k)) :=
  Def7.OmegaCube H k ∪ H.removedSet k

theorem omegaPost_compl_subset_Z (H : RegionHistory S n R k0 K) (k : ℕ) :
    (H.omegaPost k)ᶜ ⊆ (H.step k).Z

theorem omegaNext_subset_post (H : RegionHistory S n R k0 K)
    {k : ℕ} (hk : k < K) (hL : 0 < S.L)
    {x : Torus S.d (n k)}
    (hx : blockMap S.L (n (k + 1)) x ∈ (H.step k).Omega) :
    x ∈ H.omegaPost k
```
The first theorem is **PROVED** mathematically: at $k=0$, `OmegaCube` is `Set.univ`; at $k=j+1$, `RegionStep.Omega_compl_subset_Z0` and `Z0_subset_Zpre` for `H.step j` put any point outside raw $\Omega_k$ in `H.F.Zpre k`, and `RegionHistory.Z_eq` removes exactly `removedSet`. The second theorem is **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the cited proved** `RegionStep.not_mem_Z_of_mem_Omega` with `H.F.hnn k hk`: that declaration gives $x\notin(H.step k).Z$, contradicting the first theorem if $x$ were outside `omegaPost`. No `Admissible` or ripeness binder is needed. It applies at $k=k_0$ as well. The exact fine-set version follows by `FlowSteps.hnn` and `RegionSeq.cubeMap_succ`; it is the inclusion needed in SN.

For a fine old source $X$, let $(\mathcal K,W,E)$ be an explicit `Fluctuation.ModAdmissible m0 k (fine omegaPost) X` witness (the declaration is in `SizeModDefs.lean`) whose cost is $D_A(T)$. Prune $\mathcal K$ to one old $\pi'_k$ cube for each old active $\pi_k$ cell of $X\cap\Omega_k^{post}$; this preserves coverage and the property that the old tree meets every chosen cube. For each retained cube $q=\operatorname{box}(b,La_k)$ choose
$$Q(q)=\operatorname{box}(\operatorname{cbase}(a_K)b,La_K),\qquad a_K=La_k.\tag{3.1}$$
Since $q$ has side $a_K$ and its base is aligned to $a_k$, it meets at most two $a_K$ cells in each coordinate; for $L\ge2$, $q\subset Q(q)$, including cyclic seams after the standard torus lift. Put $\mathcal B=\{Q(q):q\in\mathcal K_{\rm pruned}\}$. Then
$$X\cap\widetilde\Omega_K^{pre}
 \subset X\cap\widetilde\Omega_k^{post}
 \subset\bigcup_{q\in\mathcal K_{\rm pruned}}q
 \subset\bigcup_{Q\in\mathcal B}Q.\tag{3.2}$$
Every $Q(q)$ contains an old chosen cube and is met by the old tree, giving the SN source-cover clauses. The first inclusion is the post-R lemma; the others are the admissible-cover and box-containment clauses. The record still needs `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE` for the *actual coefficient's* rooted derivative geometry, and it needs the box/cast lemma implemented in Lean. The chosen family in (3.1) need not be an actual `Cover.cubeIdx`; it is a source cover for the modular size theorem.
