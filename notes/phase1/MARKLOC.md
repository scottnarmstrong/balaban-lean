## 1. The source integrand and the cubic factor

**PROVED (P4 printed p. 690, (2.37)–(2.41)).** Put $b=\beta_k$. The Gaussian covariance is $C^{(k)}=(aL^{-2}Q^*Q+\Delta^{(k)})^{-1}$ and $\psi'=b^{-1/2}C^{(k)1/2}\hat\psi$. The fluctuation integral has density
$$d\nu^{\chi}(\hat\psi)\exp\{-\tfrac12\|\hat\psi\|^2-bV^{(k)}(b^{-1/2}C^{(k)1/2}\hat\psi)+\delta E_k+\delta F_k\}.$$
Thus the quadratic action is in the Gaussian measure; the interaction $V^{(k)}$, and separately the old corrections, are the quantities interpolated and localized. The determinant $\frac12\log\det C^{(k)}$ is treated as a separate energy term in P4 (2.54), (2.58)–(2.60).

**PROVED (P4 printed pp. 694–695, (2.51)–(2.57)).** The two-parameter probability measure $\mu_{s,t}$ has exponent $-\frac12\|\hat\psi\|^2-bV^{(k)}(b^{-1/2}C^{(k)1/2}s\hat\psi)+\delta E_k(b^{-1/2}C^{(k)1/2}s\hat\psi)+\delta F_k(b^{-1/2}C^{(k)1/2}t\hat\psi,g)$. The new energy $E_1$ integrates the $s$ derivative of the interaction and $\delta E_k$ against $\mu_{s,0}$; (2.55), (2.57) display $-b^{1/2}D_{\psi'}V^{(k)}[C^{(k)1/2}\hat\psi]$ and $b^{-1/2}D\delta E_k[C^{(k)1/2}\hat\psi]$, respectively. The mark in the current Lean energy channel is the block-masked version of the first expression at the zero class.

**PROVED (P6 printed pp. 23–25, (4.1), (4.5)–(4.9)).** P6 first writes the $-bV$ interaction as the cubic Taylor integral $-b^{-1/2}\sigma\int_0^1\frac{(1-t')^2}{2}D^3V^{(k)}(b^{-1/2}\sigma C^{1/2}t'\hat\psi)[C^{1/2}\hat\psi]^3dt'$, plus the distinct $\delta E,\delta F$ terms. The block function $V^{(k)}(z;\psi',s)$ is bounded by $9L^dK_3^2(C_3c_7)^{-1}|\psi'|^3$ in (4.5). P6 localizes the already cubic function (4.7) by the P3 Proposition 4.3 cut expansion; its **individual** localized coefficients satisfy (4.9), with $b^{-1/2}p_1^3\exp[-2\kappa d_{k+1}(Y)]$ times displayed fixed factors. This is the paper's order of operations. It does not obtain the factor by applying Cauchy to a full, unlocalized hat-field function after the cut expansion.

**PROVED (Lean scope).** `Fluctuation/MarkedCubicTaylor.lean:Phi1C_zeroClass` says $\Phi_1=-\beta V_k$ for `zeroClass`; `Fluctuation/InteractionPoly.lean:deltaForm_line` says `deltaForm` is quadratic on affine lines; `Fluctuation/InteractionBridge.lean:interactionV_eq_energy` identifies $V_k=\sum_b E_b-\frac12\delta$ under its displayed physical-domain hypotheses. `Fluctuation/WoneChartIdentity.lean:vkC_ray_vanish_chart` and `MarkedCubicTaylor.lean:VkC_rayV_vanish_of_baseDomain` give the zero, first and second ray jets of $V_k$ under their chart/domain hypotheses.

## 2. Route A: subtract the jets of each decoupled coefficient

Fix a root block $z$, its mask $Z={\tt blockU}\,z$, a block $b_0$, a localization domain $Y$, and **one pair** $(F_{1,b_0},\mathrm{Sol}_{b_0})$ furnished for that $b_0$ by `Fluctuation/MarkedEvJoint.lean:markedEv_joint_physV`. Choose this pair once per $b_0$ and use it for every clause concerning that block. At fixed physical datum $D$ and hat field $\hat\psi$, write
$$e_{b_0Y}(u,t)={\tt evDec}(F_1,\mathrm{Sol};b_0,Y,Z,u,t;D,\hat\psi),\quad J_{\le2}e(u,t)=\sum_{j=0}^2\frac1{j!}\left.\frac{d^j}{d\lambda^j}e(\lambda u,\lambda t)\right|_{\lambda=0},\quad r_{b_0Y}=e_{b_0Y}-J_{\le2}e_{b_0Y}.$$
The desired rooted mark is $G_{z,Y'}(s,\Phi)=-\beta\,\sum_{(b_0,Y):\,\operatorname{bridge}_zY=Y'}\partial_t r_{b_0Y}(s,0;D,\widehat\psi(\Phi))$, using the existing `markedOutDec` bridge and a clamped hat field $\widehat\psi(\Phi)$.

**PROVED (what the landed decoupled pair supplies).** `MarkedEvJoint.lean:MarkedBlockExpansionDecPair` clauses 1–4 and 7–8 give, for the same pair, a finite sum identity, sharp locality `AgHatDir` on $Y$, the bound $|e_{b_0Y}(u,t)|\le E_*e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}d^{\rm cov}(Y)}$ on every closed bidisc with $(a+b)q<\delta_1$, joint mark analyticity, and independence of $t$ if $Y$ misses the mask. Here $q={\tt markedRad}=\sqrt N\,|cc|\,{\tt covSupConstC}\,c_\psi$ (`Fluctuation/MarkedVBlockDec.lean:markedRad`). `MarkedEvJoint.lean:markedEv_joint_physV` adds joint analyticity in the physical parameters and hat field. At $cc=b^{-1/2}$ and $c_\psi=p_1$, $q\le C p_1/\sqrt b$ by the uniform covariance bound. **The mark variables therefore have a bidisc whose radius is at least a fixed multiple of $\sqrt b/p_1$; $q$ is a multiplier in the domain condition, not the radius of a small mark disc.** The hat field itself remains in its $p_1$ ball.

**PROVED (elementary coefficient estimate, conditional on the cited pair's hypotheses).** If $q>0$, take $R=\delta_1/(4q)$, so $2Rq=\delta_1/2<\delta_1$. Let $U>1$ be the chosen complex neighborhood radius of $[0,1]$ and assume $R\ge2U$. Two-variable Cauchy on $|u|,|t|\le R$ gives coefficients $|a_{mn}|\le E_*e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}d(Y)}R^{-(m+n)}$. Since $r$ contains only terms of total degree at least three,
$$\sup_{|u|\le U}|\partial_t r_{b_0Y}(u,0)|\le E_*e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}d(Y)}\sum_{m\ge2}\frac{U^m}{R^{m+1}}\le\frac{2E_*U^2}{R^3}e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}d(Y)}.$$
This is a **mathematical proof**, not yet a landed Lean theorem. `Fluctuation/InteractionEnergyScaled.lean:beta_mul_energyBdScaled` gives $\beta E_*=bE_{\rm const}$, and `ScaleUniform.lean:covSupConstC_le_covSupMaxC` bounds the covariance constant on the tower. Consequently one term has the explicit bound
$$|\beta|\,|\partial_t r_{b_0Y}(u,0)|\le \frac{128U^2N^{3/2}C_{\sup}^3E_{\rm const}}{\delta_1^3}\frac{p_1^3}{\sqrt b}\,e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}d(Y)},\qquad |u|\le U.$$
The tower uses $p_1>0$, $b>0$, and a positive covariance bound, hence $q>0$; a degenerate $p_1=0$ is treated by the zero hat field. The constant is uniform only after the tower bounds $C_{\sup}\le C_{\max}$ and $E_{\rm const}\le E_{\max}$ are installed. The finite sum and bridge contribute the fixed $J=c_1(d,L)(L^{m_0+m_1})^d n_{\rm Fibre}(d,L)e^{(2\kappa_{\rm dec}-1)c_{\rm Br}(d,L)}$ and lower the rate to $2\kappa_{\rm dec}-1$; no further power of $b$ appears.

**PROVED (locality argument, conditional on the cited pair).** Equality of two $e_{b_0Y}(u,t)$ for all $(u,t)$ whenever the hat fields agree at unit points in $Y$ implies equality of each derivative at $\lambda=0$ and of $r_{b_0Y}$. Likewise independence of $t$ when the mask misses $Y$ passes to $r_{b_0Y}$ and gives $\partial_t r_{b_0Y}(u,0)=0$. Thus jet subtraction preserves sharp hat locality and anchoring. `Fluctuation/SharpAE.lean:AgHatDir` reads the hat field on $Y$ itself (raw data on `thickSet Y`); `W471/Fluctuation/DecMarkBlocks.lean:decMarkG_dep` gives the clamp-to-blocks proof pattern, but is not landed and bounds the **uncorrected** mark.

**PROVED (algebraic identity under the landed physical-domain hypotheses).** Sum the local $e_{b_0Y}$ first using clause 1, then sum $b_0$. `DecMarkTotal.lean:markedFunDec_eq_blockEnergyOne` and `InteractionBridge.lean:interactionV_eq_energy` identify the total near the zero ray as $V_k(v_{u,t})+\frac12\delta(v_{u,t})$, where $v_{u,t}=u\psi'+tM_z\psi'$. The $0,1,2$ ray jets of $V_k$ vanish by `VkC_rayV_vanish_of_baseDomain`; $\delta$ is homogeneous quadratic by the definition in `ExpansionQuad.lean:deltaForm` and `InteractionPoly.lean:deltaForm_line`. Hence
$$\sum_{b_0,Y}J_{\le2}e_{b_0Y}(u,t)=\tfrac12\delta(v_{u,t}),\qquad \sum_{b_0,Y}r_{b_0Y}(u,t)=V_k(v_{u,t})$$
where the physical identity holds. In particular, $\sum\partial_tJ_{\le2}e(s,0)=\frac12\partial_t\delta(v_{s,t})|_0$. The witness-neutral `DecMarkTotal.lean:fderiv_mask_eq_neg_beta_deriv_energy_add_delta` then gives, for real $s\in[0,1]$,

The quadratic jet is **exactly** the correction displayed in `DecMarkTotal.lean:sum_markedOutDec_physical`; no extra counterterm remains. This equality is a proof schema conditional on the explicitly cited chart/domain hypotheses, not a new unconditional Lean theorem. **OPEN-JET-PHYSICAL** is the Lean lemma transporting the complex jet identity through `markedFunDec_eq_blockEnergyOne`, `interactionV_eq_energy`, and the ray-vanishing theorem with one common witness for all $b_0$ and the actual $\hat\psi$.

## 3. Competing routes and decision

**Route A — RECOMMENDED, OPEN only at the leaves just listed.** It uses the landed large mark bidisc and the existing sharp $Y$ locality. Subtract ray jets **before** taking $\partial_t$, but **after** the cut expansion; the polynomial pieces sum to half the quadratic form and cancel the W472 correction. It gives the desired $O(p_1^3/\sqrt b)$ per localized coefficient. The order differs from P6 (4.7)–(4.9), so the jet/sum commutation and termwise Cauchy lemma must be proved, as above. `Inhabited/MarkBudgetAudit.lean:dec_G_ge` lower-bounds the **uncorrected bound constant** $|\beta|F_{\rm dec}(t_0)$ by $c p_1\sqrt b$ for admissible $t_0$; it does not lower-bound the corrected coefficient and is no obstruction to A.

**Route B — VIABLE, MORE WORK.** Apply a second cut expansion directly to P6's cubic tangent expression (4.7), including the nonlocal $C^{1/2}\hat\psi$ map; prove its cut-point identity, support/read set, sharp locality after resummation, analyticity and $e^{-2\kappa d}$ bound, then redo root/fibre and common-label bookkeeping. This follows the paper's literal order and is the fallback if OPEN-JET-PHYSICAL fails for the desired family. `MarkedRootAssembly_zero` and the tower cubic constants give the global cubic identity/bound but do **not** give block locality in $\hat\psi$ (`notes/phase1/MARKDEP.md`); the extra cut expansion cannot be omitted.

**Route C — REFUTED as a cheap change.** `Thm31cde.lean:InterpMarks.dep` and `interpAvg_expansion_of_input` feed the polymer/average expansion with exactly local marks. A quasi-local $G_i$ requires a separate localization expansion and compatible decay, which is substantively route B (or A). Merely deleting or weakening `dep` leaves the consumer's hypothesis unsatisfied and proves no physical coefficient estimate.

## 4. Zero-class (c), (d), (e)

**PROVED / REFUTED distinction.** With $Z_k=\varnothing$ and $\Omega_{k+1}=\Omega''_{k+1}=\mathbb T$ (`FLUCTUATION.md` §3.1's whole-lattice case), (c) has no old boundary term $\mathcal B''^{(k)}$ and is empty; (e) has no inherited $\mathcal R_n^{(k)}$ at the zero class and is empty. **REFUTED:** (d) vanishes *automatically* because $\Omega$ is the whole torus. P5 printed pp. 505–506 and 509–510, (2.18)–(2.19), (2.34)–(2.36), constructs the short-range $\psi^{(k)}(\square,0)$ and $C^{(k)1/2}(\square,0)$ for each cube even if the physical domain is the full torus. Their difference $\delta_0^{(k)}=\delta C^{(k)1/2}\hat\psi+\delta\psi^{(k)}$ can remain nonzero, hence so can the defect (3.5)–(3.9) and the trace defect $\delta D^{(k)}$. P5 Proposition 3.2, printed p. 517, explicitly identifies an interior coefficient with the **whole-torus coefficient**, not with zero. If one instead chooses P4's global full background and covariance throughout, then $\delta_0^{(k)}=\delta D^{(k)}=0$ by definition and (d) is trivial, but that choice requires its own localization interface for the ensuing coefficients. **OPEN** in the present Lean campaign is which of these two representations the intended zero-class `E1MarksC`/determinant assembly fixes; the whole-torus geometry alone does not decide it.

**Confidence.** High on the checked P4/P5/P6 formulas, the large-mark-domain reading of the landed pair, the Cauchy arithmetic, and the nonautomatic status of P5's whole-torus defect. Medium on the total-jet-to-physical Lean bridge because it must align chart hypotheses and a single existential witness.
