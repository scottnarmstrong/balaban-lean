## Counterexample to the literal reading

Take adjacent sites $z\in Z$, $x\in Z^c$, one real component of an $N$-component field, any $d\ge3$, $L\ge3$, $\beta>0$, and tail threshold $\rho=p_1(\beta)>0$. Set $t=\psi(z)$, $|t|>\rho$. Consider the regular massive two-site action
$$
A_t(u)=\frac m2u^2+\frac J2(u-\beta^{-1/2}t)^2,\qquad m,J>0.
$$
Its second derivative is $m+J>0$, and its unique background is
$$
u(t)=\frac J{m+J}\,\beta^{-1/2}t.
$$
For **every** finite analytic radius $R>0$, take
$$
|t|>\max\{\rho,\ R(m+J)\sqrt\beta/J\}.
$$
Then $|u(t)|>R$, while the selected block remains in the tail cell. Bounding $\psi(x)$, or interpolating a covariance only on $Z^c$, changes neither inequality. Positive mass reduces the response coefficient; it does not bound it for unbounded $t$.

The coupling estimate fails even for an **entire** interaction. Fix $E,\lambda,R>0$, a charged support $S_y$ with $n=|S_y|$, and put
$$
F_y(w)=\frac{E e^{-\lambda n}}R\,w.
$$
On $|w|\le R$, $|F_y(w)|\le E e^{-\lambda n}$, the stipulated local analytic bound. At the proposed background,
$$
|F_y(u(t))|=\frac{E e^{-\lambda n}}R\,\frac J{m+J}\,\beta^{-1/2}|t|.
$$
Given any finite proposed $B_0(d,L,N,\beta)$ and $\kappa''$, choose additionally
$$
|t|>\frac{B_0R(m+J)\sqrt\beta}{EJ}\,e^{(\lambda-\kappa'')n}.
$$
Then $|F_y(u(t))|>B_0e^{-\kappa''n}$. This refutes the pointwise hB inference. It is a counterexample to the *logical reading*, not an assertion that the exact O($N$) action has this term. An interaction defined only inside $|w|<R$ also makes the domain failure immediate. A constrained minimizer could remain bounded only after changing the variational problem; its analytic dependence would need proof.

There is a separate algebraic issue. Balaban.Polymer.gasXi contains one coefficient $\beta_y\in\mathbb C$, fixed before the heavy family is chosen. A separately bounded $b_y(Z)$ need not equal it. With one coupling supported on blocks $a,b$, choose $b_y(\{a\})=b_y(\{b\})=0$, $b_y(\{a,b\})=\epsilon\ne0$. Its support meets the chosen core in all three cases, so no one $\beta_y$ represents these values. Enlarging the label family by inclusion-exclusion is possible, but new coefficient decay and entropy would need proof. Absolute estimates do not give the gas identity.

There is a **third, independent mismatch** in the proposed use of the landed final-tail gas. Its theorem final_tail_gas_bound defines each $\tau_z$ as an integral over the cell where exactly the blocks in $S_z$ are large and *all other blocks are small*. Such $\tau_z$ need not multiply into the weight of a cell with two large components. In the two-block product Gaussian example with integrand $F=1$, let $p\in(0,1)$ be the one-block large probability and $q=1-p$. The exact-cell singleton activities are $\tau_{\{a\}}=pq=\tau_{\{b\}}$, while the cell where both are large has weight $p^2$. The gas product is $p^2q^2\ne p^2$. Dividing the whole partition function by the empty-cell weight $q^2$ does not fix this: then the correct singleton activity is $p/q$, whereas the theorem's exact-cell activity is $pq$. This is a counterexample to deriving (A) from norm_setIntegral_blocksSplit_le alone, even with no boundary coupling and no interaction. P7's $T'_{k+1}$ is placed as a component factor in (4.11), rather than as an exact-cell integral conditioned on every other block being small. A component factorization or an inclusion-exclusion/normalization bridge is therefore necessary before applying gas_eq.

## Nearest workable statement and proof

Let $\mathcal B$ be a finite block set, $\mathcal Z$ a family of nonempty heavy supports $S_z\subset\mathcal B$ with cores $C_z\subset S_z$, and $\mathcal Y$ an index of connected charged supports $S_y\subset\mathcal B$. Suppose that, **after integration of each large component**, the exact last-integral algebra, divided by its heavy-family-independent small-field factor, is
$$
\Xi=\sum_{\Gamma\subset\mathcal Z\atop S_z\ {\rm pairwise\ disjoint}}
  \left(\prod_{z\in\Gamma}\tau_z\right)
  \exp\left(\sum_{y:S_y\cap\bigcup_{z\in\Gamma}C_z\ne\varnothing}b_y\right).
\tag{A}
$$
Each $b_y$ is independent of $\Gamma$. Terms wholly inside a component have been absorbed into $\tau_z$ or represented consistently in (A). The heavy weights must also be genuine component weights whose products represent simultaneous components; the exact-cell weights in the counterexample do not qualify. This is exactly the algebraic premise of Balaban.Polymer.gas_eq. P7 (4.11) motivates (A), but does not identify its $T'_{k+1}$ and $B''$ with the repository's final-tail $\tau$ and g.

Suppose, for each $y$, $b_y=\sum_{j=1}^r\epsilon_{jy}T_{jy}$, where $|\epsilon_{jy}|\le1$, with absent types zero. Assume constants $a_j\ge0,\lambda_j\ge0,\sigma_j>0,c_j\ge0$, uniform in volume and heavy family, satisfy
$$
|T_{jy}|\le a_j e^{-\lambda_jD_{jy}},
\qquad D_{jy}\ge\sigma_j|S_y|-c_j.
\tag{B}
$$
The needed analytic-domain proof is part of the first inequality in (B); it does not follow from interpolation restricted to $Z^c$. Set
$$
\kappa''=\min_{1\le j\le r}\lambda_j\sigma_j,\qquad
B_0=\sum_{j=1}^r a_j e^{\lambda_jc_j}.
\tag{C}
$$
For one common tuple $(a,\lambda,\sigma,c)$, these are $B_0=ra e^{\lambda c}$, $\kappa''=\lambda\sigma$. Then **hB follows**, with each inequality explicit:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|b_y|
&\le\sum_j|\epsilon_{jy}|\,|T_{jy}|
\le\sum_j a_j e^{-\lambda_jD_{jy}}\\
&\le\sum_j a_j e^{\lambda_jc_j}e^{-\lambda_j\sigma_j|S_y|}
\le\left(\sum_j a_j e^{\lambda_jc_j}\right)e^{-\kappa''|S_y|}
=B_0e^{-\kappa''|S_y|}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{D}
$$
These use the triangle inequality, $|\epsilon_{jy}|\le1$, (B), and $\lambda_j\sigma_j\ge\kappa''$, $|S_y|\ge0$. This tracks $d,L,N,\beta$ through $a_j,\lambda_j,\sigma_j,c_j$; these must be uniform in torus size. A difference of two separately bounded terms contributes two summands unless exact locality cancels one.

For **old** post-$R$ boundary coefficients, a version of the first half of (B) is landed: Balaban.LargeField.old_term_bound_of_D7BdryPost in LargeField/R/RGasCoreInputs.lean proves $|b_y|\le{\rm fac}\,e^{-{\rm rate}\,{\rm dExt}(S_y)}$, from D7BdryPost.bound and dExt_core_eq_d7Size, given a valid boundary label, the fine support equality, and evaluated fields in H7Domain. Thus it gives hB for the **physical gas with size dExt**. A correction to the first reading of the size issue: Fluctuation.card_le_dExt in Fluctuation/CubeSystem.lean also proves $|S|\le c_1(d,L)(1+{\rm dExt}(S))$ for sets $S$ of the physical active cells, where $c_1(d,L)=[3(2L-1)]^d$. Consequently ${\rm dExt}(S)\ge |S|/c_1(d,L)-1$, so for $\mathrm{rate}\ge0$ the physical old-term bound implies
$$
|b_y|\le {\rm fac}\,e^{\mathrm{rate}}
       e^{-(\mathrm{rate}/c_1(d,L))|S_y|}.
$$
The landed dExt_le_card_sub_one_of_connected gives the complementary upper bound ${\rm dExt}(S_y)\le|S_y|-1$. Neither comparison identifies those physical active cells with the different block-torus supports in Green/FinalTailGas.lean. That transport and the analogous coefficient estimate for newly made last-integral $B''$ are still required.

The heavy estimate is separate. For disjoint blocks of at most $m$ sites, $N$ real components, cutoff $\rho=A_1(\log\beta)^p$, and a **proved** bound $|F_z(\psi)|\le e^b$ on the entire tail cell, landed pdGaussian_tail_blocks_le and norm_setIntegral_blocksSplit_le give
$$
|\tau_z|\le e^b\left(m\,2^{N/2}e^{-\rho^2/4}\right)^{|S_z|}
=e^{b-q|S_z|},\qquad
q=\frac{A_1^2}{4}(\log\beta)^{2p}-\log m-\frac N2\log2.
\tag{E}
$$
Let $P=A_2(\log\beta)^{p_2}$, $H=H_0\log\beta$. If $|S_z|\ge1$, $q-H\ge0$, and $b+P\le q-H$, then
$$
b-q|S_z|+P+H|S_z|
=b+P-(q-H)|S_z|
\le b+P-(q-H)\le0.
$$
Consequently $|\tau_z|\le e^{-P-H|S_z|}$, exactly hτ. This is norm_setIntegral_blocksSplit_le_exp in Green/FinalTailBridge.lean. The landed tail_exponent_hq_hbp supplies these comparisons for all sufficiently large $\beta$ if $p_2<2p$, or $p_2=2p$, $A_2<A_1^2/4$. The tail-cell bound on $F_z$ is indispensable; it is a premise of final_tail_gas_bound, not a consequence of Gaussian decay. For the **component weights** required by (A), (E) is a separate obligation: the landed proof gives (E) for exact-cell weights, which generally fail component factorization. A new bridge must show genuine component weights obey the same bound, with normalization costs included in $b$ or $q$.

Given (A), (D), (E), and the connected-support, entropy and counting assumptions, Balaban.Polymer.gas_eq is an identity and Balaban.Polymer.gas_activity_bound gives
$$
|K(X)|\le3c_1e^{-P}e^{-(\kappa'-2)D(X)}.
$$
For Balaban.Green.final_tail_gas_bound, $c_1=1$, $D(X)=|X|$, $K_0''=(2d)^2$, $K_0'=1$, and the remaining explicit numerical conditions are
$$
\kappa''-\kappa'\ge\log\bigl(m_Y(2d)^2\bigr)+3,\qquad
H\ge B_0e^{B_0}+\kappa'+4+\log\bigl((2d)^2\bigr).
\tag{F}
$$
The second is gas_activity_bound.hh with $c=0,c_1=c_2=1,K_0'=1$. Here $H=H_0\log\beta$. Formula (F) shows exactly where an enlarged $B_0$ is paid. This conditional theorem does not assert the actual $B''$ has been identified with the fixed $b_y$ in (A).

## Retained background and analytic radius

The source-compatible background is $u_Z(\eta)=Q_k\phi_{k,0,Z}(\eta)$, in the notation of P7 (4.4), with $\phi_{k,0,Z}(\eta)$ the unique minimizer of its (4.5) for **retained data** $\eta$ obeying the small-field characteristic condition. The subscript $Z$ here records the geometry of the generating set $B_k(W_k)$; it does not make this a background on the entire tail cell of arbitrary $\psi|_Z$. P7 p. 530 says P2 Proposition 4.1 applies and the solution lies near $h$. Quantitative inclusion in the repository's XiDom remains to be established from the actual variational and class data.

An abstract estimate makes the precise numerical obligation clear. Suppose on $Z^c$, $u-h=G_Z f_Z(u,\eta)$, where $\|G_Z\|_{\infty\to\infty}\le1/m_*$, and on $\|u-h\|_\infty\le R_*$,
$$
\|f_Z(u,\eta)\|_\infty
\le a_*\|u-h\|_\infty+b_*\|\eta-h\|_\infty,\quad
a_*<m_*,\quad \|\eta-h\|_\infty\le\delta.
$$
Every solution in that ball satisfies
$$
(m_*-a_*)\|u-h\|_\infty\le b_*\delta,\qquad
\|u-h\|_\infty\le\frac{b_*\delta}{m_*-a_*}.
\tag{G}
$$
Indeed multiply $\|u-h\|\le m_*^{-1}(a_*\|u-h\|+b_*\delta)$ by $m_*$ and rearrange. If $f_Z$ is $a_*$-Lipschitz in $u$, its fixed-point map contracts by $a_*/m_*<1$. If $b_*\delta/(m_*-a_*)<R_*$, that map preserves the closed ball of this radius, so Banach's theorem proves existence and uniqueness. If a restricted complex covariance has row norm $C_{\rm cut}(d,L,M,\kappa)$, a retained fluctuation bounded by $\rho$ displaces the physical field by at most $C_{\rm cut}\rho/\sqrt\beta$. Thus the **value** clause of a polydisc of radius $R_{\rm val}$ follows if
$$
\frac{b_*\delta}{m_*-a_*}+\frac{C_{\rm cut}\rho}{\sqrt\beta}<R_{\rm val}.
\tag{H}
$$
The gradient, Laplacian, collar-bond and $h$-clauses of XiDom require their own estimates: (H) alone is not XiDom. Landed norm_taggedCfin_mulVec_le supplies the global cut-family row constant
$$
C_{\rm cut}=\frac43\,
\frac{2e^{{\rm pdH}(d)\kappa}\,{\rm ballConst}(d,{\rm pdEps}(d,L)/2)}
{\sqrt{{\rm finalGap}(d,L)}}.
$$
The displayed constant is proved for the global cut family. Using it for a covariance restricted to $Z^c$ additionally requires a row-bound theorem for that restriction. Theorems cutoffInteraction_final_pLog, cutoffInteraction_final_class_polydisc_local and norm_coef_cutoff_class_le_levels_of_large cover analytic cutoff integrals and volume-uniform class coefficients under their explicit chart hypotheses. None supplies (G) for a background driven by unbounded $\psi|_Z$; if $\delta=\|\psi|_Z\|$ has no upper bound, (G) cannot give uniform inclusion.

# Part 2. Completing the corrected route, and its precise physical stopping point

## 2.1. Statement of the corrected finite-volume theorem

The last integral cannot presently be proved from the cited landed declarations alone. The complete theorem below is **conditional on two explicit physical identifications**: localization of the new $B''$ as fixed coefficients, and factorization of the heavy operator after the large variables have been integrated. Neither identification follows from a Gaussian tail estimate. In particular, the conclusion of this section is a complete theorem about the resulting gas, not a claim that blocker 5 or Root-F has been closed.

Fix a finite block torus $B=\mathrm{Torus}(d,m)$, $d\ge3$, a site set $I$, and a partition into nonempty blocks $I_z$ of size at most $m_*=m_*(d,L)\ge1$ (or the singleton block family used by `final_tail_gas_bound`). Let $\gamma=\bigotimes_{x\in I}\gamma_N$ be the standard real $N$-component Gaussian and put
$$
E_z=\{\psi:\exists x\in I_z,\|\psi_x\|>\rho\},\quad
p_z=\gamma(E_z),\quad q_z=1-p_z,\quad
Q=\prod_{z\in B}q_z,\quad \rho=A_1(\log\beta)^p.
\tag{2.1}
$$
Assume $p_z\le t:=m_*2^{N/2}e^{-\rho^2/4}\le1/2$. This hypothesis is automatic for all sufficiently large $\beta$ when $A_1>0,p\ge1$ and $m_*$ is fixed. Use one **singleton heavy label per bad block** $z$, with support and core both $\{z\}$. This is a genuine choice of heavy-object labels: a set of bad blocks is represented by its unique singleton family.
For the landed generic field type $W$, replace $N$ in Gaussian exponents by $n_f=\dim_{\mathbb R}W$; the displayed $N$ is a valid upper bound whenever $n_f\le N$.

Here is the exact non-tail input needed for the physical model. Integrate the variables $\psi|_Z$ first, with $\psi|_{Z^c}$ restricted by the small-field cutoff; let $I(Z)$ denote the resulting contribution. Suppose there are numbers $\tau_z\in\mathbb C$ and a *single* family of coefficients $g_y\in\mathbb C$, charged supports $S_y\subset B$, such that for every $Z\subset B$
$$
I(Z)=Q\left(\prod_{z\in Z}\tau_z\right)
 \exp\left(\sum_{y:S_y\cap Z\ne\varnothing}g_y\right).
\tag{2.2}
$$
The coefficients $g_y$ and the labels $y$ are independent of $Z$; each $S_y$ is nonempty and connected, at most $m_Y$ labels have a given support, and $\tau_z$ is an actual one-block factor in (2.2). A background built from bounded retained data, and the localization construction in §2.3, give a sufficient route to these statements. The equation is an **identification to be proved** for P7's $T'_{k+1}$ and $B''$, not a consequence of their absolute bounds.

Suppose also that $|g_y|\le B_0e^{-\kappa''|S_y|}$, $|\tau_z|\le e^{-P-H}$, where $P=A_2(\log\beta)^{p_2}$ and $H=H_0\log\beta$, and that
$$
\kappa'\ge0,\quad
\kappa''-\kappa'\ge\log\bigl(m_Y(2d)^2\bigr)+3,\quad
H\ge B_0e^{B_0}+\kappa'+4.
\tag{2.3}
$$
Then, writing $\mathcal I=\sum_Z I(Z)$,
$$
\mathcal I=Q\,\mathrm{gasXi}(S,\{z\},\{z\},g,\tau)
=Q\,\Xi(\mathrm{shareBlock},\mathrm{gasK}(S,\{z\},\{z\},g,\tau)),
\qquad
|\mathrm{gasK}(X)|\le3e^{-P}e^{-(\kappa'-2)|X|}.
\tag{2.4}
$$
The first equality is just (2.2) summed over $Z$; the second is `Balaban.Polymer.gas_eq`; the bound is `Balaban.Polymer.gas_activity_bound`. To have decaying activities, take $\kappa'>2$. The constants in (2.3) are exact for singleton heavy labels: $c=0,c_1=c_2=K_0'=K_0''=1$. In particular there is no connected-heavy counting loss $\log((2d)^2)$ in the heavy threshold. The coupling entropy still has the factor $(2d)^2$ from the connected supports of the light labels. This abstract result is the nearest directly usable version of the landed gas theorem.

## 2.2. Bounded retained background and the analytic radius

For each $Z$, retain **only bounded data** $\eta_Z$ from the small-field characteristic domain, and let $u_Z(\eta_Z)=Q_k\phi_{k,0,Z}(\eta_Z)$ be the massive variational background of P7 (4.3)–(4.6), printed p. 530. The variables integrated on $Z$ are absent from $\eta_Z$. Thus the background is constant during the integration over $\psi|_Z$. This ordering matches P7's statement at p. 531, (4.9)–(4.10), that the resulting large-field operator is independent of a spin variable. It does **not** by itself prove the quantitative chart bounds on $u_Z$, because P2 Proposition 4.1 is invoked in P7 without a usable numerical bound in the PDF text layer available here.

The exact analytic premise is as follows. Let $R_{\rm val}=c\,\varepsilon_K$, where $\varepsilon_K=\alpha_0(1+\sum_{j\ge K}j^{-2})L^{-K\alpha}$, $\beta_K=\beta_0L^{K(d-2)}$, and $\rho_K=A_1(\log\beta_K)^p$. On each complement $Z^c$ suppose the constructed datum $D_Z$ satisfies `XiCData` at radius $\varepsilon_0$, all the `hroom`, `hc0`, `hc1`, `hsmall`, `hth`, `hm1`, `hδ₂`, `hstar`, `hK1s`, and `hsm` premises of `Balaban.Green.cutoffInteraction_final_class_polydisc_local`, and the local generating-set identity `hgs`. Suppose the restricted analytic covariance family $C_{Z^c}(s)$ has row bound $B_{C,Z}\le B_C(d,L,M,\kappa)$ throughout the polydisc $|s_e|<e^\kappa$. The required value-radius condition is
$$
\sup_{x\in Z^c}|u_Z(x)-u_{\rm center}(x)|
   +B_C(d,L,M,\kappa)\,\beta_K^{-1/2}\rho_K<R_{\rm val},
\tag{2.5}
$$
with the corresponding gradient, Laplacian, collar-bond and $h$ chart clauses included in `XiCData`. Here `u_center` is the center of the chosen chart; if the analytic function is charted around $u_Z$ itself, its first term is zero.
One sufficient **value** estimate for the first term, with constants uniform in $Z$, is the fixed-point estimate of Part 1 (G): if $u_Z-u_{\rm center}=G_Zf_Z(u_Z,\eta_Z)$, $\|G_Z\|_{\infty\to\infty}\le1/\mu$, $\|f_Z(u,\eta_Z)\|\le a\|u-u_{\rm center}\|+b_*\delta$, $a<\mu$, and $\|\eta_Z-u_{\rm center}\|\le\delta$, then
$$
\|u_Z-u_{\rm center}\|_\infty\le \frac{b_*\delta}{\mu-a};
\quad
\frac{b_*\delta}{\mu-a}+B_C\beta_K^{-1/2}\rho_K<c\varepsilon_K
\tag{2.5a}
$$
is sufficient for (2.5). Indeed multiply the fixed-point norm inequality by $\mu$, move $a\|u_Z-u_{\rm center}\|$ to the left, and divide by $\mu-a>0$. This does not replace the other `XiCData` clauses. P7's quoted variational assertion does not provide the displayed uniform $\mu,a,b_*,\delta$ for the repository's hole geometry; they are the numerical content of the proposed per-hole chart theorem. The landed global bound is
$$
B_C=\frac43\frac{2e^{{\rm pdH}(d)\kappa}\,\mathrm{ballConst}(d,{\rm pdEps}(d,L)/2)}
 {\sqrt{{\rm finalGap}(d,L)}}
\tag{2.6}
$$
from `Balaban.Green.norm_taggedCfin_mulVec_le` in `Green/FinalInteractionCutoff.lean`. For the global family, `Balaban.Green.cutConst_mul_pLog_lt_epsSeq` in `Green/FinalRadiusClass.lean` proves $B_C\beta_K^{-1/2}\rho_K<c\varepsilon_K$ under its explicit size condition $B_CA_1(4p)^p<c\alpha_0\beta_0^{1/4}$, $\alpha\le(d-2)/4$, $p\ge1$. Triangle inequality and (2.5) put the field in the *open* value chart. ## 2.3. Fixed localization of the boundary differences and $hB$

This is the exact algebra required of P7's (4.11), printed p. 532. First consider the transparent case of fixed local analytic terms $f_T$ with $T\subset B$:
$$
F_{Z^c}=\sum_{T:T\cap Z=\varnothing}f_T,
\qquad F_{Z^c}-F_B=-\sum_{T:T\cap Z\ne\varnothing}f_T.
\tag{2.8}
$$
The proof partitions the finite index set into the terms disjoint from $Z$ and those meeting it. Thus each boundary coefficient $-f_T$ is independent of $Z$ and has charged support $T$. If its scalar value after the small-field localization/integration obeys $|f_T|\le a_Te^{-\lambda|T|}$, it satisfies $hB$ with $B_0=\sup_Ta_T$, $\kappa''=\lambda$. Before that integration, (2.8) is a pointwise identity of analytic functions and does not yet supply the complex scalar `g y` expected by `gasXi`. The same identity applies to $R^{(k)}$ and to already localized old boundary terms **only if their coefficients are evaluated on a fixed bounded datum independent of $Z$**.

For the actual hole-dependent $u_Z$, subtraction yields the additional term
$$
F_{Z^c}(u_Z)-F_B(u_\varnothing)
=-\sum_{T:T\cap Z\ne\varnothing}f_T(u_\varnothing)
 +\sum_{T:T\cap Z=\varnothing}\bigl(f_T(u_Z)-f_T(u_\varnothing)\bigr).
\tag{2.9}
$$
The second sum is the missing localization theorem. A sufficient *constructive* hypothesis is a fixed family $r_y$ and charged supports $S_y$ such that this whole second sum equals $\sum_{y:S_y\cap Z\ne\varnothing}r_y$ for every $Z$, with $|r_y|\le a_ye^{-\lambda_yD(S_y)}$; the same is required for the $R$ and inherited-boundary differences. Then the coefficients of $B''$ are the sums of the $-f_T$ and $r_y$ attached to the same label. This hypothesis is stronger than a bound on each $F_{Z^c}$: it is the fixed-label property needed by `gasXi`. P7 p. 526, (3.22)–(3.24), and p. 527, (3.25)–(3.26), describe background differences near boundaries and re-localization, but do not give this final fixed-label identity and constants for the repository's $g$ as a landed theorem. P5 p. 496, (1.38)–(1.39), gives H7 decay for old boundary coefficients; it does not prove the new clause of (2.9).

The need for a *quantitative* localization theorem is logical, not merely formal. On tori with arbitrarily many blocks, fix $\epsilon>0$ and set a bounded hole-dependent difference $D(Z)=\epsilon\,\mathbf1_{Z\ne\varnothing}$. Its coverage representation has the single coefficient $g_B=\epsilon$ charged to the entire block torus $B$. This representation is unique: if $D(Z)=\sum_{S:S\cap Z\ne\varnothing}g_S$, then for every $A\subseteq B$, $\sum_{S\subseteq A}g_S=D(B)-D(B\setminus A)$. The right side is zero for proper $A$ and $\epsilon$ for $A=B$, so induction on $|A|$ gives $g_S=0$ for proper $S$ and $g_B=\epsilon$. For any fixed $B_0,\kappa''>0$, $|g_B|\le B_0e^{-\kappa''|B|}$ fails as $|B|\to\infty$. Thus uniform smallness of each $D(Z)$ and a bounded background cannot by themselves yield $hB$; P3/P6-type locality and decay of the differences must be established.

Here is the numerical conversion when the labels have physical `CubeB` supports. Write $C_1(d,L)=[3(2L-1)]^d$. `Balaban.Fluctuation.card_le_dExt` gives $|S|\le C_1(1+\mathrm{dExt}(S))$, hence $\mathrm{dExt}(S)\ge|S|/C_1-1$. If the coefficient for label $y$ is a sum of at most $J$ pieces, with
$$
|r_{j,y}|\le a_j(d,L,N,\beta)e^{-\lambda_j(d,L,N,\beta)\mathrm{dExt}(S_y)},\qquad \lambda_j\ge0,
\tag{2.10}
$$
then, term by term,
$$
|r_{j,y}|\le a_je^{\lambda_j}e^{-(\lambda_j/C_1)|S_y|},\quad
|g_y|\le\left(\sum_{j=1}^J a_je^{\lambda_j}\right)e^{-\kappa''|S_y|},\quad
\kappa''=\min_j\lambda_j/C_1.
\tag{2.11}
$$
The second inequality is the triangle inequality and monotonicity of the exponential, with $|S_y|\ge0$. Thus $B_0=\sum_ja_je^{\lambda_j}$. Differences of two separately bounded terms cost two entries in that sum. If coefficients are evaluated at $\beta$-dependent scales, (2.3) must be checked with these very $B_0(\beta)$ and $\kappa''(\beta)$; a uniform large-$\beta$ conclusion needs uniform upper and lower bounds respectively. `Balaban.LargeField.old_term_bound_of_D7BdryPost` supplies (2.10) for old terms under its domain, label and support hypotheses. **No cited landed theorem supplies (2.10) for the new second sum of (2.9)**. Also `card_le_dExt` compares physical active cells, while `final_tail_gas_bound` uses the final block torus; identifying these supports requires a further map preserving the charged cardinality or an explicit distortion constant. Without it, (2.11) is not yet the final-torus $hB$.

For a direct last-block estimate one may avoid `dExt`: if localization proves $|r_{j,y}|\le a_je^{-\lambda_j|S_y|}$ on the actual final block torus, then $B_0=\sum_ja_j$ and $\kappa''=\min_j\lambda_j$. This is the most economical target for the missing $B''$ theorem. Its demanded rate is $\kappa''\ge\kappa'+\log(m_Y(2d)^2)+3$ by (2.3); the source's qualitative assertion of exponential decay alone does not establish that numerical inequality.

## 2.4. Integrating a heavy block and paying the normalization cost

The Gaussian tail is landed as `Balaban.Green.pdGaussian_tail_blocks_le` in `Green/FinalTailBlocks.lean`. For any set $Z$ of disjoint blocks it gives
$$
\gamma\!\left(\bigcap_{z\in Z}E_z\right)
\le\prod_{z\in Z}|I_z|\,2^{N/2}e^{-\rho^2/4}
\le t^{|Z|}.
\tag{2.12}
$$
The exact-cell analogue, with all blocks outside $Z$ small, under an integrand bound $|F_Z|\le e^b$, is `Balaban.Green.norm_setIntegral_blocksSplit_le_exp` in `Green/FinalTailBridge.lean`. It proves the $h\tau$ premise used **inside** `final_tail_gas_bound` when the exponent inequalities of `Balaban.Green.tail_exponent_hq_hbp` hold. This is a bound on exact-cell integrals; it does not prove (2.2).

For the singleton corrected gas, a genuine one-block operator has the form
$$
\tau_z=\frac1{q_z}\int_{E_z}A_z(\psi_z;\eta_z)\,d\gamma_z(\psi_z),
\qquad |A_z|\le e^b,
\tag{2.13}
$$
where $b\ge0$, $\eta_z$ is fixed bounded retained data, independent of the selected heavy family, and is held fixed during this integration. In a factorizing reference model $A_z$ depends only on the variables of block $z$. In the physical model the natural retained datum $\eta_Z$ may vary with the whole selected set. To use (2.13), that variation must be transferred to the fixed localized boundary coefficients in (2.9); this is an additional obligation in (2.2). Since $q_z\ge1-t\ge1/2$,
$$
|\tau_z|\le e^b\frac{p_z}{q_z}
 \le2e^bt
 \le\exp\left(b-\frac{\rho^2}{4}+\log(2m_*)+\frac N2\log2\right).
\tag{2.14}
$$
Define $q_*:=\rho^2/4-\log(2m_*)-(N/2)\log2$. If
$$
b+P+H\le q_*,
\tag{2.15}
$$
then (2.14) gives $|\tau_z|\le e^{-P-H}$, the exact `gas_activity_bound.hτ` for singleton support. There is no untracked volume factor: the empty-sector factor is $Q$, and division by $q_z$ is paid by the explicit $\log2$ in $q_*$. The landed `tail_exponent_hq_hbp` proves the same eventual comparison with $m=2m_*$, $W'=N$, provided $p_2<2p$, or $p_2=2p$ and $A_2<A_1^2/4$, for fixed $b,H_0,m_*,N$. The elementary limit also proves $t\le1/2$ at large $\beta$. This is the complete Gaussian part of the heavy-operator proof. For P7's $T'_{k+1}$, the source bound (4.10) at printed p. 531 supplies $e^{-p_2-H_k|Z|}\sup\chi|F|$ for its own operator, but neither (2.13) nor its identification with the final Gaussian block label is landed.

There are two exact obstructions to sending these normalized singleton weights through `final_tail_gas_bound` unchanged. First, that theorem requires $\tau_z$ to be an integral on the cell where **all outside blocks are small**. In the independent $M$-block reference model with $A_z=1$ and identical $p,q$, the cell of one bad block has mass $p q^{M-1}$, whereas (2.13) equals $p/q$. An integrand representing $p/q$ on that cell must have supremum at least $q^{-M}$: indeed $|\int_{\mathrm{cell}}F|\le\|F\|_\infty p q^{M-1}$. Thus its `hF` constant $b\ge M(-\log q)$ diverges with volume whenever $0<q<1$. Second, if every connected bad set is an allowed heavy label, two adjacent bad blocks $a,b$ have both the one-object label $\{a,b\}$ and the disjoint pair of singleton labels. With $g=0$ and normalized weight $r=p/q$, `gasXi` gives $1+2r+2r^2$ for this two-block system, while its true normalized partition function is $(1+r)^2=1+2r+r^2$. `gasA` forbids overlap of heavy supports, **not adjacency**. Singleton labels remove this overcount. Consequently `final_tail_gas_bound` remains a valid abstract activity bound but cannot be the final identification theorem for (2.2) without a revised weight definition or incompatibility rule.
