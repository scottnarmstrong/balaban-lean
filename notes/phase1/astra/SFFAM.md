# W954(iv): does the tower step belong to `IsSFFamily2`?

**Finding (17 October 2026).** There is no landed producer of an `IsSFFamily2` family whose member on the chosen torus is the *successor class produced by the tower*. In fact the tower currently produces a `StepSetting`, not a successor `StepClass`. Its principal per-scale theorems evaluate the step on the supplied `zeroClass`. Running those theorems with every torus size gives per-torus estimates, but neither defines compatible successor terms nor proves the family clauses. The exact missing statements are below. Confidence: high about this Lean inventory and the logical gap; moderate about the proposed mathematical route for the unproved analytic estimates.

## 1. What the tower actually produces

`Balaban.Inhabited.StepTower` (`Inhabited/StepTower.lean:35`) and `StepTowerG` (`StepTowerG.lean:35`) package numerical rates, radii and a nonempty fluctuation core. Their `step` projections (`Inhabited/LevelCoversDef.lean:69`, `Inhabited/StepScaleDataG.lean:37`) are abbreviations for `hatStepK`/`hatStepKG` of type **`StepSetting`**, for any torus parameter `N₁`. `StepScaleData` (`Inhabited/LevelCoversDef.lean:75`) adds a core, a real chart datum, a ray and covers on *one* torus. `StepTowerRenSolG.stepPieces_fromTowerRenSolG` (`Inhabited/StepPiecesRenSolG.lean:570`) and the fixed version (`StepPiecesRenSol.lean:554`) quantify over admissible `N₁` and give step expansion/solution packages, but use `zeroClass ...` as the old class. Their quantifier has divisibility and lower-size restrictions, whereas `IsSFFamily2.torus` quantifies over every `N₂` with `[NeZero N₂]`.

The fixed `StepTower.toG` sets its numerical block index to $m_0=1$ (`Inhabited/StepTower.lean:83`); the supplied `zeroClass` has $m_0=0$. In contrast, the W954 `_X` coefficient theorem assumes $7\le C.m_0$ and $3^{C.m_0}\mid N_1$ (`LocalClassDensityIrrelChainX.lean:57`). These are distinct parameters in the current Lean and cannot be identified by coercing the tower to a class.

The explicit `zeroClass` is `Balaban.Fluctuation.zeroClass` (`Fluctuation/StepIdentityInhabited.lean:34`): $m_0=0$, zero terms and $E_k=0$. It is a chosen **input** to the step, not the class of its output. The nonzero `witClass` (`Inhabited/GeneralClassWitnessStep.lean:107`) demonstrates the distinction: `isSF2_witClass` establishes one-torus `IsSF2` on a bounded-field domain, but `GeneralClassWitness.lean:37–38` explicitly says its $h\cdot h$ energy fails the renormalization condition and is not an `IsSFFamily2` member. Thus neither `StepClass` nor a one-torus `SFTermClauses` witness implies membership. An arbitrary `C : StepClass` in `e0CoeffFamily_class_fromTower_of_irrelBounds_core`, `_of_irrelBounds`, or `_X` is an *input* with explicit clauses and bounds, not a class returned by `T.step` (`Fluctuation/LocalClassDensityIrrelChain.lean:55,395`; `...ChainX.lean:57`).

There are independent examples that the family predicate is inhabitable: `Balaban.SmallField.Def6v2.isSFFamily2_xi_witness` (`SmallField/Def6v2Inhabited.lean:539`) and `isSFFamily2_xi_witness_M` (`Inhabited/V2Wide.lean:246`). Likewise `zeroClassStep_sites_fromTowerG_rho` (`Inhabited/PhysicalZeroJoin.lean:1400`) produces site expansion coefficients for a zero-class step while expressly leaving out the paper's local chart, symmetries and other class clauses; it does not construct `Terms` or an `IsSFFamily2` successor.

There is conditional assembly machinery for an actual successor: `SFTermClauses.inherit_le` (`Fluctuation/LocalClassInherit.lean:182`) restricts old levels to an included domain, and `localClass_newLevel_of_expansion` (`Fluctuation/LocalClassDensityLevel.lean:289`) supplies the new level's term clauses and density identity given its labelled expansion and covariance hypotheses. The latter does not give `IsSFFamily2`, torus independence, coefficient flow, or the four renormalization conditions. Its hypotheses still include the actual expansion identities. In particular `LocalClassInherit.lean` records that the domain inclusion after changing the renormalized mass is open: the landed `Consts.eps` has a tail indexed by $j$, so there is no step-index radius gap with which to absorb the mass perturbation (P4 (4.19)).

### Does repeating the step on every torus give `TorusIndep`?

No. It gives a function of torus size only after the output terms have been *defined consistently*. `TorusIndep.E_eq` demands equality of the energy term for **each** finite shape $X'\subset\mathbb Z^d$ which is non-winding and a polymer on both tori, with both terms evaluated on pulled-back fields, at the corresponding centre. Equal bounds or the same construction recipe do not give equality of analytic functions. The comparison has to include the background solver, square root/covariance, decoupled activity, root and paving maps, and all counterterm subtractions on corresponding local data.

The two-torus work establishes substantial pieces: `vblockTerm_twoTorus` and `vpartTerm_twoTorus` (`Polymer/TwoTorusDirectTerms.lean:216,357`) compare direct energy pieces under box maps; `detActivityBg_twoTorus` (`TwoTorusDet.lean:1043`) handles determinant activities; `e0Coeff_twoTorus_fromTowerG` (`TwoTorusE0Coef.lean:215`) compares a *coefficient* of the zero-class step under many common-box hypotheses. `resum_gapScale_torus_full` (`TwoTorusGapStepFinal.lean:68`) assembles new energy, determinant and observable torus clauses, but **retains `hinh` for inherited terms** (`TwoTorusGapStepFinal.lean:335`), as well as data hypotheses. These are local/compact support or coefficient comparisons, not `TorusIndep.E_eq` for the complete successor `Terms.E` on all non-winding polymers and all pulled-back complex fields. Nor do they yield the `renorm` field. The natural proof is induction: use the old family's `TorusIndep.E_eq` for $j\le k$, establish an exact two-torus identity for the fully renormalized new $j=K$ term, then split the level index. This exact new-term theorem, including the inherited contribution and counterterms, has no landed producer.

There is also a genuine small-torus interface issue. The tower estimates are for sufficiently large divisible tori; the Lean family type requests all positive `N₂`. One needs either a definition/proof on the exceptional tori, or a changed family predicate that indexes admissible tori. A choice of arbitrary terms on small tori will have to preserve the cross-torus `E_eq` and the density/renormalization clauses whenever a non-winding polymer occurs.

## 2. Mathematical implication and missing estimates

Suppose a successor construction has produced common $P,Rg,\beta,a$; for every positive $N_2$, terms $T_{N_2}$, a physical density $\rho_{N_2}$, and $E_{N_2}$; and on the selected torus $P.m_0=C.m_0$, $T_{N_1}=C.Tm$, $E_{N_1}=C.Ek$. Suppose further that the construction proves exactly the five fields of `IsSFFamily2` on `completeXiDom`. **Then membership follows immediately by the structure constructor.** This is the only mathematical proof currently available for the general claim. It is a clause-by-clause implication, not a proof that the tower supplies those fields. For the route through `IrrelTorusDefectJet`, one additionally needs `CRotE` for every torus and `MomNum d L N K j ϱ P` at each inherited $j$. Neither is a field of `IsSFFamily2` or `StepClass`.

The following are the exact producer obligations, with the analytic estimates needed to fill them.

1. **Common parameters and density (`admissible`, `coeff`, `torus.density`).** Produce one admissible $P$ and a single flow $\beta_j,\nu_j,a_j$ satisfying `CoeffBounds d L K P β Rg.ν a` (`SmallField/Def6.lean:475`). Match the step's fine-unit quantities, at least $S.a=aIdeal\,L\,K\,(a_K)$, $S.\nu=Rg.sfBgMass\,K$, and $S.\beta=betaFine\,d\,L\,K\,(\beta_K)$; the first two are `ClassParamMatch.weights_eq` and `.mass_eq` (`Fluctuation/GeneralClassMatch.lean:68`), while `betaFine` is defined in `Def6v2Regime.lean:68`. Match the vacuum constant as well. Prove on every torus the **pointwise** `rho = χ_K exp(expo2 P Rg β a T_{N₂} E_{N₂})` on real realizing data and small sources (`Def6v2.lean:231`), with the physical action mass and complete background. An integrated partition-function identity alone is insufficient. The flow equations and bounds, including the $\beta$ threshold, do not follow from the numerical `StepTower`.

2. **Per-torus terms (`torus.terms`, `torus.gsStrong`).** For inherited $j\le k$, transfer the actual old terms, their analytic domain and bound $\|E_j(y,X)\|\le E_0e^{-\kappa d_j(X)}$ and the source bound (P1 (H.5), P4 (H.4), (H.7)); prove the new complete domain lies in the old domain after the mass/weight update, as explained above. For $j=K$, construct the completed labelled expansion with $\mathcal E_K(y,X)$ and $\mathcal M_K(x,X)$ analytic on the **whole** `completeXiDom` local chart, local on $X$, with `GsSpecStrong`, real rotation covariance, paving covariance, full-centre `eucEloc` and `eucMloc`, and the class bounds. `localClass_newLevel_of_expansion` proves a large part *conditional on* those expansions and covariance. Its new-energy `eucEloc` is a per-centre sum identity, not a termwise symmetry (`Fluctuation/LocalClassDensityLevel.lean:287`; `LocalClassCentre.lean:1116`). A bound only on the fluctuation ray or on the physical real slice does not prove the analytic term clause.

3. **Torus independence (`torusIndep.E_eq`).** Prove the exact non-winding two-torus identity just described for every new-level energy polymer. Finite-range intuition gives the route: if the polymer and all reads (including completion collars) fit into compatible boxes, the two constructions have identical local inputs, so their analytic coefficients agree; then remove the box-position restriction by translation/recentring. The existing two-torus lemmas address pieces, but the completed class term and its subtraction scheme must be identified on the two tori. This is an equality estimate (zero difference), stronger than the exponentially small *finite-to-infinite* winding error used for the cube filing. P4 (H.4), pp. 677–678, is the source clause.

4. **Renormalization (`renorm`).** For every $1\le j\le K$, prove the infinite-volume `RenormCond` of the *same family*: the constant reference value, the $h$ derivative, the constant-spin derivative, and the transverse second-moment limit all vanish. At inherited levels, use the induction hypothesis only after proving the terms and running mass/reparametrization agree with the new family. At $j=K$, choose the three relevant and one marginal counterterms from the exact infinite-volume jets and prove their cancellation; the moment requires a tail bound strong enough for the $|x|^2$ weight, e.g. term derivatives controlled by a polynomial in $1+d_K(X)$ times $E_0e^{-\kappa d_K(X)}$, with a positive decay-rate gap and summable rooted polymers. P1 (H.6)–(H.7), pp. 118–119, suggests finite-torus normalization, but P4 (H.5), pp. 678–679, and the Lean field are thermodynamic. One cannot replace `renorm.moment` by the first three finite-torus identities. Conversely, the thermodynamic conditions leave a finite-torus defect, which must be estimated for the cube label as described in `W970-HANDOFF.md` item 5 and `SmallField/IrrelTorusDefect{Geom,Jet,Tail}.lean`. The needed winding estimate is quantitative: after matching all reference jets of non-winding shapes, the unmatched finite and infinite tails should be bounded by a polynomial-weighted $E_0e^{-\kappa d_j(X)}$ sum beyond the torus-side threshold, hence by $C E_0e^{-\tau_{N_2}}$ for a rate gap. `sizeJ_ge_torus_threshold_of_not_small` in `IrrelTorusDefectGeom.lean` supplies the explicit lower threshold for $d_j(X)$; `norm_sum_winding_le` in `IrrelTorusDefectTail.lean` supplies the numerical summation only after the actual terms obey its pointwise hypothesis. Neither proves the filed defect bound.

5. **Auxiliary `CRotE` and `MomNum`.** `CRotE` (`SmallField/Def6v2.lean:168`) asks for complex rotation invariance of each energy term on the domain, beyond `SFTermClauses.rotE` for real rotations. Analytic continuation would require a path of rotations staying in the term domain; `Def6v2.lean` explicitly records that this path is unproved. The new terms require either such a path theorem or a direct algebraic proof of complex covariance, and the property must be inherited. `MomNum` (`SmallField/Def6InfTail.lean:394`) is numerical: $d,L,\varrho,\kappa,j$ constraints plus $\epsilon_j\le(2K_1+2)\min(c_0,c_1)$. It is potentially derivable once a common $P$ is fixed with a sufficiently small $\alpha_0$, but the tower does not select that $P$.

These clauses cannot be inferred from the exact filing identity `StepClass.sum_classLabels_irrelTorus_eq_Edens` (`SmallField/IrrelTorusSum.lean`). That theorem only uses one-torus `SFTermClauses`, geometric divisibility and a neighbourhood of the reference field. The anisotropic per-label estimate still needs `DataHypAniso` on the pulled-back step chart, the tube/domain bridge, the full torus family and its renormalization conditions. The cube branch additionally needs the actual filed relevant sum $R_j^T(y)$ bounded by $C_R E_0 L^{-(d+\alpha_3)(k-j)}$ (P1 (3.126)–(3.128)); `W970-HANDOFF.md` records that even the available shape/jet/tail results do not yet prove that bound. These are separate from W954(iv), but explain why an `IsSFFamily2` witness alone would not close the per-label chain.

## 2.1 The mathematical output on one torus

Set $K=k+1$ and retain the step torus $\Lambda_K=\mathrm{CTorus}(d,L,N_1,m_1,K)$. Input is a *real* old family of level $k$, together with its transport to this physical torus; call the transported terms $T^{\rm old,K}$ and vacuum constant $E_k$. It must agree with the class `C : StepClass d L N₁ m₁ k N ϱ` used in the step: $C.m₀=P.m₀$, $C.Tm=T^{\rm old,K}$ at every $j\leq k$, and $C.Ek=E_k$. This transport is substantive: `Terms ... k ...` and `Terms ... (k+1) ...` have different carriers. `Fluctuation/GeneralClassMatch.lean:50` and `Inhabited/GeneralClassWitnessStep.lean:49` explicitly call it `OLD-TERMS-LIFT` and say they do not perform it. At fixed physical circumference one expects to compare the old torus with side parameter $LN_1$ to the new torus with parameter $N_1$; an explicit lattice equivalence, the pullback of fields and polymers, and preservation of all class clauses are required. Copying `C.Tm` as an input bypasses this *induction* step.

The level-$K$ energy label is the **complete, renormalized** local contribution of the step. Its raw rooted density $D_E(z;q)$ comprises the fluctuation energy, determinant, old-class contribution through the marked activity, changes from the field substitution, and the local counterterms. P4 pp. 703–705, (4.14)–(4.22), is the source filing. The `e0CoeffFamily_*_fromTower*` results provide coefficients for the fluctuation-plus-determinant part (and the general-class marked activity conditionally); they do **not** by themselves give all of (4.22). The inherited `irrelTorus` pieces are a rearrangement of the old energy used as labels in the expansion: `StepClass.sum_classLabels_irrelTorus_eq_Edens` and `e0CoeffFamily_class_fromTower_of_irrelBounds_X` (`Fluctuation/LocalClassDensityIrrelChainX.lean:57`) use them to estimate the new coefficient family. They are **not added again** to $T'_K.E$; doing that would count the inherited action twice. The step observable $D_M(x;q,g)$ includes the fluctuation observable and the change of the inherited observable under the substituted field (P4 (4.18), (H.7)). Its source coefficients are a separate construction, not `e0` energy coefficients.

Choose the renormalization parameters $\xi=(\xi_1,\xi_2,\xi_3,\ldots)$ from the reference-jet equations of P4 (H.5), §5, and use them in $D_E,D_M$. Let $\mathcal T_E$ be *all* resulting energy labels with local functionals $T_\tau(z,Y;q)$ and let $T_M(x,Y;q,g)$ be the complete observable local functional. The labels must satisfy the root sum identities $D_E(z;q)=\sum_{Y\ni z}\sum_{\tau\in\mathcal T_E}T_\tau(z,Y;q)$ and $D_M(x;q,g)=\sum_{Y\ni x}T_M(x,Y;q,g)$ throughout the complex class chart, not just on `StepSetting.hh₀`'s constant-$h$ fibre. Complete each local term from $Y$ to $\mathbb B_K(Y^+)$ with `cpl` in the enlarged locality chart. If $\mathcal P_K$ is the finite paving symmetry group, define the centre-labelled averages

$$A_E(y,Y;q)=|\mathcal P_K|^{-1}\sum_{s\in\mathcal P_K}\sum_{\tau\in\mathcal T_E}T_\tau(s^{-1}y,s^{-1}Y;\operatorname{cpl}_{s^{-1}Y}(s^{-1}q)),\qquad A_M(x,Y;q,g)=|\mathcal P_K|^{-1}\sum_{s\in\mathcal P_K}T_M(s^{-1}x,s^{-1}Y;\operatorname{cpl}_{s^{-1}Y}(s^{-1}q),s^{-1}g).$$

These are precisely the formulas of the `hAE` and `hAM` arguments of `localClass_newLevel_of_expansion` (`Fluctuation/LocalClassDensityLevel.lean:289`, around lines 365–445). Centre labels are essential: a uniform division of a rooted coefficient over $Y$ does not give `eucEloc` for `fullJSymGroup` (`notes/phase1/WIDEGEN.md`, W868 D1(a)/D5(a)). Define the new `Terms` record by the **exact level split**

$$T'.E_j(y,Y;q)=\begin{cases}{\bf1}_{y\in Y}A_E(y,Y;q),&j=K,\\ T^{\rm old,K}.E_j(y,Y;q),&j\ne K,\end{cases}\quad T'.M_j(x,Y;q,g)=\begin{cases}A_M(x,Y;q,g),&j=K,\\T^{\rm old,K}.M_j(x,Y;q,g),&j\ne K,\end{cases}$$

and $T'.gs_j(Y)=\mathbb B_K(Y^+)$ at $j=K$, $T^{\rm old,K}.gs_j(Y)$ otherwise. The new-level generator is the `localB` specified by `hgs` in `localClass_newLevel_of_expansion`; $Y^+$ is the `Yp` of that theorem. The `j\ne K` branch merely totalizes Lean's `Terms.E/M/gs : ℕ → …`; the class uses $1\le j\le K$. The new `StepClass` at step index $k$ is `⟨P.m₀, T', E_K⟩` (its type is `StepClass d L N₁ m₁ k N ϱ`, because that structure's `Tm` already has torus index $K$; see `Fluctuation/StepObjects.lean:254`). For use as the *next step's* input, the same family must then be transported from torus index $K$ to index $K+1$. No automatic coercion supplies this.

There are two vacuum quantities that must not be conflated. Let $e_0^{\rm vac}$ be the total vacuum value of the **raw new local density before the reference-value subtraction** fixed by the renormalization procedure. Its subtraction is part of the finalized $A_E$, whose local reference value must vanish for `RenormCond.val`. The successor vacuum constant is uniquely forced by the density identity:

$$E_K\,S.nBlocks=S.Eppp(C)\,S.nU-e_0^{\rm vac},\qquad E_K=\frac{S.Eppp(C)\,S.nU-e_0^{\rm vac}}{S.nBlocks}.$$

Under $S.nBlocks>0$, `StepSetting.vacuumEnergy_succ_of_absorb` (`Fluctuation/VacuumEnergy.lean:46`) and `StepSetting.vacuumEnergy_succ_eq_increment` (`Fluctuation/VacuumStep.lean:88`) give $E_K=L^dE_k+g_k$ with the three logarithmic normalization terms and $-e_0^{\rm vac}/S.nBlocks$. The $L^d$ is forced by $S.nU=L^dS.nBlocks$. These theorems prove the algebra **given** the absorption identity; they do not construct $e_0^{\rm vac}$, the reference subtraction, or a bound on $g_k$. In particular, evaluating the already-centred $T'.E$ at the reference point to define $e_0^{\rm vac}$ would give zero and lose the absorbed constant.

The concrete Lean definition proposed for the structural part is the following. Here `F := Fld d L N₁ m₁ K N`, `Λ := CTorus d L N₁ m₁ K`; `AE`, `AM`, and `Gnew` have the types displayed, and their *defining equalities* are the `hAE`, `hAM`, `hgs` equations of `localClass_newLevel_of_expansion`, not free choice.

```lean
-- PROPOSAL: not landed; K is k + 1.
def successorTerms (Old : Terms d L N₁ m₁ K N ϱ)
    (AE : Λ → Set Λ → (F × F) → ℂ)
    (AM : Λ → Set Λ → ((F × F) × F) → Fin N → ℂ)
    (Gnew : Set Λ → GeneratingSet d L (N₁ * L ^ m₁) K ϱ) :
    Terms d L N₁ m₁ K N ϱ where
  E := fun j y Y Ψ h =>
    if j = K then (if y ∈ Y then AE y Y (Ψ, h) else 0)
    else Old.E j y Y Ψ h
  M := fun j x Y Ψ h g =>
    if j = K then AM x Y ((Ψ, h), g) else Old.M j x Y Ψ h g
  gs := fun j Y => if j = K then Gnew Y else Old.gs j Y

def successorClass (Old : StepClass d L N₁ m₁ k N ϱ)
    (AE : Λ → Set Λ → (F × F) → ℂ)
    (AM : Λ → Set Λ → ((F × F) × F) → Fin N → ℂ)
    (Gnew : Set Λ → GeneratingSet d L (N₁ * L ^ m₁) K ϱ)
    (S : StepSetting d L N₁ m₁ k N ϱ) (eVac : ℝ) :
    StepClass d L N₁ m₁ k N ϱ :=
  ⟨Old.m₀, successorTerms Old.Tm AE AM Gnew,
    (S.Eppp Old * S.nU - eVac) / S.nBlocks⟩
```

For the family, *each* $N_2$ has its own transported old terms, step setting, label functions, centre averages, $e_0^{\rm vac}(N_2)$, and density $\rho'_d(N_2)$. Define `TmFam' N₂ := successorTerms (OldStepFam N₂) (AE N₂) (AM N₂) (Gnew N₂)` and `EkFam' N₂ := (Sfam N₂).Eppp (OldClassFam N₂) * (Sfam N₂).nU / (Sfam N₂).nBlocks - eVac N₂ / (Sfam N₂).nBlocks`. `ρd' N₂` must be the actual small-field RG transform of `ρd N₂` with the chosen counterterms and observable source, on every physical datum in `IsSF2.density`. There is currently no all-datum, all-torus Lean `RGStep` operator of this type. A family of arbitrary functions with the correct type would not prove the induction. At the distinguished torus, the construction must prove `TmFam' N₁ = (successorClass C …).Tm` and `EkFam' N₁ = (successorClass C …).Ek`; uniform choices of counterterms and labels are needed to make this a *single family*, rather than independent per-torus renormalizations.

## 2.3 Exact induction target and open premises

The mathematical theorem is: for a family $(\rho_k,T_k,E_k)$ in `IsSFFamily2` at scale $k$ with `CRotE` and the required `MomNum`, for the specified small-field RG map and the **common** renormalization solution $\xi$, its full output family $(\rho_K,T_K,E_K)$ defined in §2.1 lies in `IsSFFamily2` at $K$, has `CRotE` on every torus, and has the required `MomNum` at all $j\le K$. At the distinguished torus, its `StepClass` is `successorClass C …`. The output density identity is part of the theorem: $\rho_K(N_2)=\mathcal R_{k\to K}^{\rm sf}(\rho_k(LN_2);\xi)$ on every physical datum, with the appropriate old/new torus equivalence and source. Without that identity, the existential class theorem could be satisfied by an unrelated family.

Here is the *exact binder/result shape* in the types of the existing class. The capitalized names in the premise line are **proposed packages of the concrete conditions listed below**, not declarations already present; `RGStep` is the proposed full-datum family transformation, and `oldLift` the proposed geometric transport. Thus the display is a specification for a future Lean theorem, not code claimed to elaborate today.

The intended package meanings are narrow. `StepNumerics` supplies the tower's positive mass, large $\beta$, the side conditions of `StepScaleData` where that record applies, and matches $S.a,S.ν,S.β$ to the old/new regime and coefficient sequences. Its extension to small or nondivisible tori is a separate obligation. `OldTermsLift` supplies the explicit physical-torus equivalence, transported old terms/density, the exact equalities at $j\le k$, preserved old clauses, `RenormCond` data and nonwinding coherence. `SuccessorOutput` contains the *unique selected* counterterm solution, the actual `StepSetting` family, raw $D_E,D_M$, `eVac`, all local labels, their prescribed paving averages, and the **pointwise** identity defining `RGStep` on every datum. `SuccessorEstimates` consists of: (i) the per-label P1 (3.128) and P4 Proposition 4.1/4.2 bounds, chart inclusion and summability needed by `localClass_newLevel_of_expansion`; (ii) `hDz`, `hDM`, `hDzfull`, `hDMfull` on the whole class chart; (iii) the old-level birth-domain transport or a new current-domain inclusion; (iv) exact nonwinding equality of the new energy terms; (v) four new renormalization jets, including the second-moment limit; (vi) uniform coefficient-flow and vacuum bounds; (vii) `CRotE` complex rotation covariance. These are **open mathematical/Lean obligations**, not fields to assume in a theorem advertised as the completed scale induction. A useful near-term *conditional assembly lemma* may take them as hypotheses and build the structure, but it would only expose the blocker, not resolve it.

Two architectural checks must precede approval. First, `completeXiDom` fixes the mass at the *current* scale for all old levels (`SmallField/Def6v2Bridge.lean:171`), whereas W868 chose birth-frozen domains; the displayed conclusion is consequently stronger than the currently justified inheritance argument. Second, `localClass_newLevel_of_expansion` is currently specialized to `minSep` and divisible tori, while `IsSFFamily2` quantifies over every `[NeZero N₂]`. Both require actual bridge theorems or an approved class-carrier change. A theorem that simply assumes `IsSFFamily2` of the successor, or chooses unrelated terms/densities to fill these gaps, would not be the P4 Theorem 1 mapping.
