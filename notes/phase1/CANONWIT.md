## T2. Use named objects; keep the old statements

**REFUTED (companion alone).** A theorem `∃ w, P(w) ∧ w = formula` is useful only if consumers use that witness or the formula. An unrelated `obtain ⟨w,…⟩` from the old `∃ w,P(w)` can still choose another term. Repeating the full existential binders and adding one more conjunct recreates the `_ksupp`, `_anchor`, `_rho`, `_value`, `_local`, `_full` maintenance cost.

**OPEN (preferred mechanism).** Define `physTt`, `physEv`, `physAct`, `physDz` and the assembled coefficient as closed expressions at the *lowest* layer that knows each formula. Prove compact property theorems, for example `physTt_spec` and `physEv_spec`, and let each landed existential statement become `⟨phys…, phys…_spec⟩` without changing its statement. Consumers that need bounds can keep `obtain`; consumers proving transport, symmetry or exact locality use the named terms. This reuses the existing proofs by moving their bodies to the property theorems, then deriving the old theorem as a short corollary. A local record packaging the parameters can shorten binders without changing any landed declaration.
