# Anchored graph counting: counterexamples to the proposed three-bound package

**12 October 2026.** This is a counterexample ruling on `VMOD-7.md` §4, not a proof of `OPEN-ANCHORED-GRAPH-GM2/GM3`. The proposed conclusions (4.1)–(4.3) do not follow from the stated incidence, completion, and chosen-family interfaces. In particular, no finite threshold on $\kappa _2$ makes (4.3) true for the charge families in the finite model below. I distinguish this interface-level counterexample from a counterexample to every possible full P6 region history: realization of the displayed model by a particular history is an additional geometric assertion, not established by the landed files. Consequently the P6-specific theorem remains open.

## 1. Source and landed statements used

P3, p. 77, (4.41)–(4.43), differentiates in the *large cubes of the complementary family* and discards components of $Y_1$ containing no part of $X_1$. On p. 78, (4.44) assigns $Y=X_1\cup\bigcup_iY^{(i)}$, where each added component has a cube touching $X_1$ along a face. On pp. 79–80, the graph joining those cubes is a **physical** tree inside $Y$: its source part $\Gamma _0$ lies in $X\subset\Omega_{k+1}$, so its new weighted cost is $L^{-1}$ times the old cost. This is the explicit hypothesis used at (4.48), $\widetilde d_{k+1}(Y)\le L^{-1}L^j\eta,d_j(X)+d(2L+1)^d|\mathcal C|$. The count (4.50) on p. 81 conditions on the **whole output domain $Y$**, whose new cubes witness the derivatives. It does not condition on the charged support of a hole polymer.

P6, p. 34, the paragraph from (4.32) to (4.34), localizes a boundary term first in $X\cap\Omega_{k+1}$, explicitly allows that intersection and $Y_1$ to have several components, assigns $Y\in\mathcal D_{k+1}(\mathrm{mod}\,\Omega_{k+1}^{c})$ as in P3 (4.44), and says that P3 (4.48)–(4.50) apply with modular sizes. This is a statement about that resummation, not a statement that an arbitrary reachable component of the later `incGraph` has P3's added-cube witnesses. The scanned page images of P3 pp. 77–81 and P6 p. 34 were checked for these claims.

The landed `Decoupled2FpGeometry.lean` definitions `incSites`, `EligC`, and `incRel`, and `Decoupled2FpCount.lean` definitions `seedClosure`, `seedSites`, and `seedParams`, provide reachability but no new-cube witness for a reached parameter. `TreeLengthCov.lean` `ChosenFamily` and `treeLenCov` count all complete chosen cubes in *positive* layers of their fine-site domain. `Polymer/Hole.lean` `HolePolymer`, `holeSupp`, and `steiner` count block polymers after their construction. `PolymerCount.lean` `gm3` requires parameter `Pset`s disjoint from its base $X_1$ and conditions on its `Ysites`; it is not (4.3).

## 2. Exact decorated output and the fixed-support obstruction

The minimal honest graph output is
$$
  \mathfrak O_{\mathcal C}=(Y_{\mathcal C},A_{\mathcal C},
     \{(P,t_P):P\in A_{\mathcal C}\},P_{\mathcal C},
     \widehat Y_{\mathcal C},\mathcal K_{\mathcal C},\Gamma_{\mathcal C}),
$$
where $Y_{\mathcal C}=\operatorname{seedSites}(\mathcal C)$, $A_{\mathcal C}=\operatorname{seedParams}(\mathcal C)$, $t_P$ records an attachment block or an explicit inside-hole label, $P_{\mathcal C}$ is hole-closed, and the last three entries give a fine-site realization, chosen family, and physical tree. A derivative family may be retained only if $\mathcal C=A_{\mathcal C}$; alternatively retain the full derivative index in the output. This decoration is injective in $\mathcal C$ when the first rule holds. **It does not make the map $\mathcal C\mapsto\operatorname{holeSupp}P_{\mathcal C}$ injective.** Formula (4.3) forgets all entries except that last support.

Here is a finite counterexample to (4.3) under the exact abstract graph and hole-polymer interfaces. Let $H_B$ be the nearest-neighbour graph on $(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^d$, with $d\ge1$ and $n\ge6$. Fix a block $b$, and put $S_0=B\setminus\{b\}$. The set $S_0$ is connected. Let $X_1=\Omega_1(H)$ be the fine sites of $b$, let $S$ be one of its sites, and let the cover contain a live centre $z$ just across its boundary with $\operatorname{footprint}(z)\cap X_1\ne\varnothing$. Set
$$T=\mathcal Q(z)\cap\operatorname{parCubes}(X_1),\qquad T\ne\varnothing,$$
and arrange that $T$ is the set of missing parameter cubes required for $z$ to be eligible; all these parameter cubes lie in $S_0$. Such data can be specified as a finite instance of the `incRel` incidence rules (one may take just one centre and one parameter); the stronger assertion that a specific P6 history supplies them is not used here. For $\mathcal C_0=\varnothing$ and $\mathcal C_1=T$, every site of $X_1$ is an active graph site and its nearest-neighbour graph is connected. Thus
$$Y_{\mathcal C_0}=Y_{\mathcal C_1}=X_1.$$
For $\mathcal C_1$, $z$ is eligible and reached from $S$ through its footprint; all $P\in T$ are reached through $z$. Hence $A_{\mathcal C_0}=\varnothing$ and $A_{\mathcal C_1}=T=\mathcal C_1$: **both families pass the effective-charge rule**. Any `HolePolymer H_B S₀` has nonempty charged support contained in $B\setminus S_0=\{b\}$, so, regardless of its hole completion or of the recorded attachments,
$$Q_{\mathcal C_0}=Q_{\mathcal C_1}=\{b\}.$$
Therefore the left side of (4.3) for $Q=\{b\}$ is at least
$$1+e^{-\kappa _2|T|}>1\qquad\text{for every finite }\kappa _2.$$
Neither a larger entropy constant nor an explicit inside-hole label repairs **(4.3) with this conditioning**. The example also shows why merely replacing `seedSites` by the decorated output cannot be followed by the undecorated `pe_hole` fiber estimate. To exclude this example for P6, one needs a proved history-specific assertion that there cannot be two retained effective families over a saturated exterior support, or one must change the sum being estimated.

The one-block exterior is only a convenient finite witness. More generally, if $E=B\setminus S_0$ is connected, $X_1=\Omega_1(H)$ contains the fine realization of **every** block of $E$, a nonempty seed reaches $X_1$, and the completion's block set contains every block met by its site projection, then $Q_{\mathcal C}=E$ for every retained $\mathcal C$: `holeSupp` cannot contain anything beyond $E$ and must contain every block of $E$. A single nonempty effective charge family alongside the empty family again gives $1+e^{-\kappa_2|\mathcal C|}>1$. This version does not require a narrow exterior region. A full-torus source $X$ is a natural test for this saturation in P6, since it covers the exterior region; proving that its actual $X_1$, operator region, and halo supply a live hole parameter is the history-specific step still missing.

## 3. A charge-free collar obstruction to (4.1)

There is a separate zero-charge problem even without a hole. Take a sufficiently large periodic grid, full top layer $F^\sharp.\Lambda_K$, and $K=j+1$. Let $X$ be one complete chosen $\pi'_j$ cube, so `treeLenCovOne` has the singleton witness $W_0$ and $\Delta=0$. Position $X$ against a face of its containing $\pi'_K$ cube $B_0$. If the interpolation core $X_0$ contains a site on that face, the positive-radius $X_0$-to-$X_1^c$ margin discussed in `VMOD-2.md` §1.3 requires $X_1$ to cross the face. When $X_1$ is a union of complete $\pi'_K$ cubes, it therefore includes the adjacent cube $B_1$. Take the legitimate choice $X_1=B_0\cup B_1$ and a nonempty seed in it.

For $\mathcal C=\varnothing$, `incSites` contains $X_1$, and its nearest-neighbour edges reach both cubes. Any completed $\widehat Y_{\varnothing}$ containing `seedSites` contains $B_0\cup B_1$. Since the top layer is full, every `ChosenFamily F♯ m₀ \widehat Y 𝒦` must include both distinct aligned $\pi'_K$ cubes. A connected witness meeting them has a nonempty physical edge set, hence positive `bondCost` (at least $1/(ML^K)$). Formula (4.1), however, gives
$$0<\operatorname{cost}_{F^\sharp}(\Gamma_{\varnothing})
   \le L^{-1}\Delta+C_{\rm graph}|\varnothing|=0.$$
No choice of $C_{\rm graph}$ resolves this. This is a counterexample to (4.1) **for a collar enlargement admitted without a term on its right side**. It is not a claim that every P6 choice of $X_0,X_1$ has this form. A theorem using such a margin must either pay its added chosen cubes through a source-size estimate, add an explicit collar cost $R_{\rm collar}$ to the right side, or prove that its actual choice of $X_1$ has no uncharged addition. The phrase “including its independently paid collar” in `VMOD-7.md` §4 does not put that payment into (4.1).

## 4. Why the Steiner bridge needs an exterior witness hypothesis

As stated, `ChosenFamily` only covers $\widehat Y\cap F^\sharp.\Lambda_n$, $n\ge1$. It says nothing about blocks of $\widehat Y$ outside all those layers. This yields a separate counterexample to the inference (4.2) from the landed definition alone. Let $S_0=\varnothing$, let $P$ be a connected path of $r$ block cells disjoint from $F^\sharp.\Omega_1$, and let $\widehat Y$ be its fine-site realization. The empty chosen family satisfies `ChosenFamily`; a singleton vertex of $\widehat Y$ with no edges is a `ConnStruct` meeting it. Hence $D=\operatorname{treeLenCov}(\widehat Y)=0$, while $\operatorname{steiner}_{H_B}(\operatorname{holeSupp}P)=r$. For any uniform $a_{\rm St}>0,b_{\rm St}<\infty$, choose $r>b_{\rm St}/a_{\rm St}$. Then (4.2) is false. This is an interface counterexample, not a claim that P6 places charged exterior cells outside $F^\sharp.\Omega_1$. That needed containment and a bound on the block distance from each charged cell to a *compulsory* chosen cube must be explicit hypotheses of a P6 theorem. A lower bound on one selected $\Gamma_{\mathcal C}$ does not fix the issue, since $D$ is an infimum over all chosen families and trees.

## 5. Corrected target and proof obligations

The counterexamples rule out the simultaneous package (4.1)–(4.3) at the displayed abstraction, so there is **no justified numerical $C_{\rm graph}(d,L)$, $a_{\rm St}$, $b_{\rm St}$, or finite $\kappa _2$ threshold for that package**. A possible P6-specific statement needs the following changes, each with its own proof:

1. Define a **modular** output size whose witness tree pays traversed bonds through each completed hole, but whose chosen cover does not require visiting every cube of a hole component. Establish a source-tree transfer in the actual $F^\sharp$ layers. Include $R_{\rm collar}$ in the size inequality unless it has already been bounded by $L^{-1}\Delta$:
   $$D_{\mathcal C}\le L^{-1}\Delta+R_{\rm collar}+C_{\rm attach}|\mathcal C|.$$
   The local ambient star estimate $d3^d$ in `VMOD-7.md` (2.3) is only part of $C_{\rm attach}$; paths, hole closure, and complete chosen-cube connectors still need bounds. This line is a target, **not** a proved estimate.
2. Charge each effective parameter to a **new exterior witness block** or keep its label in the activity index. If a parameter lies in a hole, prove that the assignment to exterior witnesses has bounded multiplicity and that witnesses were not already in the base support. A fixed-$Q$ estimate must additionally rule out a zero-charge family and a positive-charge family with the same $Q$. An alternative is to retain the full decoration in the fixed-output index and then prove a separate bound for the sum over decorations before invoking `pe_hole`. Algebraically, if $N_Q$ parameters may be invisible over $Q$, the best unrestricted subset sum is $(1+e^{-\kappa_2})^{N_Q}$, not $1$; a Peierls bound requires $N_Q\le c\operatorname{steiner}(Q)$ and enough spare decay to absorb this factor.
3. For every charged exterior block, prove top-layer coverage or a bounded-distance compulsory cover witness, **uniformly for every admissible chosen family**. Only then can the infimum $D$ control $\operatorname{steiner}(Q)$. The desired inequality should be proved first for an arbitrary admissible tree and chosen family, with constants independent of those choices, and then passed to the infimum. The value of $a_{\rm St}$ depends on the proved witness distance; assigning it now would omit a geometric hypothesis.
4. Reproduce P3's organization for the P6 boundary term: index derivatives by cubes of the complement of $X_1$, form the connected $Y_1$ components attached to the source, assign the modular $Y$ by P3 (4.44), and prove the modular counterparts of P3 (4.48) and (4.50) **for that index**. P6 p. 34 asserts those counterparts but the landed ordinary `gm2_tree`/`gm3` do not prove them for a disconnected modular source. P3's explicit threshold on p. 81 is for its own fixed-$Y$ count and cannot be transferred to fixed `holeSupp` without a proved injection preserving its witnesses.

The exact Lean dependency order is: (i) `effective_param_of_derivative` and `seedParams_eq_charges` (or an output record retaining the derivative set); (ii) `collar_cost_or_source_charge`; (iii) `modular_tree_source_transfer`; (iv) `param_exterior_witness` with bounded multiplicity and `new_witness_not_base`; (v) `hole_completion_tree` and `chosen_modular_family`; (vi) `graph_gm2_modular` with the explicit connector constant obtained in (iii)–(v); (vii) `steiner_le_cost_all_witnesses` and its infimum corollary; (viii) `graph_fibre_fixed_modular_output`, proved using the witnesses of (iv), followed by the correct decorated-activity resummation; (ix) `pe_hole`/`gm4` after their hypotheses have actually been obtained. None of (ii)–(viii) is a theorem of the cited landed modules.

The finite models in §§2–4 decide the requested **unqualified** graph-to-polymer package negatively. The full P6 history may supply restrictions that evade them; those restrictions and the modular P3 resummation remain the mathematical work needed for a positive theorem.
