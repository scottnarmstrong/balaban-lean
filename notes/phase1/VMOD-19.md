## 1. The labels cannot be erased from the stated rooted-patch record

Let $d,L\ge3$, $K=k+1$, $a_j=ML^j$, and take a torus large enough to contain one new overlapping block without wrapping. Put $\Omega_k=\Omega_K$ equal to the whole torus. Let $X$ be one old $\pi'_k$ cube whose base is aligned to the new $\pi_K$ grid. Its side is $La_k=a_K$, so it is exactly one new top cell. Use the old chosen cover $\{X\}$ and a one-vertex tree. This is `SizeModDefs.ModAdmissible`, and $\Delta_X=d_k^{\rm cov}(X\operatorname{mod}\Omega_k^c)=0$. Let $B$ be the new $\pi'_K$ cube centered on that top cell, let $\mathcal B=\{B\}$, and let $A_0$ be its aligned corner in $X$. These data satisfy SN and GRID.

For each $1\le j\le K$, take the aligned derivative index $P_j=(j,A_0)$. Its parameter cell `PolymerGeometry.Pset m₁ P_j` contains $A_0$ and lies in $X$; its new top cell is always $X$. Take $C_j=\{P_j\}$. It is a one-vertex rooted Moore forest, so AG holds. The deterministic central cube of `VMOD-18.md` (2.2) is $Q(P_j)=B$ for every $j$. Hence $S_{C_j}=B$, $Y_{C_j}=B$, and the source and completion record are identical for all $j$, whereas the ledgers $\mathcal L_{C_j}=\{(j,\operatorname{cellbase}_j A_0)\}$ are distinct. For any fixed $\kappa_2$,
$$\sum_{C:S_C=B}e^{-\kappa_2|C|}\ge K e^{-\kappa_2}.\tag{1.1}$$
The right side of proposed `VMOD-18.md` (3.7) would be $H e^{q\Delta_X}=H$. Letting $K$ grow disproves that bound with constants independent of the horizon. This is a **PROVED finite-record counterexample**. It uses only the alignment premise of landed `GraphGmExponent.boundary_gm3_fixed_Y_rate`; that theorem counts the fiber *after* fixing the ledger and is consistent with (1.1).

This example is **not** a claim that all $P_j$ belong simultaneously to `cubeIdx F^\sharp` for one actual P6 cover. They do not when they contain the same point: landed `GeneratingSet.layer_disjoint` makes its layers disjoint, and `Cover.mem_cubeIdx` requires $P_j$ in layer $j$. The lesson is exact: SN–AG–GRID plus the landed layer-cell bound `GraphGmCount.card_deriv_in_cell` do **not** imply label erasure. An actual-P6 proof of (3.7) would need an additional scale-layer packing theorem for the roots and all buried derivatives, including reactivated holes. No such theorem is landed. We choose the class that **stores** the labels and an aggregate norm, so no assertion of actual-P6 fiber failure is needed. A deterministic choice of old cover and of central completion removes tie-break multiplicity but does not remove the distinct layer ledgers in (1.1).

## 2. The first actual-history witness gap

The finite site theorem of `VMOD-18.md` uses three kinds of input. GRID is routine at the level of *region geometry*: `Region.isLBlockUnion_Omega` in `Region/Inclusions.lean` says that `RegionStep.Omega` is an $L$-block union in the coarse torus; `FlowSteps.hnn` and the fine-set map convert each coarse cube to an aligned $\pi_K$ top cell. Thus GRID is **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the landed region block-union and scale equations**, with the fine-set identification a short **OPEN** Lean bridge.

For SN, the elementary box part is available once an old chosen cover is supplied: an old $\pi'_k$ cube has side $a_K$ and fits in a new $\pi'_K$ cube of side $La_K$; a tree meeting the old cube meets its containing new cube. The missing part is
$$X\cap\Omega_K\subset B\quad\text{with $B$ made only from the old chosen cover of }X\cap\Omega_k.\tag{2.1}$$
It follows from $\Omega_K\subset\Omega_k$, but that inclusion is not a `RegionHistory` theorem. The landed `RegionStep.not_mem_Z_of_mem_Omega` gives $\Omega_K\subset Z_k^c$ after transport, which is different. `RegionHistory.Z_eq` removes selected ripe components from $Z_k^{\rm pre}$; the old $\Omega_k^c$ lies in $Z_k^{\rm pre}$ by `RegionStep.Zpre_compl_subset_OmegaDD` together with `RegionStep.OmegaDD_subset_Omega` (the combined implication is `RegionSeq.Omega_compl_subset_Zpre` in `Region/Generating.lean`), but need not remain in the post-removal $Z_k$. The `RegionSeq.nesting` theorem concerns the no-removal sequence and cannot be applied to `RegionHistory` after this substitution.

There is an exact **reset implication**. Suppose at an allowed $k\le k_0$ every component of $Z_k^{\rm pre}$ is removed. Then `RegionHistory.Z_eq` gives $Z_k=\varnothing$. If the next record has $P=Q=R=S=\varnothing$, the definitions `Region.Uprime`, `OmegaPrime`, `Omega` give $\Omega_K$ equal to the whole torus. If $\Omega_k^c\ne\varnothing$, then $\Omega_K\not\subset\Omega_k$. This set-theoretic implication is **PROVED from the landed definitions**. Its realization by a *ripe admissible* history is **OPEN-P6-RIPE-RESET-REALIZATION**: `RegionHistory.Admissible` requires every removed component to satisfy `Setting.Ripe`, and the inspected landed inhabitation theorem uses no removals. I do not call the conditional reset an actual-history counterexample.

The reset matters for the site repair itself. If a large old modular hole $F\subset X$ is reopened, the old chosen cover need only cover $X\cap\Omega_k$ and can miss $F$, whereas the new active support contains $F\cap\Omega_K$. Even with $C=\varnothing$, its new chosen-cover cost need not be bounded by $\Delta_X/L$. Thus (2.1) is the **first nonautomatic SN premise**, not a formality. A revised source record would have to charge the reactivated trace or use a new source cover. The proof of such a charge is **OPEN-P6-REACTIVATED-SOURCE**. In addition, `Def7Hist.dCovMod` uses coarse continuous segments while `SizeModDefs.dcovMod` uses a fine-site graph; their exact source-size comparison is **OPEN-P6-SIZE-BRIDGE**. No landing cited here identifies the two.

AG is later and genuinely analytic. `GraphGmDefs.Effective` and `witnessClosure` define reachability from a source base; `GraphGmTree.modular_tree_of_witnesses` assumes its attachment record. Neither `RegionHistory.Admissible` nor `RegionStep` mentions an AE coefficient, so neither can prove that a nonzero coefficient has a rooted Moore patch forest or an anchor in $B$. `OPEN-P6-SOURCE-ANCHOR` and `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE` remain. Thus SN–AG–GRID for all actual P6 histories is **OPEN**, first at (2.1). The existing data establish no counterexample to the exact P6 coefficient expansion; this distinction is necessary.
