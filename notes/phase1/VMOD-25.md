## 1. Which source roots a coefficient?

For each of the two **landed operator/cover pairs** `Balaban.LargeField.p6_coverGeom_old` and `Balaban.LargeField.p6_coverGeom_boundary`, take an injective finite enumeration $c:\iota\to\operatorname{cubeIdx}(F^\sharp)$ and $P_i=\texttt{Pset}\ m_1(c(i))$. The physical old-boundary composite $F^H_{X,\mathcal B}(u,s)$ is the P3 (4.41), printed p. 77, interpolation of the P6 (4.32) last term, printed p. 34, using respectively $H^-=\texttt{p6OldC}$ or $H^\partial=\texttt{p6BoundaryC}$ and their **common** cover $F^\sharp$. No landed declaration constructs this $F^H_{X,\mathcal B}$; `P6PairOp` constructs `Dec2.CoverGeom`, not an old boundary activity. Define its coefficient precisely by
$$a^H_{X,C}(u)=\texttt{Balaban.Localization.coef}\,(F^H_{X,\mathcal B}(u,\cdot))\,C,\qquad C\in\operatorname{Finset}\iota.$$
Let $A_X$ be the top $\pi_K$ cells meeting $X\cap\widetilde\Omega_K$. “Active-rooted” means that **each Moore-connected component** $C_0$ of the graph on $C$ with edges `LedgerCounter.MooreTouch P_i P_j` contains an $i$ such that `MooreTouch` holds between $P_i$ and some cell in $A_X$. This is stronger than the `SNAGGridRecord.forest` field: its root set $A_0$ is merely a nonempty subset of `newCubes ℬ`.

**PROVED abstractly; actual instantiation OPEN-P6-STAR-AE.** `Balaban.Localization.LocalityAEOn R S P Yc Ag F` states that $F(u,s)$ is unchanged if parameters agree for patches contained in $Y_c(C)$ and the data agree according to `Ag C`. `coef_eq_zero_of_not_connected_on`, and hence `abstract_expansion_on`, prove $a_{X,C}=0$ unless every $P_i\subset Y_c(C)$. `Balaban.Localization.localization_expansion` uses the particular seed $X_1=\texttt{Balaban.Localization.X1}\ G\ m_0\ k\ \mathcal K_X$ and $Y_c(C)=\texttt{Balaban.Localization.compY}\ c\ X_1\ C$, with `hdisj` between every patch and $X_1$. `compY` is the nearest-neighbor component of $X_1\cup\bigcup_{i\in C}P_i$ containing $X_1$. Thus a surviving patch component must join **that seed**. These declarations say nothing that replaces a seed containing $X\cup B$ by $X\cap\widetilde\Omega_K$. `evTerm` is the sum of these coefficients over a fixed `Ysites`; it is not a new coefficient-locality theorem.

**REFUTED as an implication of the landed locality and SN–AG–GRID premises.** Take an aligned $\pi'_K$ cube $B$ whose first top cell $U_0$ meets $X\cap\widetilde\Omega_K$ and whose opposite face lies deep in a new hole within the old small-field region. Let $A_0$ be sites on that opposite face, and choose a top-layer patch $P_i$ just **outside** $B$, face-adjacent to $A_0$ but separated by at least two top cells from every active cell of $X$; the hole may be arbitrarily large. Then $A_0\subset B$, $C=\{i\}$ satisfies `SNAGGridRecord.forest` with `parent i = none`, and $P_i\cap B=\varnothing$ also meets `Balaban.Localization.localization_expansion`'s `hdisj` requirement when its seed is $B$. An old chosen-cover cube meeting $X\cap\Omega_k^{\rm post}$ can lie inside $B$, with the old tree meeting it, so the remaining record fields have a set/record configuration. Realization by a particular ripe P6 history and a nonzero physical coefficient are **OPEN**. At the abstract AE level, $F(u,s)=s_i$ (or $\log e^{\lambda s_i}=\lambda s_i$) obeys locality with seed $B$, is analytic, and has a nonzero $i$ coefficient. Switching off parameters outside the *active* trace changes $F$ because its parameter read support reaches the part of $B$ in the hole. No logarithmic factorization follows from `LocalityAEOn`; a one-patch derivative already refutes the proposed deduction.

**PROVED tree charge for the cover, not by G1.** If `SNAGGridRecord.cube_met_by_tree` holds, each aligned $\pi'_K$ cube of $\mathcal B$ contains a vertex of $W$. A fixed old $\pi_k$ cell base $\texttt{cbase}\ a_k(w)$ belongs to at most $L^d$ aligned $\pi'_K$ cubes meeting $w$ (since $a_K=La_k$ and each large cube has $L$ choices per coordinate). Landed `Balaban.Fluctuation.card_image_cbase_le` then gives
$$|\mathcal B|\le 3^dL^d(1+\Delta_X),\qquad
|\{\text{top cells in }B\}|\le3^dL^{2d}(1+\Delta_X). \tag{1.1}$$
This uses the minimizing equality `SNAGGridRecord.old_minimizing`, torus divisibility $L^{m_0}\mid N_1$, and $k\le K$. It counts **hole** cubes as well as active ones. It is not `cellCount_le_of_admissible`/G1, which counts only $X\cap\Omega_k^{\rm post}$. The elementary bounded-overlap wrapper for (1.1) is not landed Lean code.

**OPEN: uniform root incidence at hole cells.** The rooted-animal bound of VMOD-24 (3.3) also needs a uniform bound on the number of actual $F^\sharp$ patches Moore-touching *each* root top cell. The landed active-halo argument gives $b_{\rm root}=3^d(L^{m_0})^d$ for active cells. It does not give this for a cell of $B$ across the old wall: `p6Fsharp_Ω_of_le` retains lower layers there, and the flat-wall construction of VMOD-24 §2 has at least $L^{(m_0+K-1)(d-1)}$ lower-layer cells in a top cell. Consequently (1.1) alone does **not** establish VMOD-24 (4.2). A usable replacement premise is `P6_ROOT_INCIDENCE_B`: every top cell of the actual chosen $B$ has at most a fixed $b_B$ incident $F^\sharp$ patches. The weaker counting substitute is a bound of $b_Bc_B(1+\Delta_X)$ on all patches incident to $B$, with $c_B=3^dL^{2d}$; that version needs a separate animal-count statement rooted at patches. Either premise must be proved for the *canonical* P6 cover or replaced by an active-rooted coefficient theorem.

**PROVED conditional algebra.** If `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE` supplies rooting at $A_X\cup\{\text{top cells in }B\}$, `P6_ROOT_INCIDENCE_B` holds, and the source-size identification below holds, put $c_R=L^dc_G+c_B$, $B_*=\max\{2,3(L+4)^d,3^d(L^{m_0})^d,b_B\}$, $z=B_*^2e^{-\kappa_2}<1$, $h=-\log(1-z)$, $H=e^{hc_R}$, and $q=hc_R$. The same depth-first proof as VMOD-24 (3.3) gives, for each fixed $X,S$,
$$\sum_{C:S_C=S}e^{-\kappa_2|C|}\le H e^{q\Delta_X}. \tag{1.2}$$
Here $|A_X|\le L^dc_G(1+\Delta_X)$ is conditional on the post-R size bridge. If `P6_ROOT_INCIDENCE_B` is absent, (1.2) is **OPEN**, even though (1.1) is proved. P6 p. 34 states that each **preliminary domain component** contains an old active-trace component. This is a statement about $Y_1$, not about each connected component of the parameter-patch graph: in the example above, $Y_1$ contains the active trace through the connected cube $B$, while its only patch attaches to $B$ in the hole. Thus the paper's sentence does **not** prove coefficient-level active rooting. Its validity for the intended interpolation, including the old spin and fluctuation reads in the hole, is **OPEN** at the landed AE interface. P3 (4.41)–(4.43), printed pp. 77–78, roots components in the stated $X_1$, not automatically in a later active trace.

## 2. The old H7 size is different

The exact accessor in the landed H7 boundary clause is `Balaban.LargeField.D7Bdry.bound` (and `Balaban.LargeField.D7Bdry2.bound`): its exponent is `Balaban.LargeField.dCovMod S.L (OmegaCube A.H k) X`, an `ENNReal` converted by `.toReal`. `Balaban.LargeField.IsCovCurve` minimizes a **continuous Euclidean segment** length on the coarse torus, with closed cubes `Balaban.LargeField.cube`. The desired $\Delta_X$ is `Balaban.Fluctuation.dcovMod m₀ k Ωpost Xfine`, minimizing the cardinality of a **nearest-neighbor fine-site graph** divided by $a_k$ (`Balaban.Fluctuation.ModAdmissible`). `Balaban.LargeField.Def7.OmegaCube` is raw pre-R; `RegionHistory.omegaPost` and `omegaNext_subset_post_fine` are landed region accessors, but `D7Bdry.bound` does not call `omegaPost`. The set inclusion $\widetilde\Omega_K\subset\widetilde\Omega_k^{\rm post}$ is **PROVED** by those region results (VMOD-21 §3); equality of the old H7 domain and the post-R fine domain is **OPEN**.

**REFUTED for the unrestricted landed size definitions:** no uniform $a>0,b<\infty$ (depending only on $d,L$) satisfies $d_{H7}\ge a\Delta_X-b$ on all admissible domains. In dimension $d\ge2$, put two aligned $\pi'_k$ endpoint blocks diagonally at a common corner. Join them in $X$ by a nearest-neighbor chain of aligned blocks taking an arbitrarily long detour; keep the two orthogonal blocks at the corner outside $X$. Set $\Omega^c$ equal to the detour blocks, so its only relevant NN component is contained in $X$ and $X\in\mathcal D_k(\mathrm{mod}\,\Omega^c)$; the two endpoint blocks are active. The two chosen cover blocks at the endpoints lie in $X$ and cover $X\cap\Omega$. The **single point at their common closed-cube corner**, represented by a zero-length `Seg`, is connected, lies in $\bigcup_{x\in X}\square_x$, and meets both cover blocks. Thus `Balaban.LargeField.dCovMod=0`. In the fine-site model there is no NN edge across the diagonal corner, and every connected tree inside $X_{\rm fine}$ meeting both endpoint blocks follows the detour; $\Delta_X\to\infty$. Use a large torus to avoid wraparound. This is an explicit `IsCovCurve`/`ModAdmissible` configuration; embedding it in an actual P6 region history is **OPEN**. It refutes a bridge derived merely from the landed definitions. The obstruction persists even if the pre/post region mismatch is removed.

**OPEN-P6-SOURCE-SIZE-BRIDGE must therefore be a new restricted-history estimate**, not an equality of existing definitions. Its sufficient form is a fine-lift/domain identification and $D_{H7}(X)\ge a\Delta_X-b$ with uniform $a>0,b\ge0$. If the old extracted bound is $\beta_k^{-2}e^{-rD_{H7}}$, then it becomes $\beta_k^{-2}e^{rb}e^{-ra\Delta_X}$. The site-transfer arithmetic remains valid provided
$$ra\ge\kappa_0+2\kappa/L,\qquad \kappa_1-1\ge\kappa_2+2\kappa dL,\qquad
\mu=\kappa_0-q\ge a_{\rm ent}(d,L)+1. \tag{2.1}$$
Under (1.2), replace VMOD-24 (4.4)–(4.5) by
$$\|B_X^+(S)\|\le\beta_k^{-2}e^{rb}H e^{-\mu\Delta_X-2\kappa D(S)},\tag{2.2}$$
$$\|B_{\rm old}^+(S)\|\le\beta_k^{-2}e^{rb}H L^dc_GK_0(d,L,\mu)e^{-(2\kappa-1)D(S)}.\tag{2.3}$$
The old-source pinned sum uses landed `Balaban.Fluctuation.G2_X` only after $\Delta_X$ is its *fine* size; alternatively a separate G2 theorem for the actual coarse H7 labels is needed. All of (2.1)–(2.3) are **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** the named open bridge, rooting/incidence premises, mixed H7 coefficient bound, and site-transfer premise. The prefactor $\beta_k^{-3/2}$ follows if $\beta_k^{1/2}\ge e^{rb}H L^dc_GK_0$.
