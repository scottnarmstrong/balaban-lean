## 1. The region step and its actual seeds

**PROVED from landed definitions.** `Region/Step.lean:135–216` gives, on the coarse $\mathbb T_K$ cube lattice,
$$U_k=\operatorname{nbhd}_{a+1}Z_k,\quad U'_k=\operatorname{blockMap}_L(U_k),\quad
\Omega'_K=\operatorname{Op}_{\rho+2}(\operatorname{nbhd}_3U'_k),\quad
\Omega_K^{\rm pre}=\operatorname{Op}_1(\operatorname{nbhd}_3P_K;\Omega'_K),\tag{1}$$
$$F''_K=\operatorname{nbhd}_{\rho+2}Q_K\cup\operatorname{nbhd}_{2\rho+4}R_K,\quad
\Omega''_K=\operatorname{Op}_1(F''_K;\Omega_K^{\rm pre}),\quad
F^Z_K=\operatorname{nbhd}_4((\Omega''_K)^c)\cup\operatorname{nbhd}_1S_K,\quad
Z^{(0)}_K=(\operatorname{Op}_{b+3}F^Z_K)^c.\tag{2}$$
`Region/Inclusions.lean:147–248` proves $\Omega_K^c\subset(\Omega''_K)^c\subset F^Z_K\subset Z^{(0)}_K\subset Z_K^{\rm pre}$ and $Z^{(0)}_K\subset\operatorname{nbhd}_{b+L+1}F^Z_K$. Thus the *new hole* is $\Omega_K^c$ from (1); $Z^{(0)}_K$ is a larger region used for the large-field component history. `History/FlowSteps.lean:119–140` uses $Z_K^{\rm pre}=Z^{(0)}_K$ for $K>k_0$ and a hull closure for $K\le k_0$; its width is $\omega_K=2\lfloor R_{\rm base}\lambda_k^2\rfloor+L+11$ (`Region/Step.lean:121–126`). `Def7Hist.OmegaCube H K=(H.step k).Omega` (`Def7Hist.lean:171`); after $R$, `History/OmegaPost.lean:39–65` defines $\Omega_K^{\rm post}=\Omega_K^{\rm pre}\cup\bigcup\mathrm{Rem}_K$. The latter has a smaller complement, and equals the raw domain if no component is removed.

**PROVED, with exact scope.** `Region/Seeds.lean:236` `RegionStep.attribution` says every $x\in Z^{(0)}_K$ is linked within that set to either an **old seed** $\operatorname{blockMap}_L(Y)$, $Y$ a component of $Z_k$, at distance $\le4r_k$, or a new cube $q\in P_K\cup Q_K\cup R_K\cup S_K$ at distance $\le5r_k$. `History/StepSeeds.lean:122` `attr_seed` restricts this to the appropriate component. `History/FlowBook.lean:239` `seed_pos` says each component of $Z_K^{\rm pre}$ contains an old part **or** a new cube. `Geometry/Components.lean:125` `comps_sep` and `History/Core.lean:197` `core_sep` separate distinct $Z^{\rm pre}$ components by $\omega_K$; they do not give a size bound for an $\Omega_K^c$ component. `History/RetainedShell.lean:50,62` `ball_nbhd_Z0` and `_S` put large balls **inside** $Z^{(0)}_K$ around $\Omega''{}^c$ and $S$, rather than bounding a component by new seeds.

**PROVED quantitative consequence of attribution for the raw hole, independent of whether a later hull closure is used.** If $A_{\rm old}=\operatorname{blockMap}_L Z_k$ and $N=P_K\cup Q_K\cup R_K\cup S_K$, then
$$\Omega_K^c\subset Z^{(0)}_K\subset\operatorname{nbhd}_{4r_k}A_{\rm old}\cup\operatorname{nbhd}_{5r_k}N,\qquad
|\Omega_K^c|\le(8r_k+1)^d|A_{\rm old}|+(10r_k+1)^d|N|.\tag{3}$$
The second inequality is the union bound for sup-norm balls on the cube torus. It also holds for any single hole component with global counts on the right. There is **no landed assertion** that every hole component contains a *new* cube or lies in a fixed-radius neighborhood of *new* cubes alone. The suggested $s(2(b+3)+1)^d$ bound overlooks $U'_k$, the $P,Q,R$ collars, and possibly the hull closure; even without old seeds, the landed general radius is $5r_k$, not $b+3$. `seed_pos` supplies an old-or-new alternative, exactly as (3) requires.

## 2. The proposed inequality is false for admissible histories

**REFUTED** for the VMOD-35 whole-component completion, with any fixed $c_1,c_2,c_3,c_4$ depending only on $d,L$ and the fixed radii:
$$d7SizePre(Y_f(J))\le c_1d^{\rm cov}_k(X\bmod(\Omega_k^{\rm post})^c)+c_2|J|+c_3\,|N\cap\{\text{hole components met}\}|+c_4.\tag{4}$$
Here is an admissible-history family and its direct geometric calculation. Fix $d\ge3$, $L\ge8$, $k=1$, $k_0=0$, large torus sides, and radii obeying `RadiusHyp`; keep them fixed while a length parameter $n$ grows. Take no removals, so `RegionHistory.Admissible` (`History/FlowBook.lean:286`) is vacuous. At step $0\to1$, $Z_0=\varnothing$, hence $\Omega'_1$ is the whole torus; choose $P_1$ to be the three-cell erosion of an aligned $L$-block rectangle $H_n$ of transverse width $L$ and longitudinal length $nL$, with $Q_1=R_1=S_1=\varnothing$. It satisfies `RegionStep.P_sub` because $\Omega'_1$ is whole. Away from the distant torus seam, $\operatorname{nbhd}_3P_1=H_n$. For an axis-parallel rectangle $F$ and $m\ge1$, $\bigl(\operatorname{Op}_mF\bigr)^c=\operatorname{nbhd}_{m-1}F$: one inclusion follows because every block containing a nearby point meets $F$ within $m-1$; for a farther point choose an $L$-block extending outward in a separating coordinate. Apply this first with $m=1$, then with $m=\lfloor2R_0\rfloor+3$. Thus $\Omega_1^c=H_n$ is already an aligned $\pi'_1$ union and $Z_1=Z^{(0)}_1=\operatorname{nbhd}_{\lfloor2R_0\rfloor+6}H_n$. This is a valid history: `RegionHistory.Z_eq` is met by defining each subsequent $Z$ from its preceding $Z^{\rm pre}$, with `rem=∅`.

At step $1\to2$ set **all new seeds empty**. Equations (1) give $U'_1=\operatorname{blockMap}_L\operatorname{nbhd}_{a+1}Z_1$, another rectangle, and $\Omega_2^c=\operatorname{nbhd}_{\lfloor R_1\rfloor+4}U'_1$ by the same rectangle identity. Choose the allowed integer $\lfloor R_1\rfloor$ so one long face of this rectangle cuts the aligned $\pi'_2$ grid strictly inside a block (there are $L$ residue choices and `RadiusHyp` imposes an upper bound, not a congruence). The long face has order $n/L$ aligned target blocks. Take an old $X_n$ containing all of $H_n$ and a connected, bounded-width chain of aligned old $\pi'_1$ blocks near one end that reaches $\Omega_2$ and crosses $Z_1$; extend it a further fixed distance if a new $Z_2$-crossing boundary label is required. Then $X_n\in\mathcal D_1(\mathrm{mod}\,\Omega_1^c)$ and a chosen cover/tree of its **active** chain gives $d^{\rm cov}_1(X_n\bmod\Omega_1^c)\le C(d,L,R_0,R_1)$ independent of $n$. For $J=\varnothing$, VMOD-35's seed contains $X_n$ and meets the single new hole; its completion must contain all of $\Omega_2^c$. Every target $\pi'_2$ box covering a site on the misaligned long face also contains active sites. Thus $Y_f(\varnothing)\cap\Omega_2$ contains at least $n/L-O_{d,L,R_0,R_1}(1)$ distinct physical $\pi_2$ cells. `Fluctuation/SizeModG1.lean:93` `G1_cellCount`, with $c_1(d,L)=[3(2L-1)]^d$ (`SizeModCore.lean:159`), yields $d7SizePre(Y_f(\varnothing))\ge[n/L-O(1)]/c_1(d,L)-1$. In (4), $|J|=|N|=0$ and the other terms stay bounded. Letting $n\to\infty$ contradicts (4). The same example is post-$R$: $\mathrm{Rem}_2=\varnothing$, so `omegaPost_eq_OmegaCube_of_rem_empty` applies. No Lean theorem packages this rectangle family yet; the displayed rectangle identity and construction are the mathematical proof from the cited definitions.

**PROVED correction to the thin-corridor diagnosis.** A long new hole need not require current new seeds every $O(b)$ cubes: its length can be inherited from the old component $Z_k$. In this example it has **zero** current new seeds. The old modular size remains bounded because the *old* hole is an aligned $\pi'_k$ union, while the newly enlarged hole cuts the *new* target grid. A charge by old seed volume, or by an old-history reserve tied to that volume, could bound the spill; that is a different statement, **OPEN-P6-OLD-SEED-CHARGE**. Formula (3) identifies the missing old term. No `$c_3$` depending only on the current radius repairs (4).

## 3. Small factors, the H7 clause, and rate arithmetic

**PROVED source reading.** P6 (3.9), printed p. 18, gives the new-step operator a gain $A_3(\log\beta_k)^{2p_0}$ per new cube, with an additional **positive error** of order $(\log\beta_k)|Z_k^c\cap Z_{k+1}|$ in the exponent. $$\kappa^{(k)}=A_3(\log\beta_k)^{2p_0}n_{\rm new}-(c_1\log\beta_k+c_2)M^dL^{-d}n_{\rm out}.\tag{5}$$
`Def7Counts.kappaStepLoc_localHistory` identifies its localized count, and `Operators/LocOpBound.locOp_pkgBound_kappa` carries the resulting factor in the **large-field operator**. **PROVED source reading and class scope.** P5 H7 (1.38)–(1.39), printed p. 497, says $|B^{(k)}(X)|<B_0e^{-\kappa d_k(X\bmod\Omega_k^c)}$ with **no seed-count credit**. P6 p. 34 explicitly obtains the old boundary coefficient bound (4.34), $\beta_k^{-3/2}e^{-(2\kappa-1)d_K(Y\bmod\Omega_K^c)}$, separately from the operator gain (3.9); (4.35)–(4.36), printed p. 35, start a later fluctuation/cluster calculation and do not amend (4.34). Landed `LargeField/Def7.lean:385–445,500–557` `D7Bdry.bound` and `D7BdryPost.bound` likewise have no seed-count field. Therefore option (ii), “multiply a deficient coefficient by the new seed factor before checking the unchanged H7 class,” is **REFUTED** for the displayed P5/P6 coefficient clause and for histories with $n_{\rm new}=0$. Option (i) would require a **new** H7/D7 class, for example $\|B_Y\|\le B_0\exp[-\tau d7SizePre(Y)+\tau c_{\rm old}|A_{\rm old}(Y)|+\tau c_{\rm new}|N(Y)|]$, plus a proved assignment of both reserves to the same operator component; this is **OPEN** and is not P5 H7.

**PROVED conditional arithmetic; inapplicable to (4).** If a separate theorem supplied a bound $\Delta_Y\le D_0+C_{\rm new}|N|$ with no inherited term, multiplying $e^{-\tau D_0}$ by a *reserved* factor $e^{-g_k|N|}$ would yield $e^{-\tau\Delta_Y}$ when $g_k\ge\tau C_{\rm new}$, after accounting for the negative out-term in (5). `RadiusHyp.le` gives $R_{k-1}=O(\lambda_{k-1}^2)$, and the paper's running $\lambda$ is logarithmic in $\beta$; a ball-count choice $C_{\rm new}\asymp C(d,L)(10r_k+1)^d$ grows like $\lambda_k^{2d}$. `Counter/Params.lean:195` has $g_k=\tfrac12A_3\lambda_k^{2p_0}$, and `Counter/Params.lean:238–239` assumes $2p_0>p_2\ge2d+3$, so this comparison is eventually possible for fixed $\tau$. The weaker threshold $p_0(\beta)=A_0(\log\beta a)^{p_0}$ (`Analytic/Constants.lean:81–83`) has degree $p_0$ and need **not** dominate $\lambda^{2d}$ under those assumptions. None of this supplies a factor for the counterexample, since $|N|=0$; the old operator reserve would have to be allocated explicitly.
