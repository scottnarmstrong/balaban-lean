## T4. Exact locality and common local formula

**PROVED (logical factorization).** A coefficient invariant under changing $\theta$ outside `sitesOf C` factors through its restriction on the image of the admissible chart restriction map; define $\widetilde c_N(r)=c_N(\theta,C)$ using any admissible $\theta$ with restriction $r$, and exact locality makes this well-defined. Such a factor exists separately on each torus. `Fluctuation/ParamActivityPhysical.lean:358–369` defines `physRestrict`; the exact-locality lane is proving the coefficient hypothesis and is not counted as landed here.

**REFUTED (locality alone implies coherence).** Exact locality says nothing about $\widetilde c_N=\widetilde c_{N'}$ on corresponding restrictions; even constant functions of the restriction can depend on torus size. A *common* $\widetilde c$ needs a shared localized formula or a proved comparison of those two factor functions. The existential expansion/value theorems do not determine individual coefficients. The first non-formal comparison inside such a formula is the T3 restricted pair value (and the covariance/finite patch and measure identities feeding it); the determinant and site-term formulas must also be transported.

**OPEN (useful shortcut).** With the exact-locality result and the formula exports, prove the equality only for the local coefficient formula on its actual read set, then infer full-input equality by restriction. This can avoid transporting unrelated summands of the *whole* expansion. It cannot avoid T2–T3 or the formula/measure comparison for the coefficient being studied.
