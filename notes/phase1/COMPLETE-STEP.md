## 2. The second dependency: complex `StepNonvanishing`

$$W_1(s,\widehat\psi)+W_2(t,\widehat\psi;g)
 =\sum_{a\in\mathcal T}H_a(s,t,\widehat\psi),\tag{2.1}$$
where $\nu_{p_1}$ is identified with the relevant product of block measures and every $H_a$ is measurable, local on a nonempty support, and obeys $\|H_a\|\le H_0e^{-(\kappa_0+\kappa_1)d(\operatorname{supp}a)}$. 2(2H_0K_0e^{\kappa_1c})c_1K_{0P}e^{c_1+\kappa'c_P}\le1.\tag{2.2}$$

For a uniform *local* birth bound and analytic dependence, the per-block polymer coefficient bound of `mayer_pe` is also needed.

What remains is **OPEN-PHYSICAL-ACTIVITIES**: prove (2.1) and (2.2) for the actual complete-background $W_1,W_2$ and complex source, with a common rate and product-measure identification.
