## T1. Exact root demand and one-step specialization

**PROVED (frozen clause).** After $d,N,\delta,\kappa,c_9,L,M,\beta_0,\beta,\nu,h,k_f$, one family $T$ is chosen **before** either torus side. For every $\ell,\ell'$ with `NeZero` and $M L^{k_f+1}\mid\ell,\ell'$, every $j\in\mathbb N$ (including irrelevant $j$, where support forces zero), $a\in A\subset\mathbb Z^d$, and arbitrary $g:\mathbb Z^d\to\mathbb C^N$, assume $4|y_\mu|<\ell$ and $4|y_\mu|<\ell'$ for every $y\in A,\mu$. Then, componentwise,
$$T_\ell\bigl(j,\bar a_\ell,\bar A_\ell,g\circ\operatorname{valMinAbs}_\ell\bigr)=T_{\ell'}\bigl(j,\bar a_{\ell'},\bar A_{\ell'},g\circ\operatorname{valMinAbs}_{\ell'}\bigr).$$
The same root statement separately imposes $T_\ell(j,x,X;g)=T_\ell(j,x,X;g')$ whenever $g=g'$ **on $X$**. The source $g$ in the two-torus clause is not assumed supported on $A$; values outside $A$ are immaterial only after exact source locality is proved. There is no requirement $\ell\mid\ell'$ or $N_1\mid N'_1$ (`Frozen/Roots/LogPartitionMultiscale.lean:42–72`).

**OPEN (step comparison to prove).** Fix one tower $T_*$, $k,m_1,\nu,\beta,p_1$ and two admissible $N_1,N'_1$ ($3\mid N_1,N'_1$, both $>5$). The fine torus sides are $s_N=N_1 3^{m_1+K}$ and $s_{N'}=N'_1 3^{m_1+K}$, $K=k+1$; the root lattices have sides $N_1 3^{m_1}$ and $N'_1 3^{m_1}$. Use the centred lifts $\iota_N:\mathbb Z^3\to\mathrm{CTorus}_N$ and $\iota_{N'}$, aligned with the same block origin. Let $A$ be an eligible finite aligned union of level-one cubes in the common quarter-box, and let $U_N,U_{N'}$ be the corresponding cube sets with `sitesOf U_N = ι_N(A)` and `sitesOf U_N' = ι_N'(A)`; the root clause for arbitrary finite $A$ also needs a separate account of supports outside this carrier. Let $y_N,i$ and $y_{N'},i$ be corresponding observable sites/components, and let $g_N=g\circ\operatorname{valMinAbs}_{s_N}$, $g_{N'}=g\circ\operatorname{valMinAbs}_{s_{N'}}$. The desired componentwise equality is
$$\operatorname{obsCoeffParam}^{N_1}_{y_N,i}\bigl((\operatorname{rawOf}D_N,g_N),U_N\bigr)=\operatorname{obsCoeffParam}^{N'_1}_{y_{N'},i}\bigl((\operatorname{rawOf}D_{N'},g_{N'}),U_{N'}\bigr).$$
At the zero-class base step $D_N,D_{N'}$ are the **defined** constant `unitBg` data of `hatStepK`; for a fibre theorem, require corresponding raw data on every site actually read by the localized term and matching constant $h_0$. Match the root cube and the coarse block indices as well as the fine sites. The quarter-box on `sitesOf U` is the intended final condition; a proof that temporarily reads a larger patch must either keep that patch nonwinding or eliminate its extra read through exact locality. Root-F's admissibility $ML^{k_f+1}\mid\ell$ is a later flow condition, not equivalent to the tower's $3\mid N_1$, $N_1>5$.

**OPEN (energy comparison).** The analogous equation for the energy family is $e^0_{z,N_1}((\operatorname{rawOf}D_N,0),U_N)=e^0_{z',N'_1}((\operatorname{rawOf}D_{N'},0),U_{N'})$, or, after `sitesOf_regroup`, equality of the site-domain terms. `E1CoeffFamily.e1CoeffFamily_value_fromTower` proves the physical marked-energy sum on the fibre only at source **zero**; `E0CoeffFamily.e0CoeffFamily_fromTower` gives `e0=dz+c` and the determinant value but its full same-family value join is W570. A source-dependent equality of the off-slice energy parameterization is a stronger specification and needs a reason to be required. Root-F's displayed terms $T_j$ are observables, so the first zero-class observable comparison does not logically need torus equality of the *output* energy. The next step uses that energy as an old class term in its action and activities; inductive coherence therefore needs energy equality, including its local coefficients, before a nonzero-class observable comparison can be propagated.

**OPEN (minimal physical isomorphism).** For every finite nonwinding support $Y$ and every intermediate eligible row/patch of its coefficient, the centred map must preserve (i) aligned fine and coarse blocks, `lvTop/lvLow`, `LocalCover`, labels and root; (ii) the decoupling parameter indexed by the same patch; (iii) the restricted interaction/mass operator and its source/data rows; (iv) each finite cut-resolvent or random-walk term used in the localized covariance, background solution and determinant; and (v) the normalized product block fluctuation measure after reindexing. The conclusion must be equality of **localized component terms**, not equality of the unrestricted covariance on the two tori. The latter is generally torus-size dependent, even at equal local data; finite-walk equality applies only before winding.
