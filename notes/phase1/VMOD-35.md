**PROVED source reading** (P3 p. 78, (4.44)). For a marked $M_1L^j\eta$ cube $P\subset\Omega_j\setminus\Omega_{j+1}$, let $Q(P)$ be its physical $\pi_j$ cell of side $ML^j\eta$. Set

Formula (1) adds **all containing physical $\pi'_j$ boxes inside that same layer**; it does not add an $R_j$ neighborhood.

## 2. The landed objects and an exact conditional construction

**PROVED landed definitions.** `PolymerGeometry.lean:253,285,421,426` has `Pset`, `Dsites G m₀ q`, `Ysites G m₀ c X₁ J`, and `compY c X₁ J`. **PROVED grid fact, with a short Lean lemma still to write.** A `Pset m₁ q` lies in one physical $\pi_K$ cell: its side $M_1L^{q.1}$ divides $ML^K$ for $q.1\le K$. The lifted $\Omega_K$ is a union of these $\pi_K$ cells (`FineSizeFrame.hM/divN₁`, `Def7FineSize.mem_d7Pull`); `P6ActiveHalo.blockMap_eq_of_mem_box` is the existing same-cell tool. Hence a parameter patch cannot literally straddle $\partial\Omega_f$. If it lies in $\Omega_f$, it is a top-layer $F^\sharp$ patch because $\Omega_f\subset(F^\sharp.\Omega K)$ (`p6_active_top_halo` gives the stronger neighboring-cell inclusion), and the lower layers are disjoint from the top layer. A patch outside $\Omega_f$ can still lie in a lower $H^\partial$ layer: `p6_boundary_domains` explicitly retains lower layers there. An enlarged `Dsites` box or a field footprint **can** cross the wall.
