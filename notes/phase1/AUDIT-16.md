## Refutation ledger

1. **Second witness for exact locality?** No. The appended exact clause sits inside the same `∀ C` conjunct after the single `∃ c` (`E1CoeffFamily.lean:376–406`). The proof obtains the same coefficient from `hmain` and `hmainY` (`:537–564`) and returns their two locality components at `:564`. Comparison of the three theorem statement spans with `git show origin/main:…` found only this appended seven-line conjunct in each: `E1CoeffFamily.lean:400–406`, `E0CoeffFamily.lean:260–266`, `StepIdentityFibreFamilies.lean:177–183`.
2. **Hidden thick-set dependence in zero-source activity?** No. `commonAct_zeroSourceAct_H_local_rawY` compares a left label at `agreeOn z.1` and invokes the scaled label lemma at that same `z` (`E1ParamRawLocal.lean:110–132`); its right-label case is parameter independent. `commonAct_zeroSourceAct_hA_rawY` converts full physical restriction on `sitesOf T` to raw agreement there, then restricts to each label's own `ysite` (`:386–407`). The source component remains present in the premise although this zero-source helper uses only raw agreement.
3. **Exact locality accidentally added to `dz` or `e0`?** No. The copied conjunct is within the `c` part of `e0CoeffFamily_value_fromTower` and `stepIdentity_zeroClass_fibre_fromTower` (`E0CoeffFamily.lean:250–285`; `StepIdentityFibreFamilies.lean:167–202`). Their `dz` and `e0` parts retain their original clauses.
4. **Unconditional coefficient transport?** No. `evTerm_boxMap`'s `hcoef` (`TwoTorusDomainFamilies.lean:549–558`) supplies precisely the equality needed for `Finset.sum_image` (`:559–562`). It does not derive equality for arbitrary `F,F'`.
5. **Unjustified inverse transport or impossible box?** No at statement level. `inv_block_transport` requires block diagonality/support and invertibility (`TwoTorusCubeOps.lean:713–727`). `cubeG_boxMap` obtains these from `box_blockAligned`, `cubeH_isUnit`, and `cubeG_supp` (`:882–900`); the image/inverse image of the interaction box is established within the nonwinding region (`:834–878`). The centre, sites, and margins are genuine restrictions; no theorem claims transport for a winding cube or arbitrary potential.
6. **Wrong base object or missing chart Jacobian?** No. The base equality is to the frozen partition function (`BasePhysicalDensity.lean:246–250`); `baseKernel` uses `βaL^d/L²` once per coarse block (`:44–48`), as in `BASESTEP.md:11–17`. `chartWeight` has the correct unscaled `ξ` formula (`HemispherePi.lean:35–37`); the separate `v=√βξ` formula is recorded at `ChartIntegral.lean:238–249`. The only scope limit is the supplied cutoff in the generic good/bad identity.
