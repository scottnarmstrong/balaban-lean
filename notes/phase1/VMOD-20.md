## 1. Which active domain enters the step after R

Write $k+1=K$, $T_j=\mathbb T_j$, and let $p:T_k\to T_K$ be the cube coarsening. For a `RegionHistory` $H$, define the union of removed components at scale $k$ by
$$R_k(H)=\bigcup_{C\in H.\mathrm{rem}(k)}C\subset T_k.$$
For $k\ge1$ put $\Omega_k^{\rm pre}=(H.\mathrm{step}(k-1)).\mathrm{Omega}$ and
$$\Omega_k^{\rm post}:=\Omega_k^{\rm pre}\cup R_k(H).\tag{1.1}$$
At $k=0$, set $\Omega_0^{\rm post}=T_0$; `RegionHistory.rem_empty` makes this the base convention. The exact source inclusion needed by SN in `VMOD-18.md` is
$$p^{-1}(\Omega_K^{\rm pre})\subset\Omega_k^{\rm post}.\tag{1.2}$$
Consequently an old chosen cover of $X\cap\Omega_k^{\rm post}$ also covers $X\cap p^{-1}(\Omega_K^{\rm pre})$. Enlarging each old aligned $\pi'_k$ cover cube to an aligned $\pi'_K$ cube containing it then gives the SN family $\mathcal B$; its member meets the old tree at the point where the old cover cube does. This last enlargement is the elementary box part of SN, subject to the torus alignment and containment in the constructed output $R_C$. The active inclusion (1.2) is **PROVED** from the landed region and history definitions, as follows.

**Post-R nesting lemma.** Assume $k<K$, `FlowSteps.hnn` at $k$, $L>0$, and `RegionHistory.Z_eq` at $k$. If $x\in T_k$ and $p(x)\in(H.\mathrm{step}(k)).\mathrm{Omega}$, then $x\in\Omega_k^{\rm post}$. The first exclusion and `RegionStep.Omega_compl_subset_Z0` for `H.step(k-1)`, followed by `RegionStep.Z0_subset_Zpre`, give $x\in H.F.Zpre(k)$. The second exclusion and `RegionHistory.Z_eq` then give $x\in(H.\mathrm{step}(k)).Z$, a contradiction. For $k=0$ the conclusion is immediate. The proof uses no ripeness property: it applies to **every** `RegionHistory`, hence to every `RegionHistory.Admissible`. It is a short new Lean lemma, not a declaration already landed. Its set form is
$$p^{-1}(\Omega_K^{\rm pre})\subset (\Omega_k^{\rm pre}\cup R_k(H)),\qquad
(\Omega_k^{\rm post})^c\subset (H.\mathrm{step}(k)).Z.\tag{1.3}$$

## 2. The exact size comparison required by site D7

For a post-R history $A$, an active top-cell set $S\subset\Omega_A^{\rm post}$, and its NN hole completion $Y_A(S)$, distinguish
$$D_c(A,S):=\operatorname{Def7Hist.dCovMod}(L,\Omega_A^{\rm post},Y_A(S)).\operatorname{toReal},\qquad
D_f(A,S):=\operatorname{SizeModDefs.dOmega}(m_0,k,\widetilde\Omega_A,\widetilde S).\operatorname{toReal}.\tag{2.1}$$
The tilde is the lift from scale-$k$ cubes to fine sites, with the scale and torus identification made explicit. `Def7Hist.IsCovCurve` minimizes the sum of Euclidean lengths of continuous segments inside coarse cubes. `SizeModDefs.ModAdmissible` minimizes the number of fine graph bonds divided by `scaleW`; they are different definitions. Neither `Def7Hist.lean` nor `SizeModCore.lean` contains a comparison theorem between them. The completion commutation $\widetilde{Y_A(S)}=\operatorname{SizeModDefs.Yhat}(\widetilde\Omega_A,\widetilde S)$ also needs a cube/fine NN-component lemma; it is not a definitional equality.

Here is the **weakest direct hypothesis for the G2 cell sums** actually used by the site D1 boundary factor. For every allowed $A$, every aligned active top cell $t$, and the chosen rate $\rho>0$, require
$$\sum_{\substack{S\ni t,\;S\ne\varnothing,\;S\subset\Omega_A^{\rm post}\\Y_A(S)\text{ valid},\ D_c(A,S)<\infty}}
 e^{-\rho D_c(A,S)}\le K_{\rm site}(\rho),\tag{2.2}$$
uniformly in $A$, the torus and $t$. This is `OPEN-P6-SITE-G2-COARSE`; it is exactly the estimate the D1 sum uses, with no unnecessary equality of sizes. A sufficient comparison, suitable for applying landed `SizeModEntropy.G2_S`, is the **one-sided** affine bound
$$D_f(A,S)\le aD_c(A,S)+b,\qquad a>0,\quad b\ge0.\tag{2.3}$$
for every valid $S$, together with the lift/completion correspondence and finiteness of $D_f$. If $\rho/a\ge\operatorname{SizeModEntropy.aEnt}(d,L)+1$, then termwise
$$e^{-\rho D_c}\le e^{\rho b/a}e^{-(\rho/a)D_f},\qquad
K_{\rm site}(\rho)=e^{\rho b/a}\operatorname{SizeModEntropy.K0}(d,L,\rho/a).\tag{2.4}$$
The sum in (2.2) injects into the one in landed `G2_S`, so (2.4) proves (2.2) **conditional on (2.3) and the lift lemma**. For the active-volume estimate, landed `SizeModCore.cellCount_le_of_admissible` and attainment give
$$|S|_{\pi_k}\le c_1(d,L)(1+D_f)\le c_1(d,L)(1+aD_c+b),
\qquad c_1(d,L)=[3(2L-1)]^d.\tag{2.5}$$
This is the direction needed to replace the P6 active-cell count by the coarse size. It is **not** proved from `dCovMod` alone. If one postulates (2.2) directly, (2.5) is unnecessary for the elementary D1 boundary-factor bound, but is needed by source-anchor counting in a P6 transfer proof.

Given a `D7BdrySite` aggregate majorant $\sum_{\ell:S_\ell=S}m_A(\ell)\le B_0e^{-\rho D_c(A,S)}$, (2.2) gives, for each active $t$,
$$\sum_{\ell:t\in S_\ell}m_A(\ell)\le B_0K_{\rm site}(\rho),\qquad
\sum_\ell m_A(\ell)\le B_0|\Omega_A^{\rm post}|_{\pi_k}K_{\rm site}(\rho).\tag{2.6}$$
The second inequality sums over active cells and counts each nonempty support at least once. Therefore $|\operatorname{bdrySumSite}|$ is at most the second right side and its exponential is at most $\exp(B_0|\Omega_A^{\rm post}|_{\pi_k}K_{\rm site})$ on a common H7 domain. The separate `read_pin` majorant gives $B_0K_{\rm read}$ for a fine site in a completed hole or read collar. These are **PROVED conditional on (2.2), D7Site, and common H7 membership**. They bound the boundary factor of D1; `PostConv`/`PreConv` still require integrability of the other factors.

For preservation, the reverse comparison is also needed if the output norm continues to use $D_c$ as in `VMOD-19.md` while `VMOD-18.md` Theorem 2.1 bounds $D_f$:
$$D_c(A,S)\le a_{\rm out}D_f(A,S)+b_{\rm out},\quad
\Delta_f(\ell)\le a_{\rm src}D_c(A,\ell.S_0)+b_{\rm src},\tag{2.7}$$
with positive $a$ and nonnegative $b$, where $\Delta_f$ is the fine modular size of the old source used in Theorem 2.1. The second inequality is a **source-domain** bridge; it does not follow merely from a comparison on completed supports. Equations (2.3) and (2.7), including the cross-lattice lift, are the precise `OPEN-P6-SITE-SIZE-BRIDGE`. No explicit numerical values for $a,b$ have been proved; writing $a=b=1$ would assert an unproved identification of a continuous segment infimum with a discrete graph infimum.

## 3. One-step preservation: exact finite-sum theorem and the missing estimate

Let $A$ be a post-R input history at scale $k$ and $A^+$ the pre-R output history at $K=k+1$. Assume the corrected top domains of §1, the SN–AG–GRID producer hypotheses of `VMOD-18.md` Theorem 2.1 for every nonzero coefficient, and both size bridges (2.7). For an old label $\ell$, put $D_-(\ell)=D_c(A,\ell.S_0)$; for a new support $S$ put $D_+(S)=D_c(A^+,S)$. Theorem 2.1 gives $D_f^+(S_C)\le\Delta_f(\ell)/L+dL|C|$, hence
$$D_+(S_C)\le\alpha D_-(\ell)+\gamma|C|+\delta,\quad
\alpha={a_{\rm out}a_{\rm src}\over L},\quad
\gamma=a_{\rm out}dL,\quad
\delta={a_{\rm out}b_{\rm src}\over L}+b_{\rm out}.\tag{3.1}$$
This is **PROVED conditional on the stated geometric record and open size bridge**. It is the exact size statement needed by the norm calculation; the read collar is a separate label field.

Here is the full transfer statement. Assume input `D7BdrySite` at $k$ with majorants $m_-(\ell)$, constants $(B_0,\kappa,K_{\rm read})$, and $\sum_{\ell:S_\ell=T}m_-(\ell)\le B_0e^{-\kappa D_-(T)}$, $\sum_{\ell:t\in U_\ell}m_-(\ell)\le B_0K_{\rm read}$. For $H\in\{H_-,H_\partial\}$ assume its **strict common H7** Cauchy expansion maps each old label and derivative set $C$ to exactly one new label (or a finite family with its multiplicity included below) with coefficient majorant
$$m_{H}(\ell,C)\le c_H m_-(\ell)e^{-\eta_H|C|},\qquad c_H\ge0,\ \eta_H>0.\tag{3.2}$$
All field/history locality, reality, symmetry, and the strict common H7 membership are the final analytic/geometric assertions `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE` and `OPEN-P6-SITE-MIXED-RESPONSE` specified below. The pure P6 step terms (old action/background and fluctuation terms with no old boundary label) must separately have a site majorant with support constant $B_{\rm st}$ and read constant $B_{\rm st}K_{\rm st}$ at target rate $\rho$. This is an **additional producer bound**, included in `OPEN-P6-SITE-MIXED-RESPONSE`; P6 p. 15 says its traditional boundary prefactor is independent of $B_0$, but does not establish its site aggregate version.

Choose $\rho>0$ with $\sigma:=\kappa-\rho\alpha\ge0$ and $\tau_H:=\eta_H-\rho\gamma\ge0$. Let $\mathcal C_H(\ell,S)$ be the finite set of derivative records of $H$ that produce active support $S$. Define the **two weighted fiber norms**
$$\begin{aligned}
F_H^{\rm supp}&:=\sup_{A^+,S}\ 
 \sum_T e^{-\sigma D_-(T)}
 \sup_{\ell:S_\ell=T}\sum_{C\in\mathcal C_H(\ell,S)}e^{-\tau_H|C|},\\
F_H^{\rm pin}&:=\sup_{A^+,t}\ 
 \sum_T e^{-\kappa D_-(T)}
 \sup_{\ell:S_\ell=T}\sum_{C:\,t\in U_{\ell,C}}e^{-\eta_H|C|}.
\end{aligned}\tag{3.3}$$
The suprema include all admissible old/new history pairs and all allowed source labels; an empty fiber has value zero. The target $U_{\ell,C}$ is the **complete** site plus action/field read collar, not merely its active support. The statement `F_H^{supp},F_H^{pin}<\infty` uniformly in history and horizon is `OPEN-P6-SITE-FIBER`. It is the minimal Schur-type input for arbitrary nonnegative old majorants subject only to the fixed-support norm. One could replace it by a stronger rooted-animal theorem for actual `cubeIdx`; none is landed.

**Finite-sum preservation theorem (PROVED conditional on the named inputs).** Under (3.1)–(3.3), deterministic coefficient sorting, and the pure-step bound, the output majorant satisfies the two quantitative D7Site fields with
$$B_0^+=B_{\rm st}+B_0e^{\rho\delta}\sum_{H\in\{H_-,H_\partial\}}c_HF_H^{\rm supp},\qquad
B_0^+K_{\rm read}^+=B_{\rm st}K_{\rm st}+B_0\sum_Hc_HF_H^{\rm pin}.\tag{3.4}$$
If $B_0^+=0$, take $K_{\rm read}^+=0$; otherwise (3.4) defines it. The output rate is $\rho$, provided $\rho\ge a_{\rm ent}(d,L)+1$ (or the direct site G2 (2.2) holds at $\rho$). Given the stated **post-R input class**, the output is `D7BdrySite` **conditional additionally on** `OPEN-P6-STAR-AE`, `OPEN-P6-SITE-MIXED-RESPONSE`, `OPEN-P6-SITE-SIZE-BRIDGE`, and `OPEN-P6-SITE-FIBER`. The complete induction separately needs `OPEN-R-SITE-NORM` to produce that post-R input class. The two H operators require separate values of $c_H,\eta_H,F_H$.

*Proof of the full combinatorial algebra.* Fix $S$ and expand the new label sum, retaining every old label and every derivative record. Apply (3.2) and then (3.1):
$$\begin{aligned}
e^{\rho D_+(S)}\sum_{\eta:S_\eta=S}m_+(\eta)
&\le B_{\rm st}+e^{\rho\delta}\sum_Hc_H
 \sum_T\sum_{\ell:S_\ell=T}m_-(\ell)e^{\rho\alpha D_-(T)}
 \sum_{C\in\mathcal C_H(\ell,S)}e^{-\tau_H|C|}\\
&\le B_{\rm st}+B_0e^{\rho\delta}\sum_Hc_H
 \sum_Te^{-\sigma D_-(T)}
 \sup_{\ell:S_\ell=T}\sum_{C\in\mathcal C_H(\ell,S)}e^{-\tau_H|C|}\\
&\le B_0^+.
\end{aligned}\tag{3.5}$$
Every interchange is a finite sum on a finite torus. The second line is exactly where the **old aggregate label norm** is used; applying a pointwise bound to each label would not justify it. For fixed fine site $t$, omit the new-size factor and sum only pairs with $t\in U_{\ell,C}$; the same interchange and old fixed-support aggregate gives $B_{\rm st}K_{\rm st}+B_0\sum_Hc_HF_H^{pin}$, the second equation in (3.4). This also sums derivative ledgers, rather than leaving one bound per ledger. Locality, H7 analyticity, and symmetries follow by finite regrouping **once** the two open producer assertions supply them for each coefficient and the common H7 set. This proves the conditional theorem. $\square$

Landed `GraphGmExponent.boundary_gm3_fixed_Y_rate` bounds the sum over $C$ only **after the complete layer-cell output ledger is fixed**. The finite SN–AG–GRID example in `VMOD-19.md` §1 has one old label with $D_-=0$ and $K$ singleton derivative records of equal new support. Choosing synthetic coefficients that attain the permitted per-record majorant makes their aggregate $Kc e^{-\eta}$ for a fixed $c>0$, while (3.1), the old aggregate norm, and each fixed-ledger count hold. In particular, `read_pin` at the old scale cannot control all new read collars without the pin half of (3.3).
