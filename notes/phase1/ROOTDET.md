## 1. Exact objects and the available complex domain

**PROVED.** For the step generating set $G'=S.G'$, take $C=\operatorname{coverOf}(G')$ (`Localization/DecCover.lean`, `coverOf`). Its finite parameter set is $\mathscr P=\operatorname{cubeIdx}(G')$; `Localization/DecoupledAnalytic.lean`, `DecParams`, is $\mathscr P\to\mathbb C$. The extension `extS G' s` equals $s_P$ on $\mathscr P$ and $1$ outside it. For a cover centre $q$, `DecCover.Qset q` contains exactly the indices described by `mem_Qset`; the coefficient of its local Green term is $s_q=\prod_{P\in Qset(q)}s_P$ (`DecCover.sProd`). In particular a cut parameter affects *all* centres whose $Qset$ contains it; a parameter patch is the resulting interaction footprint, not just one cube $P$.

**PROVED (definition at fixed fields); OPEN (physical cut family).** With the unit-lattice matrices $Q,P$ of the complex step, define the joint function $L(s,\phi,\alpha)=\operatorname{decLog}(G',a,a_1,\nu,\alpha,\operatorname{extS}(G',s),\phi,Q,P)$. For a cut-dependent background $(\phi_s(\Theta),\alpha_s(\Theta))$, put
$$L(s;\Theta)=L(s,\phi_s(\Theta),\alpha_s(\Theta)),\qquad F_z(s;\Theta)=-\tfrac12\sum_{y\in B(z)}\sum_{i=1}^N L(s;\Theta)_{(y,i),(y,i)}.\tag{D1}$$
`Localization/DecoupledSqrt.lean`, `decLog`, defines $L$ by a resolvent integral with baseline $\log a_k(1-P)+\log(a_k+a_1/L^2)P$, where $a_k$ is the weight at scale $k$. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON endpoint agreement $\phi_{\mathbf1}=\operatorname{svec}(bgFieldC)$ and $\alpha_{\mathbf1}=bgMultC$:** `Fluctuation/StepComplexOut.lean`, `logAC`, is $L(\mathbf1;\Theta)$, and `halfLogDetCC` is the sum of its block traces. `Localization/DecoupledCovPrincipal.lean`, `decLog_one_eq_logMat` and `decLog_one_principal`, identify the principal logarithm of the physical operator at **$s=\mathbf1$**. **REFUTED:** these declarations identify $L(s)$ as the principal logarithm of a separately landed operator $A(s)$ for every complex $s$; no such all-cut identity is cited or needed for (D1).

**PROVED.** `Localization/DecoupledCovAnalyticFull.lean`, `DecData`, `DecDom`, `isOpen_decDom`, and `analyticOnNhd_decLog_entry`, give joint analyticity of each entry on the open set
$$\mathcal D_{\kappa,\delta}=\{(s,\phi,\alpha): |s_P|<R=e^\kappa\ (P\in\mathscr P),\ \operatorname{IsConstraintFieldStrict}(G',\delta,\phi),\ |\alpha(w)|<\operatorname{coerciveConst}(d)/(2\operatorname{spacing}(w)^2)\}.\tag{D2}$$
The theorem assumes `XSetting`, $\kappa\ge0$, $cstarX(d,L)e^{N0(d,L)\kappa}\le L^{m_1}$, $\delta\ge0$, $\delta^2\operatorname{smallConstX}(d,L,\kappa)\le1/4$, and `IsUnitLattice`. Thus if $\kappa>0$, the closed real cube $[0,1]^{\mathscr P}$ lies strictly inside the $s$-polydisc. Around every point of that cube it contains a polydisc of any common radius $\rho<R-1$; $R-1$ is the *limiting* uniform radius, not an attained closed contour radius. **REFUTED:** the theorem supplies a radius $R>1$ automatically when only $\kappa\ge0$ is assumed.

**PROVED.** The upper bound $cstarX e^{N0\kappa}\le L^{m_1}$ limits $\kappa$ for fixed $m_1$; increasing $m_1$ can permit a larger $\kappa$ when the other inequalities can also be met. The allowed $\delta$ simultaneously shrinks as `smallConstX` grows. `Fluctuation/StepRatesDirect.lean`, `stepRates_direct_witness`, chooses $\kappa_{\rm det}=\kappa'=\tfrac32\kappa_{\rm dec}+\kappa_0+1$, but contains no inequality connecting this $\kappa'$ to the decoupling $\kappa$ of (D2). `Background/ExistenceSetup.lean`, `BgHyp`, specifies dimension, block size, separation, weights and mass, not the needed comparison. **REFUTED:** $\kappa_{\rm det}\ge\kappa'$ follows from either declaration alone.

**PROVED (uniform trace bound).** `Localization/DecoupledSqrt.lean`, `decLog_bound`, applied through `DecoupledCovAnalyticFull.covStanding_of_mem`, yields $\|L(s;\Theta)\|_{2\to2}\le B_{\log}:=\operatorname{covBLog}(d,L,a_k,a_1/L^2,\kappa)$ throughout (D2), for the stated matrix and field hypotheses. Testing on coordinate unit vectors gives
$$|F_z(s;\Theta)|\le M_z:=\tfrac12 N|B(z)|B_{\log}.\tag{D3}$$
This is uniform in the torus size when $|B(z)|$ and the standing constants are fixed; $B_{\log}$ depends on $\kappa$. `StepComplexOutAnalytic.exists_analytic_logAC` proves analyticity of the fully coupled chart $s=\mathbf1$ in $\Theta$, but does not produce the cut-dependent family in (D1). `Localization/Decoupled2Background.lean`, `i4b_analytic`, supplies an analytic decoupled background on its union data domain, as a candidate. **OPEN (DET-CHART):** transfer it to the precise step chart, prove strict (D2) throughout a common cut polydisc, local cut agreement, and endpoint agreement with `logAC`. Holding the *fully coupled* background fixed while cutting only `decLog` leaves nonlocal dependence on $\Theta$ and cannot by itself establish halo locality.

## 2. Cuts, derivatives, and entropy

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON DET-CHART.** For the jointly analytic cut family (D1), multivariate fundamental calculus gives exactly
$$F_z(\mathbf1)=\sum_{J\subseteq\mathscr P}\int_{[0,1]^J}\partial_JF_z(s_J,0_{J^c})\,ds_J.\tag{D4}$$
The $J=\varnothing$ summand is $F_z(0)$. Because the variables in $J$ are distinct, Cauchy on radius $\rho<R-1$ and (D3) give
$$|\partial_J F_z(s_J,0)|\le M_z\rho^{-|J|};\qquad |\partial_J F_z(s_J,0)|\le M_z(R-1)^{-|J|}\quad\hbox{by }\rho\uparrow R-1.\tag{D5}$$
There is no $|J|!$ factor. **REFUTED:** $R^{-|J|}$ follows from a contour centred at $s_J\in[0,1]^J$; the available distance to $|s_P|=R$ is $R-1$.

**PROVED.** `Localization/Decoupled2Geometry.lean`, `Zs`, `Yreg`, `dg5_support`, `dg5_local`, says the *Green operator* $G_s$ is supported in its active core and is local on a nearest-neighbour component of $Yreg(s)$ under `CoverBase`, `HatConn`, admissible potential and invertibility hypotheses. `Localization/Decoupled2BackgroundLocal.lean`, `i4b_local`, and `PairedLocalAgree.lean`, `extS_relParams_zero`, provide related cut/background agreement. The finite graph available for parameter counting is `Localization/MooreDegree.lean`, `mooreGraph`: distinct indices are adjacent when their `Pset m₁` patches Moore-touch. Its degree is at most $b=3(L+4)^d$ under `CubeAligned G' m₁` and $d\le\varrho L^2$ (`mooreGraph_degree_le`).

**OPEN (DET-CUT-FACTOR).** Prove a cut decomposition for the *whole* $L(s)$ of (D1): after setting $J^c$ to zero, identify the nearest-neighbour components of $Yreg(s)$ and the baseline $P,Q$ blocks, show that the resolvent integral and constrained correction have no cross-component matrix elements, and prove that the rooted diagonal trace depends only on its component. `dg5_local` supplies the Green part only. A Moore-connected set of parameter patches is a sufficient combinatorial envelope if each active centre's $Qset$ footprint is included; equality between Moore components and the actual operator blocks has not been landed. Consequently **OPEN** is the vanishing of $\partial_JF_z(s_J,0)$ whenever the parameter footprints in $J$ miss the root component. **REFUTED:** the cited Green locality alone proves block diagonality of $L(s)$ or this mixed-derivative vanishing.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON DET-CUT-FACTOR.** If the component statement holds on a neighbourhood of every cut configuration, varying a parameter whose footprint lies in a different component leaves the root trace constant there; its derivative vanishes. Inducting over those parameters shows that a surviving $J$ has an active footprint connected to $B(z)$, with nearest-neighbour components for the operator and Moore adjacency for the counting envelope. This implication needs the *neighbourhood* statement, since equality at one cut point does not imply derivative vanishing.

**PROVED (what Cauchy can pay).** Suppose, additionally, every surviving $J$ assigned to $T$ satisfies $dExt(T)\le a|J|+b_0$ and the number of connected $J$ of size $n$ for that assignment is at most $C_Ee^{\sigma n}$. Then (D5) sums to a uniform rate no larger than
$$\kappa_{\rm Cauchy}=(\log(R-1)-\sigma)/a,\quad\text{provided }R>2\text{ and }\log(R-1)>\sigma.\tag{D6}$$
Indeed $n\ge(dExt(T)-b_0)/a$ and the geometric tail is bounded by $C_EM_z e^{\kappa_{\rm Cauchy}b_0}(1-e^{-(\log(R-1)-\sigma)})^{-1}e^{-\kappa_{\rm Cauchy}dExt(T)}$. A target $\kappa_{\rm det}\ge\kappa'$ therefore requires $\log(e^\kappa-1)\ge a\kappa'+\sigma$. **OPEN:** neither this parameter inequality nor the requisite footprint-to-$dExt$ comparison is provided by `StepRatesDirect` or `BgHyp`. Even if $R$ is large, (D5) alone has no support localization and cannot be summed by $T$.

**PROVED (available counts, with their actual scopes).** `Polymer/RootedAnimals.lean`, `sum_rooted_le`, bounds $\sum_{C\in rootedSets(N(s))}t^{|C|}$ by $(1-[\max(b+1,b_{root})]^2t)^{-|s|}$ when $\deg\le b$, each root set has size $\le b_{root}$, and $[\max(b+1,b_{root})]^2t<1$. `Localization/LedgerPacking.lean`, `card_idx_in_cells_le`, gives $|idxInCells(G',m_0,R)|\le(L^{m_0})^d|R|$ when $L^{m_0}\mid N_1$. `MooreDegree.mooreGraph_degree_le` supplies $b$ above. **OPEN (DET-HULL-COUNT):** map *each* derivative footprint to a rooted animal and each animal to an admissible $\pi'_K$ domain $T$, with a bounded weighted fibre and a quantitative inequality between animal size and `dExt(T)`. None of these three counting statements alone bounds $\#\{J:T(J)=T\}$.

**OPEN (DET-WALK).** An alternative to a sufficiently large Cauchy radius is a connected resolvent walk/forest bound. The landed Green estimates include spatial decay with `gamma1 d / 8` in `Localization/Decoupled2Field.lean` (`GreenFamily` field); `Background/Calculus.lean` defines $\gamma_1(d)=\operatorname{coerciveConst}(d)/2$. A proof must differentiate the $G_s$ and $\Pi_s$ resolvent products, track each `Qset` interaction and constrained inverse, and show that each interpatch traversal pays $e^{-\mu}$ after all prefactors, with a *specified* $\mu$ (for example derived from $\gamma_1 M_1/8$ less local losses). **REFUTED:** the entrywise Green bound directly gives $e^{-\gamma_1M_1/8}$ per differentiated edge; edge separation and differentiated-resolvent estimates are separate hypotheses. **OPEN:** $\mu-\sigma\ge a\kappa'$ under the landed parameter relations. No random-walk bound with this conclusion was found among the cited declarations.

**PROVED.** `SmallField/Def6Terms.lean`, `IsPolyDom`, uses connected unions of potentially overlapping $\pi'_K$ cubes. A set of fine cubes therefore need not have a unique minimal $\pi'_K$ hull. `Localization/TreeLengthCov` also optimizes over chosen covers. **REFUTED:** there is automatically a unique, paving-equivariant smallest hull. `P3` p. 78, (4.44)–(4.45), explicitly groups many intermediate $Y_1$ under one localization domain $Y$. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON DET-HULL-COUNT:** using all eligible minimal domains with equal weights $1/|\mathcal H(J)|$ is finite and equivariant if $\mathcal H(J)$ is defined by an invariant size/order and is nonempty; summing these weights returns each derivative once. Its weighted entropy is part of DET-HULL-COUNT. **OPEN:** construct this family with `IsPolyDom`, bounded halo and the required cost. The $J=\varnothing$ term must be assigned to a nonempty rooted domain. At $s=0$, the covariance correction vanishes if every centre has a parameter in its `Qset`; the residual $P$ baseline still requires a local block calculation. Assigning $F_z(0)$ to $T=\varnothing$ fails rooted support.

## 3. An exact conditional replacement theorem

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON DET-CHART, DET-CUT-FACTOR, DET-HULL-COUNT, DET-FOREST, DET-ROOT and DET-COV.** The following is a sufficient replacement for `StepComplexThm31.nonempty_detExpansionC`, with all missing inputs exposed. Assume the chart and cut statements above; `DET-ROOT` supplies a fine root cube $b_z$ and a nonempty admissible hull for $J=\varnothing$; `DET-COV` gives paving covariance of the whole cut family and its hull weights. `DET-FOREST` supplies, for each cut derivative in (D4), a finite or absolutely summable forest decomposition into jointly holomorphic contributions $w$ with: exact reconstruction; a rooted footprint; dependence only on the field and potential in a halo $H_h(X_T)$ of a **fixed** radius $h$; and $|w|\le M_z e^{-\mu n(w)}$. Assume also that the weighted number of all contributions with a given $T$ and length $n$ is at most $C_Ee^{\sigma n}$, and $dExt(T)\le an+b_0$ for every contribution, with $a>0$, $\lambda:=\mu-\sigma>0$. The constants $a,b_0,C_E,\mu,\sigma,h$ are independent of $z,\Theta$ and torus size. Require $\lambda/a\ge\kappa'$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON those six inputs.** Assign each forest contribution to its weighted admissible hull and define $d_z(T;\Theta)$ by summing the assigned contributions and integrating the corresponding cut variables. Then:

1. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the six inputs (i).** $\sum_Td_z(T;\Theta)=F_z(\mathbf1;\Theta)=-\frac12\sum_{y\in B(z),i}\operatorname{logAC}_\Theta(y,i;y,i)$ by (D4), forest reconstruction and unit-sum hull weights. Summing over $z$ gives `halfLogDetCC` by `StepComplexOut.sum_blockU_traceC`.
2. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the six inputs (ii).** $d_z(T)=0$ if $T=\varnothing$, $b_z\notin T$, or $d_\Omega(X_T)=+\infty$, because every assigned hull is an admissible rooted domain. For active $T$, `ROOT.md` (R1) gives $dExt(T)=sizeJ(X_T)$.
3. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the six inputs (iii).** Each term is holomorphic in $\Theta$ on the common **open** chart, depends only on $H_h(X_T)$, and obeys paving covariance. For finite sums this is immediate; for infinite forest sums the assumed absolute bound is locally uniform and gives holomorphy by the Weierstrass theorem.
4. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the six inputs (iv).** The geometric tail gives
$$|d_z(T;\Theta)|\le c_{\rm det}e^{-\kappa_{\rm det}dExt(T)},\quad \kappa_{\rm det}=\lambda/a\ge\kappa',\quad c_{\rm det}=\frac{C_E M_z e^{\kappa_{\rm det}b_0}}{1-e^{-\lambda}}.\tag{D7}$$
The formula follows by summing $n\ge(dExt(T)-b_0)/a$ and $\sum_{n\ge x}e^{-\lambda n}\le e^{-\lambda x}/(1-e^{-\lambda})$. Inserting this family into `DetExpansionC` supplies its `term`, `sum_eq` and `bound` fields, plus the stronger rooted, analytic and local properties. **OPEN:** the six inputs are not collectively proved; in particular DET-FOREST and DET-HULL-COUNT are the substantive missing estimate. `nonempty_detExpansionC` instead assigns the whole trace to $T=\varnothing$ with a torus-dependent prefactor.
