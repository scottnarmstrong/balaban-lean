## 1. Why P5 completes every hole (question 1)
**SOURCE (P5 p. 493).** "All elements of the multi-index A_k are determined completely by their restrictions to the large field region Z_k ... we may localize A_k to components of Z_k". In (1.21) the operator T_k(Z, A_k cap Z) integrates psi_k over a whole component Z, and B^(k)(X, A_k) depends on A_k. So a term meeting a component Z depends on A_k cap Z for the whole Z. (2.47)-(2.48) keep the hole spins of A_k on X cap Omega_{k+1}^c as arguments of B''.
**PROVED (from (1.26)-(1.29)).** The operator bound is a supremum bound over F (chi-domains of (1.24)-(1.25)); it asks nothing about which part of Z the function F reads. So the field dependence of B does not force completion; the dependence on the history A_k cap Z does.
**PROVED (landed).** `D7Bdry` already separates the two: `loc_field` (fields on the fine set of X) and `loc_hist` (history through the whole components meeting X). Plain domains with whole-component history are therefore representable in the landed class; only `FineValid`/`DjMod` and the size would change.
**OPEN (SOURCE-silent).** Whether the analytic spaces (1.36)-(1.37), written for X cap Omega with the components cut out, and the sum (1.35) are meant to need X to contain Z. P5 (1.33) gives no reason. YM: CMP119 (2.41) (account I:12270) has X cap Z_j~ nonempty with X a plain domain; CMP122b (1.67) completes by whole components of Z but measures d_{k,Z}(Y_4) <= d_k(lab_4) for the plain label (I:64119-64137, 71860-71870, 76880-76893). That is completion of the domain with the size of the plain label, which is not P5 (1.34).

## 2. Does the core reading of blocker 4 reduce blocker 3 (question 2)
**PROVED (landed).** `dExt_core_eq_d7SizeAt`: dExt T = d7SizeAt X when X_f = Yhat(sitesOf T), site-connected. `dOmega S = dcovModE (Yhat S)` and `ModAdmissible` takes a tree Gamma inside Yhat(S), every bond at weight `scaleW` (also inside holes, VMOD-42 section 2). So d7Size does NOT count a hole as one jump. It charges (i) the active cells to visit and (ii) the shortest tree between them, through the hole if that is the shortest route. A hole with one attachment costs nothing; two attachments joined only through it cost the length. VMOD-43 section 3 says the same for a single label.
**PROVED (what blocker 4 removed).** Only the linking of labels in the gas: `dExt_unionCs_le_of_connSet` needs contact in an active cell, so two labels overlapping only in a retained hole are not linked. The size of each label is unchanged. Hence blocker 3, a statement about the size of ONE output label Y against the input size, is not reduced. REFUTED: "the core size never charges a retained hole" as a reading of dExt of a disconnected core; it charges the tree.
**What is unpaid in VMOD-42 (two collars) and VMOD-36/LFROOT3 (one collar).** VMOD-42: tree length between the collars, legitimately in D_- and D_+ for a union, not for single labels; unpaid only for the pair X_-, X_+, which blocker 4 now unlinks. VMOD-36 section 2 / LFROOT3 Q2: a single label with D_- = O(1) gets D_+(Y) >= c n from "n/L active cells along a misaligned long face" (G1_cellCount), not from the tree through the hole.
**OPEN, doubt on the second mechanism (refute-first).** `IsPiPrimeUnion` takes boxes of side L cells aligned to the CELL lattice (`Aligned (cellSide)`), overlapping at every cell offset. A union of cells such that each cell lies in an L-box inside the union is itself a union of boxes. Holes of RegionStep are neighbourhoods of radius >= rho+4 >= L of coarse sets (Omega'_K = Op_{rho+2}(nbhd_3 U'_k), VMOD-36 (1)), so each hole cell lies in such a box. Then Y = (boxes of X) union (hole) has Y cap Omega_K inside the boxes of X cap Omega_K, bounded, and no Omega(n) spill. VMOD-36 needs the rigid L-block grid (positions multiple of L cells) to cut the face; it states medium confidence "until the grid details are Lean packaged". I did not re-derive its example; this is a reading of the landed definitions, to be settled by leaf 0 below.
