## Reasoning per item

### 1. Proofs claimed complete

**Lemma 4.1 (block Poincaré).** Re-derived. In one dimension $\sum_i|u_i-\bar u|^2=\frac1n\sum_{i<i'}|u_i-u_{i'}|^2$, Cauchy-Schwarz gives $(i'-i)\sum|g_m|^2$, and for fixed $m$ the rectangle sum of $(i'-i)$ is $\frac n2ab$ with $a+b=n$, so the bound is $\frac{n^2}8$. The $d$-dimensional telescoping $u-P_Bu=\sum_\mu(E_{<\mu}-E_{\le\mu})u$ is orthogonal, each term is a one-dimensional variance of $E_{<\mu}u$, and $\|\partial_\mu E_{<\mu}u\|\le\|\partial_\mu u\|$ by Jensen. Correct, constant $\ell^2/8$ uniform. (The sharp constant $1/\pi^2$ is consistent.)

**Lemma 4.2.** Correct. $\eta^2L^k=\eta$ uses $L^k\eta=1$, and each bond is counted $L^k$ times, matching the weights $1$ and $\eta^d=L^{-kd}$.

**G-1(a).** Re-derived: $\|\partial\tilde u\|^2\ge\frac12\|\partial\tilde u\|^2+\frac12\sum_B\|\partial u\|_B^2$, then $\min(4,a_B)\ge\frac12$ and $\gamma_1=\frac14$ give $\frac12\|\partial\tilde u\|^2+\frac14\|\ell^{-1}u\|^2$. Uses only $a_j\in[\frac12,2]$, which follows from (1.4) ($\frac{1-L^{-2k}}{1-L^{-2j}}\in[1,\frac98]$, $L\ge3$; formula checked against P2 p. 608 (1.4)). Complex $\alpha$ is handled by taking real parts. Uniform in $\nu\ge0$, $k$, $\eta$, volume, layers, $\alpha$. Correct. The consequences $\|\ell^{-1}u\|\le4\|\ell f\|$, $\|\partial\tilde u\|^2\le8\|\ell f\|^2$ are correct.

**G-1(a$'$).** The algebra of $S_x$ is correct and agrees with P3 p. 62 (3.8) and the text after it (the doc's $\frac23$ vs P3's $\frac34$ is only a weaker constant; $s_2\ge\frac23aL^{-2}$ is true for $x\le a_k/8$, $a\le\frac32$, $a_k>\frac12$ with a margin of about $3\%$). Coercivity is proved for the stated hypotheses. The uniform-in-$x$ coercivity is fine; the extension to the Agmon estimate is not (F-04).

**G-1(b), explicit $\gamma_0=\frac1{2(8d+5)}$.** Re-derived in full. (2) Allocation: layers $j<k$ give $\ge\frac7{16}L^2\|\phi\|_B^2\ge3\|\phi\|^2$ ($\ell_j\le L^{-1}$, $L\ge3$); the unit blocks give $4\|\phi-P_B\phi\|^2$, absorbing $\theta_0$-terms since $4\theta_0\le\frac14\le a_k/2$. (3) $\bar\psi=Q_k\tilde\phi$: unit blocks are either inside $\Omega_k$ or disjoint from it (since $\Omega_k$ is a union of $k$-blocks), so the comparison $\tfrac12a_k\|\psi-Q_k\phi\|^2+3\|\phi\|^2_{\Omega_1\setminus\Omega_k}\ge\frac14\|\psi-\bar\psi\|^2$ is valid. (4) $\min_{\bar\psi}[\frac14\|\psi-\bar\psi\|^2+\langle\bar\psi,T\bar\psi\rangle]=\langle\psi,T(1+4T)^{-1}\psi\rangle$ (checked by differentiation), $T\le2d+1$ needs $\nu\le1$ (in (A)), so $T(1+4T)^{-1}\ge T/(8d+5)\ge\frac{1}{2(8d+5)}(-\Delta^1+\nu)$. All constants depend only on $d$ and are uniform in $k,\eta,L$ (beyond $L\ge3$), volume, $x$, generating set, $\nu\in[0,1]$. Correct. Subsidiary items (1)-(3) check out (the arithmetic $8\gamma_0<\frac12<a$, the bound $\min(8,a)L^{-2}\ge\frac12L^{-2}$). Issues: F-05 (units), F-10 (domain of $\psi$, Poincaré constant).
