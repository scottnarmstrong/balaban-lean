# The birth reference jets of the new RG terms

## Answer

The requested certificate is **false term by term for the separately specified switch contribution at $k_0$**. P6 p. 11 explicitly puts the change from a field-free to a complete background into the new $\mathscr E_0^{(k+1)}$. For one constrained sphere block that change has transverse-source Hessian $\beta/(a+m)$ at the aligned reference point. At the switch $\beta m\asymp1$, this diverges as $m\downarrow0$, whereas `PIN-I6.md` §3 requires a mass-independent $O(E_0/r_j^2)$ Hessian. Its value on a *physical pinned* constant-spin path is $\asymp m\varepsilon_k^2$, and hence violates the stated `PinJet.potential` and I-6 rate for that summand. This is the physical realization, up to a bounded coefficient and higher-order terms, of the one-site obstruction in `PIN-I6.md` §2. A cancellation with the fluctuation free energy in the grouped new coefficient is possible; neither the counterexample nor the landed identities rule it out.

The ordinary complete-background fluctuation terms of FLUCTUATION Theorem 3.1(a)–(e) are separate from this switch addition. The landed `Step*.lean` files define their global and local expressions and prove an interpolation identity, but do not prove their localized complex source derivatives or the reference-jet certificate. Theorem 3.1(a)–(e) itself is marked conditional on open localization and class-to-domain assertions in FLUCTUATION §3.1. There is therefore no proof of the requested unconditional certificate for those terms either. The weakest repair of the termwise assertion is to require PinJet only after grouping the switch with the fluctuation source free energy and proving cancellation of their large transverse jet. An alternative is to extract and track that jet explicitly. Merely narrowing the physical $h$ chart makes the value small; it does not change the Hessian at the reference point.

## 1. What the cited construction actually produces

At a regular step, `Balaban/Fluctuation/StepObjects.lean` defines `E1` by the $s$-interpolated expectation, `E0 = halfLogDetC + E1`, `F0` by the $t$-interpolated observable expectation, and `M0` as its $g$ coefficient (lines 418–441). The local energy `E0loc` is $-\frac12\sum_{y\in B(z)}\operatorname{tr}\mathbb L(y,y)+E1loc(z)$ (lines 464–484), corresponding to P4 (2.56) and FLUCTUATION Theorem 3.1(a). `F0` and `M0` are observable terms; the $g$-analytic H7 bound is their direct obligation, and an energy PinJet condition applies only to an energy coefficient extracted from them. Theorem 3.1(c)–(e) treats boundary and large-field remainder terms, not ordinary H4 birth terms. Its bounds do not themselves give source jets.

`StepIdentityReg.lean:ClassRegular` (lines 413–420) is $C^1$ regularity of old energy and observable exponents **along the real fluctuation ray**. `StepNonvanishing` (`StepIdentity.lean:902–905`) is nonzero partition functions on two interpolation segments. `prop26_complex_class` (`StepIdentityReg.lean:526–535`) yields the exact exponent identity under these premises. `StepIdentity.lean` and `StepIdentityQuad.lean` supply algebraic/variational identities; `StepMeasure.lean` supplies normalized real measures. None states a bound on $\partial^2_{b_\perp}E0loc$, a constant-potential bound, a polymerwise PinJet estimate, or the required complex $b$-domain and half-margin. In particular the global `halfLogDetC` is written with a **real** logarithm of the determinant's real part; the local complex log trace is the relevant analytic form. The real identity does not by itself extend the global expression holomorphically.

At $k=k_0$, the regular-step definitions do not encode both background conventions: `StepSetting` has one mass `S.ν` in its background and covariance data. The additional two-stage term is documented in P6 p. 11, after (2.25): first include the external field in the variational background problem without renormalization, add $-\beta_{k+1,u}C^{(k+1)}$ to $\mathscr E_0^{(k+1)}$, and then perform the ordinary complete-background renormalization. P6 (2.24) contains the source $\nu_{k+1}(\phi_{k+1}-h)$; P6 (2.27)–(2.28) includes this contribution in the new localized energy. FLUCTUATION Lemma 2.9(a)–(b) gives its exact chord identity, and §5.2 Step 5 explicitly adds the switch and old-term re-expression to $\mathscr E_0,\mathscr F_0$ at $k_0$. The claim that the scalar cost is $O(\beta^{-1})$ there is for bounded real $h$; it does not assert a transverse-source jet bound uniform in $m$.

## 2. Exact failing new energy term

Let $N\ge2$, $e=e_1$, $a\in[9/10,3/2]$, $m>0$, and $b=mh$. For one unit block spin $p$ and a unit constrained field $\phi$, consider the actual form of the local complete action
$$A_m(p,\phi)=\frac a2|p-\phi|^2+\frac m2|\phi-h|^2,\qquad |p|=|\phi|=1.\tag{2.1}$$
The field-free critical background is $\phi^0=p$. The complete one is $\phi^m=(ap+b)/|ap+b|$ for real data near $(p,b)=(e,me)$. The background-change contribution, with the sign convention of the energy exponent suppressed, is
$$D_m(p,b):=\beta\{A_m(p,\phi^0)-A_m(p,\phi^m)\}
=\beta\bigl(\sqrt{a^2+2a(p\cdot b)+b\cdot b}-a-p\cdot b\bigr).\tag{2.2}$$
Here dot products and the square root use their local holomorphic complexifications. This is exactly the one-block chord of FLUCTUATION Lemma 2.9 and the $-\beta C$ addition in P6's two-stage description, up to the overall sign. It is $O(N)$-invariant under simultaneous rotation of $(p,b)$ and analytic near the aligned real reference for every $m>0$. The calculation treats that summand separately; a coefficient formed by adding the fluctuation logarithm may have a smaller jet.

Put $p=e$ and $b=me+t e_2$. Then
$$D_m(e,me+t e_2)=\beta\bigl(\sqrt{(a+m)^2+t^2}-(a+m)\bigr),\qquad
\partial_t^2D_m(e,me)=\frac\beta{a+m}.\tag{2.3}$$
For $m\le1/10$, $a+m\le8/5$, so $|\partial_t^2D_m|\ge5\beta/8$. The birth certificate `PIN-I6.md` §3 item 1 bounds the corresponding tensor by $4M_X/r_j^2$ with $M_X=E_0e^{-\kappa d_j(X)}$ and $r_j=\varepsilon_j/K_r$. At a fixed birth level with $r_j,E_0$ fixed, take $\beta m=\Theta\in[1,L^d]$ and $\beta\to\infty$. The certificate fails whenever $\beta>32M_X/(5r_j^2)$. No choice of a smaller chart in $h$ changes the derivative (2.3).

The failure is also visible without differentiating off the physical pin. Fix $h=e$, $b=me$, and $p_\vartheta=\cos\vartheta\,e+\sin\vartheta\,e_2$. Rationalizing (2.2) gives
$$D_m(p_\vartheta,me)
=\frac{\beta m^2\sin^2\vartheta}
{\sqrt{a^2+2am\cos\vartheta+m^2}+a+m\cos\vartheta}.\tag{2.4}$$
For $0<m\le1/10$ and $|\vartheta|\le1/4$, the denominator is at most $16/5$ and $\sin^2\vartheta\ge\vartheta^2/4$, so
$$D_m(p_\vartheta,me)\ge\frac5{64}\beta m^2\vartheta^2
\ge\frac5{64}m\vartheta^2\qquad(\beta m\ge1).\tag{2.5}$$
The numerical constant is deliberately conservative. On every aligned axial germ $p=Ue,b=Ve$, $D_m=0$ as long as $aU+V$ stays on the positive branch. Thus its value and first radial-spin and longitudinal-source reference jets vanish. For a one-site term the spatial second moment is zero because both differentiated spin variables are at the same site. Consequently the four renormalization conditions do not remove this contribution: `Ttw`, `Ax`, and `Phitw` of `PIN-I6.md` §1 all vanish on the constant path, leaving `Itw=D_m`.

To compare with the birth estimate, let $s=L^{j-k}$, $m=c_*s^2$ with fixed $0<c_*\le1$, $\beta=\Theta/m$, and set $\vartheta=\varepsilon_k/2$; choose $k-j$ large enough that the pinned data and the numerical `DataHypAniso` conditions in `PIN-I6.md` §2 hold. Equation (2.5) gives $|\texttt{Itw}|\ge(5c_*/256)s^2\varepsilon_k^2$. The asserted I-6 estimate would give
$$C E_0s^{3-\alpha_1}(\varepsilon_k/\varepsilon_j)^2.\tag{2.6}$$
Their ratio is at least $5c_*\varepsilon_j^2(256CE_0)^{-1}s^{-(1-\alpha_1)}$, which diverges for $0<\alpha_1<1$. The one-site term also violates `PinJet.potential`, whose proposed right side is $12M_X Z^2s^4(\varepsilon_k/r_j)^2$. The comparison remains valid with a fixed admissible polymer containing the site, after multiplying both sides by its fixed weight and averaging its finite symmetry orbit. This is a failure for the separately localized switch action term, not a claim that a grouped final coefficient or a generic regular-step determinant or observable coefficient has the same singularity.

## 3. Smallest repair of this obstruction

Any sufficient termwise replacement retaining the I-6 rate must remove the large transverse quadratic source jet before treating the switch as an ordinary $E_0$-bounded term. The least change to the grouping is to combine $-D_m$ with the raw fluctuation free energy and prove that their second source jets cancel to the required bound, together with a uniform physical-path remainder. A bare relaxation to $|\partial^2_{b_\perp}f|\lesssim\beta$ or to $|f|\lesssim m\varepsilon_k^2$ admits (2.4) and loses the $s^{3-\alpha_1}$ gain. A pinned $h$ domain changes where values are estimated, but the reference Hessian remains (2.3).

At one unit block the minimal invariant jet to extract is
$$J_m(p,b)=\frac\beta{2(a+m)}\left(b\cdot b-\frac{(p\cdot b)^2}{p\cdot p}\right),\qquad p\cdot p\approx1.\tag{3.1}$$
It is analytic near $p=e$, is $O(N)$-invariant, vanishes on the axial germ, and has exactly the Hessian (2.3). For $p=p_\vartheta$, $b=me$, $0<m\le1/10$, $|\vartheta|\le1/4$, and $\beta m\le L^d$, direct rationalization and $|1-\cos\vartheta|\le\vartheta^2/2$ give
$$|D_m(p_\vartheta,me)-J_m(p_\vartheta,me)|
\le 2\beta m^3\vartheta^4\le2L^d m^2\vartheta^4.\tag{3.2}$$
Indeed, write $A=a+m\cos\vartheta$, $q^2=m^2\sin^2\vartheta$, $R=\sqrt{A^2+q^2}$; then $D_m=\beta q^2/(R+A)$ and $|R+A-2(a+m)|\le q^2/(R+A)+2m(1-\cos\vartheta)\le C m\vartheta^2$, while both denominators are bounded below uniformly for $a\ge9/10$. The stated constant 2 follows from $A\ge9/10-(1/10)(1-\cos(1/4))>0.89$ and the displayed bounds. With $m=c_*s^2$, the right side is $O(s^4\varepsilon_k^4)$, which has the desired I-6 power on this one-block constant path because $0<\varepsilon_k\le1$ and $0<s\le1$.

Thus, if cancellation in the grouped coefficient is unavailable, the **smallest jet-level extraction** is an explicitly tracked covariant angular source counterterm whose transverse quadratic reference jet equals (3.1), with PinJet required only of the remainder. Tracking the full $D_m$ is a simpler sufficient alternative. In the physical chart $b=mh$ the extracted term has coefficient $\beta m^2/(2(a+m))$ multiplying $1-(p\cdot h)^2$; it is an actual new running source interaction and needs its own composition, localization, and RG equations. One may try to absorb its leading $1-p\cdot h$ part into the mass coupling, but that requires a proved decomposition of the remaining angular term and its scale sum. Equation (3.2) proves sufficiency only for the displayed one-block path, not for the full multiscale class. Any full repair must prove either polymerwise jet cancellation in the grouped coefficient or analogues of (3.2), plus source/observable compatibility and transport.

For ordinary complete-background birth terms away from the switch, a conditional route is available: prove the localized Theorem 3.1(a) energy bound on an anisotropic source tube of radius $r_j=\varepsilon_j/K_r$ with the actual physical mass in `covA`, and a half-margin for every localized cluster coefficient. Its Cauchy estimates then give $|\partial_{b_\perp}^2 f_X|\le4M_X/r_j^2$ with $M_X=(NL^dB_{\mathbb L}+1)K_0e^{-\kappa_a d(X)}$; the same wide-tube hypothesis yields `PinJet.small`, `.potential`, and `.tail` with the constants in `PIN-I6.md` §5. The $g$ derivatives of `F0` and `M0` instead use the H7 coefficient bound of Theorem 3.1(b), and (c)–(e) require their own boundary/remainder bounds. This is a precise reduction to the analytic localization and domain assertions of FLUCTUATION Theorem 3.1 and `PIN-I6.md`, not a theorem currently supplied by `Step*.lean`. In particular it cannot include $D_m$ under the same $M_X$ by (2.3).

A complete polymerwise reference-jet theorem for the ordinary new terms, cancellation in the grouped switch coefficient, and a modified RG class if extraction is needed remain open.
