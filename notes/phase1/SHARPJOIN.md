## T3. From labels to a coefficient

**PROVED (finite-support logic, conditional application).** In `gsumCoeff`/the observable coefficient constructions, a nonzero term at cube set `T` can use an activity label or mark label only when its block support lies in `T`. For a label domain `Y` that is a union of the corresponding cubes, `blocksOf Y ⊆ T` implies `Y ⊆ sitesOf T`; at full level `Yhat (sitesOf T)=sitesOf T`. Bridge labels are unions containing their constituent labels (`subset_bridge`); root unions and the observable kernel's row labels are counted through their own block supports. None authorizes a contribution from a label outside `sitesOf T`. This support statement must be used for **each** activity, corrected mark, kernel row, and remainder term, not assumed merely from the name `Y`.

**OPEN (coefficient theorem).** Exact `c θ T = c θ′ T` from agreement of datum and source on `sitesOf T` follows once (i) every physical activity label, corrected bridge mark, observable kernel row, and remainder label is sharp on its **own** `Y`, (ii) the existing support/block-dependence lemmas place each such `Y` inside `sitesOf T`, and (iii) the parameter restriction translates that site agreement to the raw/source hypotheses. The finite coefficient sum then uses equality term by term; no new geometric completion or extra rate is needed. This theorem is not a consequence of the presently landed thick `e1CoeffFamily` clause (ii).
