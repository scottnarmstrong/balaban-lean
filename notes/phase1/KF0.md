# Round 36 — the one-scale high-mass branch

**PROVED (scope and status).** This is a source-to-Lean proof plan for $d\ge3$, $N\ge2$, $L\ge3$, $s=ML$, $\nu_*:=7/(8L^2)\le\nu\le1$, unit $h$, and $\beta$ large. It uses LF-BASE-KF0.md Parts B,F,G, LF-BASE-KF0-H.md Parts H.1–H.4, FINAL.md, BASE.md and the checked declarations below. The complete one-scale theorem is OPEN: the complete heavy-object factorization, its support-size activity bound, and the exact common-reference gas identity have no producer.

## The statement and frozen-root bridge

**OPEN — KF0OneScaleInput (exact target).** Fix $\delta_{\rm root}=1/8$ before $\kappa,c_9>0$; then choose $L\ge3$, $M\ge1$, $s=ML$, and $\beta_0$, independent of $\nu\in[\nu_*,1]$, unit $h$, and torus side $\ell$ divisible by $s$. For every $\beta\ge\beta_0$ and such $\ell$, provide $p_\ell(X;g)$ for every connected union $X$ of complete $s$-blocks, extended by zero to other finite sets, with $U_\ell:=\{g:\sum_{x,i}|g_i(x)|<1\}$.

**OPEN (the four target clauses).** Each $p_\ell(X;\cdot)$ is analytic on $U_\ell$, depends only on $g|_X$, and obeys $|p_\ell(X;g)|\le B_\beta e^{-\kappa |X|/s^d}$ there, with $B_\beta\to0$ uniformly in $\nu,h,\ell$. For every source in $U_\ell$, require
$$Z_\ell(g)/Z_\ell(0)=\exp\{\langle g,h\rangle+\sum_X[p_\ell(X;g)-p_\ell(X;0)]\}.\tag{K0.1}$$
**OPEN (exact lift clause).** The coefficients, including zero extensions and polymer membership, must agree exactly under the quarter-box nonwinding embeddings in Frozen/Roots/LogPartitionMultiscale.lean; exponential decay alone is insufficient.

**OPEN (rate).** LF-BASE-KF0.md (F.13) proves for the restricted all-good sector an ordinary pre-Mayer weighted sup-norm scale $C_{\rm ord}[\log(\beta a)/\sqrt\beta+\log^2(\beta a)/\beta+\log^4(\beta a)/\beta]$, with $a>1$ fixed. A possible full $B_\beta$ adds a heavy contribution $C_{\rm H}e^{-p_{\rm H}(\beta)}$ with $p_{\rm H}(\beta)\gtrsim\log^2\beta$; neither that rate nor the constants $C_{\rm H},p_{\rm H}$ are proved for the complete gas. The exact target only needs $B_\beta\to0$ and, after $\beta_0$ is chosen, $4\sqrt N B_\beta\le c_9L^{-((d-2)/2+\delta_{\rm root})}$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON KF0OneScaleInput (Cauchy construction).** Set $T_\ell(j,x,X;g)=0$ unless $j=1$, $x\in X$ and $X$ is an $s$-block polymer; otherwise set $(T_\ell(1,x,X;g))_i=\int_0^1\partial_{g_i(x)}p_\ell(X;t g)\,dt$. The fundamental theorem of calculus gives $p_X(g)-p_X(0)=\sum_{x\in X,i}g_i(x)T_1(x,X;g)_i$. A coordinate circle of radius $1/4$ around $tg$ stays in $U_\ell$ when $\|g\|_1\le1/2$, giving $\|T_1\|\le4\sqrt N B_\beta e^{-\kappa |X|/s^d}$; locality, analyticity, support and exact lift pass through differentiation and integration (LF-BASE-KF0.md §B.4).

**PROVED (principal logarithm).** For $\|g\|_1<1$, the positive normalized Gibbs measure at $g=0$ gives $\operatorname{Re}(Z(g)/Z(0))\ge e^{-1}\cos1>0$. Thus the principal logarithm is analytic on the whole source ball; (K0.1) and equality at $g=0$ make it $\langle g,h\rangle+\sum_X[p_X(g)-p_X(0)]$, with no branch ambiguity.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON KF0OneScaleInput (partial quantifier statement).** The separate, non-frozen theorem KF0RootFPartial has the frozen order $\exists\delta_{\rm root}>0\;\forall\kappa,c_9>0\;\exists L\ge3,M\ge1,\beta_0\;\forall\beta\ge\beta_0\;\forall\nu\in[\nu_*(L),1]$, unit $h$, admissible $\ell$, and then all frozen clauses with witness $k_f=0$. Indeed $\sqrt\nu\le1\le L$ and $L\sqrt\nu\ge\sqrt{7/8}>1/2$. This includes the strict high-mass branch $\nu>\nu_*$, but does not prove frozen Root-F for $0<\nu<\nu_*$; that range needs the ordinary flow and a possibly positive $k_f$.

## Exact good/bad decomposition and crossing bonds

**PROVED (chosen physical test).** Partition the torus into disjoint $s$-blocks $Q$. Fix $a>1$, $A>0$, $r_\beta=A\log(\beta a)/\sqrt\beta$, and let $\chi_Q(\phi)=\mathbf1\{\forall x\in Q,\ |\phi_x-h|<r_\beta/\sqrt\nu\}$. A block is bad if $\chi_Q=0$; no separate gradient test is needed: on two good endpoints a bond difference is at most $2r_\beta/\sqrt{\nu_*}$. For $\beta$ large enough that $r_\beta/\sqrt{\nu_*}<1/4$, every good spin lies in the northern hemisphere chart. Bonds touching bad blocks remain in the action.

**PROVED (exact sector identity).** For a block set $B$, let $D_B$ be its site union and $G_B=\Lambda\setminus D_B$; write $q=\phi|_{D_B}$. Let $H_B(q)$ be the mass on $D_B$ plus bonds with both endpoints there. Let $H_G(\phi_G\mid q)$ be the mass on $G_B$, its internal bonds, and every bond crossing $G_B,D_B$. With $I_G(q,g):=\int e^{-\beta H_G(\phi_G\mid q)+\langle g_G,\phi_G\rangle}\prod_{Q\notin B}\chi_Q(\phi_G)\,d\sigma^{G_B}$, the identity is
$$Z_\Lambda(g)=\sum_{B\subset\mathcal Q_s}\int e^{-\beta H_B(q)+\langle g_B,q\rangle}\prod_{Q\in B}(1-\chi_Q(q))\,I_{G_B}(q,g)\,d\sigma^{D_B}(q).\tag{K0.2}$$
**PROVED (why (K0.2) is exact).** This is the product expansion of $1=\chi_Q+(1-\chi_Q)$; since each $\chi_Q$ reads only its own sites, all indicators are assigned exactly once. Empty $B$ and empty $G_B$ are included.

**PROVED (the boundary activity chosen).** For $w_x=\phi_x-h$ on $G=G_B$ and exterior $q_y$, LF-BASE-KF0.md (B.1) gives
$$H_G=\tfrac12\!\sum_{\{x,z\}\subset G,x\sim z}|w_x-w_z|^2+\tfrac12\!\sum_{x\in G,y\in D_B,x\sim y}|w_x|^2+\tfrac\nu2\sum_{x\in G}|w_x|^2+\sum_{\partial(G,D_B)}(h-q_y)\cdot w_x+\tfrac12\sum_{\partial(G,D_B)}|h-q_y|^2.\tag{K0.3}$$
**OPEN (chosen boundary-activity construction).** The exact sums are retained as part of the bad object's boundary weight, with both endpoints in its declared support; its normalized activity bound has yet to be proved. In particular, the tangential force $-q_y^\perp\cdot w_x$ cannot be treated as a small ordinary interaction at arbitrary bad boundary data; no conditional minimizer is asserted.

**PROVED (raw Peierls charge).** Every bad $Q$ contains a distinct site with $\nu|\phi_x-h|^2\ge r_\beta^2$. Since all terms of $H_0$ are nonnegative, $H_0(\phi)\ge r_\beta^2|B|/2$ and
$$e^{-\beta H_0(\phi)}\le e^{-\beta H_0(\phi)/2}\exp[-A^2\log^2(\beta a)|B|/4].\tag{K0.4}$$
**PROVED (charge accounting).** This charges the bad blocks with their own mass terms, uniformly in $\nu\ge\nu_*$; a bond witness is unnecessary for this particular test.

**REFUTED (separate crossing-bond penalty).** Bounding $e^{-\beta|h+w_x-q_y|^2/2}$ by $e^{2\beta}$ after expanding (K0.3) would overwhelm (K0.4). The valid inequality is $e^{-\beta|h+w_x-q_y|^2/2}\le1$ for each real crossing bond, while (K0.4) keeps the *whole* nonnegative energy in the residual integral. No crossing factor may be discarded in the exact identity (K0.2). The passage from that residual integral to a normalized heavy activity remains OPEN.

## Chart, localization, and the open heavy theorem

**PROVED (conditional Dirichlet chart formula).** On $G$, put $K_G=-\Delta_G^{D}+\nu$, so $\langle w,K_Gw\rangle$ contains internal good bonds, one $|w_x|^2$ for every crossing bond, and the good-site mass. Write $\phi_x=h+\beta^{-1/2}(v_x+z_\beta(v_x)h)$ with $v_x\perp h$, $z_\beta(v)=\sqrt\beta(\sqrt{1-|v|^2/\beta}-1)$. Then (K0.3) becomes
$$\beta H_G=\tfrac12\langle v,K_Gv\rangle+\tfrac12\langle z_\beta,K_Gz_\beta\rangle+\sqrt\beta\sum_{\partial(G,D_B)}(h-q_y)\cdot(v_x+z_\beta(v_x)h)+\tfrac\beta2\sum_{\partial(G,D_B)}|h-q_y|^2.\tag{K0.5}$$
**PROVED (remaining chart factors).** The tangent-normal cross term vanishes because $K_G$ is scalar in spin indices; the displayed boundary linear term does not vanish. The source is $\sum_{x\in G}g_x\cdot[h+\beta^{-1/2}(v_x+z_\beta(v_x)h)]$ and the chart Jacobian is $\beta^{-(N-1)|G|/2}\prod_{x\in G}(1-|v_x|^2/\beta)^{-1/2}$, with the real good indicator retained.

**PROVED (finite-volume Gaussian change of variables).** $K_G\ge\nu_* I$, so $C_G=K_G^{-1}$ is positive and $v=C_G^{1/2}\psi$ changes the quadratic factor in (K0.5) to standard product Gaussian measure, with normalizer $(2\pi/\beta)^{(N-1)|G|/2}\det(K_G)^{-(N-1)/2}$. The $B$-dependence of $K_G$, the determinant, the good cutoff and the boundary factor in (K0.5) still require localization before Mayer/GAS can use one common reference.
