## 2. The landed amplitude and its two losses

$$\varepsilon_U\ge |\beta|\left(3c_1M^d|E|/r^3+\rho_Ec_1A_E/r+V/r+\rho_M/r\right).\tag{A}$$
The second term's $L^{(k+1)d}$ is exact. `Fluctuation/InteractionEnergy.lean`, `energyBd`, is $L^{(k+1)d}[\frac12(a_k/L^{2k})N(\delta_1+V)^2+\frac12N(4d+\nu+\rho_4)V^2]$. **PROVED (the source allowance is fatal at fixed scale).** Assume the positive standing constants $Q:=cK2'(d,L,N,famConst(d,L,BG_2,\kappa))>0$ and $C:=\sqrt N\,covSupConstC(d,L,N,a_k,\kappa)>0$, and $\beta>0$.
