## 1. Source construction and the landed full-level model

**PROVED (P2 p. 607, (1.1); P3 pp. 68–69, (4.4)–(4.6)).** P2's ambient generating set has nested domains $\Omega_1\supset\cdots\supset\Omega_k$ with $\operatorname{dist}(\Omega_n^c,\Omega_{n+1})>R M L^n\eta$ in fine units ($R M$ is the dimensionless separation). P3 uses a *second* block size $M_1=L^{m_1}$, smaller than the RG block size $M$, and constructs a local set by adding $5L$ layers of $M_1L^n\eta$ cubes at each descent from $n+1$ to $n$. In its notation, $\Omega'_n=(\Omega'_{n+1})^{\sim5L}$, (4.5)–(4.6), with the new neighbourhood understood in $M_1L^n\eta$ cubes. The separation is $5LM_1$ in units $L^n\eta$, not the ambient $RM$; these constants need not be equal. P3 p. 69 permits a geometric buffer parameter $r_0=5$ for (4.8) and requires, for its displayed sufficient geometry, $M\ge6M_1$. That $r_0$ must not be identified with the `BgSetting.r₀` of the landed hatted engine. A larger ambient $M$ can make $RM$ strictly larger than $5LM_1$; equality is nowhere required.

**PROVED (P3 p. 68, (4.4), and p. 69, (4.7)–(4.9)).** There are two uses of the notation. For an *old* level-$i$ polymer $X$ viewed at ambient level $k\ge i$, P3 first sets $\Omega'_k$ to the union of the $LM_1$ cover cubes at level $k$ containing a level-$i$ cover cube contained in $X$; this is a cover envelope and may exceed $X$. It then descends by (4.5)–(4.6). P3 (4.7) proves that its innermost domain lies within $X_0=(X'')^{\sim4L}$ in current-scale $M_1$ cubes, using $(L-1)M_1+5LM_1(L^{-1}+\cdots+L^{-(k-1)})<4LM_1$. This is the local background used in (4.9). For a *new* level-$j$ output polymer that is already a union of the relevant top cover cubes, the top layer is the corresponding part of $X$; this is the case modeled by `minimalGenSet` with `cubeInterior X=X`. Confusing the old-polymer envelope with this aligned output case changes the top domain.

**PROVED (P3 pp. 78–79, (4.44)–(4.47)).** After resummation at level $K=k+1$, the top domain is exactly $\Omega'_K=Y\cap\Omega_K$. For $n<K$, P3 forms $\Omega'_n$ from the $5L$-cube enlargement of $\Omega'_{n+1}$ *together with* $Y\cap\Omega_n\cap\Omega_{n+1}^c$. The second summand preserves the original lower-layer parts of $Y$; omitting it produces the wrong mixed-level generator. If the ambient separation is at least $5LM_1$ at each lower step, each enlarged higher layer remains inside $\Omega_n$, so $\Omega'_n\subset\Omega_n$; equivalently one may intersect the enlarged part with $\Omega_n$ and prove equality under that condition. P3 replaces the generator inside each pre-resummation formula and proves the resulting term analytic on $\Xi_K^c(\mathbb B_K(Y);1,\varepsilon')$ in (4.46), with (4.47) bounds. P5 p. 489, (1.8), restricts these inherited $E^{(j)}(y,X)$ to $X\subset(Z_k^c)^\approx$; p. 490 says they remain whole-lattice terms independent of the chosen $Z_k^c$. P5 p. 491, (1.12), makes the analogous restriction for $M^{(j)}(x,X)$.

**PROVED (landed model).** `LargeField/Background/MinimalGenSet.lean:66–108` defines $C_n$-cubes of side $s_n=M_1L^n$, `cubeInterior j X`, and `nearStep n A`: $x$ is admitted exactly when some $y\in A$ has $\operatorname{supDist}(C_n(x),C_n(y))\le5L$. `minLayer j X j=cubeInterior j X`, `minLayer j X n=nearStep n (minLayer j X (n+1))` for $n<j$, and it is empty above $j$. `minimalGenSet` (line 195) proves nesting, $n$-block alignment and separation $\rho_{\min}=5LM_1$ via `torusDist_gt_of_not_mem_nearStep`. `StepGenSets.lean:130` defines `genSF j W` by this constructor after `cubePre`; `Def7SmallField.lean:238` uses it for $\mathbb B_k(W_2(Y))$. **OPEN:** a landed general constructor matching P3's mixed-level $Y\cap\Omega_n\cap\Omega_{n+1}^c$ recurrence and the old-polymer top envelope; `minimalGenSet` by itself models the aligned full-level output case, not both source constructions.

## 2. Why the present fluctuation radius cannot be used with this parent

**PROVED (exact conflict).** `I5Fan.BgSetting` requires `hr : r₀ M₁ ≤ ϱ−1` and `hr₀ : 4dL³ ≤ r₀`. Setting the *parent* $G_p=\mathbb B_j(X)$ gives $\varrho=5LM_1$, so these imply $4dL^3M_1\le5LM_1-1$, impossible for $d,L\ge3$, $M_1\ge1$. This refutes the direct `BgSetting` instantiation used in `CHART.md`. It does **not** refute construction of $\mathbb B_j(X)$ as a `GeneratingSet`, nor `BgHyp` for it, nor a local fluctuation theorem with different geometry.

**PROVED (where the two hypotheses go).** `I5Fan.point_facts` passes both fields to `I5Chain.i5_stage1`; the latter uses `I5Close.composed_close_X0`, which uses `DecoupledFieldX0.scaled_dist_X0` and `DecoupledFieldX0.i4a_close_X0`. `DecoupledCovWeighted.I4c_close_sqrt_X0` uses the same distance estimate for the covariance square root. In `scaled_dist_X0` (lines 266–375), `hr` supplies $T\le\varrho-1$, needed when a candidate path crosses to a layer more than one above the source and again to compare the physical distance to `G.pathDist`. The proof bounds a parameter-box/cube distance by $4dL\,M_1L^z$ with $z\le n+2$, hence by $4dL^3M_1L^n$; this is the actual source of the $L^3$ loss. The returned distance is $((r_0-4dL^3)M_1/L)$ in path units. `i4a_close_X0`, `composed_close_X0`, and `I4c_close_sqrt_X0` use $r_0\ge4dL^3$ to make this distance nonnegative and place it in an exponential decay bound. `I5Chain.ChainNum.close` uses that exponential through `composedCloseBound`. Thus the $L^3$ is part of the present multiscale decoupling geometry, not merely a superfluous numerical field in `BgSetting`.

**PROVED (separate, weaker use).** `Fluctuation/PhysicalReadsE.lean:100–148` uses `hr₀` only to prove $d(3\ell-2)<r_0M_1\ell$ for an `approxDep` read of a `composeDep` site. Here $r_0M_1>3d$ suffices for $\ell\ge1$, and the convenient sufficient $r_0\ge4d$ suffices when $M_1\ge1$; `radius_lt` currently assumes the stronger $4dL^3$. Weakening this isolated E-read lemma does not remove the $4dL^3$ loss in `scaled_dist_X0` or the need for a positive decoupling distance. `I5Inhabited.rhoSep` takes a much larger parent separation and sets $r_0=324=4\cdot3\cdot3^3$ at $d=L=3$; it is an example of the *parent* hypotheses, not a construction of P3's $5LM_1$ local set as parent.

**REFUTED (option a for the existing engine).** The pair $r_0M_1\le5LM_1-1$, $r_0\ge4dL^3$ cannot be made compatible merely by replacing the E-read estimate with $r_0\ge4d$. **PROVED:** the sufficient pair used by the present decay proof is $r_0M_1\le\varrho-1$ and $r_0\ge4dL^3$; to obtain a strictly improving decay exponent from this particular bound, take $r_0>4dL^3$. A weaker *new* geometric theorem could change these requirements; none is landed. **REFUTED (option b as the canonical object):** a wide `nearStep` with $w$ $C_n$-cube layers and $wM_1\ge r_0M_1+1$ could satisfy the engine's parent separation, but it would no longer be P3's $5L$-layer construction or its (4.7) $4LM_1$ range. **PROVED (option c, source-faithful):** retain the narrow P3 generator as a separate local object and prove the local chart/fluctuation estimates it needs, rather than silently identifying it with the wide parent geometry. The global parent used to *construct* or compare the term and the local generator on which the output term is analytic have different roles.

## 3. Support, collar, and the actual halo comparison

**PROVED (landed full-level range).** Assume $2\le L$, $1\le j\le q$, $X$ is a finite union of $C_j$-cubes, and $G=\operatorname{minimalGenSet}(j\le q,X)$. `MinimalGenSet.exists_near_of_mem_minLayer` gives, for every $x\in\Omega_n(G)$, $n\le j$, a $y\in X$ such that
$$ (L-1)\operatorname{supDist}(x,y)+(5L+1)M_1L^n\le(5L+1)M_1L^j.\tag{3.1} $$
In particular `exists_near_of_mem_minLayer'` gives $\operatorname{supDist}(x,X)\le11M_1L^j$. A collar site is `Adj` to some $x\in\Omega_1(G)$ (`Background/Data.lean:49–59`), hence its cyclic sup-distance to $X$ is at most $11M_1L^j+1$ by the triangle inequality. Thus the exact class-neutral target is
$$\operatorname{dataSites}G=\Omega_1(G)\cup\operatorname{collar}G\subset\{z:\operatorname{setDist}_{\infty}(\{z\},X)\le r_j\},\qquad r_j=11M_1L^j+1.\tag{3.2}$$
The extra union with `collar G` changes nothing because `dataSites` already contains it. Formula (3.1) provides the sharper layer-by-layer radius; (3.2) is a convenient uniform envelope. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `MixedLocalGen` recurrence and ambient separation:** the same radius relative to $Y$ holds for the P3 mixed-level output because every fresh lower-layer site lies in $Y$, and each enlarged site descends from an earlier $Y$ site. The old-polymer top-envelope case instead requires adding the explicit top-envelope radius before using (3.2).

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the identified torus transport and (3.2).** In `Def7SmallField`, let $H=\lfloor R(k-1)\rfloor_++1$, $\sigma_k=L^kM$ fine sites per current $k$-cube, and let $X\subset C.\mathrm{sset}(\operatorname{thickY}R\,Y)$. If
$$r_j=11M_1L^j+1 < H\sigma_k=(\lfloor R(k-1)\rfloor_++1)L^kM,\tag{3.3}$$
then $\operatorname{dataSites}B_j(X)\subset C.\mathrm{sset}(W_2(R,Y))$. Indeed, a point outside $W_2=\operatorname{thick}(\lfloor R(k-1)\rfloor_+)(\operatorname{thickY}R\,Y)$ is at `supDist` at least $H\sigma_k$ from *every* point over `thickY R Y` by `R/HaloDistance.sset_dist_of_not_mem_thick`; (3.2) contradicts this. This uses the landed convention `thick r=nbhd (r+1)`, so no extra unrecorded cube is spent. For $j\le k$, $M\ge6M_1$, $R(k-1)\ge5$ and $L\ge3$, (3.3) follows numerically. The required alignment and transported `B_j(X)` identification are **OPEN** in the actual Def7 class.

**REFUTED:** (3.3) by itself proves that `C.chiY` covers every datum or collar bond. `chiY` tests cubes in $Y$, while the resulting support can lie in the second halo $W_2\setminus Y$. **OPEN (`collarInterfaceCover`, `referenceR12Margin` of `Q13-TRANSFER.md`):** assign each site and each `IsInterfacePair`, including collar–collar pairs, to an actual active history factor: `chiY` for its own $Y$ stencil, an appropriate `ChiDomProp` or quiet-kernel factor for any shell site those predicates really test, or a boundary/window factor where present; then establish the same-datum R1/R2 slack on that assignment. A local generator removes the whole-torus counterexample but does not manufacture such factors. Both scheme A and the hybrid need this last step.
