## T1. Exact first transform and its chart

**PROVED (frozen starting density).** Put $\Lambda_0=\mathbb T_{Ln}$, $\Lambda_1=\mathbb T_n$, $\phi_x\in S^{N-1}$, $|h|=1$, and let $d\sigma^{\Lambda_0}$ be `aprioriMeasure`. With `Frozen/Model/Energy.lean:energy`,
$$H_0(\phi)=\tfrac12\sum_{x,\mu}|\phi_{x+e_\mu}-\phi_x|^2+\tfrac\nu2\sum_x|\phi_x-h|^2,\quad
\rho_0(\phi;g)=e^{-\beta H_0(\phi)+\sum_{x,i}g_i(x)\phi_i(x)},\quad
Z(g)=\int\rho_0(\phi;g)\,d\sigma^{\Lambda_0}(\phi).$$
This is exactly `Frozen/Model/PartitionFunction.lean:partitionFunction`; its $g$ is complex. There are no input $E_0$, $\mathcal E^{(0)}$, or $\mathcal M^{(0)}$ terms in this frozen density. P4 (2.42) instead displays a finite-$\lambda_0$ flat-spin integral with a length penalty and an $E'_0$ convention; its chart formula is not literally a sphere-measure theorem.

**PROVED (actual block transform).** For $y\in\Lambda_1$ set $Q\phi(y)=L^{-d}\sum_{x:\operatorname{blockMap}(L,n)x=y}\phi_x$ and $c_0=\beta a L^{d-2}>0$. The finite-$a$ kernel is the normalized Gaussian `LargeField/Operators/Quiet.lean:gaussT`, $t_0(\vartheta,w)=(c_0/2\pi)^{N/2}e^{-c_0|\vartheta-w|^2/2}$; `ShellVolume.lean:lintegral_gaussT` proves its integral is one. The factor $L^d$ in $c_0$ converts P4 (2.42)'s fine-site $aL^{-2}$ block quadratic to one factor per coarse block. The formal sharp map $\delta(\vartheta-Q\phi)$ is its singular $a\to\infty$ idealization, **not** the kernel of the finite-$a$ step. For $z_1>0$ and $D=N|\Lambda_1|$,
$$\bar\rho_1(\vartheta;g)=\int\Big[\prod_{y\in\Lambda_1}t_0(\vartheta_y,Q\phi(y))\Big]\rho_0(\phi;g)\,d\sigma^{\Lambda_0}(\phi),\qquad
\rho_1(\psi_1;g)=z_1^D\bar\rho_1(z_1\psi_1;g),\qquad
Z(g)=\int_{(\mathbb R^N)^{\Lambda_1}}\rho_1(\psi_1;g)\,d\psi_1.$$
The last identity follows from Gaussian normalization, Fubini, and the $z_1$ change of variables; it has no small-field restriction. It is (II.7.1) of `LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` with the paper's scale-one rescaling understood.

**PROVED (first sector split as a mathematical identity).** P5 (2.5)–(2.6) at $k=0$, in `LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` Definition II.7.1, uses for each new cube $c$ the product $\chi'_0(c)=A_cB_c$: $A_c$ tests $|\vartheta_y-Q\phi(y)|<2\delta_0$ for its blocks; $B_c$ tests $|\partial\phi(b)|<\delta_0$ for every bond meeting $c$, and in regime B also $|\phi_x-h|<\delta_0\nu^{-1/2}$ on its sites. In regime A ($k_0\ge1$) there is no $h$ site test. The hard-spin length condition is automatic. Exactly $1=\prod_c(\chi'_0(c)+(1-\chi'_0(c)))=\sum_{\mathsf P_1}\prod_{c\in\mathsf P_1}(1-\chi'_0(c))\prod_{c\notin\mathsf P_1}\chi'_0(c)$; the complement goes into first large-field records, with the subsequent $\mathsf Q_1,\mathsf R_1,\mathsf S_1$ tests, and must remain in $\rho_1$. `LargeField/Base/BaseRecord.lean:chiBlockA`, `chiBondB`, `chiPrimeZero` encode these first tests; `KF0BlockSplit.lean:partitionFunction_eq_sum_kf0Sector` is a different, site-only exact split and does not supply the P5 record.

**PROVED (hard-spin action identity, conditional on a real constrained minimizer).** On a fixed good component, hold exterior spins and $\vartheta$ fixed, and let $e=\phi^{\mathrm b}$ solve the constrained Euler–Lagrange equation with multiplier $\alpha$. With $\mu=0$ for the regime-A *background* and $\mu=\nu$ for regime B, write $G_\alpha^{-1}=-\Delta_\Omega+aL^{-2}Q^*Q+\mu+\alpha$, where $Q^*=L^dQ^\top$ in the fine-site inner product. If $u=\phi-e$, then $A_{\mu}(\phi)-A_{\mu}(e)=\frac12\langle u,G_\alpha^{-1}u\rangle$ exactly, using $e_x\cdot u_x=-|u_x|^2/2$; this is `LargeField/Base/ChordQuadratic.lean:action_sub_eq_chord_quadratic` under its stated `IsEL` and unit-field hypotheses. In regime A the physical $\nu$ term remains separately as $\beta\nu\langle e-h,u\rangle+\beta\nu\|u\|^2/2$. P4 (2.48) gives the corresponding finite-$\lambda$ Hessian with $\nu_0+\alpha_1$.

**PROVED (hemisphere chart and exact remainder).** On the good support, `SpinsNearBackground.lean:spins_near_background` supplies the needed proximity under `BaseSpinSupport` and its background hypotheses. At each site use $v_x\in e_x^\perp$, $|v_x|<\sqrt\beta$, and
$$\phi_x=\sqrt{1-|v_x|^2/\beta}\,e_x+\beta^{-1/2}v_x,qquad
w_x=\sqrt\beta\big(\sqrt{1-|v_x|^2/\beta}-1\big),\qquad
u_x=\beta^{-1/2}(v_x+w_xe_x).$$
The product sphere measure becomes $\beta^{-(N-1)|\Omega|/2}\prod_x(1-|v_x|^2/\beta)^{-1/2}dv_x$ on the tangent balls. `SphereChart.lean:hemiE` and `HemispherePi.lean:lintegral_sphere_pi_hemisphere` prove the geometry and product measure. Thus the exact exponent relative to the background contains $-\frac12\langle v,G_\alpha^{-1}v\rangle-V^{(0)}(v)$, with
$$V^{(0)}(v)=\langle v,G_\alpha^{-1}(we)\rangle+\tfrac12\langle we,G_\alpha^{-1}(we)\rangle
+\mathbf1_{\rm A}\,\beta\nu\big[\langle e-h,u\rangle+\tfrac12\|u\|^2\big]-\langle g,u\rangle-\sum_x\log(1-|v_x|^2/\beta)^{-1/2},$$
if source and Jacobian are both placed in $e^{-V^{(0)}}$; moving those last two summands to a separate activity is equivalent. The normal function $w$ is quadratic near zero, so the action itself has no additional hard-spin polynomial interaction. The displayed formula is the direct hard-spin counterpart of P4 (2.45)–(2.47), not P4's finite-$\lambda$ formula verbatim.

**PROVED (quadratic representation, conditional on coercivity).** Set $P_e(x)=e_x\otimes e_x$, $Q_e=1-P_e$, $A_e=Q_eG_\alpha^{-1}Q_e+P_e$, and $C^{(0)}=A_e^{-1}$; an independent unit Gaussian in the normal directions embeds the tangent integral into $\mathbb R^{N|\Omega|}$ without changing its value after its normalization is included. This is the $\lambda=\infty$ form of P4 (2.49)–(2.50); the finite-$\lambda$ normal mode in P4 is different. `LargeField/Base/BaseResolvent.lean:baseOp`, `baseResolvent`, `baseOp_isCoercive_of_close`, and `BaseResolventMain.lean:baseSqrt_block_decay` prove parts of this operator theory when $\alpha$, $e$, $\mu$, and decoupling parameters meet their displayed hypotheses. A local determinant, $C^{(0)1/2}$ change of variables, and the chart activity yield P4 (2.50); P4 (2.51)–(2.54) then define the first energy and source increments. **OPEN:** the complete physical hard-spin integral with its P5 cutoffs has not been identified with that local Gaussian activity in Lean.

**PROVED (index distinction).** At level zero the integration variable is $\psi_0:=\phi$ itself, an exactly constrained spin, with no old background or old $E/M$ terms. The first background $e=\phi^{\mathrm b}_1$ is constructed only after $\vartheta$ and exterior spins are fixed; its multiplier $\alpha$ enforces the constraint on that minimizer, while the chart enforces it for all integrated spins. `Fluctuation/StepObjects.lean:StepSetting.hk1` and `CriticalPoint.lean:IsLevel.bounds` make a direct $k=0$ instance of the generic step impossible.
