# Q13 round 23: the common tube and regional assignment

## Summary

**OPEN:** the landed real response and analytic background theorems do not prove `regionalCommonTube`. A uniform tube is possible under a quantitative *joint data–mass* margin, but neither the sharp retained-region cutoff nor the current H4/H7 clauses supply that margin. In particular the proposed passage from real $O(\nu_j)$ response to complex $O(\nu_j)$ response by Cauchy and Schwarz is invalid without a mass-uniform complex mass disk. The RGFREE-3/6 transverse-source examples leave a pinned, fixed-$h$ tube open; their source $h$ diverges as $\nu_j\downarrow0$.

**PROVED:** `R/RegionShrink.lean:EresJ2_shrink_total` is the exact four-piece algebra for regional $E$. **OPEN:** it has not been assigned to the actual new LF kernel and D7 boundary activity; in scheme A a complete-to-free evaluation must also be inserted. The per-polymer additive D7 distance asserted in Q13-GROUPED (9) is false for a boundary cluster label carried by several polymers. An aggregate distance or a two-budget estimate is the appropriate replacement.

## 1. What a common tube would say

Fix an inherited $j\le k_0$, a current step $q>j$, a real unit $h_*$, the full real datum $D=(\Psi,h_*)$ of the retained region $Y'$, and each inherited generating set $G_j(P)$ meeting the proposed boundary assignment. At level $q$ the physical solver mass is $\mu_q=\nu_qL^{-2q}$, while its inherited dimensionless mass is $t_j=\mu_qL^{2j}=\nu_j$. For the **same** $D$, let $\phi_q^m(D)$ and $\phi_q^0(D)$ be complete and massless solutions. `Def7SmallField.lean:SFCtx.psiJ` composes either complex solution with the same lifted block datum; denote the two instantiations by $\psi_{j,Y'}^m(D)$ and $\psi_{j,Y'}^0(D)$. The existing `psiJ` selects one mass `cs.mu q`, not a landed pair. `SFCtx.phiBar` takes a real part and is only the real action field; it is not the holomorphic complexification used in the tube. For the shrink formula also use $\psi_{j,Y}^m(D)$, since changing $Y$ changes `genW` and the composition.

The target `regionalCommonTube` is an open polydisc $T_{j,q}(D)$ about the real retained datum in complex block/spin variables and $h$, with radius at least the **actual** inherited H4/H7 trial radius after pullback through `psiJ`, such that both compositions and every interpolation used to localize their difference satisfy the relevant predicates with a fixed smaller-disc margin. For inherited $E,M$, these are `SFTermClauses.analyticE/M` and `boundE/M` on `completeXiDom` = `xiFieldDom` = `Def6.XiDom` at mass $\mu_q$ (the birth tag is $j$); evaluating $E$ at $\psi^0$ still needs membership in this *complete* term domain. The old $R$ terms require `RTermsH4.analytic/bound`, whose displayed predicate is `XiDom` at `Def6.bgMass`, $h\in\texttt{hDomR}$ and $\|g\|_1<1`; any v2 replacement must specify its mass. D7 needs `D7Bdry2.analytic/bound` on `hSetB2 hv rH` and `H7Domain` = `DeltaDomain1` $\wedge$ `DeltaDomain2` $\wedge$ `FluctDomain` $\wedge$ $\|g\|_1<1` (`Characteristic/Analytic.lean:173`). The H7 predicates are on multiscale complex fields; they do not follow merely from XiDom.

The physical observable uses complex $g$ and fixed real unit $h_*$. That is enough for the frozen root, but the **landed** H4/H7 class asks analyticity in complex spin variables, with H4 also in $h$ on its stated domain and v2 D7 on a pinned $h$ ball. The LF (S5) continuation asks $|h-h_*|<1$ on its spin domain (LF-RECONSTRUCTION Definition II.5.7). Thus a real-slice estimate alone cannot be substituted into these clauses. A rebuilt class could make $h$ real for the root, but would require a new proof of the later analytic rotation and locality steps.

## 2. Counterexamples and the cutoff question

RGFREE-3 §2.1 takes $b=me_1+i\lambda\varepsilon^2e_2$, $h=e_1+i\lambda\varepsilon^2m^{-1}e_2$; it lies in the broad complex XiC source domain and has a switch chord of order $\beta\varepsilon^4$. RGFREE-6 §2 uses the real transverse source $b=me_1+qe_2$, $q=\sigma\varepsilon^2$ fixed, $h=e_1+(q/m)e_2$, with the same diverging chord. Both refute a bound uniform over the **mass-independent $b$ disc**, including its mass-free XiDom, not a tube $|h-h_*|\le r_h$ with fixed $r_h$: on such a tube $|b-mh_*|\le mr_h\to0$. The imaginary RGFREE-3 point is outside the real cutoff; the RGFREE-6 real point moves the complete background a fixed $O(q)$ while the sharp tolerance $\delta_j\asymp\beta_j^{-1/2}\lambda_j^{p_0}$ tends to zero at a fixed switch scale as $\beta\to\infty$. Hence it is outside a **complete-background** retained cutoff for large $\beta$. A massless-only cutoff can accept $\Psi=e_1$ at that point, so exclusion from the actual scheme-A interface requires a proved complete-data overlap or an inner cutoff; `cutoffDataRegularity` in Q13-TESTS is still OPEN. Strict cutoff membership alone also has no fixed numerical margin at its boundary.

Here is the precise missing quantitative lemma, `regionalStrictTube`. Choose normalized complex data norms $\|\cdot\|_{j,G}$ dominating every `StrictData`, `XiCBounds`, `DeltaDomain1/2` and H7 test after pullback. Let $\rho_j>0$ be the **minimum numerical slack** of those tests at the real datum for both solver masses and all supports used; let $C_{D,j}$, $C_{h,j}$ and $C_{t,j}$ bound the changes in every test per unit current-scale complex displacement, per unit source displacement $\nu_j(h-h_*)$, and per unit normalized complex mass $t=\mu L^{2j}$, respectively. Require the same holomorphic solution branch and a uniform bound $B_j$ for both composed fields on the product
$$\|z-D\|_q<r_{0,j}:={\rho_j\over4C_{D,j}},\quad |h-h_*|<r_{h,j}:={\rho_j\over4C_{h,j}\nu_j},\quad |t|<t_{0,j}:={\rho_j\over4C_{t,j}},\tag{2.1}$$
with $\nu_j\le t_{0,j}/2$. The $h$ radius is written in physical $h$ units, since the source enters as $\nu_jh$; the constants include the scale and composition maps. Let $r_{\rm H4/H7,j}$ be the largest current-variable trial radius required after pullback; it is of order $\varepsilon_q$ for spin variables (and $\varepsilon_{1,q}$ for fluctuation variables), whereas the inherited XiC spin margin is of order $\varepsilon_j$. In particular, if its available spin slack is $\eta\varepsilon_j$ and the pullback bound is $C_{\rm cmp,j}r$, a trial radius $r=a\varepsilon_q$ requires $aC_{\rm cmp,j}\varepsilon_q\le\eta\varepsilon_j$. Demand $r_{0,j}\ge(1+\tau)r_{\rm H4/H7,j}$ and $r_{h,j}\ge1+\tau$ (or the pinned D7 radius plus $\tau$), for fixed $\tau>0$. Equivalently the needed numerical hierarchy is $\rho_j\ge4C_{D,j}(1+\tau)r_{\rm H4/H7,j}$, $\rho_j\ge4C_{h,j}\nu_j(1+\tau)$, and $\rho_j\ge8C_{t,j}\nu_j$. Choose the geometry, trial fractions and $\tau$ first; prove a uniform lower slack on an inner cutoff for **all** histories next; only then choose $\lambda_*$ and the initial $\beta$ so these inequalities hold for all $j\le k_0$. No current theorem supplies that lower slack or the inequalities uniformly.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `regionalStrictTube`:** analyticity jointly in $(z,h,t)$ and Cauchy's formula in the *mass* variable give
$$\|\psi_j^m(z,h)-\psi_j^0(z,h)\|\le {2B_j\over t_{0,j}}\nu_j,\qquad \|z-D\|_q\le r_{0,j}/2,\ |h-h_*|\le r_{h,j}/2.\tag{2.2}$$
Indeed the circle $|t|=t_{0,j}$ bounds $\partial_t\psi$ by $B_j/(t_{0,j}-|t|)\le2B_j/t_{0,j}$ on $0\le t\le\nu_j$, and integration gives (2.2). The complex extension and `psiJ` composition factors are absorbed into $B_j$. `prop51_mass_analytic_uniform` (`Background/Prop51Mass.lean:266`) supplies joint analyticity on a union of *open strict-data sets* with uniform contraction constants, and `theorem52_analytic` (`Background/Theorem52Analytic.lean:127`) supplies data analyticity, but neither states $\rho_j$, $B_j$, the pullback constants, or (2.1) for actual histories. `massChange_sameData_response_unit` (`R/MassResponseHyp.lean:134`) is a real estimate with `IsEL`, `BgBounds`, Green and smallness premises; transport from its solution to `psiJ` is also OPEN.

The proposed real-response-plus-Cauchy inference is **REFUTED as a general analytic argument**. On $|\Im z|<R$, $f_m(z)=m\exp(i z\log(1/m)/R)$ obeys $|f_m(x)|=m$ for every real $x$ and $|f_m(z)|\le1$ on the strip, but at $z=-ir$ it equals $m^{1-r/R}$, not $O(m)$ for any fixed $0<r<R$. Joint analyticity in a mass disk of radius bounded below, as in (2.1), repairs exactly this defect. A common tube may exist at the physical retained data; its uniform radius is **OPEN**, and the cutoff alone gives no asserted inequality $r_{0,j}\ge r_{\rm H4/H7,j}$.

For a wholly real route, the partition and real (S2) bounds can use Q13-GROUPED (10). To put the resulting *difference* into H4 or D7, the localization and Cauchy derivatives in `SFTermClauses.analyticE/M`, `RTermsH4.analytic`, `D7Bdry2.analytic`, and the next fluctuation I-5/I-6 step consume its complex continuation. One may keep $\psi^0$ as the LF analytic variable only after constructing new localized activities whose difference is represented by real-contour integrals and then proving their analytic continuation and bounds independently. That construction is **OPEN**; it is not a consequence of the real partition identity.

## 3. Exact energy algebra and the unproved physical assignment

Use the notation of `R/RegionShrink.lean:163–256`: $R=C.\mathrm{alignedIn}_jY$, $R'=C.\mathrm{alignedIn}_jY'$, $B=C.\mathrm{alignedIn}_j(\operatorname{thickY}Y)$, $B'$ similarly, $P=C.\mathrm{sset}(\operatorname{thickY}Y)$ and $P'$ similarly. With $Y'\subset Y$, write $\mathcal E(R,B,P;\psi)=\sum_{y\in R}\sum_{X\ni y,X\subset P}E_j(y,X;\psi,h)-\sum_{y\in R,x\in B}\mathrm{corrPair}_j(y,x;\psi,h)$, including both $v_1$ and $v_2\nu_jh$. Then `EresJ2_shrink_total` proves **exactly**
$$\begin{aligned} \mathcal E(Y;\psi_Y)-\mathcal E(Y';\psi_{Y'})={}&D_j(\psi_Y)+C_j(\psi_Y)-Q_j(\psi_Y)+V_j(\psi_Y,\psi_{Y'}),\\
D_j={}&\sum_{y\in R\setminus R'}P_P(y),\quad C_j=\sum_{y\in R'}\sum_{X\ni y,X\subset P,X\not\subset P'}E_j(y,X),\\
Q_j={}&\mathrm{Corr}(R\setminus R',B)+\mathrm{Corr}(R',B\setminus B'),\\
V_j={}&\mathcal E(Y';\psi_Y)-\mathcal E(Y';\psi_{Y'}).\tag{3.1}
\end{aligned}$$
Finite polymers in the Lean torus make this purely finite algebra. The smaller regional factor $\mathcal E(Y';\psi_{Y'})$ is **kept** with the retained SF region. A summand of deleted centres $D_j$ or correction flux $Q_j$ supported wholly in the new LF component is a candidate new LF kernel term; a summand touching retained sites belongs in D7. The crossing-polymers $C_j$ have a retained centre and support outside the new halo, hence belong in D7. The re-evaluation $V_j$ is assigned by its *localized response support*: terms whose response path is wholly in the new component join its kernel, and those reaching retained sites join D7, even when their original $E$ polymer sits well inside $Y'$. This response-path decomposition has not been proved, so these are assignment targets, not equalities of existing kernels.

For scheme A, the new LF component uses $\psi^0$ while the departing SF energy in (3.1) uses $\psi^m$. Add and subtract the same region evaluated at $\psi^0$ and group the difference as Q13-TESTS (A2): $\Delta\sum I_j+\Delta\mathcal B_j+\Delta T_j^Y$. Q13-GROUPED (2) cancels the near $v_1$ channel only conditional on `regionalRadialCoefficient`; its halo and omitted-tail estimates are conditional on `regionalHaloDistance` and counts. Formula (3.1) alone has no kernel assignment. The single missing **identity-level** rule is `regionalSupportAssignment`: a history-compatible localization map for $D,C,Q,V$ and the complete/free addition, with exact conservation of each summand, correct old-variable dependence, $g$ marks and sources, supports either wholly new LF or in an admissible D7 boundary polymer, and analytic norm bounds there. `Fres` and `Rres` need analogous shrink/re-evaluation maps. This rule is **OPEN**; it is what `regionalEnergyIdentity` must assert before the A2 comparison can be charged to an actual step.

## 4. The D7 tail distance

Q13-GROUPED (9) assumes, for **each** omitted $j$ polymer $P$ assigned to a D7 label $U$,
$$d_j(P)\ge H_{j,q}+c_Dd_q^{\rm cov}(U\bmod\Omega_q^c).\tag{4.1}$$
This is **REFUTED for ordinary cluster assignment**. In a planar boundary strip, take a shortest connected $P_0$ from a retained centre across the halo at one boundary point; $d_j(P_0)=H_{j,q}+O(1)$. Attach a chain of ordinary adjacent polymers $P_1,\ldots,P_n$ running tangentially along the $\Omega_q$ side of the boundary. The connected cluster has one D7 support $U_n$ with $d_q^{\rm cov}(U_n\bmod\Omega_q^c)\to\infty$, while the assigned crossing polymer $P_0$ retains its fixed size. No $c_D>0$ makes (4.1) hold for each member. This does not refute a specially defined single-polymer label; none is landed.

A viable replacement is **OPEN** `regionalClusterTailAssignment`: for a cluster $\Gamma$ with at least one omitted polymer, prove $\sum_{P\in\Gamma}d_j(P)\ge H_{j,q}$ and $\sum_{P\in\Gamma}d_j(P)\ge c_Dd_q^{\rm cov}(U\bmod\Omega_q^c)$, plus a cluster entropy bound. Then $\sum d_j(P)\ge\frac12(H_{j,q}+c_Dd_q^{\rm cov})$, so an available decay rate $\rho$ yields $e^{-\rho H_{j,q}/2}e^{-\rho c_Dd_q^{\rm cov}/2}$ after counting. To meet D7 rate $\kappa$ (or its pre-class $3\kappa/2$), require $\rho c_D/2\ge\kappa$ (respectively $\ge3\kappa/2$); this constant loss must be chosen before the LF entropy ledger. Geometry alone proves the first inequality for the crossing member conditional on `regionalHaloDistance`; the second and cluster count are **OPEN**.

2. **REFUTED:** real response plus separate analyticity implies complex $O(\nu_j)$; per-member additive D7 distance (4.1) for cluster labels.

4. **OPEN A:** uniform strict margins/composition, `regionalSupportAssignment`, `regionalClusterTailAssignment`, composed response, and grouped coefficient/halo/count inputs.
5. **OPEN hybrid:** `hybrid_sf_factorization`, complete-action LF (S0)–(S5) bounds including the unit $h$ ball, inner cutoff overlap and $R$ numerator; it still needs ordinary shrink assignment.

7. **Verdict:** no proved shorter *mathematical* list; A is the recorded choice, while hybrid's one factorization theorem contains several independent estimates. Neither scheme has a closed interface.
