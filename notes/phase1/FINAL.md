## 1. Final Hessian and coercivity

**PROVED (real algebra; P4 (2.23)–(2.24), p. 687, checked against the PDF image; P4 (6.4)–(6.5), pp. 719–720).** Let $k=k_f>0$, $n=L^k$, and let the fine and coarse unit tori have sides $nm$ and $m$. Set $a=a_k\in[1/2,2]$, $v=\nu_k\in(7/(8L^2),1]$, $H=n^2(-\Delta_{nm})+v+aQ^*Q$, and $G=H^{-1}$; $Q$ is the normalized block average in `Green/UnitLattice.lean`. The final quadratic form acts on $(\mathbb R^N)^{T_m}$, without a next-scale averaging term. At the constant saddle $\phi=h$, $\alpha=0$, $|h|=1$, P4 (2.23) gives
$$D_k=aI-a^2QGQ^*+a^2QGh(h^*Gh)^{-1}h^*GQ^*.\tag{F.1}$$
Here $h$ denotes insertion of the constant spin direction at every fine site; the inverse in the third term is on fine scalar fields. Positivity of $H$ makes that term nonnegative.

**PROVED (exact split).** Let $P_h=hh^*$ in the spin indices. Since $H$ and $Q$ are scalar in those indices, $G$ commutes with $P_h$, $h^*Gh=G$ as a scalar spatial operator, and $Gh(h^*Gh)^{-1}h^*G=GP_h$. Hence
$$D_k=aP_h+U_k(I-P_h),\qquad U_k=aI-a^2QGQ^*.\tag{F.2}$$
The scalar $U_k$ is exactly `Green.unconstrainedUnitOperator` with fine block size $n$ and mass $v$; `Localization/DecoupledIdentMain.covOp` contains the constrained Schur complement *plus* a next-scale $a_TL^{-2}P$ term, so it is not the final operator without a new specialization and identification theorem.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the stopped-flow bounds $a\in[1/2,2]$, $v\le1$.** `Green/UnitLattice.lean` has `unconstrainedUnitOperator_lower` and `unconstrained_gamma_ge`, giving $\langle u,U_ku\rangle\ge\gamma_0\langle u,(-\Delta_m+v)u\rangle$ with the explicit $\gamma_0=(8d+3)^{-1}$. For the longitudinal part, $\langle u,a u\rangle\ge\gamma_0\langle u,(-\Delta_m+v)u\rangle$: the torus Laplacian has norm at most $4d$, and $(8d+3)a\ge4d+3/2>4d+v$. ## 3. Massive covariance, square root, and locality

$$|(U_k+t)^{-1}_{xy}|\le {2\over g_*+t}e^{-r_*d(x,y)}.\tag{F.4}$$

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the same bridge.** Equation (F.2) gives $C_k=D_k^{-1}=a^{-1}P_h\delta_{xy}+U_k^{-1}(I-P_h)$; each spin entry is at most $(2+2/g_*)e^{-r_*d(x,y)}$. `Localization/DecoupledCovPrincipal.lean`, `sqrtMat`, uses the Stieltjes integral $\pi^{-1}\int_0^\infty t^{-1/2}(D_k+t)^{-1}dt$.
