The $\delta_1$ inequality controls the physical hat field through $c_\psi$ and has no extra $R_\sigma$ factor. ## 2. Which tilt is needed by the abstract theorem?

P6 (4.63) explicitly writes the measure $d\mu_{1,t}(\beta_k^{-1/2}\sigma,\psi)$: its $\sigma$ multiplies the fluctuation field, not $g$; the Cauchy circle has inverse radius $\beta^{-1/8}\eta^{\gamma-\alpha_1}$. P6 (4.61) separates the localized operator $H_kC^{(k)1/2}$ from the expectation of $\psi'$; (4.64) bounds the latter. `StepComplexOut.M0C` encodes the physical $t$ integral with $W(t)=S.WoneC\,C\,1\,x+S.WtwoC\,C\,g\,t\,x$. `StepActivityDirectGeneral.segAct` represents this by $A_0(1)-A_0(t)+A_g(t)$ on the source segment.

`OPEN-OBS-PHYSICAL-TILT.` To reproduce that paper interpolation, evaluate both direct-term families on $\sigma t\widehat\psi$ and assemble the above difference. `physical_directTerms_eta_scaled` supplies fixed-datum analytic $\sigma$ terms under the ray hypothesis; `ParamActivitySubspace.labelActFamilyOn` transports a given direct-term family through an open parameter chart. `StepComplexJoint.JointRep.W1/W2` supply the full-chart analytic exponents and fibre identities. The missing theorem identifies the assembled complex-$t$ exponent with $W_1^C(1)+W_2^C(g,t)$ on one common relative chart and proves the required complex-$t$ label analyticity. `StepActivityBoundC` only supplies the real segment.

`PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OPEN-OBS-PHYS-ACT.` A cheaper tilt suffices for `Thm31b.ObsMarks`: suppose $A_{\rm phys}(t)$ is a label activity on a complex neighbourhood $\mathcal U\supset[0,1]$, holomorphic there, globally bounded by $\epsilon_{\rm base}$, with $W(t)=\sum_y A_{\rm phys}(t).H_y$ almost everywhere. Define $A(t,\sigma).H_y=\sigma A_{\rm phys}(t).H_y$ for $t\in\mathcal U$, $|\sigma|<R_g$, and $0$ elsewhere. Give it the same labels, entropy and dependence, with prefactor $\epsilon_H=R_g\epsilon_{\rm base}$; zero extension makes the bound global. Since $R_g>1$, $A(t,1)=A_{\rm phys}(t)$ on the physical segment, $A(t,0)=0$, $\sigma\mapsto A(t,\sigma).H_y$ is entire on the disc, and $t\mapsto A(t,1).H_y$ is holomorphic on $\mathcal U$. Boundary continuity is absent from `ObsMarks`.

This scalar interpolation is mathematically valid for `Thm31bSchwarz.Gst_schwarz`: the consumer sees only the physical activity at $\sigma=1$, vanishing at $0$, disc holomorphy and a common bound. It does not assert that intermediate $\sigma$ equals P6's physical measure. Its cost is $\epsilon_H\le(4C_U/C_R)b^{s-1/2}$; require $b^{s-1/2}\le\Theta C_R/(4C_U)$ in the initial threshold. No growing-disc physical assembler is needed for this route. `OPEN-OBS-PHYS-ACT` includes the full-chart source-dependent activity, its exponent identity with `JointRep.W1/W2`, holomorphy in $t$, and a uniform base bound; the current $R_\sigma=2$ fixed-step theorem alone does not discharge it. `LabelAct.cscale` handles factors of norm at most one; a small generic wrapper is needed for the factor $\sigma/R_g$ and prefactor $R_g\epsilon_{\rm base}$.

For the wrapper, first cast $A_{\rm phys}(t)$ to the larger prefactor $R_g\epsilon_{\rm base}$, then multiply it by $\sigma/R_g$ on the disc. The resulting value is $\sigma A_{\rm phys}(t)$; the strict-disc inequality gives $|\sigma/R_g|<1$. Outside the disc, use the zero activity with the same labels.
Only $\sigma=1$ is used in `ObsMarks.hW` and `ObsMarks.hF`. The normalized measure and denominator in `M0C` therefore stay physical; the artificial continuation is solely a proof device for the Schwarz estimate.
The physical $t$ activity must use the same fixed label type and block split throughout $\mathcal U$. Choosing a fresh label expansion at each complex $t$ would not prove `ObsMarks.holt`.

## 3. The observable row: correct variable and missing localization

`PROVED CONDITIONAL ON` the hypotheses of `InteractionFPart.fsite_expansion`: for a root $x\in X_1$, it supplies a finite family $E_Y(D,\psi')$ with exact sum $\delta\phi_x(\psi')=\sum_Y E_Y(D,\psi')$, locality in $\psi'$ on `psiThick Y`, analyticity on `XiCRaw × FlBall Hm δ₁`, and $|E_Y|\le Q\delta_1e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}\operatorname{treeLenCov}(Y)}$, $Q=cK2'(d,L,N)\texttt{famConst}(d,L,BG_2,\kappa)$. `subset_of_mem_polymerDomains` gives $x\in Y$. Under `IsLevel Gp K`, `Prop33Level.treeLenCov_eq_treeLenCovOne` identifies the size in this bound.

For the independent Gaussian coordinate $u\in S.U$, $a\in\mathrm{Fin}\,N$, put $v_{u,a}=S.\texttt{shiftUC}(e_{u,a})=b^{-1/2}C^{1/2}e_{u,a}$ (`StepComplexLocal.shiftUC_eq_smul_mulVec`), and
$$h_Y(x;u,a)=\left.\partial_\zeta E_Y(D,\zeta\,\mathrm{lift}\,v_{u,a})\right|_{\zeta=0},\qquad h(x;u,a)=\sum_Y h_Y(x;u,a).$$
Finite linearity yields $\partial_t\delta\phi_x(t\,S.\texttt{shiftUC}\widehat\psi)|_{0}=\sum_{u,a}h(x;u,a)\widehat\psi(u,a)$. `Background.LinearResponse.linearResponse` identifies this total derivative with the constrained Green response **conditional on** its Standing, solution-family and differentiation hypotheses; it does not localize the row.

`PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OPEN-OBS-COV-NORM.` If $\|\mathrm{lift}\,C^{1/2}e_{u,a}\|_{\mathrm{FlBall\ norm}}\le C_{\rm cov}$ uniformly, then the coordinate disc has radius $\rho_c=\delta_1\sqrt b/C_{\rm cov}$; `ObsCauchyTwoOrders.norm_deriv_zero_le` gives $|h_Y|\le Q C_{\rm cov}b^{-1/2}e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}d(Y)}$. A safe proposed $C_{\rm cov}$ is $\sqrt N\,\texttt{covSupMaxC}$ after proving the norm conversion. This is a size bound for each coordinate, **not a spatial decay bound in the distance from $u$ to $Y$**.

For the remainder along a full cutoff fluctuation $\widehat\psi$, let $q=C_{\rm cov}p_1/(\delta_1\sqrt b)\le1/4$. The scalar ray $E_Y(D,\zeta\,S.\texttt{shiftUC}\widehat\psi)$ has radius at least $1/q$, bound $Q\delta_1e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}d(Y)}$, and derivative remainder at $\zeta=t$ bounded by $8Q\delta_1q^2e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}d(Y)}$ on $|t|<3/2$ by `ObsCauchyTwoOrders.norm_remExt_le`. This supplies the pre-label $8Q C_{\rm cov}^2p_1^2/(\delta_1b)$; label transport is still OPEN-OBS-REM.

`REFUTED: direct bridge of these $h_Y$.` $C^{1/2}e_{u,a}$ generally has nonzero entries far from $u$. Thus $h_Y(x;u,a)$ can be nonzero when `psiThick Y` misses the physical block of $u$. Locality of $E_Y$ in its *physical* fluctuation argument does not imply locality in the independent $\widehat\psi$ coordinate. `MarkedRootGeometry.bridge` requires $(\texttt{psiThick Y}\cap\texttt{fineBlock}\,z).\texttt{Nonempty}$; its cost bound cannot be invoked for this unlocalized $h_Y$. `MarkedHatRows` explicitly masks *after* $C^{1/2}$ for this reason, but its row-agreement statements do not by themselves provide an exponentially decaying coordinate expansion.

In particular, a global coordinate bound on $C^{1/2}$ cannot substitute for its spatial decay: the same bound would allow a flat matrix column reaching every block. The row theorem must charge a path or polymer cost between $u$ and $Y$.

`OPEN-OBS-COVROW.` First localize the operator $H_kC^{1/2}$, as P6 (4.61)–(4.62) requires, or prove an equivalent covariance-column polymer expansion. It must write $h(x;u,a)=\sum_{Y\in\mathcal Y_{u,a}}h^{\rm loc}_Y(x;u,a)$ with exact finite sum, $x\in Y$, a connected domain meeting the mark block or a controlled two-root join, $|h^{\rm loc}_Y|\le Q C_{\rm cov}b^{-1/2}C_{\rm op}e^{-a d(Y)}$, and uniform coefficient-fibre/entropy bounds. `RowsCov.I4c_local_sqrt_rows`, `RowsHat.hatPsiF_rows` and `MarkedHatRows.markedHatPsiF_rows_bridge_of_cover` provide agreement interfaces; an actual operator-row expansion and its decay/count are still required. A two-root bridge is needed if its domains do not meet the mark block; specify its cost and fibre bound in that theorem.

`PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OPEN-OBS-COVROW` in its near-block form: let $z=\texttt{unitBlock1}\,u$ (type `RootIdx d L N₁ m₁ K`), $T_Y=\texttt{blocksOf}\,m_0\,K\,\texttt{Set.univ}\,(\texttt{bridge}\ m_0\,z\,Y)$, and
$$hker(x;u,a,T)=\sum_{\substack{Y\in\mathcal Y_{u,a}\\T_Y=T}}h^{\rm loc}_Y(x;u,a).$$
`RootMarkGeom.cellOf_eq_rootCube` makes the mark cube $bm(u,a)=\texttt{rootCube}\,z$. `MarkedRootGeometry.subset_bridge` retains $x\in Y$ and hence its cube $b_x$; `rootCube_mem_blocksOf_bridge` supplies $bm\in T_Y$ if $\texttt{psiThick Y}$ meets $\texttt{fineBlock}\,z$. `bridge_isDj` and `treeLenCovOne_bridge_le` give cost $c_{\rm Br}=L^d c_G(d,L)$; `fibre_weighted_le` gives $n_{\rm Fibre}=2^{L^dL^d}$.

The mark index is the independent unit site and component $(u,a)$; the bridge index $z$ is its coarse output block. Confusing $u$ with $z$ would give the wrong mark cube and wrong count.

Under the stated coefficient bound with $a=2\kappa_{\rm dec}$, the explicit row constant is $C_{\rm row}=C_{\rm op}n_{\rm Fibre}e^{2\kappa_{\rm dec}c_{\rm Br}}$ and $|hker|\le C_{\rm row}Q C_{\rm cov}b^{-1/2}e^{-2\kappa_{\rm dec}dExt(T)}$, hence also at $\kappa_h=2\kappa_{\rm dec}-1$. The size conversion uses `SizeModG4.G4_dOmega_le` on the bridged `IsDj` domain and the cube/site inverse; it costs no exponent. Exact finite regrouping proves $\sum_T hker(x;u,a,T)=h(x;u,a)$, and the two support clauses of `ObsMarks` follow. All of this remains conditional on OPEN-OBS-COVROW, including the indicated family $\mathcal Y_{u,a}$.

The exact mark multiplicity of a cube $c$ is $N\cdot\#\{u\in S.U:S.\texttt{cellOf}\,m_0\,u=c\}$. `DetCauchyBgActivity.card_blockU_le` gives at most $L^d$ unit points over one coarse index; `RootMarkGeom.cellOf_eq_rootCube` identifies their cube. Thus $n_{\max}\le NL^d$ **conditional on** injectivity of $z\mapsto\texttt{rootCube}\,z$ on the relevant root indices; this small combinatorial lemma is `OPEN-OBS-NMAX`. Use the exact finite maximum if that injectivity is not available.

`PROVED.` `ObsMeanBlock.blockMark`, `integral_blockMark` and `blockMark_fields` close the centered, clipped block-coordinate mean under their stated $p_1>0$ and block-split premises. `MarkedTwoMarkLocal` concerns a different two-mark construction and does not supply the present covariance row.

## 4. Observable amplitudes and class comparison

Assume OPEN-OBS-COVROW and the remaining marked-remainder localization. Take $F_m=p_1\le A_1w$, $n_{\max}\le NL^d$, $C_h=C_{\rm row}Q C_{\rm cov}/\sqrt b$, and $\kappa_h=2\kappa_{\rm dec}-1$. `Thm31b.ObsMarks.B1` then gives
$$B_1\le C_{PE}C_{\rm row}Q C_{\rm cov}A_1 NL^d c_1K_c^2e^{(\min(\kappa_h,\kappa')-\kappa_0)c_G}\ C_R\,b^{-1/2-s}w^4.$$
The factor is $C_R$, since $1/R_g=C_Rw^3/b^s$; the reciprocal $1/C_R$ in `OBSMARKS.md`'s shorthand for this bound was wrong. Let the remainder weighted-label sum satisfy the explicit `OPEN-OBS-REM` estimate $\sum_rF_r e^{-\kappa_0\mathrm{dl}(y_r)}\le C_{\rm rem}\,8Q C_{\rm cov}^2A_1^2w^2/(\delta_1b)$. `ObsCauchyTwoOrders.norm_remExt_le` supplies the analytic $8$ once its disc hypotheses hold, and `ObsMarks.B2` then gives $B_2\le8C_{PE}C_{\rm rem}Q C_{\rm cov}^2A_1^2w^2/(\delta_1b)$.

For $\kappa'=3\kappa_{\rm dec}/2+\kappa_0+1$, $\kappa_h=2\kappa_{\rm dec}-1$ and a sufficiently large fixed $\kappa_{\rm dec}$, `Thm31b` permits $\kappa_b=3\kappa_{\rm dec}/2$. `SmallField.Def6.IsSF.boundM` at $d=L=3$, $j=k+1$ asks for $c_9\,3^{-(k+1)(1/2+\delta_o)}e^{-P.\kappa\,\mathrm{sizeJ}}$, with $c_9=\beta^{-1/4}$ in the phase-1 choice. Require $P.\kappa\le\kappa_b$. `RootActiveSize.isPolyDom_and_dExt_eq_sizeJ` gives $dExt(T)=\mathrm{sizeJ}(\texttt{sitesOf}\,T)$ on nonempty finite-size supports; the remaining rooted output conversion and symmetry are `OPEN-OBS-CLASS`.

After dividing by the class amplitude, the first term is bounded by a fixed constant times $\beta^{-1/4-s}3^{-k(s-\delta_o)}w^4$, and the second by a fixed constant times $\beta^{-3/4}3^{-k(1/2-\delta_o)}w^2$ (also absorb $3^{1/2+\delta_o}$). Since $s>\delta_o$ and $\delta_o<1/2$, enlarge $\beta_0$ once to make their sum at most one uniformly in $k$. This is `PROVED CONDITIONAL ON` OPEN-OBS-COVROW, OPEN-OBS-REM and OPEN-OBS-CLASS; no claimed class witness currently follows from the landed fixed-datum bounds alone.
