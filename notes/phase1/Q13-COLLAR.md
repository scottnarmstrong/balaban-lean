## Verdict

**PROVED:** the history predicates have more information than `chiY`: `ChiDomProp` controls multiscale bonds and spin lengths on its specified sets, and the landed window lemmas transfer part of that information to actual ripe windows. ## 2. The proposed `cutoffCollarPropagation`

**OPEN — precise target.** Let $H$ be as above, $1\le k\le k_0$, and let a retained component and its lineage carry *all* factors of the kept regional kernel, including ancestor `chiDom`, the current four quiet cube products, `chiNew`, and, when a P7 window is formed, its retained small-field functions. For every inherited regional $G_i(P)$, $i\le k$, and for the same physical data $D_i=(\Psi_i,h_*)$, let $T_{i,G}^{(1)}$ be every `InPsiTilde.r1` interface value and $T_{i,G}^{(2)}$ every `r2` spin-square value. The desired assertion is
$$T_{i,G}^{(a)}\le C_{i,G}\delta_i\le(1-s_*)\varepsilon_i\quad(a=1,2),\qquad 0<s_*<1,\tag{2.1}$$
with the same $s_*$ for all admissible histories, $P$, $i$ and both solver masses $0,\nu_i$. R1/R2 are mass independent because $D_i$ is unchanged. The numerical last inequality follows from Q13-MARGIN (1.2) if $C_{i,G}$ is bounded uniformly, or from the paper's polynomial $\widehat W_k$ bound when the appropriate $\widehat W_k\delta_i/\varepsilon_i\to0$ is proved at the actual inherited indices. This is **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** `regionalSupportAssignment`, `referenceR12Margin`, `windowReferenceField` and `collarInterfaceCover`; the condition is not discharged by the current Lean theorems.

**Proof of the conditional reduction.** `ChiDomProp` yields $3dL\delta_{i-1}$ or $3\delta_i$ for each R1 bond and R2 site that its two clauses cover. `bond_le_Creg_of_chiDomProp` (`R/WindowRegSetBond.lean:105`) converts the former to $C_{\rm reg}\delta_i$ under `IsAdmDelta`; `windowOfHistory_hTband_of_chiDom` transfers that bound to bonds with one endpoint in `regSetT`. `chiPrimeNew` and `chiNext` supply the stated current-scale bounds where their cube products act. `chiDom` and `chiNew` supply the fluctuation bounds on their specified sets. For a window datum $D^{\rm ph}$, the conditional collar theorem bounds $D^{\rm ph}.\Psi-D^0.\Psi$ by $C_{23}\widehat W_k\delta_{k_1}$; adding the R1/R2 bounds of $D^0$ and using $|u^2-v^2|\le(|u|+|v|)|u-v|$ gives (2.1) on covered sites. `regionalSupportAssignment` and `collarInterfaceCover` must place **both endpoints** of every inherited R1 interface in those sets. These are the only non-arithmetic steps of this reduction. The additional real source tests then have R3 $\le2\nu_i$ and R4 $=0$ for unit $h_*$, as in Q13-MARGIN §2.

**OPEN — the exact uncovered test.** `XiBounds.collarBond` and `InPsiTilde.r1` include adjacent *collar–collar* sites of each inherited $G_i(P)$. `GradClose` reads bonds based at its tested interior set; `windowOfHistory_hTband_of_chiDom` reads same-layer bonds with one endpoint in `regSetT`; `WinBond` also asks for cross-layer bonds (`R/DeltaPsiDef.lean:104–115`). `inner_collar_data_change_lt_actual_of_chiDom` bounds a **difference of two data fields at one collar site**. None states a total bound for every adjacent pair of collar sites of an arbitrary inherited $G_i(P)$. The paper controls such data through the whole generating-set condition $\tilde\Psi(Y;\delta)$ in P5 (1.24), its support induction P5 (1.22)–(1.25), and the reference/composition estimates of P7 (2.48)–(2.49) (LF-RECONSTRUCTION §§II.3.2, III.4.4). In Lean the relevant predicates exist, but the history-to-inherited-generating-set support and reference estimates are missing **theorems**, not merely a missing characteristic-function definition. A separate `chiFluct` or `chiNext` cannot repair a collar pair outside its own tested cubes.

**OPEN — strict approximate-base tests.** Even after (2.1), `StrictData` also asks for scaled first and second differences of the chosen $\phi_0$, its multiplier, forcing, data change and sphere defect (`Background/ContractionAnalytic.lean:66–82`). Choosing an exact unit massless background makes the sphere tests immediate; BG `BgBounds` bounds the derivatives and multiplier only after a *quantitative* `InDomainR(G_i(P),0,\eta_i)` witness is constructed. P7 (2.49), p. 509, provides a source estimate for derivative and multiplier changes on a specified window region, but a landed theorem assigning it to every inherited $G_i(P)$ and proving fixed slack is still needed. The existential `InUnionDomainR` factor in `quietKernel` (`Operators/Quiet.lean:175–190`) supplies membership for its one local generating set, without a common distance from its boundary.

## 3. Physical same-datum mass path

**PROVED (test arithmetic; short Lean package).** Fix a level-$i$ generating set $G$, a real massless base $\phi_0$ with $|\phi_0(x)|=1$ on $G.\Omega_1$, a real unit $h_*$, and fixed $\alpha_0,\delta\Psi,f_1(0)$. For normalized complex mass $t=mL^{2i}$, use the `Edat` path
$$q(t)=\bigl(\phi_0,\alpha_0,\delta\Psi, f_1(0)\mathbin{\pm}m(\phi_0-h_*)\bigr),\qquad m=tL^{-2i},\tag{3.1}$$
and `massShift G N 0 (q(t),m)`, so its multiplier is $\alpha_0+m$. The sign is fixed by the chosen Euler–Lagrange convention; the bounds are identical. For every $x\in G.\Omega_1$, `spacing_le` gives $G.\mathrm{spacing}(x)\le L^i$, and $|\phi_0(x)-h_*|\le2$. Hence the scaled P4a test changes by at most $|t-u|$ and the scaled P4b test by at most $2|t-u|$ between $t,u$; P1, P2, P3, P4c, P4d and P4e are fixed. Thus the maximum of the eight normalized test values is $2$-Lipschitz, i.e. $C_m=2$. This calculation uses `ContractionAnalytic.StrictData`, `Prop51Mass.massShift` (line 149), `Background/Contraction.IsPerturbed` (line 49), `pertSource` (line 506), and `GeneratingSet.spacing_le` (used in `Background/Def5Response.lean:387`). `BgBounds` is needed to establish the *initial* P1/P2/P4a margin, not for this Lipschitz constant. An explicit Lean theorem for (3.1) is **SHORT**, not landed.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `uniformBaseStrictMargin`:** if all eight `StrictData` tests at $t=0$ have *raw scaled* slack $s_i\ge s_*>0$ against fixed BG thresholds $\delta_0,\delta_1$ uniformly over the selected actual data and $G_i(P)$, then $|t|<t_0:=s_*/4$ preserves slack at least $s_*/2$. This is a separate condition from the $\varepsilon_i$-scale `XiC` margin. `prop51_mass_analytic_uniform` (`Background/Prop51Mass.lean:266`) gives one analytic solution on the union of strict domains with mass-independent $c_6,\delta_*,K_2$, but supplies no such $s_*$. In the genuine early regime, $\nu_i\le Ee^\epsilon\beta^{-1}L^{-i(d-2)}$; choosing initial $\beta\ge8Ee^\epsilon/s_*$ gives $\nu_i\le t_0/2$. The order is: fix geometry and BG constants; prove a history-uniform $s_*$; then choose $\lambda_*$ and $\beta$ to pay the cutoff/domain ratio and the last inequality. The physical identification of (3.1) with the same external datum is an elementary rearrangement of `IsPerturbedM`; a packaged same-datum theorem and the uniform initial margin remain **OPEN**.
