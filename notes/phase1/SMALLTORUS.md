**PROVED (index geometry).** With cover `m₀=1`, `M=L^{m₁+1}`, final output `J=k_f+1`, and admissible `ℓ=qML^J` (`q≥1`), representing the *same* torus as `CTorus d L N₁ m₁ J` forces `N₁=qL`. **PROVED (cheapest existential choice).** Root-F existentially chooses `L`. Taking `L≥6` and retaining `m₀=1`, `M=L^{m₁+1}`, `N₁=qL` makes **every** admissible torus satisfy `5<N₁` and `L∣N₁`.
