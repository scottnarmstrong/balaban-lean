## T1. Landed geometry and exact use

**PROVED (landed theorem).** `Localization/DecoupledFieldX0.lean:269–378`, `scaled_dist_X0`, has `S : DGSetting G a ν`, finite $X_0,X_1$, and
$$h_N:\quad x\in X_0\cap\Omega_1(G),\quad d_T(x,y)<r_0M_1\ell(x)\ \Longrightarrow\ y\in X_1,$$
$$h_r:\quad r_0M_1\le\varrho-1.$$
For $w\in W_{\rm par}(G,X_1)$ it concludes
$$\operatorname{ofReal}(D_{\rm old})\le d_G(x,w),\qquad D_{\rm old}:=(r_0-4dL^3)M_1/L,$$
where $M_1=L^{m_1}$, $\ell(x)=L^{\operatorname{layerIndex}(x)}$, and $d_T$ is the torus $\ell^1$ distance. The theorem itself has no `hr₀` hypothesis. The proof uses $h_r$ first to make $D_{\rm old}\le\varrho-1$, excluding $\operatorname{layerIndex}(w)\ge\operatorname{layerIndex}(x)+2$ via `pathDist_separation_le`, and then to fit the physical lower bound beneath the truncated-distance cap in `pathDist_ge_torusDist`. A site $w$ at layer at most $n+1$ belongs to an `intBox z` with $z.1\le n+2$; `torusDist_box_cube_le` then bounds its distance to a parameter-cube site outside $X_1$ by $4dL^3M_1L^n$. Subtraction from $h_N$ gives the displayed result.

## T3. A precise conditional replacement

**PROVED (mathematical lemma conditional on the stated footprint; not landed in Lean).** Let $G$ be a generating set with $L\ge3$, $\varrho\ge1$, $M_1\ge1$, and define $D:=M_1/L$. Let $X_0,X_1$ be finite, require $h_N$ above with $r_0=5$, and $D\le\varrho-1$. Replace `hr₀` by this *sharp footprint certificate*:
$$\tag{SF4} \forall x\in X_0\cap\Omega_1(G),\ n=\operatorname{layerIndex}(x),\ \forall w\in W_{\rm par}(G,X_1)\cap\Omega_1(G),\ \operatorname{layerIndex}(w)\le n+1\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists y\notin X_1:\ d_T(w,y)\le4M_1L^n.$$
Then the proposed `scaled_dist_X0_sharp` conclusion is $\operatorname{ofReal}(D)\le d_G(x,w)$ for every $x\in X_0\cap\Omega_1(G)$ and $w\in W_{\rm par}(G,X_1)$. If $w\notin\Omega_1$, the path distance is $\top$. Otherwise, a path cost $<D\le\varrho-1$ excludes a jump by two layers (`pathDist_separation_le`), and (SF4) supplies $y\notin X_1$. The contrapositive of $h_N$ gives $d_T(x,y)\ge5M_1L^n$; the triangle inequality gives $d_T(x,w)\ge M_1L^n$. Since $D L^{n+1}=M_1L^n$ and $D\le\varrho-1$, this quantity is also at most $\varrho L^{n+1}-1$. `pathDist_ge_torusDist` proves the claim. The same proof takes the infimum to give `siteSetDist ≥ ofReal D`. For $\varrho=5LM_1$, $D\le\varrho-1$ holds at $L\ge3,M_1\ge1$. If $X_1$ is the whole torus, `parCubes` and $W_{\rm par}$ are empty, so the statement is immediate.

**OPEN (the geometric content).** The landed `torusDist_box_cube_le` proves only a $4dL^3M_1L^n$ loss after the two possible layer rises; it does not prove (SF4). The paper's condition $6M_1\le M$ can help prove $h_N$ for its $X_0,X_1$, but alone says nothing about the landed `Wpar` footprint. A cube-index proof of (SF4), or a revised parameter footprint with a proved comparison to the decoupling operator, is required. Even with (SF4), the existing `DGSetting` consumers cannot be instantiated at $5LM_1$ by the preceding obstruction.

**OPEN (consumer replacements).** Define the sharp composed prefactor by substituting $D=M_1/L$ into $B_{\rm comp}(D)$ and change `ChainNum.close` to $N B_{\rm comp}(M_1/L)\le\delta_3$. Reprove `i4a_close_X0`, `composed_close_X0`, `I4c_close_sqrt_X0` with $D$, and the read-radius lemma from $h_N$ using the direct inequality $d(3L^n-2)<5M_1L^n$ (for $d=3$, it suffices that $M_1\ge2$). The numerical gate then needs a separate proof in the tower's quantifier order; choosing $M_1$ large after the fixed rates is a possible route, subject to the exact dependence of `closeConst` and $\delta_3$. No claim of $e^{-\gamma M}$ follows from this proposed $D$.
