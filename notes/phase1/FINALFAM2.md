## T3. What the tags localize

**PROVED (word algebra).** For a Neumann word in $G_*K^n$ with centers $z_0,\ldots,z_n$ and intermediate sites $x=x_0,\ldots,x_{n+1}=y$, its parameter monomial is
$$\prod_{j=0}^n {\tt tagMon}({\tt macTags}(z_j,x_j,x_{j+1}),s).$$
Its parameter support is the **union** $V=\bigcup_{j=0}^n{\tt macTags}(z_j,x_j,x_{j+1})$ for a nonzero word; repeated tags produce powers, not a new tag. If $s\in{\tt supportedOn}\ C$, the word is zero unless every factor's tag set is contained in $C$. For the mixed coefficient of one such monomial, all tags in $C$ must also occur, so its support is exactly $C$ (with multiplicities allowed). **OPEN:** passing this wordwise assertion through the infinite Neumann sum in Lean; the finite inverse-congruence argument in the next paragraph avoids that interchange. The elementary vanishing of `tagMon V s` when $V\nsubseteq C`, together with the finite tagged sums and inverse congruence, is cheaper in Lean than differentiating an infinite Neumann series. `FinalBoxResolvent.lean:taggedMat_poly` proves polynomial parameter dependence of each factor; `Localization/AbstractExpansion.lean:coef_congr`, `coef_eq_zero_of_inv`, and `coef_congr_of_agree` are the coefficient consumers.

**OPEN Lean targets.** `tagMon_zero_of_not_subset` states $s\in{\tt supportedOn}\ C\land V\nsubseteq C\Rightarrow{\tt tagMon}\ V\ s=0$. `taggedGfin_congr_supportedOn` states: if two tagged families have equal $\chi_zG_z(x,y)$ and $R_z(x,y)$ **whenever** `macTags z x y ⊆ C`, then their `taggedGfin` matrices agree for every $s\in{\tt supportedOn}\ C$; consequently their `coef` values agree. `macTags_cover_box_and_ends` states that, when `macTags z x y ⊆ C`, the whole $B_z$ and endpoints $x,y$ lie in $Y(C):=\bigcup_{p\in C}{\tt macBox}\ p$.
