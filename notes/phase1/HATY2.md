## T1. What `thickSet Y` contains

**PROVED.** Put `K=k+1`, `ℓ=3^K`, and `a=cellSide 3 1 m₁ K=3^(K+1+m₁)`. A `CubeB 1 K univ` is an aligned `a`-box, and `IsDj 1 K Y` is a nonempty connected union of these `π′_K` boxes (`Fluctuation/CubeSystem.lean:36-48`; `SizeModDefs.lean:46-66`). **PROVED.** `approxDep G u` contains `u` and bases of blocks active at `u`; `resSites G x` contains `x`, the full aligned `G.blk (layerIndex x) x`, and every nearest neighbour `x±eμ`; `resDep G x=⋃_{u∈resSites G x}approxDep G u` (`Localization/DecoupledBackgroundLocal.lean:36-53`). At the physical full-level `IsLevel G K`, all sites lie in layer `K`, `G.blk` has side `ℓ`, and `G.Ω 1=univ` (`Localization/FootprintMargin.lean:188-255,327-330`). Thus `thickSet G Y=Y∪⋃_{x∈Y}resDep G x` (`Localization/I5Local.lean:82-84`).

**REFUTED.** `thickSet G Y⊆Y` is false for every nonempty proper `IsDj` domain on the connected full torus. Choose adjacent sites `x∈Y`, `v=x±eμ∉Y`; then `v∈resSites G x`, `v∈approxDep G v`, hence `v∈resDep G x⊆thickSet G Y`. This uses only the nearest-neighbour term, even before spline interpolation. `SupportMarginExample.exists_outside_approxDep` gives the stronger independent fact that a positive-weight active block base may also lie outside a proper full-level cell union (`Localization/SupportMarginExample.lean:114-140`).

**PROVED (exact excess and width).** The excess is exactly `{v∉Y | ∃x∈Y∩G.Ω 1, ∃u∈resSites G x, v∈approxDep G u}`. At a full level, each coordinate of `u` is at periodic lift distance at most `ℓ` from `x`, and each coordinate of `v` at most `2ℓ−1` from `u`; so the excess is in the exterior sup-distance collar of width `3ℓ−1` about `Y` (`FootprintMargin.lean:218-285`). Since `3ℓ−1<a`, an excess site lies in the same or one of the immediately adjacent `π′_K` boxes of a box meeting `Y`; only sites near a boundary can be outside. At mixed layers replace `ℓ` by the relevant `3^(layerIndex x)` and active-layer sizes; the single top-level bound needs `IsLevel G K`.

**PROVED (qualified margin).** `resDep_subset_hatBox` says `resDep G x⊆hatBox z` **when** `z∈cubeIdx G` and `x∈intBox z`; the hat box is the enlarged *interaction box of z*, not a `π′_K` box of `Y` (`FootprintMargin.lean:285-297`; `DecoupledGeometry.lean:121-122`). If also `hatBox z⊆Y₁`, the reads lie in `Y₁`. `footprint_subset_component` and `biUnion_resDep_subset` apply this to eligible sites `SY G X₁ 𝒞 Y₁`, not to all `x∈Y₁` (`FootprintMargin.lean:315-340`). This is the existing margin on which a sharp physical-locality proof can build.
