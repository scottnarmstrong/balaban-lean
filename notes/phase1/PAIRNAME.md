Neither `i4b_analytic` nor pointwise uniqueness supplies the needed ambient extension. The first unmarked failure is the pair-analyticity input to `fanF_analytic` in `InteractionSymmSol.lean:128–137`; the marked failure is `vblock_expansion_hat_marked_joint_pair` at `MarkedEvJoint.lean:485`.

No such retraction-and-agreement theorem was found in the cited interfaces. Its proof would have to cover the actual $U_F$, not just the relative chart.
