# Q13 round 30: transfer to composed inherited fields

**PROVED (scope).** This note uses the landed predicates at the real, fixed unit field $h_*$. Here $G_k=C.\mathrm{genW}(Y)=\mathrm{genSF}_k(W_2(Y))$, $D_k=\mathrm{liftSingle}(G_k,\psi_k,h_*)$, and $F_j=C.\mathrm{psiJ}(Y,\psi_k,h_*,j)$ for $j\le k$. The formula for $F_j$ is `sfPull` of `psiSF`, hence a `composeField` over $G_j^W=\mathrm{genSF}_j(W_2(Y))$ and $G_k$, with $\phi_k=\mathrm{bgFieldC}(G_k,D_k)$ (`Def7SmallField.lean:238–266`, `Background/StepBackgrounds.lean:93–107`). The older block fields tested by history factors are separate variables; equality with $F_j$ is not part of a characteristic function.

**PROVED (verdict).** Composition has an all-site quantitative theorem, so no decay argument is needed *after* $D_k$ is shown to be in a quantitative real background domain on **every** site and interface pair of $G_k$. That antecedent is not established by the present factor assignment, and the regional inherited term has a second chart-agreement obligation. Consequently the full `ComposedFieldTransfer` from active factors is **OPEN**; Theorem 7.3 proves its analytic middle arrow, not its first or last arrow.

## Exact transfer statement and tests

**PROVED (definitions).** `InPsiTilde G ν η Ψ h_*` (`Background/Data.lean:260–277`) has R1 $\|\Psi(z)-\Psi(z')\|<\eta$ for **every** `IsInterfacePair G z z'` (including two collar sites), R2 $\bigl||\Psi(z)|^2-1\bigr|<\eta$ for **every** `dataSites G` site, R3 $\nu L^{2\ell(z)}|1-\Psi(z)\cdot h_*/|\Psi(z)||<\eta^2$, and R4 $\nu L^{2k}||h_*|^2-1|<\eta^2$. `XiBounds` (`SmallField/Xi.lean:75–100`) adds seven strict tests: scaled `grad`, `lap`, `mult`, and `close` of the **background of the composed datum**; `collarBond` of that datum's $\Psi$; scaled `mass` distance of its background to $h_*$; and `hnorm`. R1 supplies `collarBond` and R2 supplies the solver's unit-sphere input, while `theorem71` supplies the first four and mass once the full real domain holds. `StrictData` (`Background/ContractionAnalytic.lean:66–82`) has eight different tests on an approximate base and its forcing; neither R1/R2 nor `XiAt` by itself asserts those eight tests.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `ParentDomainFromFactors`, `CanonicalPrecedes`, `RegionalLocalPairAgreement`, and `ScaledR12` (the proposed `ComposedFieldTransfer`).** For each retained nonzero term and each $j\le k$, choose the canonical local generator $H=B_j(X)$ and a proof $p:H\prec G_k$. `ParentDomainFromFactors` must produce a *specified* $0<\eta_k\le c_1^{\rm BG}/(2C_4K_1)$ and `InDomainR G_k ν_k η_k D_k`, using the actual $\psi_k$ on **all** `dataSites G_k` and **all** R1 pairs. Form $D_{j,X}=\mathrm{composeData}(p,a,\mathrm{bgField}(G_k,a,\nu_k,D_k),D_k)$, where $a=\mathrm{compWeight}(L,a_1)$, $C_4=4d+8$, and $K_1=\mathrm{bgK1}(d,L,N)$. Require $\rho_j=C_4K_1\eta_k$ to satisfy the `lemma62` smallness conditions, $\rho_j\le c_1^{\rm BG}$, and $(K_1+1)\rho_j\le\varepsilon_j-s_j$ for a uniform $s_j>0$. Then $D_{j,X}$ has R1/R2 bounds $<\rho_j$ on **every** datum site/pair of $H$, and `XiAt H a ν_k k 1 ε_j` holds with the displayed R1/R2 slack. `RegionalLocalPairAgreement` says $D_{j,X}.\Psi$ and its source equal the actual $F_j$ pair **on $X$**, so `LocalE`/`LocalM` identify the inherited term. A claim that $F_j$ itself passes R1/R2 on every site of $H$ is stronger and is not supplied by this local-chart transfer.

**PROVED (landed analytic arrow).** `Background/CompositionDomain.lean:440,449–510,618–625` (`compC4`, `lemma62_core`, `lemma62`) proves the child `InPsiTilde` bound $\rho_j=C_4K_1\eta_k$ on its whole data set, including the collar–collar case explicitly treated in `lemma62_core`. `Background/Theorem73.lean:246–300` (`theorem73_real`) packages domain membership and exact restriction of the child background/multiplier from the parent; its `thm73C1` is $c_1^{\rm BG}/(2C_4K_1)$. `SmallField/XiD2.lean:116–153` (`psiTilde_subset_xiAt`) converts the quantitative child domain to all seven `XiBounds` tests when $(K_1+1)\rho_j\le\varepsilon_j$. Thus R1/R2 slack is at least $\varepsilon_j-\rho_j$, and the first four and mass Xi tests have slack at least $\varepsilon_j-K_1\rho_j$ in their scaled units; a positive $s_j$ makes all strict. For a complex trial, `psiTildeC_subset_xiC` requires its own complex witness and the stronger budget $\varepsilon_{\rm trial}+(2K_1+2)(2C_4K_1\eta_k)\le\varepsilon_j$ (see `Q13-MARGIN.md` (2.2)).

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `QuantitativeBgWitness` and `SameDatumMassPath`.** For an exact real reference solution on the **same** $H$ and same datum, `theorem71` yields base gradient/Laplacian/multiplier costs $K_1\rho_j$; the exact sphere gives $|\phi_0|=1$ and zero defect/defect Laplacian, and zero data perturbation gives P4c $=0$. If $K_1\rho_j\le r_0/2$ and normalized mass forcing is $\le r_1/2$, the eight `StrictData(H,N,r_0,r_1)` tests have raw margin at least $\min(r_0/2,r_1/2,1/2)$ (`Q13-TRANSFER.md` §3). This is a separate strict-tube statement, not a consequence of the numerical Xi budget alone.

## Where the factor-to-composition proof stops

**PROVED (factor locations).** `ChiDomProp` (`LargeField/Characteristic/OperatorDomain.lean:129–144`) tests the history multiscale block field on `genBlocksIn` at tolerance $3dL\delta_{i-1}$ in its first family and `scaleBlocksIn` at $3\delta_i$ in its second. `C.chiY` multiplies `Reg` only over cubes of $Y$ (`Def7SmallField.lean:341–349`); its `InUnionDomainR` conjunct is existential in the radius, so it gives no chosen $O(\delta_k)$ R1 witness for the full $G_k$ collar. `Q13-COVER.md` (1) puts retained shell sites within $2\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor+3$ of $Y$ in $\Omega_k''$ and identifies a candidate first-family factor there. The remaining exact bridges are `TopBlockTransport`, `ActiveFactor`, and `FactorBondLift` (especially collar–collar pairs). Sites in $Y$ also need the `chiY` stencil lift. These are the subclaims of `ParentDomainFromFactors`; they are **OPEN** as a universal statement for the actual D1 integrand.

**REFUTED.** “The solver is applied to $\psi_k$ restricted to $W_2(Y)$, therefore its datum is already small field” reverses the implication. `bgSF` is definitionally `bgFieldC` even off the contraction domain, with an off-domain default value in `Background/Def5.lean:57`; the useful `BgBounds` and `theorem73_real` require `InDomainR` first. `C.genW` also has one-site collar data beyond $W_2(Y)$ (`Q13-COVER.md` §2). `chiY` does not test all of those sites, and the conditional shell assignment is not yet a proof of R1/R2 for them.

**OPEN — `CanonicalPrecedes` and `RegionalLocalPairAgreement`.** For canonical $B_j(X)$, `LOCALGEN.md` (3.2)–(3.3) gives a prospective support inclusion under radius and torus transport hypotheses. One must prove $B_j(X)\prec G_k$ and identify the local chart on $X$. The literal $F_j$ uses $G_j^W$, whereas $D_{j,X}$ uses $B_j(X)$; the fields can differ away from $X$ because their layer indices differ there. `SmallField/GsSpecAgree.lean:31–105` lands exactly this **pattern** for `levelGS`: `localComposedData`, `localPairAt_agree`, `localPair_bg_agree`, and term locality `LocalE`/`LocalM` (`:145–158`). Its regional `genSF` analogue is not landed. Moreover `GsSpecStrong` alone allows $B_j(X)=\mathrm{levelGS}\,j$ with whole-torus support (`Q13-TRANSFER.md` §2), so canonical upper support must be an explicit premise.

**REFUTED.** Unit microscopic spins do not imply $|\psi_k|\le1$ for every real $\psi_k$ in D1: `LargeField/Def7.lean:277–290` states D1 for **all** real block fields. The real background has unit length only *after* its domain hypothesis and `theorem71`. No unconditional maximum principle or energy bound for `bgFieldC` on arbitrary far-field data is landed. `Def5Response.lean:287` (`theorem72_linearResponse`) differentiates only inside `InUnionDomainC`; `local_step` and `i4b_local` in `Localization/DecoupledBackgroundLocal.lean` and `Decoupled2BackgroundLocal.lean` compare data already in their complex domains and give exact local agreement under their stated restrictions. Green decay under a standing domain hypothesis does not supply an a priori bound on untested data or a path into the domain. Thus the proposed “tested part plus exponential remote remainder using $B=1$” is not presently a proof.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `UniformDomainPath`, `UntestedAmplitudeBound`, and `WeightedResponseRow`.** A valid replacement would fix a reference top datum $D^T$ agreeing with $D_k$ on tested sites, require every $D^T+t(D_k-D^T)$ in a common `InUnionDomainC`, put $B=\sup_U|D_k-D^T|$, and prove for perturbations supported on untested $U$ a row bound $|\partial_D\phi(x)[E]|\le C_{\rm resp}B e^{-\gamma\,\mathrm{dist}(x,U)/L^j}$. Integration and the `composeField` formula give a composed-site difference $u=C_{\rm comp}C_{\rm resp}B e^{-\gamma r}$ at distance $r$ in scale-$j$ cubes (one may take $C_{\rm comp}=2$ if the direct datum at that site agrees). Then R1 changes by at most $2u$ and R2 by at most $(2B_{\rm ref}+u)u\le5u$ if $B_{\rm ref}\le2$, $u\le1$. For slack $c\delta_j$, require $r\ge\gamma^{-1}\log(5C_{\rm comp}C_{\rm resp}B/(c\delta_j))$. With $\delta_j=A_0\lambda_j^{p_0}e^{-\lambda_j/2}$, this is $r\gtrsim\lambda_j/(2\gamma)-p_0\log\lambda_j/\gamma+O(1)$: logarithmic power $p=1$ in $\beta_j$ if $B$ and the response constants stay bounded. The genuine witness $R_j=32\lambda_j^2$ (`LargeField/Def7WitLF.lean:14–30`) is asymptotically larger, but $r=0$ where an untested site touches the data set; no halo helps there. `Geo.hRlow` alone does not prove these three hypotheses.

**OPEN — `ReferenceR12Margin`.** The reference for each inherited $G_i(P)$ needs the *same* all-site R1/R2 bound, with a specified $\eta_i=O(\delta_i)$ and a comparison to the physical datum on every site (including both endpoints of collar–collar pairs). If the reference parent has quantitative `InDomainR` and the same chart conditions, `lemma62` gives $C_4K_1\eta_k$ for its local composed reference. If physical and reference composed data differ by $u_i\le1$ on every data site, with reference norm $\le2$, their R1/R2 costs increase by $2u_i$ and $5u_i$ respectively (`Q13-TRANSFER.md` §3). The landed window response controls only its stated regions; the required all-site $u_i$ and chart identification are not established.

## Ten-line verdict

1. **PROVED:** `lemma62_core` gives all-site child R1/R2 at $C_4K_1\eta_k$ from a quantified parent domain.
2. **PROVED:** `theorem73_real` restricts the parent background and multiplier to the child.
3. **PROVED:** `psiTilde_subset_xiAt` gives seven real Xi tests if $(K_1+1)C_4K_1\eta_k\le\varepsilon_j$.
4. **OPEN:** `ParentDomainFromFactors` has no universal all-site/collar proof for the active D1 history.
5. **OPEN:** `CanonicalPrecedes` and `RegionalLocalPairAgreement` have not been landed for regional $B_j(X)$.
6. **OPEN:** `ReferenceR12Margin` and the all-site physical/reference response remain separate inputs.
7. **REFUTED:** off-domain `bgFieldC` or real block-field unit bound closes any of lines 4–6.
8. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `ParentDomainFromFactors`, `CanonicalPrecedes`, `RegionalLocalPairAgreement`, and `ScaledR12`:** the local-chart `ComposedFieldTransfer` follows with Xi slack $s_j=\varepsilon_j-(K_1+1)C_4K_1\eta_k>0$.
9. **OPEN:** if those factors fail, an inner characteristic must test all parent and used local-chart R1/R2 data at $\eta_i/2$, including collar–collar pairs; also impose R3/R4 or prove them from real unit $h_*$ and $2\nu_i<\eta_i^2$.
10. **OPEN:** its complementary sector needs an action/small-factor estimate, for example $|T[(1-\chi_{\rm inner})\cdots]|\le e^{-\gamma_{\rm gain}\beta_i\delta_i^2}$ per witness with a rate that pays the polymer count. P5 pp. 493–494 (1.18)–(1.25), p. 508 (2.31)–(2.32), and P6 (2.1)–(2.3) describe existing factors and a quadratic boundary correction, not this added all-site factor or its complementary-sector bound.
