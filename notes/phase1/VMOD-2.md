## 1. The auxiliary generating set and its actual margins

### 1.1 A finite-grid halo lemma

Use cyclic sup-distance of cube indices, and assume the torus has more than five cells at the largest cover scale, as required by `DGSetting.big`. For a union $U$ of $\pi_k$ cubes let $A_0=\operatorname{cl}_{\pi_K}U$ be the union of $\pi_K$ cubes meeting $U$ and let $A=\operatorname{Halo}_{\pi_K}^1 A_0$, the union of the coarse cells at cyclic sup-distance at most one from $A_0$. This is P6 p. 8's extra $\pi_{k+1}$ halo for $j=k$; P6 §4 p. 23 uses the unhaloed closure for the individual interaction expansion. Then:

1. $A$ is a union of $\pi_K$ cubes, hence of $M_1\ell_K$ cells and of $K$-blocks. Its indicator is constant on every $M_1\ell_K$ cell.
2. Every site $x\in U$ has $\operatorname{dist}_\infty(x,A^c)>LM\ell_k$ in fine-site units, up to the harmless convention of whether the terminal site has distance $LM\ell_k$ or $LM\ell_k+1$. In particular, the strict physical ball of radius $r_0^*M_1\ell_k$ lies in $A$ by (A).
3. If $U=Z^{\sim r}$ and $W=Z^{\sim2r}$ on the $\pi_k$ grid, then
$$\operatorname{dist}_\infty(A,W^c)\ge (r-2L-2)M\ell_k.\tag{1.1}$$
The loose integer $2L+2$ absorbs the closure cell, halo cell and boundary convention. In particular $A\subset W$ if $r>2L+2$.

**Proof.** Write a $\pi_K$ cell as an $L^d$ array of $\pi_k$ cells. A cell meeting $U$ contains every $x\in U$ assigned to it. Every coarse cell whose index differs by at most one in each coordinate is in $A$. A site outside $A$ belongs to a coarse cell differing by at least two in some coordinate. From the farthest fine site of the original cell to the first site of that outside cell there is one entire coarse cell, or $L$ fine cells, between them. This proves the second assertion and (A) supplies $LM\ell_k\ge(r_0^*+4L)M_1\ell_k$. A site of $A_0$ is at most $L-1$ $\pi_k$ indices from a site of $U$; the halo adds at most $L$ further indices. Using $2L+2$ for both endpoint conventions, the triangle inequality and $\operatorname{dist}_\infty(U,W^c)\ge rM\ell_k$ give (1.1). All operations are finite unions of aligned cells, proving the first assertion. Periodic wrap changes neither argument: use cyclic index distance and the stated large-torus condition. $\square$

This is the exact geometric content of the *one coarse halo*. In particular, it pays the **old layer-$k$** physical radius $r_0^*M_1\ell_k$ even for an $X\subset U$ touching the outer face of $A_0$. It does not say that the ball lies in a separately chosen $X_1\subset A$.

### 1.2 Construction and separation

Define a depth-$K$ generating set $C$ on the same fine torus by
$$\Omega_n(C)=\Omega_n(H^-)\quad(1\le n\le k),\qquad \Omega_K(C)=A,\qquad\Omega_{K+1}(C)=\varnothing.\tag{1.2}$$
This is the layerwise meaning of $(H^-\cap A^c)\cup A^{(K)}$: the lower domains do **not** get cut out. The top layer is $A$ and layer $k$ becomes $W\setminus A$. The needed premise $A\subset W=\Omega_k(H^-)$ follows from (1.1) and $R_{k-1}\ge R_{\rm base}\ge4L+20$ in `LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` I.1.3 row 7. Nesting and every old separation condition follow immediately because the old domains are unchanged. The only new separation check is
$$x\notin\Omega_k(C)=W,\quad y\in A\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{dist}_1(x,y)\ge(r-2L-2)M\ell_k.\tag{1.3}$$
The original minimal-set separation parameter is $\varrho_3=5LM_1$ (`LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` II.2.3(a)). Since $r\ge4L+21$ and $M/M_1\ge14d$,
$$ (r-2L-2)M\ge(2L+19)(14d)M_1>5LM_1=\varrho_3.\tag{1.4}$$
Equations (1.3)–(1.4) prove the new interface separation for a `GeneratingSet` with parameter $\varrho_3$. The lower layers of $H^-$ are unions of $M_1\ell_n$ cells by the recursive construction of `LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` II.2.2, and $A$ is a union of $M\ell_K$ cells. Thus $C$ satisfies `CubeAligned C m₁`, provided the torus divisibility hypothesis of that declaration holds. These are **proved conditional on the stated, proved paper-level LFR dilation and minimal-set facts**; they are not Lean declarations in the landed localization directory.

The original, unhaloed $A_0$ is also a generating-set top layer under a similar margin, but it fails the edge test below. The halo changes no physical operator and no old domain $\Omega_n(H^-)$.

### 1.3 What the halo does not prove: the $X_1$ collar

`DecoupledFieldX0.lean` `i4a_close_X0` has two distinct premises. Its `hN` requires the physical $r_0M_1\ell_H(x)$-ball around **each** $x\in X_0\cap\Omega_1(H)$ to be contained in the actual interpolation set $X_1$. Its `hr` requires $r_0M_1\le\varrho_H-1$, where $\varrho_H$ is the *global* separation parameter of the operator generating set. Its conclusion is $e^{-\gamma_w(r_0-4dL^3)M_1/L}$; setting $r_0=r_0^*$ gives $e^{-\gamma_wM_1}$.

The finite-grid lemma proves only $B(x,r_0^*M_1\ell_k)\subset A$ for $x\in U$, $H^-$ of layer $k$ there. It does **not** imply `hN` when $X_1$ is the union of the selected output $\pi'_K$ cubes covering the source. For a one-cube source $X$ touching a face of its selected $\pi'_K$ cube, choose $x\in X_0$ on that face and the neighboring site $y$ across the face. Then $\operatorname{dist}(x,y)=1<r_0^*M_1\ell_k$, but $y\notin X_1$. Both sites can lie in $A$, so adding the halo does not repair this `hN`. The repair must either choose $X_0$ with the full collar already in the selected $X_1$, as `VMOD.md` §1.3 proposed, or enlarge $X_1$ and prove a new source-size estimate that charges this enlargement. In particular, setting $X_1=A$ without paying for $A\setminus X$ is invalid for GM2: with an input consisting of one chosen cube, $\mathcal C=\varnothing$ and $d_j^{\rm cov}(X)=0$, the claimed $d_K^{\rm cov}(Y)\le L^{-1}d_j^{\rm cov}(X)$ would force every output containing a large connected $A$ to have size zero.

There is a second, numerical failure. For the minimal $H^-=\mathbb B_k(W)$, `LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` II.2.3(a) provides $\varrho_H=\varrho_3=5LM_1$. At $d=3,L=3$ one has $r_0^*=327$, whereas $\varrho_3/M_1=15$. Thus
$$r_0^*M_1\le\varrho_3-1\qquad\hbox{is false for every }M_1.\tag{1.5}$$
More generally $4dL^3+L>5L$. One may instantiate a `GeneratingSet` with a larger $\varrho$ only after proving a larger *actual* separation; the minimal construction adds just $5L$ layers of $M_1\ell_n$ cells at each nonempty interface, so its stated uniform parameter does not admit that increase. Therefore the landed `i4a_close_X0` cannot be invoked for this $H^-$ merely from LFR II.2.3. The row 5a inequality $(4L+r_0^*)M_1\le LM$ concerns the **history/coarse halo**, not the global separation of the minimal operator set. Conflating these two distances was the main error in `VMOD.md` (1.2).

For the numerical test $d=L=3$, row 5a chooses $m_0=5$, so $M/M_1=243$ and $LM=729M_1$. The one-halo inequality is $339M_1\le729M_1$, while the separate old-operator `hr` inequality would be $327M_1\le15M_1-1$. This demonstrates in one allowed constant row why the halo statement can be true while the landed closeness premise is false.

The same issue appears even before closeness: `Decoupled.lean` `DGSetting.cover_sep` requires $\varrho_H\ge4dLM_1$. For $d\ge3$, $5LM_1<4dLM_1$. Thus the landed same-set DG and NC3 wrappers cannot be instantiated for the minimal $H^-$ with the separation parameter supplied by LFR. A direct geometric proof for these particular recursive sets could replace the wrapper's global premise; the wrapper itself is not such a proof.

An exact repair for closeness is a *local* replacement for `scaled_dist_X0` and `siteSetDist_X0`, with the physical $X_0$-to-$X_1^c$ collar and the actual layers on that collar as hypotheses, rather than `hr`. Inspecting `scaled_dist_X0` shows why this is additional work: `hr` is used first to infer that a short path cannot jump more than one layer, and again to bound the separation term in `pathDist_ge_torusDist`. The halo lemma alone supplies neither inference for a general $w\in Wpar(H,X_1)$. Name the replacement **OPEN-LOCAL-X0-DIST**. Its target lower bound is exactly $\operatorname{siteSetDist}_H(x,Wpar_C(X_1))\ge M_1$ for $r_0=r_0^*$, with $C$ defining parameter incidence and $H$ defining path distance. This local statement would give the same exponential after the existing `i4a_close` argument; it is not proved by `i4a_close_X0` as landed.

### 1.4 The block-count constant is not available from the landed theorem

`Localization/BlockCount.lean` `blockCount_fam` concludes
$$n_C(Y)\le 3^{d+1}L^{2d+1}\bigl(d_C^{\rm cov}(Y)+1\bigr).\tag{1.6}$$
The factor $L$ in $L^{2d+1}$ is present in the declaration and in `LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` I.1.3 row 5. The theorem assumes, in the present notation,
$$\varrho_C\ge4dL^{1+m_0+m_1}=4dLM.\tag{1.7}$$
It also assumes $L^{m_0}\mid N_1$, a chosen family and a connected tree witness. The auxiliary $C$ just constructed is known uniformly with $\varrho_C=5LM_1$ because its old interfaces are exactly those of $H^-$. Since $M/M_1\ge14d$, the premise (1.7) is unavailable by a factor of at least $M/M_1$. Increasing the new-interface margin in (1.3) does nothing to the unchanged old interfaces. Thus (1.6) is a **proved cited theorem conditional on a hypothesis not discharged here**, not a proved block count for $C$.

This is not a counterexample to the numerical inequality (1.6) itself. It is a counterexample to the proposed application of `blockCount_fam`. A valid repair is **OPEN-BLOCKCOUNT-MINIMAL**: prove (1.6) directly for the recursive $5L$-cell minimal sets and the one inserted top layer, or replace it by $n_C(Y)\le C_{d,L}^{\sharp}(d_C^{\rm cov}(Y)+1)$ and redo row 5 and every entropy constant with the proved $C_{d,L}^{\sharp}$. The current `blockCount_fam` proof calls `layer_near` with (1.7); deleting that binder is not a syntactic refactor.

The *cover-scale* counts can nevertheless retain their stated constants by a different argument. The recursive old interfaces have cyclic **sup** separation at least $5LM_1\ell_n=5M_1\ell_{n+1}$ between $\Omega_n^c$ and $\Omega_{n+1}$; (1.3) gives at least this at the inserted interface. Let $x\in C.\operatorname{layer}n$ and $\chi_C(z,x)\ne0$ for a center of scale $m$. The bump support puts $x$ within $\frac32M_1\ell_m$ in each coordinate of that center. If $m\ge n+2$, then $x\notin C.\Omega_{m-1}$ and the center lies in $C.\Omega_m$; separation gives distance at least $5M_1\ell_m$, a contradiction. If $n\ge m+2$, the center is outside $C.\Omega_{m+1}$ and $x\in C.\Omega_{m+2}$; separation gives at least $5LM_1\ell_{m+1}$, also a contradiction. Hence $|m-n|\le1$. At each of these at most three scales, an interval of radius $\frac32$ aligned cells contains at most three centers per coordinate. Therefore
$$\#\{z:\chi_C(z,x)\ne0\}\le3^{d+1}.\tag{1.8}$$
This proves the same numerical multiplicity as `Cover.lean` `card_chi_ne_zero_le` directly from the *sup* geometry, although the landed theorem itself still requires the stronger $\ell^1$ separation binder.

Likewise let $z'$ be a parameter center in $\mathcal Q_C(z)$ and let $y$ be the witness in its aligned cell meeting $\widehat D_z$. By `Cover.lean` `hatRad`, $\operatorname{dist}_\infty(y,z)<(5/2+3/4)M_1\ell_m$ when $M_1\ge4L^2$. If $z'$ has scale $n\ge m+2$, then $z\notin C.\Omega_{m+1}$ and $y\in C.\Omega_{m+2}$; separation is $\ge5LM_1\ell_{m+1}>3.25M_1\ell_m$. If $m\ge n+2$, then $y\notin C.\Omega_{m-1}$ and $z\in C.\Omega_m$; separation is $\ge5M_1\ell_m>3.25M_1\ell_m$. Both contradict the witness distance, so again $|n-m|\le1$. The remaining coordinate count in the proof of `Cover.lean` `card_Qset_le` uses only alignment and bounds at most $(8L)^d$ centers per scale. Thus
$$|\mathcal Q_C(z)|\le3(8L)^d=:N_0(d,L).\tag{1.9}$$
The finite-grid proofs of (1.8)–(1.9) are complete; replacing the strong-separation calls in the Lean wrappers by these sup-separation lemmas remains implementation work. They do not establish (1.6), whose neighborhoods have the much larger side $M\ell_n$.

### 1.5 The two-component test history

Here is a small **finite-grid test** for the proposed inference; admitting precisely this configuration under the full region-history rules is a separate verification. Along one coordinate take three consecutive $\pi_K$ cells $Q_0,Q_1,Q_2\subset U\subset W$, with $Q_0$ at an outer face of $A_0$. Let the new charged region contain $Q_0\cup Q_2$, let $B=Q_1$ be one full component of $\Omega_K^c\cap\Omega_k$, and take the connected modular source $X=Q_0\cup B\cup Q_2$ (times one cell in the other coordinates). All old sites of $X$ are charged, while $X\cap\Omega_K=Q_0\sqcup Q_2$ has two components. The halo adds the cell $Q_{-1}$ beyond the outer face of $Q_0$, so the finite-grid proof gives the old-layer physical collar even at that face. Choose $X_0$ containing the last site of $Q_0$ at that face. The source witness tree can run along the three cells. This is the smallest combinatorial case any claimed modular cover lemma must handle; the earlier failed global-separation inequalities already give a counterexample to the proposed use of the landed theorems without relying on history admissibility.

This test exposes two different omissions. If $X_1$ consists only of the output cubes covering $X\cap\Omega_K$, then $X_1$ is disconnected and the original tree $\Gamma_0\subset X$ passes through $B$, so $\Gamma_0\not\subset X_1$. One cannot use the ordinary `gm2_tree` argument or the one-component `AE2` setup with this $X_1$. If one instead completes $X_1$ by the **whole** component $B$, $X_1$ can be connected, but $B$ may extend beyond $A$ and its physical connecting length must remain charged in the modular size. The proof obligation is a relative component closure with a connected base, preservation of the chosen tree, and no added charged output cube. This is exactly **OPEN-GM2-HOLE-GEOMETRY** of `VMOD.md` §1.3. `Polymer/Hole.lean` `pe_hole` is a counting theorem for its Steiner size and does not establish this geometric base case. This example does not refute a correctly formulated modular GM2; it refutes the proposed argument that the one coarse halo alone settles it.

## 2. NC for a cube of $C$ cut from $H^-$ or $H^+$

### 2.1 What the landed proofs actually assume

For an active center $z$ of $C$, let $D_z=\square_z(C)$, side $5M_1\ell_{m(z)}$, and cut precisely the $H$-bonds crossing $D_z$, where $H=H^-$ or $H^+$. The separation and alignment of **$C$** prove that $D_z$ is a coordinate cube; they do not assert that its center belongs to $H.\operatorname{layer}m(z)$. This matters because `BoxCube.lean` `alignedCube_box` and `CubeRegimes.lean` `AlignedCube.foldSetting` take **the same generating set** for the center layer and the operator.

The reusable declaration is instead `ReflectedGeneratingSet.lean` `FoldSetting H q s c`. It asks: $1\le q\le\operatorname{depth}(H)$; positive side $sL^q$ smaller than the torus; $L^q$-divisible corner; **every point of $D_z$ and its one-site coordinate collar belongs to $H.\Omega_1$**; and $D_z\cap H.\Omega_{q+1}=\varnothing$. Under those exact hypotheses, `NC3.lean` `pointwise_cut_cube`, `holder_pair_cut_cube`, `value_cut_cube`, and `laplacian_cut_cube` already apply to the cut operator of $H$ and retain their constants depending on $(d,L)$, independent of the cover. The reflected generating set, metric, operator and source identities are `reflectedGS`, `pathDist_fold_le`, `operator_intertwine`, `reflU_inv`, and `reflSol_reflU` in `ReflectedGeneratingSet.lean`, `ReflectionMetric.lean`, `ReflectionOperator.lean`, `ReflectionGreen.lean`, `ReflectionSource.lean`, and `NC3.lean`. No proof inside those declarations equates cover and operator sets; the identification happens upstream in `alignedCube_box`.

Consequently the reflection transfer is **not verbatim** as a proof of the mixed pair. One can reuse all of R1–R4 and the final NC3 bounds *after* a pair-specific `FoldSetting` proof. For a top cube $m=K$ and the old $H^-$, choose $q=k$, $s=5M_1L$, and the same box corner: $sL^k=5M_1L^K$, the corner is $L^K$-aligned and hence $L^k$-aligned, and `noTop` is automatic since $H^-.\Omega_{k+1}=\varnothing$. Its cube and collar lie in $H^-.\Omega_1$ because (1.1), row 7 and (A) give more than $\frac52M_1\ell_K+1$ from $A$ to $W^c$. This proves the *top, old-operator* folding statement.

The lower old-operator cubes also fold, by a direct version of R0 for the recursive minimal set. A center of $C$ layer $m<K$ is a center of $H^-$ layer $m$ (for $m<k$ this is equality of layers; for $m=k$ it is a subset). For $m\ge2$, its complete aligned $M_1\ell_m$ center cell lies in $H^-.\Omega_m$. By II.2.2, $H^-.\Omega_{m-1}$ contains every $(m-1)$-cell at cyclic sup-index distance at most $5L$ from that cell. A point of the $5M_1\ell_m$ interaction box, and any one-site coordinate neighbor, is at sup-distance at most $3M_1\ell_m+1$ from the center cell; in $(m-1)$-cell units this is at most $3L+2\le5L$ because $L\ge3$. Thus the box and collar lie in $H^-.\Omega_{m-1}\subset H^-.\Omega_1$. If $m+2\le k$ and a box point belonged to $H^-.\Omega_{m+2}$, the same recursion would put the center in $H^-.\Omega_{m+1}$: the center-to-box-point sup-distance is at most $3M_1\ell_m$, whereas the $(m+1)$-level added collar is $5L M_1\ell_{m+1}=5L^2M_1\ell_m$. This contradicts that the center is in layer $m$. If $m+2>k$, absence of $\Omega_{m+2}$ is automatic. The corner is divisible by $L^{m+m_1}$ and hence by $L^q$ for $q=\min(k,m+1)$; the side and torus bounds are the cover arithmetic in `BoxCube.lean`. Therefore `FoldSetting H^- q s c` holds for every lower $C$ cube of scale $m\ge2$. This proof uses the *sup-index* recursion rather than the generic `AlignedCube.rho=4dLM_1`, which fails for this minimal set. At $m=1$, use the separate bounded-scale NC3 route after proving source scale $j'\le2$ from the same recursion. The corresponding Lean bridge from the LFR recursive construction remains to be implemented, but the geometric argument is complete under that cited construction.

For $H^+$, the top auxiliary layer includes $A\setminus\Omega_K(H^+)$, where the physical operator may have an internal Dirichlet wall. A $C$ top cube can meet $H^+.\Omega_1$ but contain sites outside it, or have its face on that wall. No consequence of $A\subset W$, cube alignment or the new top-interface separation forces `FoldSetting.mem_Ω` or `FoldSetting.collar` for $H^+$. For a concrete abstract counterexample, take a wide aligned $A$ and choose an admissible minimal operator set generated by a proper aligned island strictly inside $A$. Place an auxiliary $M_1\ell_K$ center cell so that its $5M_1\ell_K$ interaction cube straddles the island's $\Omega_1$ boundary. The center is in $C.\operatorname{layer}K$, the cube has active $H^+$ sites, but a point of the cube is outside $H^+.\Omega_1$. The `FoldSetting` conclusion is false. The present source history has precisely such potentially proper $U_{k+1}$ pieces (`LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` II.2.4(iv)); an additional history-specific exclusion of the straddling configuration has not been proved. The face case is sharper: if the physical $\Omega_1$ wall coincides with a cube face, reflecting the cut cube as a Neumann face changes the retained Dirichlet wall form, so `operator_intertwine` does not follow from the existing R3.

### 2.2 The parts of NC that are independent of reflection

Let `BlockAligned H D_z` mean that the cube contains or misses every complete $H$-block of every layer it meets; equivalently its cut is `CutAligned H (cubeCut H D_z)` by `Multiscale/CutOperator.lean` `cubeCut_aligned`. This is the exact geometric obligation for NC1 and NC2. A sufficient local condition is
$$\forall x\in D_z\cap H.\Omega_1,\quad\operatorname{layerIndex}_H(x)\le m(z)+m_1;\tag{2.1}$$
the cube faces are multiples of $L^{m(z)+m_1}$, so a smaller $H$-block cannot cross a face. This is the same arithmetic as `BoxGeometry.lean` `box_sameCell` and `box_blockAligned`, with (2.1) replacing their same-set `layer_le_of_mem_box`. On the artificial top layer (2.1) is immediate because $\operatorname{depth}(H)\le K=m(z)$. Elsewhere it is a **mixed layer-bound obligation**, not a theorem of `CubeAligned C m₁` alone.

For $H^-$, the direct R0 argument of §2.1 gives $j\le m(z)+1$ throughout a lower interaction cube (its `noTop` statement), so (2.1) follows since $m_1\ge2$. Thus the old-operator cube cuts are block-aligned. For $H^+$ the same implication still needs a comparison of its physical layers with the layers of $C$ on each box; the fact that its top region lies in $A$ suffices on top boxes but does not settle lower boxes meeting the new physical interface.

Conditional on (2.1), `Multiscale/CutOperator.lean` `cubeCut_aligned` and the landed cut coercivity/Agmon chain give NC1 (invertibility) and NC2 (block $L^2$ decay) for the operator $H$, with the same potential bound $\|\alpha(x)\|\le\operatorname{coerciveConst}(d)/(2\ell_H(x)^2)$, admissible weights, $0\le\nu$, and the mass cap of `DGSetting`. For the $x\ge0$ covariance variant, `Localization/XCutOperator.lean` `cutXOperator_isUnit` and `x_agmon_energy_cut` give the corresponding NC1/NC2 under its `CutAligned` and coefficient assumptions. This is **proved conditional on the geometric `BlockAligned` hypothesis**, using cited landed results. Neither energy theorem supplies the pointwise/Hölder NC3 bound for a mixed wall cube. The $m=1$ bound may use `NC3Small.lean` `small_value_cut`, `small_pointwise_cut`, `small_laplacian_cut`, `small_holder_pair_cut`, but each requires source-block scale $j'\le2$; the same-set deduction of that scale in `DecoupledCube.lean` uses `layer_le_of_mem_box` and must be replaced by (2.1) strengthened to $j'\le2$ for a layer-1 center.

NC4 is then the finite-dimensional inverse identity: the entries of $H_{D_z}(\alpha)$ are affine in $\alpha$, NC1 gives a nonzero determinant throughout the admissible open set, and the adjugate divided by that determinant is holomorphic. Uniform NC2/NC3 constants on the admissible set give the required analytic bounds. This proves **analyticity conditional on NC1 and the missing mixed NC3 estimate**, with no new reflection argument. The $x$-variant needs only the block $L^2$ bounds in `LOCALIZATION.md` DG7, not pointwise NC3; its uniformity in $x\ge0$ is the landed `XCutOperator.lean` energy statement.

### 2.3 Minimal repair for mixed NC3

There are two sufficient routes; neither has a landed theorem for the stated mixed history.

* **Face-compatible reflection.** Prove for each active $(C,H,z)$ an explicit `FoldSetting H q s c`, or a signed/Dirichlet reflection theorem with the same cut operator when a wall meets a face. Then call `pointwise_cut_cube`, `holder_pair_cut_cube`, `value_cut_cube`, `laplacian_cut_cube` without changing their proofs. The top $H^-$ cube satisfies the ordinary branch above. The top $H^+$ cube straddling a physical wall requires the new branch. Its statement must preserve the two-center Hölder right side; `NC3-astra.md` §R0 and `NC3.lean` `holder_pair_cut_cube` show why a one-center estimate for disconnected touching blocks is false.
* **Interior equation.** Prove NC3 directly for a `CutAligned H` cube at observation sites in the support of $\chi_z(C)$, with no cut bond meeting their $2L^2\ell_H$ interior balls, using the local `lemma_loc_a/b/c` and the cut NC2 estimate.
