**OPEN recommendation (b), five reasons.** (1) The numerical and geometric general leaves are already present. > **Decision text for the author (OPEN proposal).** Choose a parallel general record and endpoint interface, with each old $3,3,2$ theorem kept at its exact statement and converted to a corollary when its general proof lands.
