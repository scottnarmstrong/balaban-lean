## 3. The non-factorization of the cutoff: how the papers handle it

**PROVED (mechanism).** The full cut integrand does not factor over the cut because the P5 bond test |∂φ(b)| < δ₀ of a good cube reads spins on both sides (`chiBondB`), and e is extended by the retained spin. The papers do not interpolate that factor. They delete it first: on the support of the new fluctuation cutoff (|ξ_z| small, P5 (2.39)–(2.47), cutoff in the real Gaussian coordinate ψ′) every old factor χ′⁽⁰⁾(□) that reads an integrated site equals 1 (P5 Lemma 2.1 = LF-RECONSTRUCTION Lemma III.7.1 = P4 (4.11)–(4.12) at k ≥ 1); the factors that remain read only retained spins and θ, so they leave the integral (BASE-KERNEL-4 Lemma 2.2, proved under the margin inequalities (2.4)/(2.6)). The cut interpolation then acts only on the Gaussian covariance with the cutoff fixed in the real ψ coordinates (LF-BASE-KF0 (A.2)); the product reference measure of ψ′ is what the marked-cube expansion needs.
**OPEN (CT, the margin lemma).** The background inequalities (2.6) for every full footprint, including bonds across ∂Ω₁″, do not follow from `spins_near_background` (it only gives C_hs L δ₀ ≫ δ₀/4). No provider exists (BASE-KERNEL-4 §5 row 3). BASE-KERNEL-4 Counterexample 2.1 shows a record-wide half-threshold margin is false; only the footprint J₊(Z′) is claimed.
