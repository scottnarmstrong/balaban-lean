**PROVED definition.** `HasLocPrinciple G Dom r Ys dY κ Kloc Kcl f u` means there is one family $t_Y((\Psi,h),\xi)$ such that, for every $D\in Dom$ and $\xi\in P_r$, $f(D,u(D,\xi),\xi)=\sum_{Y\in Ys}t_Y(D,\xi)$; each term is jointly `AnalyticOnNhd ℂ` on `expDom G Dom r`; equal $\xi$ and `RawAgree Y` imply equal terms; and *for every fixed* $\xi\in P_r$ and $M$, the bound $\|f(D,v,\xi)\|\le M$ for all $D\in Dom$, $v\in Kcl(D,\xi)$ implies $\|t_Y(D,\xi)\|\le Kloc\,M e^{-\kappa dY(Y)}$. This last implication is stronger than a bound only at $u(D,\xi)$. `IsLocExp` has the same sum, joint analyticity and `RawAgree` fields, plus the direct bound $\|t_Y\|\le B e^{-\kappa dY(Y)}$ on `expDom`. Both definitions allow a set-valued `Dom`; they do not themselves assert that it is open.

**PROVED formulas.** `renC1On` is $\sum_{x\in B}\texttt{renC1Site}(x)$: on $\Omega_1$ its site density has the block terms $(\xi_3/2)(\Psi-Q\phi)^2$, $(1+\xi_3)[\zeta\Psi(\Psi-Q\phi)+(\zeta^2/2)\Psi^2]$, and $(\nu\xi_2/2)(\phi-h)^2$, plus at every site $(\xi_3/2)$ times the bond energy; $\zeta=\varepsilon\xi_1$, with bilinear complex squares (`RenormExpBounds.renC1Site`, `renC1On`). `renC2On` sums over $x\in B\cap\Omega_1$ the density $\frac12(1+\xi_3)\sum_i\delta\phi_i(x)[H_D^{(0)}(\alpha_u+\nu_u)\delta\phi]_i(x)$ (`renC2Site`, `renC2On`). `renMassU ν ξ = ν(1+ξ 1)/(1+ξ 2)` (`RenormExpLemma35.renMassU`); `renMassU_sub_norm_le` gives $|\nu_u-\nu|\le(32/15)\nu\|\xi\|$ when $\nu\ge0$, $\|\xi\|\le1/16$.

**PROVED conditional solution.** `IsRenSol G a ν εζ ε r B F` says $F$ is jointly analytic on `renDom G a ν ε r`, solves `IsPerturbedM` with mass `renMassU ν ξ`, background $(\phi_D,\alpha_D)$, sources $\varepsilon_\zeta\xi_1\Psi$ and $(\nu_u-\nu)(h-\phi_D)$, has `Small` and `SupBounds` at $K_2B\|\xi\|$, and is unique among sufficiently small solutions. `exists_isRenSol` constructs it with $\varepsilon_\zeta=\varepsilon$, $B=8\varepsilon$, $r\le1/16$, $K_1C_1\varepsilon+3r\le\delta_*$ and $8\varepsilon r\le\delta_*$, under its `BgHyp` and class smallness premises. `renSol_mem_pertClass` and `XiCOneReal.mem_bgClass` put the true fields in the two `Kcl` classes. These results concern $s=1$; they do not provide a common decoupled $F_s$.

## Reuse and the three open principles

The physical theorem must transport its `CubeB`/cover polymers to the support type of `RenormExpInput` and fix a nonnegative $dY$ with a scale uniform fibre count.

**PROVED reusable engine; OPEN substitution.** `LocalityAEOn` and `abstract_expansion_on` in `I5Param.lean` turn one analytic decoupling function $A(D,\xi;s)$ on $|s_P|<e^{\kappa_1}$, a uniform $M$, and a two-data agreement law into connected coefficients: coefficient norm $\le M e^{-(\kappa_1-1)|C|}$, vanishing off connected labels and equality under `Ag C`. The coefficient is formed with the *same* $s$ for the background and perturbation. Grouping labels by $Y(C)$ gives the required principle only after proving $\sup_{k,N_1,Y}\ e^{\kappa dY(Y)}\sum_{C:Y(C)=Y}e^{-(\kappa_1-1)|C|}\le Kloc<\infty$, with the correct rooted and geometric weights; this is the exact constant obligation, not a landed numerical value. The admissible $M$ is any bound over the whole decoupled `Kcl`, so this grouping must preserve the universal transfer clause. `i5_functional_hat_dir` is a **PROVED conditional** fluctuation-functional result with a hat variable and its own `FlReal` domain; its theorem statement is not a direct instance for these three functions. `Decoupled2BackgroundLocal.i4b_local`, `Decoupled2FieldLocal.i4a_local`, and `Decoupled2SolveAnalytic.i4a_analytic` provide the background and mode-U comparison/analyticity mechanisms **conditional on their cover, `DecHyp`, `DataAgree`, `FamSpec` and strict-domain hypotheses**. The first two are stated at fixed real base mass. None asserts the following three principles on `XiCOneReal`.
