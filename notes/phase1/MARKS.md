## 2. The proposed differentiation and its exact obstruction

**REFUTED.** The claim that $G_{z,Y}=D_{\psi'}T_Y[1_{B(z)}\psi']$ is directly defined by the landed `DirectTerms` is false: $T_Y$ is a function of two **hat** variables, two ray directions, $u$, and $\Theta$, while the masked direction in (1) is a **physical-shift** direction. On the invertible covariance chart, write $K_\Theta=C^{(k)1/2}(\Theta)$ and $M_z=\texttt{maskUC}\ z$. For $y=\beta_k^{-1/2}K_\Theta x$, the hat direction corresponding to $M_z y$ is
$$h_z(\Theta,x)=K_\Theta^{-1}M_zK_\Theta x,\qquad D_xW_1(u,x)[h_z]=u\,f_z(u,x).\tag{3}$$
The factor $u$ follows from $W_1(u,x)=\Phi_1(u\beta_k^{-1/2}K_\Theta x)$. Formula (3) also requires a complex inverse and the appropriate chain rule. At $u=0$ the quotient must be extended analytically. `Kinv` (`StepObjects.lean`) is defined using a real matrix inverse; `Kinv_K` (`StepIdentity.lean`) proves it is an inverse under its regularity hypotheses, while `isUnit_det_covSqrtC` (`StepComplexLocal.lean`) proves pointwise complex invertibility under its own hypotheses. A holomorphic inverse on the chosen family chart $V$ would still be needed to use (3) as a family formula.

**REFUTED.** Sharp locality of $H_Y$ in the hat variables assigned to cubes of $Y$ implies $D_xH_Y[h]=0$ when $h$ vanishes on those variables. It does **not** imply vanishing when $Y$ misses the cubes of the marked unit sites in $B(z)$, since $h_z=K_\Theta^{-1}M_zK_\Theta x$ can be nonzero on distant hat variables. A two-site algebraic example proves the implication false: for $K=\begin{psmallmatrix}1&t\\t&1\end{psmallmatrix}$, $0<|t|<1$, let $H_{\{2\}}(x)=x_2^3$, $y=Kx$, and mark the physical site $1$. Then $\partial_{y_1}H_{\{2\}}(K^{-1}y)=3x_2^2(K^{-1})_{21}$ is generally nonzero although its hat label is $\{2\}$. This refutes the proposed support test **as a deduction from the landed locality fields**; it does not assert that the Balaban covariance equals this toy matrix.

**REFUTED.** The claim that a Cauchy estimate for $D_xT_Y$ follows from `LocInt.analytic` is false: that field is analytic in $(\Theta,u)$ at fixed hat fields, with no complex hat-field neighbourhood or bound there. The needed derivative could be recovered from the explicit analytic expansion before its information is reduced to `LocInt`, but is not contained in the current `DirectTerms` interface. Continuity of $T_Y$ on `physCarrier` does not by itself imply continuity of $D_xT_Y$ there.

**PROVED (root geometry).** The types matter: `S.blockU z` is a set of **unit-lattice** points, whereas `rootCube z` (`RootPavingMap.lean`) is a cube of the **fine** torus. Define the fine lift $\widetilde B(z)=\{x:\texttt{unitBlock1(unitBlock x)}=z\}$; `unitBlock1_unitBlock` (`DecoupledCovUnit.lean`) identifies it with the fine coarse block of side $L^{k+1}$, based at `rootSite z`. Its points lie in `rootCube z`, the $\pi_{k+1}$-cube of side $a=L^{k+1+m_0+m_1}$ (`PolymerGeometry.cellSide`). Every $b\in\texttt{S.blockU z}$ has its representative `unitRep b` in $\widetilde B(z)$ by `unitBlock_unitRep` (`StepObjects.lean`). Equivalently, `S.cellOf b = rootCube z` (`HatLabelsSplit.lean`): both are the unique aligned $\pi$-cube containing that representative. `IsDj` (`SizeModDefs.lean`) means a connected union of $\pi'_{k+1}$-cubes of side $La$, each with base aligned to the $a$-grid. If $Y$ meets $\widetilde B(z)$ at $x$, `IsPiPrimeUnion` supplies such a $\pi'$-cube $P\subseteq Y$ containing $x$. ## 3. A viable rooted construction and the missing analytic estimate

**OPEN (ROOT-MARK-EXP).** Introduce a *separately marked* decoupled expansion before covariance assembly. Here $\Phi_1^\mathbb C(\Theta,\cdot)$ denotes the required analytic extension to the ambient parameter chart; it agrees with the landed complex step on the physical constant-field slice. Use the same sitewise clamp as `StepSetting.hatOf` (`HatLabelsDep.lean`): for an arbitrary block configuration $\Phi$ set $x_\Phi=\texttt{hatOf}_{\rho}(\Phi)$ and $y_\Phi=\beta_k^{-1/2}K_\Theta^\mathbb C x_\Phi$. Then $x_\Phi$ stays in the fixed hat disk for **every** $\Phi$. Expand the difference
$$\Delta_z(\Theta,u,t,\Phi)=\Phi_1^\mathbb C(\Theta,u y_\Phi+tM_zy_\Phi)-\Phi_1^\mathbb C(\Theta,u y_\Phi)=\sum_{Y\in\mathcal Y_z}R_{z,Y}(\Theta,u,t,\Phi)$$
on an open $t$-disc about zero, with one finite domain family $\mathcal Y_z$ independent of $(\Theta,u,t)$. The physical insertion is held at $B(z)$ while varying the covariance-decoupling parameters; the initial occupied $\pi_{k+1}$-cube is `rootCube z`, and every coefficient is indexed by a connected `IsDj` domain $Y$ containing it. Set $M_{z,Y}=\partial_tR_{z,Y}|_{t=0}$. Differentiating this **finite** identity gives $\partial_t\Delta_z|_{t=0}=D\Phi_1^\mathbb C(\Theta,u y_\Phi)[M_zy_\Phi]=\sum_YM_{z,Y}$. For $\rho\ge p_1$, `StepSetting.hatOf_restr` (`HatLabelsDep.lean`) and the support of $\nu_{p_1}$ identify $y_{BS.\mathrm{restr}\,x}$ with `shiftUC x` almost everywhere, so the real-slice identity is exactly (2). This route inserts an independent $t$, so it does not divide by $u$ at zero. The resulting marks must satisfy
$$\sum_{Y\in\mathcal Y_z}M_{z,Y}(\Theta,s,BS.\mathrm{restr}\,x)=f_z(\Theta,s,x)\quad(\nu_{p_1}\text{-a.e. }x),\qquad |M_{z,Y}|\le C_M\varepsilon_{\rm phys}e^{-a\,\texttt{treeLenCovOne}(Y)}.\tag{4}$$
**OPEN (ROOT-MARK-EXP/MARK-CUBIC).** Here $a=2\kappa_{dec}-1$, $\varepsilon_{\rm phys}=C_*(\log\beta_k)^q/\sqrt{\beta_k}$ with $q=3p$ from PHYSCUBIC, and $C_M$ is independent of $k,N_1,\beta_k,z$. The bound in (4) is required for every $u,\Phi$ and every parameter value accepted by `ParamMarkFamily`; `InterpMarks.bound` has no carrier restriction. The clamp supplies the bounded hat input, and one may set $G=0$ for $\Theta\notin V$ and $u\notin B(0,R_\sigma)$, as the landed activity family does outside its analytic domain. A uniform coefficient bound $|R_{z,Y}(t)|\le C\varepsilon_{\rm phys}e^{-a\,dl(Y)}$ on a physical insertion disc $|t|<r_t$ would give $C_M=C/r_t$ by Cauchy. The $R_{z,Y}$ must be jointly analytic in $(\Theta,u,t)$ on an open neighbourhood of the relevant parameter set and disc; the marks must be local in the hat blocks of $Y$, analytic jointly in $(\Theta,u)$ on $V\times B(0,R_\sigma)$, continuous in $\Phi$ on `physCarrier`, measurable, and local in $\Theta$ on the appropriate thickening of $Y$. They must include an analytic value at $u=0$. These are explicit inputs to be proved, not consequences of the unmarked (S) alone.
