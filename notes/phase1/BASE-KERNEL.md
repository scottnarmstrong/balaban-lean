## 1. Geometry, variables, and every real cutoff

Let $c$ denote a cube of $\pi_1$, $B_1(y)$ a fine $L$-block, and $b=(x,x+e_\mu)$ a positively oriented nearest-neighbour fine bond. A bond is counted once. Write $c(b)$ for the set of cubes containing an endpoint of $b$; it has at most two elements. Every set below is first formed on the full torus and then restricted to one component $X=Z'$ of $Z^{\rm pre}_1$; a condition on a cube means that the complete cube is in the indicated set. Put

$$\Omega=\Omega_1=\operatorname{Op}_1(\mathsf P_1^{\sim3};\mathbb T_1),\qquad \Omega''=\Omega''_1=\operatorname{Op}_1(\mathsf Q_1^{\sim(\rho+2)}\cup\mathsf R_1^{\sim(2\rho+4)};\Omega),\quad\rho=\lfloor\widehat R_0\rfloor.$$

Here `BaseRecord.Omega` and `BaseRecord.OmegaDD` are the landed set expressions; `BaseRecord.P`, `.Q`, `.R`, `.S` are the four record sets, and `RegionHistory.baseRecord` realizes a history's step-zero record. Let $D_-=X\cap(\Omega\setminus\Omega'')$, $D_+=X\cap\Omega''$, $D_{\rm out}=X\cap\Omega^c$, interpreted as fine sites when used for spins and as unions of complete cubes when used for cube products. The chart variables $\psi'_0(x)\in\mathbb R^N$ are retained on $D_-$; the variables on $D_+$ belong to the first small-field integral. The kernel's spin variables are $\phi_x\in S^{N-1}$, $x\in D_{\rm out}$, with geometric surface measure $\sigma$, of total mass $Nv_N$. The output is $\psi_1$ on $T^{(1)}\cap V_X$, and $	heta=z_1\psi_1$. There are no old variables or old boundary terms. The output Gaussian factors are exactly

$$\prod_{y\in T^{(1)}\cap D_{\rm out}}z_1^N t_0(\theta_y,(Q\phi)_y),\qquad t_0(v,w)=\left(\frac{\beta aL^{d-2}}{2\pi}\right)^{N/2}\exp\left[-\frac{\beta aL^{d-2}}2|v-w|^2\right].$$

For every cube $c$, define $A_c=\prod_{y\in T_1(c)}\mathbf1[|\theta_y-(Q\phi)_y|<2\delta_0]$ and

$$B_c=\prod_{b:c\in c(b)}\mathbf1[|\phi_{b_+}-\phi_{b_-}|<\delta_0]\;\prod_{x\in c}\mathbf1[\nu|\phi_x-h|^2<\delta_0^2]^{[k_0=0]}.$$

The second product is absent in regime (A), $k_0\ge1$. Thus $\chi'^{(0)}_c=A_cB_c$ is exactly the landed `chiPrimeZero`, with `chiBlockA` and `chiBondB`; the latter uses the equivalent squared site inequality. The initial density $\rho_0=\exp(-\beta H(\phi)+\langle g,\phi\rangle)$ has **no separate $\chi_0$**. Its record expansion is precisely $\prod_{c\in\mathsf P_1}(1-A_cB_c)\prod_{c\notin\mathsf P_1}A_cB_c$, the `chiPrimeZero_decomposition` identity. Each $B_c$ tests *all* bonds having either endpoint in $c$, including a bond crossing the boundary of $c$, of $\Omega$, or of $\Omega''$. The bond has one Boltzmann energy term, although its inequality can occur in two $B_c$'s. By LF-RECONSTRUCTION Lemma II.7.2(d), if $c\in\mathsf P_1$, both endpoints of every tested bond lie in $D_{\rm out}$ and in the same component $X$.

For the exact split into a retained kernel and an integral over $D_+$, a $B_c$ bond or site indicator is assigned to the retained factor if all its spin arguments lie outside $D_+$; otherwise it is assigned to the $D_+$ integral, with the exterior spins as parameters. Call these products $B_c^-$ and $B_c^+$; $B_c=B_c^-B_c^+$. This rule assigns a crossing bond to the integral when its endpoint in $\Omega''$ is integrated, and to the kernel when both endpoints are retained. It is a partition of *indicators*, not of the bond energy. In particular $B_c^+=1$ for $c\in\mathsf P_1$, so the bond witness survives. A corresponding partition of $A_c$ by its spin arguments is needed for cubes in $\Omega$; $A_c$ on $D_{\rm out}$ depends only on retained spins and the Gaussian differences $u_y=\theta_y-(Q\phi)_y$. It is not legitimate to discard $B_c^+$ or to claim that its integral is analytic: that is the cutoff-transfer assertion stated in §6.

## 2. Conditional Hessian, covariance, and chart normalization

Fix exterior retained spins, coarse data, and one finite chart domain $D\subset\Omega$ determined by the local generating set of the component. The conditional spin action on $D$ has the form $A_D(\phi_D)=\frac12\langle\phi_D,H_{0,D}\phi_D\rangle-\langle S_D,\phi_D\rangle+\mathrm{const}$, with

$$H_{0,D}=-\Delta_D+aL^{-2}Q_D^*Q_D+\mu_{\rm bg}I,\qquad Q_D^*=L^dQ_D^{\mathsf T},\qquad \mu_{\rm bg}=\begin{cases}0,&k_0\ge1,\\\nu,&k_0=0.\end{cases}\tag{2.1}$$

$-\Delta_D$ is the Dirichlet restriction of the fine nearest-neighbour lattice Laplacian: its diagonal counts all $2d$ incident oriented bonds, including those crossing $D$--$D^c$; the exterior endpoint contributions are in $S_D$. $Q_D$ is the block average on the 1-blocks of the generating set meeting $D$, with the exterior spin contributions likewise moved to $S_D$. The actual generating set, its collar, and its local boundary data are those of LF-RECONSTRUCTION Definition II.2.7(ii),(iv), not an arbitrary truncation of the torus operator. The background $e_x=\phi^{\rm b}_x\in S^{N-1}$ solves $H_{0,D}e-S_D=-\alpha_xe_x$, and the chord Hessian is

$$H_D=H_{0,D}+\operatorname{diag}_{x\in D}(\alpha_x I_N),\qquad P_e=\bigoplus_{x\in D}e_xe_x^{\mathsf T},\quad Q_e=I-P_e,\quad A_e=Q_eH_DQ_e+P_e,\quad C^{(0)}=A_e^{-1}.\tag{2.2}$$

This is P4 (2.48)--(2.49) at $\lambda_0=\infty$, in the simpler hard-spin chart of LF-RECONSTRUCTION Lemmas II.7.3--II.7.4 and LF-BASE-KF0 (A.3), (A.6). In regime (A), the physical field term $\frac{\beta\nu}{2}\sum_{x\in D}|\phi_x-h|^2$ is *outside* $H_D$ and remains an explicit chart interaction; in regime (B) it is included in $H_{0,D}$. Thus the unqualified $+\nu$ in LF-BASE-KF0 (A.6) describes the complete-background convention and cannot be used for regime (A). The constrained chord identity is exact: $A_D(\phi)-A_D(e)=\frac12\langle\phi-e,H_D(\phi-e)\rangle$ for every hard-spin configuration with the fixed exterior data.

For $v_x=\sqrt\beta\,Q_e\phi_x$, set $z_x(v)=\sqrt\beta(\sqrt{1-\beta^{-1}|v_x|^2}-1)e_x$, $J_{\beta,D}(v)=\prod_{x\in D}(1-\beta^{-1}|v_x|^2)^{-1/2}$, and $v=Q_eC^{(0)1/2}\psi'_0$. Then $\phi_x=e_x+\beta^{-1/2}(v_x+z_x(v))$. With $m_D=(N-1)|D|$, the **full-domain**, exact real chart measure relative to a standard $N|D|$-dimensional Gaussian is

$$\exp V_D\,J_{\beta,D}(v)\exp\{-\langle v,H_Dz(v)\rangle-\tfrac12\langle z(v),H_Dz(v)\rangle\},\qquad V_D=\frac{m_D}{2}\log\frac{2\pi}{\beta}+\frac12\log\det C^{(0)}.\tag{2.3}$$

It carries the chart-domain indicator $|v_x|<\sqrt\beta$ and, in regime (A), the exact additional field factor $\exp[-\frac{\beta\nu}{2}\sum_{x\in D}(|\phi_x-h|^2-|e_x-h|^2)]$. Equation (2.3) is LF-BASE-KF0 (A.3), including its mixed $\langle v,H_Dz\rangle$ term; dropping that term for a nonconstant background is wrong. On the real support of LF-RECONSTRUCTION Lemma II.7.5, $|Q_e\phi_x|\le1/4$, hence $J_{\beta,D}(v)\le(1-1/16)^{-|D|/2}\le2^{|D|}$.

The local substitution in a large-field kernel needs a *specified family* $C^{(0)1/2}_{\rm loc}(c,s)$, with its support, decoupling parameters, principal branch, determinant/logarithm allocation, and comparison with $C^{(0)1/2}$. The available LOC I-4c Lean family does not instantiate (2.2): `Localization.XSetting.hk` requires $1\le k$, and its `covOp` is a block Schur complement rather than $Q_eH_DQ_e+P_e$ (LF-BASE-KF0 §A.5). GREEN §4.4(ii) supplies a real gap and decay, not the required exact localization. Until this base-specific family and the identity that moves its discrepancy into localized small-field terms are proved, $C^{(0)1/2}_{\rm loc}$ and the separate $D_-$ interaction in row 7 of the table are **named obligations**, not defined Lean functions. The exact reference normalization is (2.3); an implementation must count it once, either in $b_0$ or in the corresponding P6 vacuum constants.

## 4. The action needed for the bond witness

Define the fine field $\widehat\phi^X$ on $X^+=X\cup\{\text{nearest-neighbour sites of }X\}$ by $\widehat\phi^X_x=\phi_x$ for $x\in\Omega^c\cap X^+$, and $\widehat\phi^X_x=e^{\rm loc}_x$ for $x\in\Omega\cap X^+$. Here $e^{\rm loc}$ is the component's local background with the mixed lift of the retained spin and output data, as in LF-RECONSTRUCTION Definition II.2.7(iv). Boundary values outside $X$ are the actual lifted data fixed for the component. Let $\mathcal B_{\rm int}(X)$ be the positively oriented bonds with both endpoints in $X$, and $\mathcal B_{\rm st}(X)$ those with exactly one endpoint in $X$. Define

$$\begin{aligned}\mathcal A^{\rm base}_1(X)={\beta\over1+\xi^{(0)}_3}\Bigg\{&{a_1L^{d-2}\over2}\sum_{y\in T_1(\Omega\cap X)}|\psi_1(y)-(Q\widehat\phi^X)(y)|^2+{1\over2}\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_{\rm int}(X)}|\partial\widehat\phi^X(b)|^2\\&+{\mu\over2}\sum_{x\in X}|\widehat\phi^X_x-h|^2+{1\over4}\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_{\rm st}(X)}|\partial\widehat\phi^X(b)|^2\Bigg\},\qquad\mu=\nu_1L^{-2}.\tag{4.1}\end{aligned}$$

This is Definition II.5.4 with the level-zero layer $\Lambda_0=\Omega^c\cap X$ evaluated at the **spin field**, as P2's $a_0=+\infty$ convention and LF-RECONSTRUCTION Definition II.5.4 require. The coarse term is present only on the first small-field layer $\Omega$; `actionG` gives its precise generating-set version when more local layers are present. Here $a_1=\texttt{cs.aw}(1)$ is the scale-one action weight. Its coefficients and the interior/star-bond partition are those of `LF1/Setup.lean` `actionG` and `Operators/MainAction.lean` `starBondE`; the replacement needed at $k=0$ is the field $\widehat\phi^X$, not a new value of `cs.aw`. In regime (A), $e^{\rm loc}$ is computed with background mass zero, while the physical $\mu$-term in (4.1) remains present, as LF-RECONSTRUCTION Definition II.5.4 says. Formula (4.1) counts each interior fine bond once and each star bond with coefficient $1/4$.

`mainActionLoc` currently calls `locPhi`, which extends the local background by `locData` outside its generating set. `BgData.clip` retains only the local data sites $\Omega_1\cup\text{collar}$ (`Background/Data.lean`); beyond that collar it need not equal the retained fine spin. Thus `mainActionLoc` cannot be used as `baseMainAction` without a theorem identifying its field with $\widehat\phi^X$ on every endpoint of every bond and every mass site in (4.1). In particular the required bond-witness lemma is, for each failed bond $b$ of a $c\in\mathsf P_1\cap X$,

$$\mathcal A^{\rm base}_1(X)\ge{\beta^{(1)}_0\over2}|\partial\phi(b)|^2,\qquad \beta^{(1)}_0={\beta\over1+\xi^{(0)}_3}\ge\beta/2.\tag{4.2}$$

It follows from Lemma II.7.2(d), $b\subset\Lambda_0\cap X$, and the explicit second sum of (4.1). No background-gradient estimate can replace (4.2). The site-witness analogue is $\mathcal A^{\rm base}_1(X)\ge\beta^{(1)}_0\mu|\phi_x-h|^2/2$ for $x\in c\in\mathsf P_1$ in regime (B).

## 5. Exact `(S5)` target and conditional structure proof

For $k_0\ge1$, the required base `(S5)` is the following statement for each component $X$. The real kernel is a *finite sum* $K_0=\sum_w\mathbf1_{A_w}\exp(-a_w)$. Each $A_w$ is Borel and invariant when the same real $R\in O(N)$ rotates every output, spin, and fluctuation variable, with $h$ fixed. The exponent $a_w(x;h)$ is real on real arguments, obeys $a_w(Rx;Rh)=a_w(x;h)$, and extends holomorphically in all variables and $h$ to the neighbourhood of $A_w\times S^{N-1}$ made of displacements of modulus $<\varepsilon_j$ for each scale-$j$ variable and $<1$ for $h$. On this entire complex neighbourhood,

$$|a_w(x;h')-a_w(x;h)|\le3|X|_1|h'-h|.\tag{5.1}$$

This is LF-RECONSTRUCTION Definition II.5.7 `(S5)` with $C_{\rm S5}=3$, including its analytic-extension clause; joint real $O(N)$ covariance alone is weaker. The chart indicators remain real indicators under complex continuation. On their support, the chart square root and $-\log J_\beta$ extend by the branch at the real background provided the complex displacement keeps $|\beta^{-1}v_x\cdot v_x|<1$; the bilinear dot product has no conjugation. The base covariance and its principal functions must be holomorphic on the same neighbourhood, and the local cutoff-transfer identity must leave $A_w$ independent of $h$ in regime (A). These are separate analytic obligations. If they hold, $a_w$ is the sum of the Gaussian quadratic exponent, the base main action, the retained chart interaction, and $-\log J_\beta$, with the positive normalizing constants absorbed consistently in $b_0$. Only the physical $\mu$-term of (4.1) depends on $h$; $\beta^{(1)}_0\mu=\beta_1\nu_1L^{-d}\le e^{2\epsilon}L^{-d}$, and the H7 bounds on the fields give (5.1), as in LF-RECONSTRUCTION Lemma II.7.7(e). In regime (B), `(S5)` is not required.

Under the cutoff-transfer and localization obligations, Lemma II.7.7 has the following proof. The identity $1-AB=(1-A)+A(1-B)$ and `baseF_decomposition` give (1.1). Only $A_c(u)$ in the outside product reads $\psi_1|_{\Omega^c}$; the other retained indicators, chart terms, background, and action read $\psi_1$ on $\Omega$, so $\widetilde K^w_0$ is independent of the outside output. All real factors are nonnegative Borel, and `baseF_mem_unit` gives $0\le F^w_0\le1$; `chiNew` supplies the support condition. This proves `(S1)`. The ledger of §3, once its `SF` estimates are supplied, gives reality, source support, joint H7 analyticity and the stated bound for `(S0)`. No component is ripe at scale one (LF-RECONSTRUCTION Lemma II.7.2(c), landed `base_noRipe`), so `(S3)` is empty. Lemma I.3.9 and the local-covariance core equality give `(S4)`; ordinary exponential decay alone does not give exact locality. The preceding paragraph gives `(S5)`. These uses of the open obligations are necessary premises; the printed proof of Lemma II.7.7 does not discharge them.

## 6. Conditional `(S2)` proof and exact remaining obligations

Let $\mathcal N_1\cap X$ be the new-seed cubes and $\kappa^{(0)}(X)=A_3(\log\beta)^{2p_0}|\mathcal N_1\cap X|_\#-(c_1\log\beta+c_2)|X|_1$, exactly LF-RECONSTRUCTION Lemma II.7.2(e), landed `base_budget`.
