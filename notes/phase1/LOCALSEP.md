## The parameter in the sources and in Lean

**PROVED.** `Multiscale/GeneratingSet.lean:60–71` defines `GeneratingSet d L N₀ k ϱ`; its separation field says that for $1\le n<k$, $x\notin\Omega_n$, $y\in\Omega_{n+1}$, one has $\operatorname{dist}(x,y)>\varrho L^n$ in fine-site units. Thus $\varrho$ is a coefficient of $L^n$, not a number of $M_1L^n$ cubes.

**PROVED.** P2, printed pp. 607–608, (1.1)–(1.6), uses nested domains $\Lambda_n$ with successive boundaries separated on the scale $RML^n$ (with the paper's lattice spacing restored); this is a condition on a chosen background generating set, not the definition of the minimal set attached to a polymer. P3, printed pp. 68–69, (4.4)–(4.6), starts $B_j(X)$ at the union of $M_1L^j$ cubes contained in $X$ and makes each lower layer the union of $M_1L^n$ cubes at cube-index distance at most $5L$ from the next layer. `LargeField/Background/MinimalGenSet.lean:66–94` implements exactly this fixed rule as `cubeInterior`, `nearStep`, `minLayerAux`, `minLayer`; `minSep L M1` at line 176 is $5LM_1$, not $5L$ unless $M_1=1$.

**PROVED.** P3, printed p. 78, (4.44)–(4.45), assigns the output polymer $Y$ a *mixed-level* generating set: top layer $Y\cap\Omega_{k+1}$, lower layers the $5L$ enlargement of the next layer together with $Y\cap(\Omega_n\setminus\Omega_{n+1})$. Its analytic domain is (4.46), followed by the bound (4.47). This is not simply the unrestricted $B_{k+1}(Y)$. P5, printed p. 488, (1.4), evaluates the background on $B_k(W_k)$ for its twice-enlarged region $W_k$; it does not replace the $5L$ recursion by a variable-radius one.

**PROVED.** `Background/ExistenceSetup.lean:35–47` sets $R_0(d,L)=\max\{4dL,1+2d\log L/(\operatorname{agmonRate}(d)/4)\}$ and `BgHyp.hϱ` requires $R_0(d,L)\le\varrho$. The uses `BgHyp.green` and `BgHyp.approxSetting` at lines 49 and 68 pass this bound to the Green and approximation estimates underlying `Background/Contraction.lean` and `Background/Def5.lean`. The parent of `Localization/I5Fan.lean:62–82` has the additional, stronger requirements $r_0\ge4dL^3$ and $r_0M_1\le\varrho_{\rm parent}-1$.

**REFUTED.** `SmallField/GsSpecPoly.lean:150–155` defines `polyGS` with $M_1=1$, $N'_1=N_1L^{m_1}$; therefore its type has $\varrho=\operatorname{minSep}(L,1)=5L$. At $d=L=3$, `bgR0 3 3` is at least $36$, so this particular `polyGS` cannot be equated with `Tm.gs j X` in `Terms ... (bgR0 3 3)`. This type failure does not say that the paper's $B_j(X)$ has inadequate separation: its intended $M_1$ is free.

## Source-faithful generator at a prescribed class separation

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** $M_1=L^{m_1}$ and $R_0(d,L)\le\varrho\le5LM_1$. On the class torus $T=\operatorname{CTorus}(d,L,N_1,m_1,k)$, identify $T$ with the torus of `minimalGenSet` at $(M_1,N_1,q)=(L^{m_1},N_1,k)$ by $(L^{m_1}N_1)L^k=(N_1L^{m_1})L^k$. Convert $X\subseteq T$ to a finite set, take `minimalGenSet hj X`, and retain its layers while lowering its separation index from $5LM_1$ to $\varrho$. Call the resulting proposed class object $B^{M_1,\varrho}_j(X)$; this name is **not** a landed declaration.

**PROVED.** The index-lowering operation is valid whenever $\varrho\le\varrho'$ and $L>0$: copy `Ω`, `top`, `subset`, and `blockUnion`; for `separation`, $\varrho L^n\le\varrho' L^n<\operatorname{dist}(x,y)$. Hence $B^{M_1,\varrho}_j(X)$ is a `GeneratingSet ... ϱ` with exactly P3's layers. The generic Lean constructor `GeneratingSet.weakenSep` and class-torus wrapper are **OPEN** as declarations.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** $X\in\mathcal D_j$, $1\le j\le k$. A class polymer is a union of cubes of side $L^{j+m_0+m_1}$, hence of $M_1L^j$ cubes; thus `cubeInterior` at its top layer equals $X$. `SmallField/GsSpecStrong.lean:minimalGenSet_core` gives $X\subseteq\Omega_1$, no layers above $j$, and $X$ in layer $j$; `SmallField/GsSpecDef.lean:GsSpecCore.toStrong` gives `GsSpecStrong` after transport and index lowering. The needed divisibility follows from the proof pattern of `SmallField/GsSpecPoly.lean:isPolyDom_blockUnion`, with divisor $M_1L^j$ instead of $L^j$; that replacement is **OPEN** in Lean.

**PROVED.** `LargeField/Background/LocalGenNeg.lean:bgR0_le_minSep_iff` proves $R_0(d,L)\le5LM_1$ exactly when $R_0(d,L)/(5L)\le M_1$; `bgR0_le_minSep_of_ceil` gives the ceiling criterion. Since $L\ge3$, a finite $m_1$ can satisfy $L^{m_1}\ge\lceil R_0(d,L)/(5L)\rceil$. Add this finite lower bound to row 6 of `FLUCTUATION.md` §1.5, before the later $\alpha_0,E_0,\beta$ choices. In particular $\varrho=R_0(d,L)$ is available without altering P3's layer rule.

**PROVED.** More generally, for every fixed finite $\varrho\ge R_0(d,L)$, choosing $m_1$ with $L^{m_1}\ge\lceil\varrho/(5L)\rceil$ gives the same construction at that prescribed index. This choice has to precede fixing the class's $m_1$; after $m_1$ is fixed the source generator only admits indices at most $5LL^{m_1}$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** the torus transport above. The landed `LocalGen.localGen_layer_range` and `LocalGen.localGen_dataSites_range` in `LargeField/Background/LocalGen.lean:160–175` apply at arbitrary $M_1$: for every data site $z$ of $B_j(X)$ there is $x\in X$ with $\operatorname{dist}_\infty(z,x)\le r_j:=11M_1L^j+1$. Index lowering changes no data site. `minimalGenSet_mono` gives $B_j(X)\prec B_j(X')$ for $X\subseteq X'$; `Background/Composition.lean:83–86` permits the two sets in `Precedes` to have different separation indices. `SmallField/GsSpecStrong.lean:precedes_levelGS` gives $B_j(X)\prec\operatorname{levelGS}(j')$ for $j\le j'\le k$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** aligned class symmetries. Translation by a multiple of $L^{j+m_0+m_1}$, coordinate flips and swaps preserve the $M_1L^n$ cube partitions for $n\le j$ and their index sup-distance. Induction on `minLayerAux` therefore gives $B_j^{M_1,\varrho}(rX)=rB_j^{M_1,\varrho}(X)$ layer by layer. The existing $M_1=1$ induction is `SmallField/GsSpecPolyEqv.lean:plAux_equiv`; its $M_1=L^{m_1}$ transport is **OPEN** in Lean. Across tori the same induction agrees on common lifts **conditional on** the entire $r_j$ envelope of $X$ admitting the same non-winding lift; non-winding of $X$ alone does not supply that margin. The required family compatibility is **OPEN**.

**REFUTED.** At fixed $M_1$, reindexing cannot give $\varrho>5LM_1$; the required inequality reverses. One can define a different radius-$t$ recursion, $t=\lceil\varrho/M_1\rceil$, replacing `5*L` in `nearStep` by $t$. The same outside-cube argument gives separation $>tM_1L^n\ge\varrho L^n$; summing the at most $(t+1)M_1L^n$ displacement at each descent gives data-site radius $r_j(t)=\lceil(t+1)/(L-1)\rceil M_1L^j+1$. Cube-index isometries still give covariance, and the core/strong arguments persist for cube-union $X$. But this is **not** P3's prescribed $B_j(X)$ or its mixed output (4.44); its range, localization-cube and covering-cost constants must be proved anew. No such variant is landed.

**REFUTED.** The `rhoSep m₁` of `Localization/I5Inhabited.lean:216–218` exceeds $5LL^{m_1}$ at $d=L=3$ because it contains $36\cdot3^{m_1}+324\cdot3^{m_1}$ in addition to $R_0$. Thus the source-faithful local child cannot have that same separation index. `LargeField/Background/LocalGenNeg.lean:not_bgSetting_minSep` proves that *as parent* it cannot satisfy `BgSetting`: $r_0\ge4dL^3$ and $r_0M_1\le5LM_1-1$ are incompatible. A wide auxiliary parent and a local child may have different indices; `Precedes` allows this. The actual parent/child transfer remains **OPEN**.

## Halo, size and regional comparison

**PROVED.** `LargeField/Def7SmallField.lean:210–216` defines `thickY` as $Y^{\approx}=\operatorname{thick}_{\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor}Y$ and `W2` as $W_2=\operatorname{thick}_{\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor}(Y^{\approx})$. Since `LargeField/Geometry/Distance.lean:291` defines `thick r` with cube-neighbourhood radius $r+1$, $W_2$ has cube-index radius $2(\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor+1)$, or the conservative fine-site radius $(2\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor+3)ML^k$ after allowing a terminal cube. Put $H=\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor+1$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** common torus and $M=cM_1$. If $X$ lies in the first halo $Y^{\approx}$ and $r_j<HML^k$, every data site of $B_j(X)$ lies in the second halo $W_2$; if $X\subseteq Y$, the sufficient bound is $r_j<2HML^k$. Equivalently, the exact depth formulation is $\operatorname{dist}_\infty(X,(\operatorname{sset}W_2)^c)>r_j$. For the source generator, $j\le k$, $c\ge14$ from `LargeField/Operators/Setup.lean:Geo.hc`, and $H\ge1$, one has $r_j\le11M_1L^k+1<14M_1L^k\le HML^k$ when $M_1L^k\ge1$. This is the required inequality between separation and the halo: $\varrho\le5LM_1$ and $11M_1L^j+1<HML^k$; at $\varrho=R_0$ it is met by the row-6 $M_1$ choice and landed $c\ge14$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** the same $M_1$, ambient torus and $X\subseteq\operatorname{cubePre}(W_2)$. Since `LargeField/Background/StepGenSets.lean:130–136` defines `genSF j W₂` as `minimalGenSet j (cubePre W₂)`, `minimalGenSet_mono` gives $B_j(X)\prec\operatorname{genSF}_j(W_2)$, and `LocalGen.localGen_dataSites_mono` gives inclusion of their data sites. The extra depth condition above puts those data sites inside the physical region $W_2$ as well. `localE` and `localM` concern the *term's field dependence on $X$*; `XiDom` can inspect the larger data-site envelope. No claim that $d(X)$ charges this envelope follows: the P3 range/locality estimates and the covering-rate argument must absorb it.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** $M=cM_1$, $c\ge14$ and aligned $\pi_j$ cubes. `MinimalGenSet.minimalGenSet_range` at lines 451–471 places each domain-test site in the same or an adjacent $\pi_j$ cube of $X$ (it only needs $12M_1\le M$); `minimalGenSet_disjoint` at lines 582–594 controls separate polymers under $14M_1\le M$. These are the source's finite enlargement bounds relevant to the polymer covering rate. They do not change `localE`'s support $X$ or authorize replacing $d(X)$ by the size of the data-site envelope without a covering estimate.

**OPEN.** `LargeField/Def7SmallField.lean:SFCtx.hT` identifies total torus sizes but does not force its `geo.M1` to equal the class $L^{m_1}$; nor does it supply $X\subseteq\operatorname{cubePre}(W_2)$ for every output piece or the history-factor cover. Those are separate alignment, support and transfer premises. At the genuine successor interface $B_{k+1}(Y)$ and `genSF k(W₂)` also live at different levels; the same-torus `Precedes` statement above is not that interface. P3 (4.44)–(4.47) and the regional-to-output factor transfer remain **OPEN**.

**OPEN.** For the alternative radius-$t$ recursion, the halo condition becomes $r_j(t)<HML^k$, equivalently $\lceil(t+1)/(L-1)\rceil M_1L^j+1<HML^k$; P3's fixed $12M_1\le M$ locality and $14M_1\le M$ disjointness hypotheses no longer suffice if $t$ is large. Enlarging $M/M_1$ and redoing the polymer covering estimate might work, but the source's unchanged output size charge does not prove it.
