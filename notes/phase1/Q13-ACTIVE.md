## 2. What ActiveFactor actually follows from

**REFUTED.** `Def7.integrand … x ≠ 0` alone does not imply `ChiDomAt` for any retained component: this `integrand` contains no `P_c.T.w` or `P_c.T.K`. A point outside the domain can have a nonzero boundary/small-field factor while `locOp_vanish_chiDom` makes its kernel zero. The appropriate antecedent is `W_A(ψk,g,x) ≠ 0`, or the stronger integrated statement `postTerm_A(ψk,g) ≠ 0`.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `W_A(ψk,g,x) ≠ 0`.** Finite-product nonvanishing in `Def7Hist.gWeight` gives `P_c.T.w(o_c,x_c,h_v,g) ≠ 0` for every `c∈PostComp A k`; since `LFKernel.w` is `K*exp(b)`, `P_c.T.K(o_c,x_c,h_v) ≠ 0`. Unfold `postOp` to `locOp`. The contrapositive of `LocOp.lean:locOp_vanish_chiDom` gives `ChiDomAt Cx.geo Cx.cs A.H k c.1 o_c x_c`. This is a pointwise support proof and needs no positivity or cancellation argument. The internal witness is the `c` coordinate of the **same** product configuration `x`; its type is `SubCfg … (P_c.A)` and supplies the existential internal-variable set in `hAct`.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `postTerm_A(ψk,g) ≠ 0`.** If `W_A(ψk,g,x)=0` for every `x`, then `gOp` integrates the zero function and `postTerm_A=0`. Its contrapositive yields some `x` with `W_A≠0`; the preceding pointwise argument supplies `o_c,x_c` for every retained component. `D1Form`/`PostConv` supply integrability where the density is used. A nonzero value of the *total* density gives some nonzero history term by the finite sum, but a zero history term can still contain nonzero pointwise values through cancellation.

**REFUTED (the full-field equality in the landed `hAct`).** `Def7Hist.lean:outOf` restricts `ψk` to `Vblk R a_c k`; `Operators/Variables.lean:outField` extends an output by **zero** off that set. Hence `outField N (outOf R a_c k ψk)=ψk` on `Vblk`, while global equality requires `ψk(b)=0` at every block outside `Vblk`. D1 quantifies over arbitrary real `ψk` and has no such support clause. The `hAct` in `History/ParentDomain.lean:inPsiTilde_genW_of_factors` asks for equality on the whole `BlockAt` torus; it is strictly stronger than the operator support statement, even though the operator and small-field factor use the same input `ψk`.

**OPEN (exact repair).** Replace the full equality in `hAct` and the uses of `rw [←hψ]` by the local equality `∀ b∈Vblk R a_c k, o_c ⟨b,hb⟩=ψk b` (or by equality only at the tested top blocks of the relevant datum sites and pair endpoints). `outOf` proves the first form immediately. `TopBlockTransport.lean:datum_value_eq_tested,chiDomAt_pair_bounds` already establish that these tested blocks belong to `Vblk` and relate their `subMultiscaleField` values to `o_c`; their `outField` specialization must be generalized to this local equality. Then the repaired parent theorem's `hAct` is an exact consequence of `W_A≠0`. No changed characteristic domain or new estimate is needed for this bookkeeping step.
