## 2. Radius and its actual use

**PROVED (qualitative radius):** For each admissible fixed $S$, `hatStepK_physV` plus `isOpen_physV` gives $r_D(S,t)>0$ such that $q\in rawSub\,2\,G'$ and $\|q-q_0\|<r_D$ imply $q\in\texttt{XiCRaw}\,G'\,(\texttt{compWeight}\,3\,1)\,\nu\,K\,1\,t$. Equivalently every such $q$ has an `XiCData` datum. The proof is `Localization/XiCRawOpen.lean:xiCRaw_eventually`: retain one real centre and witnesses, shift the complex displacement by $q-q_0$, use `theorem72_analytic` for continuity of the background, and intersect finitely many strict conditions. `Fluctuation/DetPrefactor.lean:exists_chart_disc` gives an equivalent line-disc radius at every datum. Neither declaration supplies a numerical lower bound.

Because `XiCRaw G' (compWeight 3 1) ν K 1 t` and $q_0$ do not involve $\beta,p_1,\delta$, the existential radius may be chosen as $r_D(k,N_1,m_1,\nu,t)$, independently of those three step parameters. This is a logical consequence of the definitions; it does not remove possible $k$ or $N_1$ dependence.

**OPEN uniform bound:** A scale- and volume-independent $r_D\ge c(t)>0$ in the raw **sup norm** is plausible at this full-level unit datum but is not a consequence of strictness alone. `Background/Def5Response.lean:theorem72_linearResponse` gives weighted `SupBounds` for the field derivative under `SrcBound`, and `srcBound_source` bounds that source by $(3+2dL^2)\|E\|_\infty$, uniformly in volume, but only on its smaller `bgC2` domain and without a stated analogous weighted multiplier bound or a common line-segment domain of prescribed length. `theorem72_analytic` supplies continuity, not a Lipschitz constant. The needed assertion is a uniform response estimate on $\|q-q_0\|<c(t)$ for **all** eight weighted `XiCBounds` left sides and `InDomainC`, including the multiplier and a fixed real centre. If bounds $L_j$ are proved for normalized left sides with positive physical margins $m_j$, one may take $r_D=\frac12\min_j m_j/(1+L_j)$; here $m_j$ include $t,t^2,\delta_*,\delta_*^2$, and $L_j$ must be independent of $k,N_1,\nu,\beta$. This formula is **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OPEN-RESPONSE-UNIFORM**; no such $L_j$ is presently landed. In particular the $h$ dot condition has a $t^2$ margin and constrains any prospective bound to that order when $\nu3^{2K}$ is near $1$. The justified statement is the scale-dependent $r_D(S,t)>0$ above; no contrary example to a uniform raw radius is established.

An explicit scale-dependent bound can be organized without naming an unavailable Lipschitz theorem: for each fixed $S$, choose the finite list of normalized strict left sides $f_j$ used in `xiCRaw_eventually`, their physical gaps $m_j>0$, and local moduli $\omega_{j,S}(r)=\sup_{\|q-q_0\|\le r}|f_j(q)-f_j(q_0)|$ on the relevant background domain. Continuity gives some $r>0$ with $\omega_{j,S}(r)<m_j$ for every $j$; take half the minimum of those radii and the `InDomainC` radius. This is an existence construction, **not** a numerical estimate, and the background-domain membership has to be established before evaluating its $f_j$.

The product norm on `physParam` uses the ordinary finite-product source sup norm. At $g=0$, a sup ball inside $\|g\|_1<1$ has radius at most $1/M$, where $M$ is the number of source site-component coordinates; $M$ grows with $N_1$. Hence a **uniform product-sup radius is REFUTED** as volume grows. Use the mixed raw-sup / source-$\ell^1$ neighbourhood $\{\|q-q_0\|<r_D,\ \|g-g_0\|_1<1-\|g_0\|_1\}$. There is no common source radius around every point of the strict unit ball. `DetPrefactor.norm_sub_le_of_chart_disc` gives a Cauchy difference constant $2M/r_0(D)$ for a bounded analytic output after a disc is known; using it to establish the disc itself would be circular. `DetOutputTerms.DetExpansionLocC` already has a uniform term bound and analytic-at assertions on the whole chart; datum Cauchy **derivative** constants uniform in scale would require a uniform radius. Ordinary pointwise analyticity/locality and the fixed-datum activity bound require only some radius per scale.

For the source obstruction, take every one of the $M$ complex coordinates equal to a positive real $r$. Its source sup norm is $r$ and its $\ell^1$ norm is $Mr$; once $r>1/M$ the point is outside `l1Ball`. This is independent of the datum radius and of the determinant bound.

## 3. Off-real exponent, family theorem, and Lean leaves

**PROVED:** `Fluctuation/StepComplexNonvanish.lean:StepSetting.ae_exponentsC_eq` identifies the real and complex exponents almost everywhere on `stepSegments` for a **fixed real** `StepSetting` datum, under `RayData`-type hypotheses. It does not compare two complex formulae at varying data. `Fluctuation/StepComplexOut.lean:WtwoC_eq_sum` expands the complex observable exponent algebraically, with the old complex pairing retained. `ParamActivitySubspace.lean:physical_directTermsFamilyOn` converts a supplied `DirectTerms` over `physSΛ` and `DiagCont` into a relative family, while `labelActFamilyOn_eq_dtAct` identifies its activity with that supplied record's `dtAct`. Neither creates the physical `DirectTerms` nor proves its sum equals the exponent.

**OPEN-EXPONENT-FAMILY, precise target:** Define $\mathcal W_1(D;s,x),\mathcal W_2(D,g;t,x)$ by the **same complex formulas** as `StepSetting.WoneC/WtwoC`, with every occurrence of $S.D'$ and the induced $\psi^c_k$, covariance square root, and composed data evaluated at $D$. Construct a pair of `DirectTermsFamilyOn` records $I_0,I_{\rm src}$ over the **same** $V=\texttt{physV}\ S\ 1\ \nu\ t$, with one compatible label union and block split; the auxiliary parameter of each record is one complex variable. From their activities define $A_e(s,0)=A_{0,e}(s)$ and $A_e(1,t')=A_{0,e}(1)+A_{{\rm src},e}(t')$ (the source family has zero value at $t'=0$). For every $e=(q,g)\in V$, let $D=\texttt{bgOfRaw}\,q$ (unique, with `XiCData` membership by $e\in V$). The target equality is, for every $(s,t')\in\texttt{stepSegments}$,

$$\mathcal W_1(D;s,x)+\mathcal W_2(D,g;t',x)
=\sum_{y:\mathrm{Lab}}A_e(s,t').H_y\bigl(\mathrm{BS}.\mathrm{restr}\,x\bigr)\qquad\nu_{p_1}\text{-a.e. }x.$$

Here `Lab` is the common finite label type obtained by combining the two families; its construction and the zero-value statement belong to the proposed theorem. `StepSetting` itself cannot simply be replaced by $S[D]$: `Fluctuation/StepObjects.lean:StepSetting` stores both $D'$ and a **real constant** $h_0$ with $D'.h=h_0$, whereas an arbitrary `XiCData` datum has complex, potentially block-dependent $h$. Introduce a datum-parameterized complex exponent with fixed geometric/rate setting, or restrict the chart and prove that restricted chart is still an appropriate complex-open domain. The first route retains `physV`.

An identity theorem does not fill this gap from `ae_exponentsC_eq`: that theorem gives equality of real and complex exponents at the original fixed datum, not equality of the proposed term sum and complex exponent on an open real form. Moreover the analytic family and a common almost-everywhere set for varying data are not yet supplied. A several-variable identity argument could be used only after proving both sides analytic on a connected relative complex neighbourhood, their equality on an open totally-real subset of `rawSub`, and a parameter-independent exceptional set (or pointwise identities). Direct substitution into the complex formula is the smaller route; **OPEN** is the exact value identity for the constructed physical `DirectTerms` on the whole complex chart.

The `rawSub` carrier is a complex linear subspace (`Localization/XiCRawOpen.lean:rawSub`), not itself a totally real set. Its conjugation-fixed real form could serve an identity theorem after checking conjugation stability and a real-open set of identities, but neither hypothesis is provided by `ae_exponentsC_eq`. Equality at one real point in a positive-dimensional complex chart has no uniqueness force.

**OPEN family theorem, exact scope:** Choose $\kappa_0=aEnt(3,3)+1$, $\kappa_{dec}=100(\kappa_0+c_1(3,3)+10)$, $\kappa'=3\kappa_{dec}/2+\kappa_0+1$. There should exist $m_1,t,\delta,\beta_0,C_E,C_F>0$, chosen before the scale, such that for each $k\ge1$, $3\mid N_1$, $N_1>5$, $0<\nu$ with $\nu3^{2K}\le1$, $\beta\ge\beta_0$, $0<p_1\le A_1(\log b)^p$, a common pair $I_0,I_{\rm src}$ on $V$ supplies, **for every** $g\in\texttt{l1Ball}$, a `StepActivityBoundC S (zeroClass ...) g` at $(q_0,g)$ with $\kappa_H=2\kappa_{dec}-1$ and $\varepsilon_H\le C_E(\log b)^{3p}/\sqrt b+C_F(\log b)^p/\sqrt b$. The common families satisfy the displayed exponent identity at **every** $e\in V$, plus the strict marked and determinant premises above. The `StepActivityBoundC` small-amplitude field `hε` then requires enlarging $\beta_0$ so this bound is below its explicit threshold. At $q_0$ the source-dependent worker is in progress; `SourceSliceScaled.stepActivityBoundC_zeroClass_direct_physical_scale_zeroSource` proves only $g=0$.

The chart tension is substantive. `physV S 1 ν t` has the correct $a_1=1$ and already includes the **unscaled** source coordinate; do not take an extra product with a source ball. The assembler theorem `SharpAssemblyScaled.physical_directTerms_eta_scaled` lives on `physSΛEta S 1 ν t (1/β)`, a subset of `physSΛ`, and evaluates at $g/\beta$ (`SourceSliceScaled.sourceSlice_mem`). The scalar $R_\sigma$ in that assembler controls its auxiliary source analyticity; the bounded $R_{\sigma,m}$ in the marked fluctuation disc is a separate parameter. Renormalization later uses `XiCOneReal`, where $h''=0$, and strict mass-shift inequalities (`notes/phase1/LOCREN.md`); that is an additional relative or ambient-extension problem, not implied by openness of `physV`.
