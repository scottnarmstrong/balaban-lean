## 1. The margin, including every row

Put $K=k+1$, $\ell=L^K$, $h=L^k=\ell/L$, $B=L^{K+m_1}$ and $S=(N_1L^{m_1})L^K$. Assume $L\ge3$, `IsLevel Gp K`, `IsLevel Hm k`, `DGSetting Gp …`, the cover `coverOf Gp`, and the component and eligibility hypotheses of `DecoupledFieldLocal.hdich_aux`. A member $z$ of `cubeIdx Gp` has $z.1=K$: `Weights.mem_blockIdx` and `Cover.mem_cubeIdx` put its base in a layer, while `StepIdentityQuad.IsLevel.not_mem_layer` empties every layer other than $K$. Likewise all `Hm` layers other than $k$ are empty. The following is the complete support list for a row at $x$; zero coefficients can only shrink it.

| Matrix or source definition | Row sites read at $x$ in the full-level case |
| --- | --- |
| `Multiscale.Operator.dirichletOperator`, via `fullMatrix`, `avgMatrix`, `layerProjMatrix`; `Lattice.Laplacian.latticeLaplacian` | $x$, $x\pm e_\mu$, and the aligned $K$-block of $x$ (side $\ell$) for `Gp`; for `Hm`, its $k$-block (side $h$). The diagonal $\alpha(x)$ reads only $x$. |
| `Multiscale.XVariant.xDirichletOperator`, via `xAvgMatrix` and `XVariant.layerBlockMatrix` | $x$, $x\pm e_\mu$, the $k$-block of $x$ (side $h$), and the $K$-block (side $\ell$). Here is the coordinate proof, including periodic wrapping. For $x\in\operatorname{intBox}z$, `Decoupled.mem_intBox`/`Cover.InBox` supply, separately for each coordinate $\mu$, an integer lift $t_\mu$ of $x_\mu$ with
$$|t_\mu-c_\mu|<\tfrac52B,\qquad c_\mu=(z.2_\mu)_{\rm val}+(B-1)/2.$$
For a site $u$ in the same aligned $K$-block as $x$, choose its lift relative to $t_\mu$; then $|u_\mu-t_\mu|\le\ell-1$. A nearest neighbour gives $\le1$; an `Hm` row or `unitQ` gives $\le h-1$; `unitP` and either top projection of `xDirichletOperator` give $\le\ell-1$. These statements follow directly from `jBlock`/`blockProjMatrix`, `latticeLaplacian`, and `unitBlock1`; they remain true for cells crossing the torus seam because the lifts can be shifted by multiples of $S$.

For `approxDep Gp u`, `DecoupledBackgroundLocal.approxDep` adds $u$ and bases $q.2$ of `Weights.active Gp u`. Every active $q$ has $q.1=K$. `Background.Bump.bumpCentre`, `BSpline.abs_lt_of_bsp_ne_zero`, and `BSpline.exists_of_perN_ne_zero` give a lift $b_\mu$ of its base with
$$-\ell-\tfrac12<u_\mu-b_\mu<2\ell-\tfrac12;
\quad |u_\mu-b_\mu|\le2\ell-1,$$
because $u_\mu-b_\mu$ is integral. This uses the spline radius $3/2$ and the centre $(\ell-1)/2$; `Weights.active_dist` alone gives only distance to *some point* of a block, so cannot replace this base estimate.

Define $S_Y$ as `DecoupledFieldLocal.SY Gp X₁ 𝒞 Y₁`. Let $R_Y$ contain $S_Y$ and all fine sites read from a row at $x\in S_Y$ of either Dirichlet operator, the $x$-variant, `unitQ`/`unitP` through $x$, or `resSites Gp x`. For every $x\in\operatorname{intBox}z$ occurring in $S_Y$ and every such row site $y$, $|y_\mu-t_\mu|\le\ell-1$ (or $\le1$). Thus, coordinatewise,
$$\max_{v\in\operatorname{resDep}_{Gp}(x)}|v_\mu-t_\mu|\le3\ell-2,\qquad
\max_{v\in\operatorname{approxDep}_{Gp}(y)}|v_\mu-y_\mu|\le2\ell-1,$$
and $|v_\mu-t_\mu|\le3\ell-2$ for $v\in\operatorname{approxDep}_{Gp}(y)$. For a pure `Hm`/$Q_k$ row the last bound improves to $2\ell+h-2$; for a nearest-neighbour row it is $2\ell$. The $K$-block bound covers the `unitP` row. Periodic representatives can be chosen successively along these differences and then shifted by $S$ to match the original box lift.

`Cover.hatRad` and `DecoupledGeometry.hatBox` give $\operatorname{hatRad}-\operatorname{intRad}=3L^2\ell$. Since $3\ell-2<3L^2\ell$ for every $L\ge3$ (at $L=3$: $3\ell-2<27\ell$), each of the preceding sites is in `hatBox z`, by `BoxConn.mem_boxSet`. Eligibility and `DecoupledFieldLocal.hhat_aux`/`hdich_aux` put that whole hat box in $Y_1$. Consequently
$$\boxed{\displaystyle\bigcup_{x\in S_Y}\operatorname{resDep}_{Gp}(x)
\ \cup\!\bigcup_{y\in R_Y}\operatorname{approxDep}_{Gp}(y)\subset Y_1.}\tag{FP}
$$
**PROVED** under the stated full-level, eligible-centre, and component hypotheses. For a `Dec2.SYF` centre, first use `Dec2.footprint`/`SYF_subset_Y` and the same connected-hat-box dichotomy to put its hat box in $Y_1$; its operator-row footprint then obeys the same estimate. There is no failing $L=3$ row, no change to `hatRad` or the label, and no cost to rate (8) of `TRUE-LOCAL.md`. A Lean proof of the new combined inclusion is **OPEN**.

**Decision B — chart needed.** Even if item 7 proves global coefficient factorization on $Y$, it proves it only for *two data in the global* `XiCRaw Gp …`. The output class asks for analyticity on the local $\Xi^c(G_p[Y];1,\varepsilon)$ domain. These sets are not related by a bare restriction: `SmallField.Xi.XiBounds.grad/lap/close` and `XiCBounds.grad/lap/close` quantify over the generating set's $\Omega_1$, which changes from the whole torus to $Y$ (with deterministic lower boundary layers). For example, the zero extension of constant unit local data across a proper boundary has a bond jump of size $1$, violating the global gradient inequality $|\partial\bar\phi|<\varepsilon/\ell$ when $\varepsilon<\ell$; the global close inequality at an exterior zero-data site is likewise not supplied by the local class. Thus the needed inclusion $\Xi^c(G_p[Y];1,\varepsilon)\to\Xi^c(G_p;1,\varepsilon)$ on strict mark circles fails for the naive zero extension. This example does not preclude a specially constructed chart.

**OPEN-PAPER-REPLACEMENT (minimal statement).** For every fixed $Y$, build the P3 (4.44)–(4.47) generating set $G_p[Y]$ from $Y\cap\Omega_K$ and its prescribed boundary layers, and a local analytic chart on an open neighbourhood of $\Xi^c(G_p[Y];1,\varepsilon)$ times the strict mark and hat balls. For each physical $C$ with $Y(C)=Y$, its coefficient computed with $G_p[Y]$ equals the global physical coefficient on their common domain; the local coefficient has the same bound and depends only on local raw data and `blocksOf Y`. The chart need only cover the strict Cauchy circles actually used by `I5Expansion.coef`. Global factorization alone proves neither this inclusion nor local analyticity.
