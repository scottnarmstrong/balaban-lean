## 1. The two sizes and the exact active-support comparison

Put $a_K=\operatorname{cellSide}(L,m_0,m_1,K)=L^{m_0+m_1+K}$ and $\mathcal B=\operatorname{CubeB}(m_0,K,\mathrm{univ})$. **PROVED.** `Fluctuation.CubeSystem.sitesOf` sends $T\subseteq\mathcal B$ to $X_T=\bigcup_{b\in T}\operatorname{box}(b,a_K)$. `CubeSystem.dExt` is $d_\Omega(X_T)$ when the latter is finite, and $M_{\rm big}=|\mathbb T|^2+1$ when it is $+\infty$. `SizeModDefs.dOmega` is the extended modular covering cost of $\widehat Y_\Omega(X_T)$ if that set is site-connected and $+\infty$ otherwise. At $\Omega=\mathrm{univ}$, $\widehat Y_\Omega(X_T)=X_T$. `SmallField.Def6Terms.IsPolyDom m₀ K X` is precisely a nonempty, nearest-neighbour-connected union of $\pi'_K$ cubes, whose boxes have side $L a_K$ and bases aligned at $a_K$. `Def6Terms.sizeJ m₀ K X=\operatorname{treeLenCovOne}(m₀,K,X)$, the infimum of normalized bond cost over a chosen $\pi'_K$ cover and a connected structure within $X$ meeting that cover (`Localization.TreeLengthCov`). The prime cubes may overlap; they are not a fixed disjoint tiling.

**PROVED (size lemma).** If $T\ne\varnothing$ and $d_{\mathrm{univ}}(X_T)<\infty$, then $X_T\in\operatorname{IsPolyDom}(m_0,K)$ and
$$dExt(T)=sizeJ(m_0,K,X_T).\tag{R1}$$
Proof: finiteness of `dOmega` gives `SiteConnected X_T` and finite `dcovModE m₀ K univ X_T`. `SizeModEntropy.exists_admissible_of_ne_top` gives a `ModAdmissible` family; `SizeModG1.modAdmissible_iff_chosen` turns it into a `ChosenOne` family covering $X_T$, hence $X_T$ is a union of $\pi'_K$ cubes. Nonemptiness follows from $T\ne\varnothing$ and nonempty boxes. This is `IsPolyDom`. Now `CubeSystem.dExt_eq_toReal` and `SizeModG1.dcovMod_of_subset` reduce $dExt(T)$ to `treeLenCovOne m₀ K X_T`, proving (R1). The converse also holds: if $X_T\in\operatorname{IsPolyDom}$, `SizeModCore.dcovModE_ne_top_of_IsDj` and connectedness give finite `dOmega`, so (R1) follows again.

**PROVED.** `Localization.TreeLengthPaper.treeLenCovOne_le_treeLenAll` and `treeLenAll_le` give, for $X_T\in\mathcal D_K$,
$$dExt(T)=sizeJ(X_T)\le treeLenAll(X_T)\le A_{\rm paper}dExt(T)+B_{\rm paper},\quad A_{\rm paper}=1+2dL c_1(d,L),\quad B_{\rm paper}=2dL c_1(d,L),$$
where $c_1(d,L)=(3(2L-1))^d$. The landed class bound uses `sizeJ=treeLenCovOne`, so its needed comparison is **$A=1,B=0$**, not the large $A_{\rm paper}$. For $d=L=3$, $A_{\rm paper}=60751$, which cannot be paid from the displayed step rate budget.

**PROVED (direction and rate arithmetic).** To turn $|e_z(T)|\le B_z e^{-\kappa'dExt(T)}$ into a bound at rate $\kappa_{out}$ on $R_z(T)$, one needs $sizeJ(R_z(T))\le A,dExt(T)+B$ with $A>0$, giving
$$e^{-\kappa'dExt(T)}\le e^{\kappa'B/A}e^{-(\kappa'/A)sizeJ(R_z(T))}.\tag{R2}$$
The reverse size inequality does not give this estimate. If reassignment fibres have cardinal at most $C_{\rm fib}e^{\sigma sizeJ(X)}$, the required rate is $\kappa_{out}\le\kappa'/A-\sigma$. In `Fluctuation.StepRatesDirect.stepRates_direct_witness`, $\kappa_{in}=\kappa_{dec}$, $\kappa'=\tfrac32\kappa_{dec}+\kappa_0+1$, $\kappa_{out}=\tfrac32\kappa_{dec}$; hence the available loss at $A=1$ is $\kappa_0+1$. With $\sigma=0$, $A\le1+(\kappa_0+1)/(\tfrac32\kappa_{dec})$. Its witness has $\kappa_{dec}=100(\kappa_0+c_1+10)$, so this ratio is $<1+1/150$; any multiplicative cost $A\ge2$ fails. The desired direct assignment pays no geometric or fibre rate cost.

## 2. Which cube sets are active, and the rooting map

**PROVED.** `Polymer.MarkedCubesVanish.Gst_eq_zero_of_dOmega_top` makes every $G^*(Y_0,T)$ zero when $d_\Omega(X_T)=+\infty$. Therefore `Thm31CoeffA.gsumCoeff` and `interpCoeff` vanish there, by their finite mark sum and interpolation integral. `MarkedCubesLocal.marked_cubes_explicit` also makes $G^*(Y_0,T)$ zero unless `blocksOf(Y₀) ⊆ T`; each `CubeLabels.blocksOf_nonempty` is nonempty. Thus a nonzero interpolation coefficient has $T\ne\varnothing$, finite $d_\Omega(X_T)$, and by (R1) a class polymer $X_T$. This is an analogue of the `bx∉T → obsCoeff I T=0` clause of `Thm31CoeffB.thm31b_coeff`, but it only anchors the term to *some mark label*, not necessarily to the energy root $z$. **PROVED:** $T=\varnothing$ contributes no interpolation coefficient, since its modular covering cost is $+\infty$; `e1Coeff I D ∅` can still equal `D.term ∅`.

**OPEN (ROOT-MARK-ANCHOR).** Choose the canonical level-$K$ aligned fine root $y_z$ of the coarse block $B(z)$ and let $b_z$ be its $\pi_K$ cube. Construct `E1MarksC` for the physical derivative $D\Phi_1(s\psi')[1_{B(z)}\psi']$ so that every contributing mark label $y_{0,i}$ has $b_z\in\operatorname{blocksOf}(y_{0,i})$, with the same uniform rate and prefactor as in `PARAM.md`. Then the preceding support clause yields $b_z\in T$ whenever `interpCoeff I_z T ≠ 0`, so $y_z\in X_T$. The type `Thm31cde.InterpMarks` has no such root field; `Thm31Witness.nonempty_e1Marks` uses one whole-torus label and $\kappa'=0$, which is not the required uniform construction. **REFUTED:** the fact that the total derivative uses `maskU z` forces arbitrary `E1Marks` labels to contain $b_z$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON ROOT-MARK-ANCHOR and ROOT-DET-SUPPORT below.** For every *active* pair $(z,T)$, meaning $e_z(T;\Theta)$ is not identically zero, define
$$R_z(T):=X_T=sitesOf(T).\tag{R3}$$
The two support inputs force $b_z\in T$, $T\ne\varnothing$ and $d_\Omega(X_T)<\infty$. Hence $R_z(T)\in\operatorname{IsPolyDom}(m_0,K)$, $y_z\in R_z(T)$, $sitesOf(T)\subseteq R_z(T)$ (equality), and $sizeJ(R_z(T))=dExt(T)$. For inactive pairs, define the coefficient contribution as zero and leave $R_z(T)$ unused. If a total map is required by a type, send inactive pairs to the whole torus; this preserves containment and symmetry, but its raw fibre may be large. The **weighted nonzero fibre**, which is the one in `boundE`, remains at most one.

**PROVED (fibre), conditional only on the canonical injective $z\mapsto y_z$.** At fixed $y$, at most one $z$ has $y_z=y$. At fixed $X$, `CubeSystem.sitesOf_injective` (using $L^{m_0}\mid N_1$ and $K\le K$) gives at most one $T$ with $X_T=X$. Thus
$$\#\{(z,T):y_z=y,\ R_z(T)=X,\ e_z(T)\not\equiv0\}\le1.\tag{R4}$$
There is no reassignment entropy: $C_{\rm fib}=1,\sigma=0$. Summing over different $y$ is part of `Def6.Edens`, not a fibre of `Def6.Eloc` at one root.

**PROVED (geometric covariance); OPEN (coefficient covariance).** A paving symmetry is generated by translations by $a_K$, reflections $x_\mu\mapsto a_K-1-x_\mu$, and coordinate exchanges (`Def6Terms.pavingSymGroup`). Each generator permutes aligned $a_K$ boxes; hence it induces $T\mapsto rT$ with $sitesOf(rT)=r(X_T)$. Defining the transformed coarse block by its level-$K$ base gives $y_{rz}=\operatorname{blockBase}(L,K,r y_z)$, the root convention of `Def6.eucE`. Because a $\pi'_K$ domain is a union of $L^K$ blocks, $r y_z\in rX_T$ implies $y_{rz}\in rX_T$. Therefore (R3) satisfies $R_{rz}(rT)=rR_z(T)$. `Def6InfPaving.pavingSymGroup_goodMap/isPolyDom_image_of_mem_pavingSymGroup` supply the landed set-transport geometry. The *value* identity $e_{rz}(rT;r\Theta)=e_z(T;\Theta)$ is a separate **OPEN** input `ROOT-COEFF-COV`; neither `E1Marks` nor `DetExpansion` asserts it.

**REFUTED (the enlargement shortcuts).** The union of all $\pi'_K$ cubes meeting $T$ and one root cube is equivariant, but it need not be connected when the root is far from $T$. A connected hull then may have $sizeJ\asymp\operatorname{dist}(z,T)/a_K$, while a single distant $\pi_K$ cube has bounded $dExt$ (or even $dExt=0$ when already a $\pi'_K$ domain); no torus-independent $A,B$ follows. A fixed disjoint paving by side-$La_K$ cubes also fails to respect translations by $a_K$, and need not exist unless the torus side is divisible by $La_K$. Active marked sets need no such closure because (R1) already supplies the $\pi'_K$ union.

## 3. The determinant must have the same rooted support

**PROVED.** `Thm31a.nonempty_detExpansion` and `StepComplexThm31.nonempty_detExpansionC` assign the entire trace to $T=\varnothing$ and take $c_{\rm det}=|v|e^{\kappa''M_{\rm big}}$. This does not satisfy (R3), and `StepComplexOutAnalytic.exists_analytic_logAC/exists_analytic_halfLogDetCC` proves analyticity of the fully coupled logarithm/trace, not a spatial expansion. `Localization.DecoupledCovAnalyticFull.analyticOnNhd_decLog_entry` provides joint analyticity in decoupling variables and background fields on its strict `DecDom`; `Localization.DecoupledSqrt.decLog_bound` is an operator-norm bound. Neither provides the rooted decay estimate.

**OPEN (ROOT-DET-SUPPORT and ROOT-DET-RATE; canonical construction).** Let $\mathscr P$ be the finite equivariant set of decoupling parameters for the covariance/operator and $\mathbb L(s;\Theta)=\log A(s;\Theta)$ on one common branch. Set $F_z(s;\Theta)=-\tfrac12\sum_{y\in B(z),i}\mathbb L(s;\Theta)_{(y,i),(y,i)}$. Multivariate fundamental calculus gives the exact expansion
$$F_z(\mathbf1;\Theta)=\sum_{J\subseteq\mathscr P}\int_{[0,1]^J}\partial_JF_z(s_J,0_{J^c};\Theta)\,ds_J,\tag{R5}$$
including $J=\varnothing$. Localize each derivative by the decoupled resolvent/LOC V3 into rooted $Y\in\mathcal D_K$ containing $y_z$, using an equivariant finite partition of unity over eligible $\pi'_K$ hulls when more than one is possible. Write the resulting analytic local terms as $f_z(Y;\Theta)$; require their sum to be (R5), their external dependence to lie in the stated halo of $Y$, and their bound to survive the hull and covering costs. Push each $Y$ to $T=blocksOf(m_0,K,\mathrm{univ},Y)$ and define $d_z(T;\Theta)=\sum_{Y:blocksOf(Y)=T}f_z(Y;\Theta)$. Since `sitesOf_blocksOf` gives $X_T=Y$ for these domains, every active determinant $T$ is finite, nonempty and contains $b_z$ when the chosen root is included as a whole $\pi_K$ cube. The $J=\varnothing$ term must be assigned to rooted $\pi'_K$ cubes, not to empty $T$. An equal-weight assignment among all minimal eligible hulls is equivariant; a single arbitrary hull choice need not be.

**OPEN (exact replacement of `nonempty_detExpansionC`).** Prove one *family* $d_z(T;\Theta)$ on the chart with (i) $\sum_Td_z(T;\Theta)=F_z(\mathbf1;\Theta)=S_\Theta.\mathrm{halfLogDetCC}$ restricted to $B(z)$; (ii) $d_z(T;\Theta)=0$ if $b_z\notin T$ or $d_\Omega(X_T)=+\infty$; (iii) analyticity, halo locality and paving covariance; and (iv) $|d_z(T;\Theta)|\le c_{\rm det}e^{-\kappa_{det}dExt(T)}$, uniformly in $z,\Theta$ and torus size, with $\kappa_{det}\ge\kappa'$. Then these terms form `DetExpansionC` at each datum, with the added rooted-support fields. The needed new estimate is a connected off-diagonal/forest bound for derivatives of $\mathbb L(s;\Theta)$ plus a hull-counting bound; the landed operator norm and total-trace analyticity do not imply it. The same real restriction supplies `DetExpansion` once complex/real compatibility is proved.

$$Tm_{out}.E\ K\ y\ X\ \Psi\ h:=\sum_{z:y_z=y}\ \sum_{T:R_z(T)=X}e_z(T;\Psi,h),\tag{R6}$$
with zero terms omitted.
