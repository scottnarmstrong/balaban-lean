## 3. Revised family clause and $(\mathrm{CONT})$

For every $0<\nu\le\nu_*$ require a complete-background pointwise Def-6 member with $m_j(\nu)>0$, the physical action and `XiDom` formed with $\mu_j(\nu)$, and uniform class constants on a common torus and common local-term indices. The running $\beta_j(\nu),a_j(\nu),m_j(\nu)$ must converge at each fixed $j$; $m_j(\nu)\to0$. Use $b_j=m_j(\nu)h$ as the complex source coordinate. Each localized $E$ and $M$ term must be defined and converge uniformly on a **mass-independent, nonempty relatively open set of block-constant $(\psi,b)$ data** (and $\|g\|_1<1$ for $M$) contained in the corresponding positive-mass $\Xi^c_j$ domain for all sufficiently small $\nu$. This matches the quantifiers of the landed draft `SmallField/Def6.lean:529–560` `ContAtZero`, after replacing its current switch-dependent `bgMass` by the complete mass.

For preservation by the step and RG coefficient extraction, require in addition fixed closed Cauchy polydiscs about all reference data inside common relatively open working neighbourhoods, with uniform half-margins, or prove analytic continuation and uniform bounds from the bare `ContAtZero` sets to such polydiscs. The landed `ContAtZero` gives only **some** common nonempty open set for each term, so this extra premise cannot be omitted. On these sets require uniform zero-free control of the normalized local fluctuation factors and uniform convergence of the complete background, covariance, local determinant logarithm, local cluster coefficients and their needed derivatives. Then Cauchy's formula gives derivative convergence on smaller polydiscs; the abstract `Flow/RGInfty.lean:231` `tendsto_rg_solution` can transport it to $\xi$ after the complete RG data bounds have been proved. These are **OPEN-COMPLETE-FAMILY** premises, not consequences of pointwise `BgHyp` alone.

There is a source-coordinate subtlety at $\nu=0$. In fine units the Euler–Lagrange source is $\mu_jh=L^{-2j}b_j$. On a fixed complex $b_j$ disc, $h=b_j/m_j$ is unbounded as $m_j\downarrow0$, but the source remains finite. The limiting analytic map must therefore be obtained from the complete equations rewritten in the independent variables $(m_j,b_j)$ and extended to $(0,b_j)$; it is **not** the field-free solver obtained by simply setting $m_j=0$ with a fixed finite $h$ (which would erase $b_j$). Terms such as the $m_j|h|^2$ vacuum constant must be canceled or normalized before taking this extension. On the physical real path $b_j=m_jh\to0$, the two evaluations may agree at $b=0$, but that pointwise fact gives no complex Cauchy neighbourhood. This joint source-coordinate extension and uniform nonvanishing are the precise missing family proof.

Thus Theorem 5.3 should assert the $\nu\downarrow0$ limit only for families satisfying this revised `(CONT)` **and** the working-neighbourhood/complete-background extension hypothesis. Lemma 5.8's current first sentence, “eventually every scale is $\nu$-free,” and its subsequent free-background contraction argument are withdrawn. A new proof may use a uniform contraction of the complete equations in $(m,b)$, but must check its domain and normalization explicitly. The $\xi(0,k)$ value is defined as the limit of complete positive-mass solutions; it is not a free-mode RG solution.
