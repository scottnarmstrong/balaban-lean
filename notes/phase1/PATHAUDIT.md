## 4. Convention and vacuity cross-checks

- **Mass.** `sfBgMassAt` uses physical mass at all scales; `lfBgMassAt` is field-free at and below `k₀` under scheme A. `bgMass k₀` cannot silently replace the small-field mass. `SCALEUNIF.md` shows `BgHyp.massLe` only for `ν_k≤L⁻²`; this is not the entire late massive interval. Hybrid changes the interface only after its factorization is proved.
- **Sizes and generators.** `DetCauchyHull.dExt_hullOf_le` uses the π′ hull and slope `2dL`; P6 fine chosen-cover `d^cov`, coarse H7/source sizes and site-support size need explicit one-sided bridges. `levelGS` proves `GsSpecStrong` but not local history support. `XiCRaw` is open relative to `rawSub`, and has empty ambient interior (`Localization/XiCRawOpen.lean`: `interior_xiCRaw_eq_empty`, `isOpen_preimage_xiCRaw`); physical analytic families must use the relative parameter space (`Fluctuation/ParamActivitySubspace.lean`: `isOpen_physV`).
