# Q13 round 29: history factors at the second halo

12 October 2026. Status convention: **PROVED** is a deduction from the cited definitions and landed lemmas; **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** names an additional exact premise; **OPEN** has no complete proof here; **REFUTED** identifies a false proposed implication. The Balaban repository was read only.

## Verdict

**PROVED:** The proposed separation from *every* historical seed is false as stated: a component removed by $R^{(k)}$ belongs to $Y=Z_k^c$, and its seeds belong to $W_2(Y)$. The correct separation concerns seeds whose lineage is retained in a component $C$ of $Z_k$. For such a component, every scale-$k$ cube of $C$ at distance at most $2a+3$ from $Y$, $a=\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor$, lies in $\Omega_k''$. This includes the second halo and the possible one-cube extension of the data of $C.\mathrm{genW}(Y)$. The proof uses the actual component separation and the radius of $Z_k^{(0)}$; ripeness is not its source.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `TopBlockTransport` and `ActiveFactor`:** On a nonzero retained-component operator term, `ChiDomProp` at $j=k-1$ tests the top-scale multiscale blocks in that shell with tolerance $3dL\delta_{k-1}$. This is a more precise candidate than an unnamed “factor of the last small-field step.” **OPEN:** those tests, `chiY`, and the other active factors have not been transported into all eight `StrictData` tests of the *actual* local background, notably collar–collar R1 bonds and the composed inherited $j$-fields. P5 (2.31)–(2.32) gives an exponentially small *quadratic boundary term*; it does not assert this missing pointwise regularity. Thus `collarInterfaceCover` and `referenceR12Margin` are narrowed, not closed.

## 1. Geometry, with the radii used in the code

**PROVED (definitions).** At scale $k\ge1$, put $a=\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor$, $b=\lfloor2R_{k-1}\rfloor$, $D=2(a+1)$, and $\widehat a=\lfloor R_{\rm base}\lambda_{k-1}^2\rfloor$. The component width is $\omega_k=2\widehat a+L+11\ge2a+L+11$, by `History/FlowSteps.lean:34–40`, `FlowSteps.RH.le`, and the fact that the running radius is no larger than the a priori radius. `Geometry/Distance.lean:285–294` gives $W_2(Y)=\operatorname{nbhd}_{D}(Y)$; $2a\le b\le2a+1$. `Region/Step.lean:120–196` sets $Z_k^{(0)}=(\operatorname{Op}_{b+3}F^Z)^c$. `Region/Inclusions.lean:114–132` says a point of $(Z_k^{(0)})^c$ is at distance at least $b+3$ from $F^Z$. `History/Core.lean:196–203` (`core_sep`, from `comps_sep`) separates distinct components of $Z_k^{\rm pre}$ by at least $\omega_k$.

**PROVED (retained-shell lemma).** Let $C$ be a retained component of $Z_k^{\rm pre}$, $x\in C$, and $\operatorname{dist}_\infty(x,Y)\le D+1=2a+3$, where $Y=Z_k^c$. Choose $y\in Y$ at that distance. If $y\in Z_k^{\rm pre}$, it belongs to a removed component different from $C$, contradicting $\omega_k>2a+3$. Therefore $y\notin Z_k^{\rm pre}$, hence $y\notin Z_k^{(0)}$. If $x\notin\Omega_k''$, `Inhabited/RipeGeoCollar.lean:71–79` (`ball_Z0`, a lemma for **any** `RegionStep`) gives $\operatorname{nbhd}_{b+6}\{x\}\subset Z_k^{(0)}$, contradicting $\operatorname{dist}(x,y)\le2a+3\le b+3$. Thus
$$C\cap\operatorname{nbhd}_{2a+3}(Y)\subset\Omega_k''\subset\Omega_k.\tag{1}$$
This argument uses `RegionHistory.Z_eq`, `core_sep`, `ball_Z0`, and `OmegaDD_subset_Omega`; it does not use `H.Admissible`. The strict inequality $2a+3<\omega_k$ follows from `RH.le`. `Geo.hRlow/hRmono` and (Λ18) are needed for the wider history/parameter construction, not for this one-step implication once $R_{k-1}$ and `RH.le` are fixed.

**PROVED (active seed margin at scale $k$).** Let a seed lie in $C$. For a new $P,Q,R$ cube, `Inclusions.lean:221–230` puts its centre outside $\Omega_k''$; `ball_Z0` then gives a $b+6$ ball in $Z_k^{(0)}$. For a new $S$ cube, `S_subset_FZ` and `nbhd_FZ_subset_Z0` give a $b+3$ ball. For a seed from an earlier scale whose lineage survives, its scale-$k$ image lies in $U'_k\subset(\Omega'_k)^c\subset(\Omega_k'')^c$ (`Inclusions.lean:183–210`), so the $b+6$ ball applies. A $y\in Y$ within any of these balls would either be outside $Z_k^{\rm pre}$, contradicting the ball, or in another component, contradicting `core_sep`. Hence the scale-$k$ distance from such a seed centre to $W_2(Y)$ is at least $b-2a+5$ for $P,Q,R$ and surviving old seeds, and at least $b-2a+2$ for $S$. In particular their respective four-neighbourhoods and one-neighbourhoods miss $W_2(Y)$. These are centre-to-halo bounds; they do not include removed seeds.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `ScaleLiftDistance` (the block-map metric conversion).** If an old scale-$j$ seed cube, $j<k$, maps to the above scale-$k$ centre and $q=L^{k-j}$, the lower bound in scale-$j$ cubes is $(b-2a+4)q+1$ from $W_2(Y)$: distinct aligned $k$-cubes at distance $m$ have fine-block distance at least $(m-1)q+1$. For a new $S$ seed at $j=k$ it is $b-2a+2$. **OPEN:** the exact torus/coarsening transport of this formula is not a landed history theorem. **REFUTED:** replacing “surviving old seed” by “every seed of every step” in either formula; a removed seed is in $Y$. `RipeGeoCollar.deepA` (`:97–120`) produces one point for its special witness, not a universal seed assertion.

## 2. Which factor actually reads a shell site?

**PROVED (the available supports).** `Def7SmallField.lean:210–243,341–349` uses $G_k=C.\mathrm{genW}(Y)=\mathrm{genSF}_k(W_2(Y))$ but multiplies `chiY` only over cubes of $Y$. `Characteristic/Stencil.lean:178` bounds a `chiK` stencil by three cubes about its own cube. Thus `chiY` alone does not read a shell cube at distance at least four from $Y$. `Characteristic/OperatorDomain.lean:129–144` defines `ChiDomProp`; its first family, at $j=k-1$, tests `PsiTildeMulti` on `genBlocksIn B k (nbhd 1 {y})` with tolerance $3dL\delta_{k-1}$ when $y\subset Z_k$ and $y\in\mathrm{lhOmegaSim}_k$. Its second family has tolerance $3\delta_j$ only on the explicitly stated cubes outside $Z_j$ and the localized $\Omega_{j+1}^{\sim}$. `Operators/Quiet.lean:127–192` has `chiK`, `chiPrimeNew`, `chiNext` on `quietA1`–`quietA4` only when the step is quiet; it cannot be assigned to every nonquiet history. `StepData.dom` guarantees vanishing off `ChiNewAt`, and `LocOp.lean:214–258` plus `DomainIdentity.lean:1004–1077` identify the domain of the recursive component operator with `ChiDomAt`.

**PROVED (geometric candidate for the current field).** A site of $\operatorname{dataSites}G_k$ is over a cube at most one scale-$k$ cube from $W_2(Y)$ by `Characteristic/Stencil.lean:68–91` (`dataSites_cube_near`) and the landed $11M_1L^k+1<M L^k$ range (`Geo.hc\ge14`). If that cube lies in a retained component $C$, (1) puts it in $\Omega_k''$. The localized history includes it in $\mathrm{lhOmegaSim}_k$, so the first family of `ChiDomProp` applies to its top block. A pair in $C$ whose two top blocks are adjacent is read by `PsiTildeMulti` if both top blocks belong to the indicated `genBlocksIn` set. The same current factor can potentially read a $C$–$Y$ interface pair because localized $\mathrm{lhOmega}$ includes the complement of $C$ (`OperatorDomain.lean:100–120`).

**OPEN — `TopBlockTransport`.** Prove, at the exact `blockCfgOf`/`cubeMap` types, that every $G_k$ datum site and every `IsInterfacePair`, including collar–collar pairs, in the retained shell maps to the *same* top-scale values in `genBlocksIn (histGen H_C) k (nbhd 1 {y})` for one valid $y$. Prove the corresponding statement for sites in $Y$ from `chiY`'s `Reg`: `RegIneq` gives `BlockClose` and `GradClose` on its stencil, while its `InUnionDomainR` conjunct supplies only an existential R1 radius, so a separate bond lift is needed for collar–collar pairs. **OPEN — `ActiveFactor`:** establish for each nonzero D1 integrand that the specific localized component operator supplying that `ChiDomAt` is active with the same multiscale field as the background datum. `locOp_vanish_chiDom` is the landed support theorem; the D1/post-`R` transport is the remaining interface.

**OPEN — inherited-field assignment.** For $B_j(X)$, $X\subset Y^\approx$, `LOCALGEN.md` (3.2)–(3.3) gives $\operatorname{dataSites}B_j(X)\subset\operatorname{sset}(W_2(Y))$ under the stated radius and alignment hypotheses. Formula (1) places its retained-shell sites in $\Omega_k''$ at scale $k$, but its physical datum is the composed $j$-field, not automatically the $k$-block of `ChiDomProp`. One must identify its reference field, layer by layer, with the history multiscale field or bound their difference. `chiDomAt_succ_iff` propagates a domain along ancestors; it does not provide this data equality. The one-cube extension for $G_k$ is a further reason not to replace `dataSites` by $W_2(Y)$ silently.

**OPEN (the requested dichotomy).** The landed files do not yet prove that every actual datum site and interface pair is tested with the needed tolerance, and the preceding geometry does not exhibit a retained-shell site that escapes *all* active factors. It does rule out two proposed assignments: `chiY` alone beyond its three-cube stencil, and a quiet-kernel factor in a nonquiet step. ## 3. Tolerances and the strict tube

**PROVED (same-field numerical conversion).** At a retained current top block, the direct history test costs $t_k=3dL\delta_{k-1}$. `R/ChainSeq.lean:169` gives $t_k\le3dL e^\epsilon L^{(d-2)/2}\delta_k$ under its `RunningHyp`; for an inherited $j\le k-1$, `deltaRun_decay` (`:125`) gives $t_k\le3dL e^\epsilon L^{-(k-1-j)/3}\delta_j$. The first estimate is the comparison with the current complex radius $\varepsilon_k$; the second merely converts a number to the inherited scale. Neither transfers a composed field or proves a collar bond. The other `ChiDomProp` family costs $3\delta_j$ at its own scale. `Q13-MARGIN.md` (1.1)–(1.2) proves $\sup_{i\le k_0}\delta_i/\varepsilon_i\to0$ under the genuine early-regime hypotheses.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `FactorSiteAssignment`, `FactorBondLift`, `ComposedFieldTransfer`, and `ReferenceR12Margin`.** Let the resulting same-datum reference bounds on every used $G_i$ be $C_{\rm ref}\delta_i$, and let the physical datum differ at **every** datum site by at most $u_i\le1$, with $|\Psi_i^0|\le2$. Then R1 costs at most $C_{\rm ref}\delta_i+2u_i$ and R2 at most $C_{\rm ref}\delta_i+5u_i$, also for a collar–collar pair. If the parameter ledger gives $\sup_{H,i,G}(C_{\rm ref}\delta_i+5u_i)/\varepsilon_i\le1/2$, the real centre has `XiC` R1/R2 slack at least $\varepsilon_i/2$. This is the explicit reduction of `Q13-TRANSFER.md` §3, not a proof of its premises.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `QuantitativeBgWitness` and `SameDatumMassPath`.** If the same exact massless reference solution has $K_1\eta_i\le r_0/2$ in `StrictData` with fixed BG radii $r_0,r_1>0$, and $2|t|\le r_1/2$ for the normalized mass path, the raw BG slack is $s_{\rm BG}=\min\{r_0/2,r_1/2,1/2\}>0$ (`Q13-TRANSFER.md` §3). Set $t_0=s_{\rm BG}/(2C_m)$ once the path Lipschitz constant $C_m$ is proved; then choose the initial $\beta$ so $\sup_{i\le k_0}\nu_i\le t_0/2$ and the preceding $\delta/\varepsilon$ inequalities hold. R1/R2 do not depend on the solver mass; for real unit $h_*$, R3 costs at most $2\nu_i$ and R4 is zero. **OPEN — `uniformBaseStrictMargin`:** the named factor, all-site reference, and mass-path premises have not all been established for $G_k$ or the inherited $B_j(X)$.
