## 2. Parameter theorem, including its exact premises

Let $V\subseteq U$ be open in the finite-dimensional complex raw-data space; for the slice, read every conclusion as restriction of a representative on $V$. Suppose the following named input **PARAM-MARK-FAMILY** holds: one common $BS,Lab,\iota,y_0$ and functions $A_\Theta(s),G_{z,i,\Theta}(s)$ for $\Theta\in V$, such that at every physical slice datum they satisfy all fields of `E1MarksC`; for every $s\in[0,1]$ and fluctuation configuration their scalar values are holomorphic in $\Theta$, jointly continuous on compact parameter/fluctuation sets, and their bounds and the marked Kotecký–Preiss majorant hold uniformly on a neighbourhood of each $\Theta_0\in V$ and on one positive mark disk. Assume also **PARAM-DET-LOCAL**: $d_z(T;\cdot)$ is analytic on $V$, is a `DetExpansionC.term` on the slice, and has the stated local and uniform bound. These are hypotheses, not results of `StepActivityBoundC`.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON PARAM-MARK-FAMILY and PARAM-DET-LOCAL (analyticity).** For every $z,T$, $e_z(T;\cdot)$ is analytic on $V$. Proof: the pre-summed Mayer activity is a finite sum of integrals of products of $\exp A_Y$ and $\lambda G_i$; the fixed product measure is supported on a compact fluctuation ball. Apply `Analysis.ParamIntegralAnalytic.analyticAt_integral_of_continuousOn` to each such integral on a compact parameter neighbourhood. The cluster term `Polymer.Local.Pterm` is then analytic. The common Kotecký–Preiss envelope makes its series `PY` locally normally convergent (`Polymer.Holomorphic.pe_PY_analyticOnNhd`). Take the derivative in the mark at $\lambda=0$ by Cauchy's circle formula on the *common* positive disk, interchanging its contour integral with the locally uniform cluster sum; this is the parameter argument of `Polymer.MarkedHolo.holo_of_KP`, currently stated for one complex parameter and therefore needing the same finite-dimensional version. Finally integrate over $s\in[0,1]$, again by the compact parameter-integral lemma. Add the analytic $d_z$. The proof needs a common mark disk and local uniform majorant: pointwise holomorphy of $A_Y$ alone does not justify differentiating `Gst = D1(Ff \ldots)` or summing `PY` in $\Theta$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON PARAM-MARK-FAMILY plus external agreement and PARAM-DET-LOCAL (locality).** Let $X_T=\mathrm{sitesOf}(T)$ and $H_T=\mathrm{thickSet}(G',X_T)$, enlarging $X_T$ to include the stated mark halo $\operatorname{st}(y)$ whenever that halo is outside the chosen label. If $\Theta,\Theta'\in V$ agree on $H_T$, assume each label activity $A_Y$ agrees whenever the raw data agree on $\mathrm{thickSet}(G',Y)$, each mark $G_i$ has the same property for its enlarged label $Y_{0,i}$, and $d_z(T;\Theta)=d_z(T;\Theta')$ under the same $H_T$ agreement. Then $e_z(T;\Theta)=e_z(T;\Theta')$. Indeed `Polymer.MarkedCubesLocal.Pst_ext_congr/Gst_ext_congr` and `Thm31CoeffA.gsumCoeff_ext_congr` compare labels whose blocks lie in $T$; their site sets lie in $\widehat Y_\Omega(X_T)$, which equals $X_T$ when $\Omega=\mathrm{univ}$. Monotonicity of `thickSet` transfers agreement on $H_T$ to each such label. The mark and determinant comparisons finish (P1). The landed `gsumCoeff_ext_congr` assumes *sitewise* dependence of activities and marks on an external field; the preceding thick-set argument is its proof with the raw-data agreement relation substituted. **REFUTED:** `LabelAct.dep` alone supplies external-datum locality; it concerns fluctuation blocks only. The raw-data radius is `thickSet(G',X_T)` plus any explicitly enlarged mark/root halo, not simply $X_T$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON uniform `E1MarksC` constants and PARAM-DET-LOCAL (bound).** If $\epsilon_H,\kappa_H,\kappa_0,K_c,\kappa',F_{0,i},Lab.dl(y_{0,i})$ are uniform on $V$, and $\|d_z(T;\Theta)\|\le c_{\rm det}\exp(-\kappa''dExt(T))$ with $\kappa''\ge\kappa'$, then
$$\sup_{\Theta\in V}\|e_z(T;\Theta)\|\le\left[C_{PE}(d,L,K_c,\kappa_H,\kappa_0,\kappa')\sum_iF_{0,i}e^{-\kappa_0Lab.dl(y_{0,i})}+c_{\rm det}\right]e^{-\kappa'dExt(T)}.\tag{P2}$$
Proof: apply `Thm31CoeffA.interpCoeff_spec` at each $\Theta$ and `DetExpansionC.bound`, then the triangle inequality and $dExt(T)\ge0$. For the real step, the identical argument is landed as `Thm31CoeffA.thm31a_coeff`. An estimate uniform on all $V$ requires those *same* constants on all $V$; compact-local bounds only give (P2) on the corresponding smaller neighbourhoods.

**OPEN (PARAM-MARK-FAMILY).** `E1Marks`/`E1MarksC` (`Thm31cde.InterpMarks`) records $Lab,\iota,y_0,A,G$, the fluctuation dependence `dep`, mark size `bound`, holomorphy `holA/holG` in interpolation $s$, and the almost-everywhere identities `hW/hF`. It has no $\Theta$ argument, no datum-holomorphy field, no external locality and no uniformity across a chart. `Thm31Witness.nonempty_e1Marks` produces a fixed zero-class $N=2$ witness using `oneBlockSplit`, one whole-torus label and $\kappa'=0$; its `P` is chosen at fixed $S$. `Thm31WitnessMain` packages fixed witnesses. Neither yields (P1) with a useful uniform spatial rate. `HatLabelsDirect.DirectTerms` and its `labelAct_of_directTerms` turn localized $T_Y$ into activities at one $\Theta_0$; `LocInt.analytic` concerns the summands, and the required physical sharp locality/input assembly remains open in `SharpEngine`. They supply the route to a common $A_\Theta$, not an existing family theorem. Marks require a jointly analytic and local *derivative* of the physical $\Phi_1$ with a uniform $F_0$ and mark-disk margin; analytic activities alone do not imply this.

**OPEN (chart to class).** Even a proof on $V\cap U_{h_0}$ does not by itself give the class clause `analyticE` in both $(\Psi,h)$ variables or `localE` for all fields. One needs ambient analytic representatives for varying complex $h$, the amended old-class domain inclusion, and an extension/descent from physical chart data to the full class domain; see `GENERAL.md` and `OUTPUT.md`. The support/root allocation and its covering-rate cost are separate `OUTPUT-ROOT-EMBED` and `OUTPUT-ROOT-RATE` inputs.

## 3. Determinant coefficient

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the cited chart inequalities.** `StepComplexOutAnalytic.exists_analytic_halfLogDetCC` gives one analytic function on the whole chart $U$ equal on the fixed-$h_0$ slice to $-\tfrac12\sum_{y,i}(\log A_C(\Theta))_{(y,i),(y,i)}$. `exists_analytic_logAC` gives analytic matrix entries. These are total traces. **PROVED:** `Thm31a.nonempty_detExpansion` and `StepComplexThm31.nonempty_detExpansionC` place the entire trace in $T=\varnothing$ and choose $c_{\rm det}=\|v\|e^{\kappa''M_{\rm big}}$. That term is analytic if the trace is, but it reads the whole torus, has no root support, and its bound is volume dependent. **REFUTED:** this witness is a local, uniform determinant expansion.

**OPEN (PARAM-DET-LOCAL; constructive prescription).** Fix a cube partition $\mathcal B$ and, for each $U\subseteq\mathcal B$, decouple the fluctuation operator across the boundary of $U$ (equivalently use the corresponding decoupled covariance/logarithm), with the same branch of $\log A_U(\Theta)$ on an open chart. For each unit site $y\in B(z)$ let $b_y$ be its cube and put $F_{z,y}(U;\Theta)=0$ if $b_y\notin U$, otherwise $-\tfrac12\sum_i(\log A_U(\Theta))_{(y,i),(y,i)}$. Define the canonical connected contribution
$$d_z(T;\Theta)=\sum_{y\in B(z)}\sum_{U\subseteq T}(-1)^{|T\setminus U|}F_{z,y}(U;\Theta).\tag{P3}$$
Finite Möbius inversion gives $\sum_Td_z(T;\Theta)=\sum_yF_{z,y}(\mathcal B;\Theta)$ exactly. If $A_U$ factors over connected components and each root trace reads only the component of $b_y$, pairing subsets differing in a component disjoint from $b_y$ cancels (P3); only root-connected $T$ remain. Analyticity is by finite sums after uniform invertibility and a common logarithm branch for *all* $U$. External locality is on the raw-data halo of $T$ if each decoupled operator/logarithm has that locality. A bare $2^{|T|}$ estimate on (P3) loses too much; the required $c_{\rm det}e^{-\kappa''dExt(T)}$ must come from off-diagonal decay, decoupling derivatives/forest interpolation, label entropy, and the covering enlargement, with a proved positive rate slack $\kappa_{\rm avail}-\kappa''$ and uniform $c_{\rm det}$. `exists_analytic_halfLogDetCC` does not give the all-$U$ invertibility, locality, or rate. The $b_y$ rooting avoids assigning the full determinant to the empty support; a later class-root map must still group the $y$ contributions without losing its rate.
