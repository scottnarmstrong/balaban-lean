## 1. Where Bałaban files the old terms after the step

**SOURCE (P1 p. 113, (H.2)).** "The function $\mathscr E^{(j)}(\psi_j,\nu_jh)$ is constructed at the $j$th step … The effect of subsequent renormalization steps is that the variable $\psi_j$ is replaced by the corresponding functions $\psi^{(j)}_k$."

**SOURCE (P4 p. 681, Theorem 2, (1.8); p. 682).** $\mathscr E^{(j)}_{k+1}=\mathscr E^{(j)}_k$, $\mathscr M^{(j)}_{k+1}=\mathscr M^{(j)}_k$ for $j\le k$. P4 p. 682: "the functions with the subscript '$k+1$' are restrictions of the functions with the subscript '$k$' … the 'new' action $\mathscr E_{k+1}$ … can be obtained from the 'old' one $\mathscr E_k$ replacing the functions $\psi^{(j)}_k$ in (H.2) by $\psi^{(j)}_{k+1}$, and adding a new contribution $\mathscr E^{(k+1)}$. The same is true for $\mathscr F_{k+1}$." The domains shrink with $k$ (same page; P4 p. 704, (4.19): $\Xi^c_j(\mathbb B_j(X);1,\varepsilon_j)\subset\Xi^c_j(\mathbb B_j(X);1,\varepsilon_{j,u})$, proof deferred).

**SOURCE (P4 p. 689, (2.32)–(2.36); pp. 694–695, (2.51)–(2.60); p. 697).** With $\psi=\psi^{(k)}(\theta)+\psi'$: $\mathscr E^{(j)}(\psi^{(j)}_k(\psi))=\mathscr E^{(j)}(\psi^{(j)}_{k+1}(\theta))+\delta\mathscr E^{(j)}(\delta\psi^{(j)}_k(\psi'))$ (2.35). The first summand stays in the exponent outside the fluctuation integral (2.36), (2.41), (2.63); only $\delta\mathscr E_k$, $\delta\mathscr F_k$ enter $d\mu_{s,t}$ (2.51). The new terms are built from derivatives along the ray: $\mathscr E^{(k+1)}_1=\int ds\int d\mu_{s,0}\,\beta_k^{-1/2}\langle\partial_{\psi'}(\delta\mathscr E_k-\beta_kV^{(k)}),C^{1/2}\psi\rangle$ (2.55), localized per block by restricting the scalar product to $y\in B(z)$ (2.57), $\mathscr E^{(k+1)}_0(z)=-\frac12\sum_{y\in B(z)}\operatorname{tr}\log(\cdot)(y,y)+\mathscr E^{(k+1)}_1(z)$ (2.60); $\mathscr F^{(k+1)}_0=\langle g,\beta_k^{-1/2}\mathscr M^{(k+1)}_0\rangle_1$ with $\mathscr M^{(k+1)}_0$ built from $\partial\delta\phi_k$ and $\partial\delta\mathscr M^{(j)}$ (2.52)–(2.53). P4 p. 697: "The 'old' terms $\mathscr E_k$ and $\mathscr F_k$ satisfy all the inductive hypothesis (H.2)–(H.5), (H.7) but now the $j$th terms depend on the unrenormalized function $\psi^{(j)}_{k+1,u}(\theta)$". The renormalization step does the same (pp. 704–705, (4.17)–(4.18), (4.20)–(4.22)): only $\mathscr E^{(j)}(\psi^{(j)}_{k+1}+\delta\psi^{(j)}_{k+1})-\mathscr E^{(j)}(\psi^{(j)}_{k+1})$ enters $\mathscr E^{(k+1)}_u$, and (4.21) keeps $\sum_j\sum_y\mathscr E^{(j)}(y;\psi^{(j)}_{k+1})$ at level $j$.

**SOURCE (bounds).** Old terms: (H.4) $E_0e^{-\kappa d_j(X)}$ and (H.7) $c_9\xi_j^{\frac{d-2}2+\gamma-\alpha_1}e^{-\kappa d_j(X)}$ on $\Xi^c_j(\mathbb B_j(X);1,\varepsilon_j)$, unchanged (restricted). **SOURCE (P3 pp. 77–79, (4.41)–(4.47)).** P3 expands the *whole* function $\mathscr E(X;\psi^{(j)}_k(\psi^{(k)}(\theta)+\psi'))=\sum_{Y_1\supset X_1}\mathscr E'(X,Y_1;\theta,\psi')$ (4.43), resums to $Y\in\mathcal D(\mathbb B_{k+1})$ (4.45), replaces $\mathbb B_{k+1}$ by $\mathbb B_{k+1}(Y)$ inside each term (p. 79), gets analyticity on $\Xi^c_{k+1}(\mathbb B_{k+1}(Y);1,\varepsilon')\times\{|\psi'|<\delta_1\}$ (4.46) and $|\mathscr E'(X,Y)|<Ee^{-(\kappa_1-1)|\mathcal C|}e^{-(\kappa-1)d_j(X)}$ (4.47). The inner sets $\mathbb B_j(X)$, $\mathbb B_k(X)$ of (4.32) are not recomputed. These are pieces of the integrand of $\delta\mathscr E_k$ (functions of $\theta$ and $\psi'$), not class terms. P4 p. 704: "We cannot use the local representations of the actions $\mathscr E^{(j)}$ … we need the whole resummed $\mathscr E^{(j)}$ in order to apply the representation (3.127) [1] in terms of irrelevant expressions."

**SOURCE (P5 p. 489, (1.7)–(1.8)).** With large fields the old terms are the whole-lattice $\mathscr E^{(j)}(y,X)$ restricted to $y\in Z^c_k$, $X\subset(Z^c_k)^\approx$: still level $j$. **Answer (1).** Each old term keeps its level $j$, its domain $X\in\mathcal D_j$, its generating set $\mathbb B_j(X)$ and its function (restricted to the smaller $k+1$ domain), with the old (H.4)/(H.7) bound at level $j$; it is evaluated at $\psi^{(j)}_{k+1}$. ## 4. `eucEloc` for `fullJSymGroup`

**PROVED (deduction; confirms W851 item 4).** With $E(y,Y)=|Y\cap\Lambda|^{-1}S(Y)$, $S(Y)=\sum_{x\in Y\cap\Lambda}g(\bar\Psi_x)$: $E_{\rm loc}(y)=\sum_xc_y(x)g(\bar\Psi_x)$, $c_y(x)=\sum_{Y\ni x,y}|Y\cap\Lambda|^{-1}$. Polymers of $\mathcal D_{k+1}$ are unions of paving cells, so $Y\ni x$ iff $Y\supseteq C(x)$. Take $x,y$ in one cell $C$, $x$ in its last layer in direction $\mu$, $y$ not, and $r$ the translation by $L^{k+1}e_\mu$: $ry\in C$, $rx\in C'$ (next cell), and $\{Y\supseteq C\cup C'\}\subsetneq\{Y\supseteq C\}$ (a $\pi'$-cube contains $C$ and not $C'$); all summands are positive, so $c_{ry}(rx)<c_y(x)$ and `eucEloc` fails for $r\in$ `fullJSymGroup` $\setminus$ `pavingSymGroup`.
