Both parameter vectors vanish outside `C` and have norm `< R` on `C`; they need agree only at those `i ∈ C` with `P i ⊆ compY C`. The conclusion is equality of the *fan functional* at `(u,s)` and `(u',s')`, not merely equality of coefficients [I5Param.lean:91, 231–234; PhysicalVRawY.lean:429–446]. The relation demands equality of both raw components on `compY C` and hats at its unit points [PhysicalVRawY.lean:434–439; PhysicalFNuFanRawY.lean:73–90, 134–145]. Since `compY C ⊆ thickSet (compY C)`, the old thick-agreement premise implies the new exact-agreement premise; no reverse implication is asserted. 2. **K1 vacuity probe — no general `VACUOUS` finding.** For connected `C`, `P i ⊆ compY C` holds for every marked `i`, so `s=s'` on `C`; the claim still compares distinct admissible raw data that may differ outside `compY C`. For `C={i}` it asserts equality of the decoupled functional for equal live parameter `s_i`, raw agreement on `compY {i}`, and hats there. This can be substantive when that component is proper. For disconnected `C`, locality instead forces the coefficient to vanish [AbstractExpansion.lean:276; I5Param.lean:236–254]. The theorem does not prove that every concrete tower has a proper component and two distinct admissible data, so instance-by-instance nonvacuity is not established. If `X₁` is the whole site set, `compY C` is already global.

The component is an `IsNnComp`, and `rowsDep_subset_component` proves that every datum read by the *eligible* rows `SY` lies in `Y` [FootprintFl.lean:49–59]. `i4b_local_of_agree_on_component` uses exactly this inclusion to recover background and fluctuation equality on eligible rows [FootprintFl.lean:61–90]; `hatPsiF_rows` and `fluct_correction_rows_of_agree_on_component` propagate it to hatted field and correction [RowsCompose.lean:292, 345]. The fan wrapper derives the cuts and component from `supN`, while block support/margin places the actual read sites in the component [PhysicalVRawY.lean:72–78, 395–397, 447–465]. This is the intended decoupling mechanism, rather than functional constancy at a degenerate parameter vector.
