# Q13 round 26: transfer from history factors to inherited domains

## Verdict

**REFUTED geometric prerequisite; OPEN full transfer:** a site-by-site assignment of *every* datum site of `Tm.gs j X` to a **nearby** history characteristic function cannot follow from `IsSFFamily` or even the strengthened `GsSpecStrong`. The admitted choice `Tm.gs j X = levelGS j` has the whole torus as its interior for every small polymer $X$. Thus a local $X\subset Y^{\approx}$ can have an inherited generating set reaching arbitrarily far outside the stencils of `chiY` and of the local component's `ChiDomProp`. The first failure of the proposed local proof is support containment, before any estimate of a collar–collar bond or `StrictData` slack. The formula `C.genW Y=\mathbb B_k(W_2(Y))`, $W_2(Y)=Y^{\approx2}$, has a related buffer: `chiY` tests cubes in $Y$, while the background data live on $W_2(Y)$ and its collar.

**OPEN for Balaban's intended minimal supports:** P5 (1.8), (1.24)–(1.25), (1.36) and the boundary construction give a route to controlling the local terms, but neither the current landed class nor its history lemmas identify all the data sites and reference fields needed for a uniform strict tube. P5 explicitly treats the part of $\Omega_1(W_k)$ in $Z_k$ as boundary terms in (2.31)–(2.32), p. 508; the full $W_k$ is not simply declared regular by $\chi_k(Z_k^c)$. The same support issue precedes both scheme A and the hybrid.

## 2. Site assignment and its first failure

**PROVED (the assignment that is actually encoded).** `C.chiY Y` is `cubeProd chiK` over `cubeFin Y` (`Def7SmallField.lean:341–345`). Each `chiK` reads a single-scale configuration on its local `genCube` and tests `Reg` on the cube's one-neighbourhood (`Characteristic/Functions.lean:80–97`); the stencil lies in its three-neighbourhood (`Characteristic/Stencil.lean:178`). The quiet kernel uses `chiK` on `quietA1`, `chiPrimeNew` on `quietA2` and `quietA3`, and `chiNext` on `quietA4`, followed by `chiNew` and an `InUnionDomainR` indicator for its **own** `locGen` (`Operators/Quiet.lean:153–192`). The inherited `ChiDomProp` has first-clause tolerance $3dL\delta_{i-1}$ on `genBlocksIn B i (nbhd 1 {y})$, second-clause tolerance $3\delta_i$ on `scaleBlocksIn i (nbhd 1 {y})$, and fluctuation tolerance $C_{\psi'}p_0(\beta_i)$ on its stated blocks (`Characteristic/OperatorDomain.lean:129–144`). `DomainIdentity.lean:chiDomAt_succ_iff` proves the product induction under `AncCover`. These are **LANDED**, each on its own named set; none quantifies over every site of `C.genW Y` or `Tm.gs i X`.

**REFUTED (a concrete generating-set containment).** `SmallField/Def6.lean:IsSF.gs_spec` (line 206) says only $X\subset\Omega_1(\mathrm{Tm.gs}\ i\ X)$ and no layer above $i$. `SmallField/Def6Inhabited.lean:zeroTerms` (line 46), with `zeroTerms_gs` (line 54), sets `Tm.gs i X=levelGS i` for *every* $X$, whose $\Omega_1$ is the whole torus (`Def6Terms.lean:51`). This choice occurs in `isSF_zero` (line 75) under its stated density parameters; the counterexample here concerns the structural support inference, not every fixed choice of Def7 constants. Moreover `GsSpecDef.lean:levelGS_strong` proves that the same choice satisfies `GsSpecStrong`; therefore the v2 `gsStrong` clause alone does not add an upper support bound. On a sufficiently large torus take $Y$ and an allowed nonempty $X\subset Y^{\approx}$ with $W_2(Y)$ proper; pick $z\notin W_2(Y)$, far beyond the three-neighbourhood of each $\square\subset Y$. Then $z\in\Omega_1(\mathrm{Tm.gs}\ i\ X)$ while no `C.chiY` factor reads it. This explicitly refutes the proposed inference $X\subset Y^{\approx}\Rightarrow\operatorname{dataSites}(\mathrm{Tm.gs}\ i\ X)\subset W_2(Y)$, and hence the requested **local** assignment of that site from $\chi_Y$. Any claim that a *different* history factor reads every such $z$ requires a new global support theorem; it is not in `gs_spec`, `gsStrong`, or `chiDomAt_succ_iff`.

**PROVED (a second support mismatch).** `C.genW Y=genSF k (W2 R Y)` with $W_2(Y)=\operatorname{thick}_{\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor}\operatorname{thick}_{\lfloor R_{k-1}\rfloor}Y$ (`Def7SmallField.lean:210–243`), whereas `C.chiY` multiplies only over $Y$. Thus `chiY=1` by itself assigns no factor to a $k$-block in $W_2(Y)$ beyond every $Y$ cube's three-neighbourhood. For such a block, changing its field leaves `chiY` unchanged by `chiK_stencil`, while the datum of `C.genW` can change there. This is a **REFUTATION of `chiY=1` alone implying (1.2)** whenever that geometric shell is nonempty; it is not a refutation of a yet-unproved implication using *all* active history factors. A collar–collar pair in the shell has the same problem; `IsInterfacePair` includes it, while `Reg` only tests its specified interior `S,T` and the `InUnionDomainR` conjunct gives no $O(\delta_i)$ witness.

**PROVED (what the paper does).** P5 (1.4), p. 489, defines $W_k=(Z_k^c)^{\approx2}$; P5 (1.18)–(1.19), p. 493, restricts the small-field characteristic to cubes in $Z_k^c$; P5 (1.24)–(1.25), p. 494, gives the *separate* operator-domain conditions on the lineage and the new boundary layers. P5 (1.36)–(1.39), p. 497, defines analytic domains only for boundary polymer $X$ crossing the small/large-field interface, using the two cube classes and fluctuation domain. P5 (2.31)–(2.32), p. 508, explicitly splits the part of $\Omega_1(W_k)$ in $Z_k$ into boundary terms after observing that the integration region $Z_k^c$ does not cover all of $\Omega_1(W_k)$. These passages do not state a global theorem that every test of every `Tm.gs i X` is controlled by a retained sharp factor.

## 3. Conditional strict slacks and the reference theorem

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `collarInterfaceCover` and `referenceR12Margin`:** suppose the factor assignment gives direct R1/R2 costs at most $C_{\rm fac}\delta_i$, the reference datum satisfies (1.1), and for every data site $z$ the physical-minus-reference difference has norm at most $u_i=C_{23}\widehat W_k\delta_{k_1}$ (the window estimate is currently landed only on its stated inner collar/interior regions). Then R1 of the physical datum costs at most $C_{\rm ref}\delta_i+2u_i$; R2 costs at most $C_{\rm ref}\delta_i+(2B_i+u_i)u_i$ if $|\Psi_G^0|\le B_i$. Choosing $B_i\le2$, $u_i\le1$ gives the explicit bound $C_{\rm ref}\delta_i+5u_i$. A lower-scale collar–collar pair needs this **at both endpoints**. If the landed parameter ledger establishes $\sup_{H,G,i}(C_{\rm ref}\delta_i+5u_i)/\varepsilon_i\le1/2$ for the actual assigned indices, (1.2) follows with $s_*=1/2$. Q13-MARGIN proves $\sup_i\delta_i/\varepsilon_i\to0$; it does not supply the support assignment, the all-site $u_i$ bound, or the stated $\widehat W_k\delta_{k_1}/\varepsilon_i$ comparison for every inherited $i$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON a quantitative `InDomainR` witness:** `Background/Def5.lean:bg_real` (line 123) gives an exact real unit solution and `BgBounds G D \phi\alpha (K_1\eta_i)` from `InDomainR G 0 η_i D`, $0<\eta_i\le c_1^{\rm BG}$; `theorem71` (line 171) returns these bounds for each supplied witness. It does **not** produce (1.1): R1/R2 are hypotheses of `InDomainR`. Choose $\phi_{0,G}=\bar\phi$ using the *same* datum on $G.\Omega_1$ and collar, $\alpha_{0,G}=\alpha$, $\delta\Psi=0$, and $f_1(0)=0$. Then P1, P2 and P4a of `StrictData` cost at most $K_1\eta_i$, P3 costs $1$, P4c/d/e cost $0$, and at mass $t$ P4b costs at most $2|t|$ by Q13-COLLAR §3. With fixed BG thresholds $r_0,r_1$, if $K_1\eta_i\le r_0/2$ and $2|t|\le r_1/2$, the eight raw margins are at least
$$s_{\rm BG}=\min\{r_0/2,r_1/2,1/2\}>0.\tag{3.1}$$
For a window comparison with nonzero $\delta\Psi$, P4c costs its all-data-site bound $u_i$ and P1/P2 must use the extended reference field; (3.1) then needs $u_i\le r_1/2$ as well. The R3/R4 mass conditions require a separate real-domain check, $2\nu_i<\eta_i^2$ and R4 $=0$. These steps are **OPEN** because the hypotheses are not implied by the existing factor assignment.

## 4. Minimal repair and cost

**PROVED (necessary for a local-support proof):** require the actual P3 minimal `Tm.gs i X` or add an upper-support axiom `dataSites(Tm.gs i X)\subset X^{+r}` with a fixed buffer $r$ compatible with $X\subset Y^{\approx}$ and $W_2(Y)$. `GsSpecStrong` is insufficient, as `levelGS_strong` shows. This removes the whole-torus counterexample, but does not establish the remaining collars and reference-field bounds. **OPEN (sufficient shared repair):** add an inner sharp characteristic on the datum sites and R1 interfaces of the used `C.genW Y` and canonical `Tm.gs i X`, at a tolerance $C\delta_i$, together with exact support maps for the actual histories. Alternatively restrict each term to a generating set whose data sites already lie under existing factors and prove the P5 (2.31)–(2.32) boundary decomposition preserves the action. Either route costs a complementary $1-\chi_{\rm inner}$ sector: an action/small-factor gain, localization, polymer count and norm bound are required before the original partition identity is recovered. No such gain is landed for this added factor. The repair is shared by A and the hybrid; the hybrid already anticipates an inner cutoff, while A would need the same support guarantee or a restriction of regional terms.
