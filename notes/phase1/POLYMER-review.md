## Reasoning per item

**1. The theorem PE.** The compatibility with holes ($X\not\sim Y$ iff $(X\cap Y)\setminus S_0\ne\emptyset$) is consistent: reflexive, symmetric, and with test objects $\square\not\sim X$ iff $\square\in X$. The Kotecký–Preiss condition is the standard one with weights $a$, $b$.

The tree recursion is right. For a rooted tree on $\{0,\dots,n\}$ with labelled vertices, decomposing at the root gives the exponential of the sum over children of $\kappa(Y)F^{(h)}(Y)$. The induction $F^{(h)}(Y)\le e^{a(Y)}$ uses (KP) with $e^{a+b}$ as required. (b) follows from $\mathbf 1[\exists i]\le\sum_i$, symmetry, and $\lvert\varphi^T\rvert\le\tau(G)$. The spanning-tree count rooted at 1 is the tree sum $F(X_1)$.

Absolute convergence for (a) works because every cluster has an $X_i\not\subset S_0$, hence touches some test object. The identification $\Xi=\exp L$ is fine: the formal identity in the $z_X$ gives the identity in $s$, and the Cauchy product of absolutely convergent series closes it. Parts (c)–(f) are immediate. PE-L is checked: the $a(Y)\le c_1(1+d)$ step uses $\alpha\le 1$. The (G3) step gives $\sum b(X_i)\ge\kappa'(d(Y)+c)$.

The hole analysis is the only place where I disagree with the text. See R5, R6. The Steiner size does satisfy (G1) and (G3) (with $c=0$), and the (G2) count $(2d)^{2(n-1)}2^n\cdot 2^{2dn}$ is right once R6 is granted.

**2. BKAR.** See R14. The step "$\rho$ affine in each coordinate, so $\partial_F\rho=\prod_{e\in F}(-\zeta_e)\prod_{e\notin F}(1-x_e\zeta_e)$" is correct. The Penrose bound and sign follow without anything beyond the real-valued formula. The statement that the route "gives the Penrose tree bound" is correct.

**3. Marked version and P9 (3.13).** (M1)–(M3) are correct. (M2) is Cauchy on circles of radius $r_j'<r_j$ with the limit $r_j'\uparrow r_j$. $\partial_1\partial_2\log\Xi=\partial_1\partial_2 L$ near $\lambda=0$ is exact, and the truncated expectation is $\partial_1\partial_2 L$. The conclusion $Y\supset X_1\cup X_2$ with $Y$ a union of a connected cluster follows from (M1). The overclaim is the passage from this abstract setting to P9's actual expectation (R3). The "defect" in P9 is overstated (R4).

The $H_0>2B_0K_0M^{-d}$ condition is stated in the introduction to (1.2) as an example ("for example"), so the document's claim that it is not needed beyond $\beta$ large is plausible. **5. Frozen surface.** `IsPolymer` is block-union plus nn-connected. PE's cluster unions are connected unions of connected block sets, hence nn-connected block sets, so the vanishing clause is delivered. Dependence on $g|_X$ is from locality. Analyticity is from (d) plus Hartogs-free finite-dimensional holomorphy. The bound in block counts is from PE-L with $S_0=\emptyset$ and $d=\lvert X\rvert$, subject to R2. The torus-independence clause is delivered conditionally (R15). Open: R1 (principal log) and R2 (rate).
