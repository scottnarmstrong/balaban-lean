## 1. Modular I-5 and the auxiliary cover

### 1.1 Which generating sets occur

Work in the fine torus at depth $K=k+1$, with $1\le j\le k$. Write $G^+=\mathbb B_{k+1}$ for the actual new background set; $G^-=\mathbb B_k(W_k)$ for the actual old operator and fluctuation set, transported to the same torus; $G_j(X)=\mathbb B_j(X)$ for the source term. These sets have different operators. The new background solves the $G^+$ problem, whereas the fluctuation correction solves the $G^-$ problem in preserving mode, with the extended background field and multiplier from $G^+$ as input. The decoupling *cover* $C$ is a fourth object. It supplies the cubes, partition of unity, interaction cubes, parameter incidence, and one vector $s$. The source and target analytic sets are respectively the appropriate $\Xi_j^c(G_j(X);1,\varepsilon_j)$ or H7 history domain and $\Xi_{k+1}^c(G^+(Y);1,\varepsilon')$ or H7 history domain; they are not a single data domain.

P3 (4.41), p. 77, interpolates the sum of the background composition and the fluctuation correction with the **same** parameter family; there the parameter cubes are described as determined by $\mathbb B_k$. P6 §4, p. 23, explicitly uses the modified set
$$C_0=(\mathbb B_k(W_k)\cap A^c)\cup A^{(k+1)},\qquad A=\operatorname{cl}_{\pi_{k+1}}((Z_k^c)^\approx),$$
when it localizes the interaction, so that $X_1$ is contained in a top layer even at points that are actually at scale $k$. P6 does not separately axiomatize its cover and physical operator, or prove in that paragraph that the modified interpolation at $s=1$ equals the original fluctuation problem. Recovering that equality requires the cover/operator distinction below. For the old-action $j=k$ terms, P6 p. 8 takes **one further $\pi_{k+1}$ halo**, $A^\sim$, in its pseudoscales construction. Thus the unhaloed $\mathbb B^\sharp_{k+1}$ of FL Definition 2.7 is insufficient as a uniform Lemma 2.8 statement for all three uses: a set $X$ can touch the edge of $A$ and its prescribed $r_0$ collar can leave $A$. A safe common choice is
$$A^\sharp=\operatorname{cl}_{\pi_{k+1}}((Z_k^c)^\approx)^\sim,\qquad C=\bigl(G^-\cap(A^\sharp)^c\bigr)\cup(A^\sharp)^{(k+1)}.\tag{1.1}$$
The halo is only one large cube for the geometry, not an extra physical top-layer interaction. One may use $C_0$ for the P6 p. 23 interaction when its individual seed cube has the required collar. Formula (1.1) is the uniform design for the requested modular theorem.

The halo has a cost. Let $r_k=[R_{k-1}]+1$ be the radius of $(-)^\approx$ in $\pi_k$ cubes, $M=L^{m_0}M_1$, and let $h_{k+1}$ be the maximum $\pi_k$-cube displacement caused by taking the coarse closure and one coarse halo (one may use the safe bound $h_{k+1}\le 2L+2$). A **sufficient**, explicitly checkable margin is
$$r_k-h_{k+1}>8L M_1/M+\varrho/M+2.\tag{1.2}$$
The left side times $ML^k$ then pays for the $r_0=8$ collar, separation $\varrho L^k$, and the one-cube range of a minimal generating set. This formulation avoids asserting an unverified equality between the various versions of $R_k$. LFR Lemma I.3.5(f) supplies the inner distance from $Z_k$ to $\Omega_{k+1}$; LFR Lemma II.2.3(c) supplies the one-cube range of minimal sets. One must prove (1.2) from the actual row-order choices of $R_{k-1},M,M_1,\varrho$ for **each** admitted $k$, including the first step. The assertion that $R_k$ is “large” is not this proof. Under (1.2), the definition of $W_k=(Z_k^c)^{\approx2}$ gives $A^\sharp\subset W_k$ with the stated collar; nesting, block alignment, and all older separation conditions are inherited from $G^-$. Across the new top interface, the collar proves the separation directly. This geometric implication is proved by the triangle inequality for cube distance and the one-cube range, conditional on the numerical margin (1.2). Call the still unverified numerical and alignment instantiation **OPEN-COV-MARGIN**.

### 1.2 The operator theorem needed from NC

The exact NC input is **NC for the cube cut of the *operator* generating set**. For $H=H_{G^-}(A)$ or $H_{G^+}(A)$, and every interaction cube $\square_z$ of $C$, cut the bonds crossing $\partial\square_z$ in $H$ and invert that cut operator. Uniformly in admissible $A$, $z$, and $x\ge0$ in the covariance variant, one needs: (i) the coercive inverse and its analytic dependence on $A$; (ii) block $L^2$ Agmon decay; (iii) the interior pointwise, first difference, Laplacian, and Hölder bounds on the support of $\chi_z$; (iv) the corresponding $x$-operator bounds; and (v) equality of the cut and uncut operator rows on the stencil of $\chi_z$. Constants must depend on $(d,L,N)$ and the admissible weight bounds, not on $k$, the torus, $M_1$, or the hole. This is the cut-bond version of LOC §3.1 NC1–NC4, with the cover cubes supplied separately. It is **OPEN-NC-MIXED** until the landed NC results are applied to every $C$ cube and the cross-interface block geometry is checked. There is no need for an operator whose weights interpolate between $G^-$ and $G^+$.

The other needed facts about $C$ are a short interface: $\sum_z\chi_z=1$ on each operator domain; $\operatorname{supp}\chi_z\subset C_z\subset\square_z\subset\widehat\square_z$; at most $3\cdot5^d$ interaction cubes meet a point; $\ell_H|\partial\chi_z|+\ell_H^2|\Delta\chi_z|\le C_\chi^\sharp/M_1$ for $H=G^-,G^+$; the commutator $[H,\chi_z]$ is supported inside $\square_z$ and has the resulting $O(C_\chi^\sharp/M_1)$ bound; $|\mathcal Q(z)|\le N_0(d,L)$; and $\widehat\square_z\subset\bigcup_{P\in\mathcal Q(z)}P$. On the artificial top layer $\ell_{G^-}\le\ell_C=L^{k+1}$, but the block stencil of $G^-$ is finer, so one may take $C_\chi^\sharp\le L C_\chi$ after verifying the interface stencil. This factor changes $c_*$ and the lower threshold for $M_1$; it does **not** permit silently reusing the numerical $c_*$ in a theorem about $G^+$.

These properties yield the parametrix proof without rebuilding the analysis. Define $G^H_{z}(A)$ from the cut of $H$, $K^H_z=[\chi_z,H]$, $G^{H,*}_s=\sum_zs_z\chi_zG^H_z1_{\square_z}$, $\mathcal K^H_s=\sum_zs_zK^H_zG^H_z1_{\square_z}$, and $G^H_s=G^{H,*}_s(1-\mathcal K^H_s)^{-1}$. The cut-row identity and partition give $HG^{H,*}_1=1-\mathcal K^H_1$, hence $G^H_1=H^{-1}$. NC and the overlap bound give $\|\mathcal K^H_s\|\le c_*^\sharp e^{N_0\kappa_1}/M_1\le1/4$ if $M_1\ge4c_*^\sharp e^{N_0\kappa_1}$. The weighted bound and resolvent identity follow as in LOC DG1–DG4. The zero pattern $s_z=\prod_{P\in\mathcal Q(z)}s_P$ gives DG5 and DG5′; the pair induction in LOC I-4a(e) then gives fluctuation locality with the *same* $s$. This is a complete deduction **conditional on OPEN-NC-MIXED and the cover-interface properties**, not an unconditional proof of those properties.

### 1.3 Size with a hole and GM2

Write $\Omega^-:=\Omega_k$ and $\Omega^+:=\Omega_{k+1}\subset\Omega^-$. A modular domain contains or misses each connected component of $\Omega^c$ as a whole (P5 (1.33), p. 496). For a modular $X$ meeting $\Omega$, define $d^{\rm cov}_q(X\bmod\Omega^c)$ by minimizing $M^{-1}$ times the **actual length** of a connected tree $\Gamma\subset X$ meeting a chosen family of $\pi'_q$ cubes contained in $X$ whose union covers $X\cap\Omega$. This is the covering adaptation of P5 (1.34). Passage through a hole is allowed, but its length is **charged**: P5 says the size measures distances between charged components. Contracting a hole to zero cost would change the theorem and can destroy the entropy bound. The empty charged intersection has no modular size and is assigned to the pure-hole bookkeeping before any bound is written.

Here is the precise geometric statement needed. Let $X\in\mathcal D_j(\bmod(\Omega^-)^c)$ and $X\cap\Omega^+\ne\varnothing$. Only its charged footprint and the interpolation collar are required to lie in $A^\sharp$; a full hole component of $X$ may extend beyond it. Choose a family $\mathcal K_X$ and a minimizing tree $\Gamma_0\subset X$ for $d^{\rm cov}_j(X\bmod(\Omega^-)^c)$. Let $X_1$ be the union of the $\pi'_{k+1}$ cubes containing cubes of $\mathcal K_X$ that cover $X\cap\Omega^+$; choose the prelocalized evaluation core $X_0$ so that its $r_0$ collar is **inside this $X_1$**. No uncharged halo may be added to $X_1$ at this point: its size would be positive even for $\mathcal C=\varnothing$ and would invalidate (1.3). If the original support lacks the collar, its enlargement and the resulting change of $\Delta$ must be paid for before applying this statement. Use $C$ to define the parameter cubes $\mathfrak P(X_1)$ and $D(P)$. For a connected parameter family $\mathcal C$, let $V(\mathcal C)=X_1\cup\bigcup_{P\in\mathcal C}D(P)$, keep the components meeting $X_1$, and complete them under components of $(\Omega^+)^c$ to obtain $Y[\mathcal C]\in\mathcal D_{k+1}(\bmod(\Omega^+)^c)$. The geometric hypotheses must ensure $X\subset Y[\mathcal C]$ and that the completion does not add charged $\pi'_{k+1}$ cubes beyond the chosen covering family. Then
$$d^{\rm cov}_{k+1}(Y[\mathcal C]\bmod(\Omega^+)^c)\le L^{-(k+1-j)}d^{\rm cov}_j(X\bmod(\Omega^-)^c)+d(2L+1)^d|\mathcal C|\le L^{-1}d^{\rm cov}_j(X\bmod(\Omega^-)^c)+d(2L+1)^d|\mathcal C|.\tag{1.3}$$

**Proof of (1.3) from the stated geometric hypotheses.** Keep $\Gamma_0$ inside $Y$; its output-scale cost is at most $L^{-(k+1-j)}$ of its input-scale cost because both modular covering sizes use their uniform scale units on the whole tree, including passages through a hole. This conversion does not require the whole source to lie in the artificial top layer. Each charged output cube inherited from $X_1$ contains a chosen input cube met by $\Gamma_0$. For every added component, connect a spanning tree through its $\pi_{k+1}$ cubes to the nearest inherited cube. LOC §4.2 and the landed `gm2_tree` count at most $(2L+1)^d$ such cubes per $P$ and give added cost at most $d(2L+1)^d|\mathcal C|$. Filling a component of the new hole asks for no additional **cover cube**, while its connecting path was already included in the connected tree just built; no edge has been assigned zero length. Prune cycles and take the infimum. This proves the bound. What remains open is the antecedent $X\subset Y$, locality of the source H7 function when full hole components are retained, and the charged-cube condition for the actual P6 domains and selected collar, especially when $X\cap\Omega^+$ has several components. Call this **OPEN-GM2-HOLE-GEOMETRY**. The landed `gm2` proves the nonmodular case; the landed `pe_hole` proves a separate counting/cluster theorem for Steiner size. Neither has (1.3) as its conclusion.

For the exponential arithmetic put $G_0=d(2L+1)^d$ and $\kappa_2=\kappa_1/2-1$. If $L\ge3$, $\kappa\ge3\kappa_0+3$, and $\kappa_1\ge4G_0\kappa$, then (1.3) implies
$$ (\kappa_1-1)|\mathcal C|+(\kappa-1)\Delta\ge\kappa_2|\mathcal C|+\kappa_0\Delta+2\kappa d^{\rm cov}_{k+1}(Y[\mathcal C]\bmod(\Omega^+)^c),\quad\Delta=d^{\rm cov}_j(X\bmod(\Omega^-)^c).\tag{1.4}$$
Indeed the coefficients left after subtraction are $\kappa_1/2-2\kappa G_0\ge0$ and $\kappa-1-\kappa_0-2\kappa/L\ge\kappa/3-1-\kappa_0\ge0$. This part is **proved**. If also $\kappa_1\ge4d\log((2L+1)L^{m_0})+2$ and $L^{m_0}\ge14d$, the GM3 sum over parameter families is at most one by the same finite product estimate as LOC §4.2. The hole does not enter that parameter count.

## 2. The old action and background on the new domain

### 2.1 Exact algebra and two distinct changes

Let $D=Z_k^c$, $D_+=Z_{k+1}^c$, and use P6's notation $\theta'_0$ for the old lifted data and $\theta$ for the localized new data. Let $G'_+$ be the old mixed boundary generating set of P6 (2.12), and let $G_+(W_{k+1})$ and $G_+(\partial\Omega_{k+1})$ be the actual local sets of P6 (2.12), (2.15), respectively; LFR Definition II.2.4(iv) records the latter. The background identity of P6 (2.12) is the composition identity followed by the defining perturbation identity,
$$\phi(G'_+;\theta'_0)=\phi(G_+(W_{k+1});\theta)+\delta\phi(G_+(W_{k+1});\delta\theta'),\qquad\delta\theta'=\psi(G_+(W_{k+1}),G'_+;\theta'_0)-\theta.\tag{2.1}$$
The datum $\delta\theta'$ vanishes on $W_{k+1}$, and P6 (2.13) bounds its response on $(D_+)^\sim$ by $K_3K_4\delta e^{-\gamma_0(2MR_k-M-2M_1)/4}<e^{-R_k}$. ## 3. The fluctuation field and the least costly Lean interface

### 3.1 Why `dropTop` alone fails

The source formula is P3 (4.41), p. 77: $F(s)=E(X;\psi(G_j(X),G_k(X);\psi(G_k(X),G_{k+1};\theta,s)+\delta\psi(G_k(X),G_k;\psi',s)))$. P6 §4, pp. 23–24, says that it constructs $\delta\phi_k(s),\delta\alpha_k(s)$ with the modified top-layer generating set and extends $\phi_{k+1}(s),\alpha_{k+1}(s)$ in the *same* $s$; it does not explain the cover/operator identification needed at $s=1$. In the present physical conventions, the construction needs one cover and two operator families, with an explicit $s=1$ identification for each. The hybrid cover is mathematically necessary in the present geometry. An independent cover of $G_k=\operatorname{dropTop}(G_{k+1})$ and a parameter push-forward is not enough: a fine cube $S_Y^\flat$ can leave $S_Y^+$, and equality of the input multiplier on $S_Y$ does not give equality on the larger set the fluctuation fixed point reads. This is precisely where AE2 would fail.

Thus the landed theorem is not a modular or fluctuation I-5.

### 3.2 Minimal abstraction, and what can stay

Introduce `DecCover C` separately from an operator generating set `H`. It may be a structure over the same fine torus containing `cubeIdx`, `chi`, `intBox`, `hatBox`, `coreBox`, `Qset`, `sProd` and `parCubes`, or a typeclass exposing the current concrete cover. Then change the definitions to `cubeH C H`, `Gstar C H`, `Kcal C H`, `Gs C H`, and `DecParams C`. Their algebraic and analytic proofs need the listed properties, not the equation $C=H$. The concrete old theorem is a wrapper using `C=H`; the new instance uses $(C,G^-)$ and $(C,G^+)$ with exactly the same `DecParams C`. Generalize DG1, DG2, DG4, DG5′, DG6 and the `x` variants through this interface. Only the cover/operator compatibility and the few lemmas proving that the footprint lies in $S_Y(C)$ require new geometric proofs. The identity at $s=1$ must identify `Gs C G^- 1` with the inverse of the **old** operator, not with the new background inverse.

Keep a concrete compatibility wrapper so existing users of `Gs G` and the already landed `i5_background` still elaborate. Estimate 3,500–6,000 changed/new Lean lines for the cover abstraction and paired DG/I-4a transport, with a wide variance from casts and analytic types; this is a planning estimate, not a measured count.
