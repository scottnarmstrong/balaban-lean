**WEAKER THAN CLAIMED if read as Theorem 3.1(b) — `thm31b_zeroClass_fromTower` and `_small`.** The Lean result is an exact sum over **all finite sets of level-`1` block cubes** `T'` at `K=k+1`, with `E(T')=0` unless the root cube lies in `T'`, and `‖E(T')‖≤C obsPrefactor(p,s,β3^k) exp[-(3κdec/2)dExt(T')]` (`ObsUnconditional.lean:273–291`). **The decay bound has content, but does not alone certify localization.** `dExt(T')` is `dΩ(sitesOf T')`: the modular covering/tree bond cost of the union of the cubes, with a finite large penalty if no admissible connected cover exists (`Balaban/Fluctuation/CubeSystem.lean:101–110`; `Balaban/Fluctuation/SizeModDefs.lean:87–116`; `Balaban/Localization/TreeLength.lean:41–48`). Hence arbitrarily putting all of `M0C/betaU^(1/2)` on a chosen large or disconnected `T'` is **not** free: its value must obey the exponentially smaller coefficient cap, including the penalty case. A one-set allocation is still logically possible whenever the entire observable satisfies that chosen set's cap; the theorem does not force separate coefficients to depend only on their cubes. No explicit `Classical.choice` picks `g`-dependent coefficients in these target proofs (`ObsUnconditional.lean:203–208,240,291,313–319`; `Thm31bFromTower.lean:124–133`), but `∃ E` occurs **after** `∀ g,y,i`; any later choice of these witnesses need not be analytic or coherent in `g`.

## A4. Rotation (`Balaban/Model/SymmetryPartition.lean`)

**FAITHFUL — `partitionFunction_rot`.** The frozen integrand is `exp(-β energy(h,φ)+Σg_iφ_i)` (`Balaban/Frozen/Model/PartitionFunction.lean:21–26`). `rotSource R g` uses `Σ_j R_{ji}g_j`, hence is `Rᵀg` (`SymmetryPartition.lean:28–37`); `Σg_i(Rφ)_i=Σ(Rᵀg)_jφ_j` is proved at `:47–57`, and measure preservation plus rotation of the energy yield `Z(Rh,g)=Z(h,Rᵀg)` at `:59–72`. The indices and direction agree with the frozen definition.

**FAITHFUL analytic transport, WEAKER THAN A FULL-UNIT-BALL ROOT-F TRANSPORT.** `rotFamily_analytic` assumes analyticity of `T` on `‖g‖₁<√Nρ` and concludes it for `R(T(Rᵀg))` on `‖g‖₁<ρ` (`SymmetryPartition.lean:212–230`). This direction is forced by `‖Rᵀg‖₁≤√N‖g‖₁` (`:100–116`); the factor is attained for `N=2` (`:145–172`). With only a coordinate-vector theorem on the unit ℓ¹ ball, the lemma gives radius at most `1/√N`, not the unit ball needed for a general unit external field. That transport requires a larger input source domain, a source norm invariant under rotations, or another argument.
