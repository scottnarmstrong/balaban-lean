# From step activities to output terms

12 October 2026. This is a design for the whole-lattice step at $K=k+1$, on the same ambient fine torus as `Fluctuation.StepClass`. **PROVED** refers to the stated scope of a landed declaration or to the displayed algebra; **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** names additional hypotheses; **OPEN** names a missing physical construction; **REFUTED** identifies an invalid shortcut. File prefixes locate declarations, rather than giving Lean namespaces. All repository files cited below were read only.

## 1. What the landed outputs actually contain

**PROVED.** `StepNonvanishBound.StepActivityBound` supplies complex block activities $A(s,t)$, their fluctuation-block dependence, bounds and the almost-everywhere exponent identity. It contains no first-order mark for the derivative of `Phi1`, no `ObsMarks` kernel or remainder, and no varying block datum. `Polymer.MayerMarked.mayer_marked` gives marked scalar expectation identities and one/two-mark bounds under its own measurability, support, decay and numerical premises; it is not a constructor for `SmallField.Def6.Terms`.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON E1Marks and DetExpansion.** At a *fixed* `S : StepSetting …`, `C : StepClass …` and coarse root $z$, `Thm31a.thm31a_of_input` gives $S.E0loc\,C\,z=\sum_T e_z(T)$, $|e_z(T)|\le B_z e^{-\kappa' dExt(T)}$, where $T:\mathrm{Finset}(CubeB\ m_0\ K\ \mathrm{univ})$ and $B_z=C_{PE}\sum_i F_{0,i}e^{-\kappa_0\mathrm{dl}(y_{0,i})}+c_{det,z}$. Its proof supplies the following *canonical formula once its witnesses are fixed*:
$$e_z(T)=D_z.\mathrm{term}(T)+\int_0^1\!Gsum\bigl(BS.\mu,I_z.Lab,(I_z.A(s)).H,I_z.y0,(i\mapsto I_z.G(i,s)),T\bigr)\,ds.\tag{1}$$
The first summand is `DetExpansion.term`; the second is exactly the witness in `Thm31cde.interpAvg_expansion_of_input` and `Thm31Avg.avg_integral_expansion`. Thus (1) can be defined **now for one fixed datum and chosen witnesses**. `Thm31a.thm31a_of_input_const` gives the stated prefactor when its two separate constant bounds hold.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON ObsMarks.** `Thm31b.thm31b_of_input` gives a fixed-$g$, fixed-root scalar coefficient $m_{y,i,g}(T)=kernelMarkSum(I.hker,I.E1,T)+I.Erem(T)$ with $m(T)=0$ if $I.bx\notin T$ and $|m(T)|\le(I.B1+I.B2)e^{-\kappa_b dExt(T)}$ for $\kappa_b\le\min(\kappa_h,\kappa')-\kappa_0-1$. `Thm31bStep.thm31b_of_input_M0` identifies $S.M0\,C\,g\,y\,i=\beta_U^{1/2}\sum_T m_{y,i,g}(T)$. `StepObs.F0_eq_pair` identifies $F0$ with the source pairing of $\beta_U^{-1/2}M0$. None of these declarations gives a jointly analytic, field-dependent family of vector terms.

**PROVED.** `Thm31cde.thm31c_of_input`, `thm31d_of_input`, `thm31e_of_input` expand abstract interpolation averages under `InterpMarks` and explicit size inequalities. Their docstring states that the subdomain $\mathcal B,\mathcal R,Z_k,\Omega_{k+1}$ objects are not defined in this whole-lattice interface. `Thm31WitnessMain.thm31a_witness` and its (c)–(e) companions are existential demonstrations at $d=L=3,N_1=6,k=1,N=2$, zero class; `Thm31Witness.oneBlockSplit` puts the whole fluctuation in one cube. They are not uniform output-term constructions. In particular, `Thm31a.nonempty_detExpansion` may put the entire trace into $T=\varnothing$ with a torus-size-dependent constant.

## 2. Definition of the proposed output family, with exact missing data

Let $\mathbb B=CubeB(m_0,K,\mathrm{univ})$ and $X_T=sitesOf(T)$. `SmallField.Def6.Terms` has exactly the fields `E j y X Ψ h`, `M j x X Ψ h g`, and `gs j X`; `SFTermClauses` checks them for $X\in IsPolyDom(m_0,j)$ and a root in $X$. `Def6.Eloc` sums `E` over such $X$ at an $L^j$-aligned $y$, and `Def6.Mloc` similarly sums `M` at $x$. This determines the rooting convention: the $z$ in `E0loc z` must remain the level-$K$ root, while the support assigned to a coefficient must contain its representative $y_z$.

**PROVED (same ambient $K$); OPEN (class induction across ambient tori).** The *formal record* may copy `Tm_out.E j := C.Tm.E j` and `Tm_out.M j := C.Tm.M j` for $1\le j\le k$, since `StepClass.Tm : Terms … K …` already lives on the output torus. `StepObjects.composedDataAt`, `composedPairAt`, `EcompAt`, `FcompAt` define the old composed action in this same ambient space; `StepComplex.composedDataAtC` is its complex counterpart. No declaration named `psiJ` occurs in these modules: this is the landed composition implementation. An actual input `IsSFFamily2` at torus index $k$ supplies `Terms … k …`, not the `Terms … K …` required by `StepClass`; a uniform `OLD-TERMS-LIFT` and its class-clause preservation are **OPEN**. `Def6v2Renorm.IsSFFamily2.renormalization_aniso_scaled` consumes an *existing* class and proves I-6; it does not lift terms or perform this step.

**OPEN (OUTPUT-ROOT-EMBED and OUTPUT-ROOT-SUPPORT).** Choose an aligned representative $y_z$ with `jBlock … K K y_z = z`. For each $(z,T)$ with nonzero (1), supply a translation-covariant rooted support $R_z(T)$ with `IsPolyDom m₀ K (R_z(T))`, $y_z\in R_z(T)$, and $X_T\subseteq R_z(T)$. A preferred case is $R_z(T)=X_T$ when $X_T$ is already a connected $\pi'_K$-polymer containing $y_z$. `CubeSystem.sitesOf` and `sitesOf_injective` are landed for $\pi_K$ cube sets, but neither `Thm31a.thm31a_of_input` nor `DetExpansion` asserts that $X_T$ is a $\pi'_K$-polymer or contains $y_z$. The empty-$T$ determinant witness disproves either inference from the theorem statement. Enlarging $X_T$ to include the root requires a quantitative cost lemma. A whole-torus assignment is a formal finite-torus fallback and supplies no uniform class rate.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OUTPUT-PARAM-COEFF, OUTPUT-ROOT-EMBED, OUTPUT-ROOT-SUPPORT.** Let $e_z(T;\Psi,h)$ be a *single*, parameter-dependent version of (1), defined as a function on all class fields and agreeing with the physical `E0locC` coefficients on the step chart. Then a type-correct new energy field is
$$Tm_{out}.E\ K\ y\ X\ \Psi\ h=\sum_{\substack{z:y_z=y}}\ \sum_{\substack{T:R_z(T)=X}}e_z(T;\Psi,h),\tag{2}$$
with zero outside the displayed rooted supports; `Tm_out.gs j X := levelGS j hj` for $j\le K$ (where `hj : j ≤ K`), and `levelGS K le_rfl` otherwise, is definable now. `GsSpec.levelGS_strong` supplies `gsStrong` and the core of `gs_spec` for $1\le j\le K$. Formula (2), as a *symbolic* `Terms` record with coefficient families passed as arguments, can be written now. Producing `OUTPUT-PARAM-COEFF` from a physical step is **OPEN**: the landed $I_z,D_z$ are fixed-datum witnesses, and arbitrary `Classical.choose` at each $(\Psi,h)$ has no locality, analyticity or symmetry guarantee. `StepComplexOutAnalytic.exists_analyticAt_E0C_of_analyticAt_Edens` is a function-level analytic result for the **total** $E0C$ on a chart slice; it does not provide analytic $e_z(T;\Psi,h)$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OUTPUT-M-FAMILY and OUTPUT-M-ROOT.** For each $(x,i)$ construct *joint* coefficients $m_{x,i}(T;\Psi,h,g)$ from the explicit `Thm31b` formula, with $bx(x)\in T$ and a support $R_x(T)\ni x$. In the same source units, set `Tm_out.M K x X Ψ h g i := ∑_{T:R_x(T)=X} m_{x,i}(T;Ψ,h,g)`. Indeed `thm31b_of_input_M0` gives $M0=\beta_U^{1/2}\sum_Tm(T)$ and `StepObs.F0_eq_pair` gives $F0=\beta_U^{-1/2}\langle g,M0\rangle=\langle g,\sum_Tm(T)\rangle$. Any change of source units in the new class belongs in `OUTPUT-DENSITY-IDENTITY`; the factor $\beta_U^{1/2}$ must not be inserted into `Tm_out.M` itself. The definitions of `M0` and `M0C` exist. Their assembly into a jointly analytic vector on the full `l1 g<1` domain, with the strict `boundM` constant, is **OPEN**. Copy old `M` only under `OLD-TERMS-LIFT` and the old-composite identity below.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OUTPUT-OLD-COMPOSE and OUTPUT-COUNTERTERM.** To identify `expo2` for `Tm_out`, prove for every real/complex output datum that the sum of its copied $j\le k$ terms, composed from the *new* coefficients and mass, equals `EcompAt C.m₀ C.Tm S.a S.ν … S.psiKd` and the analogous `FcompAt`, up to the explicitly defined counterterms. The `StepObjects.EcompAt_top/FcompAt_top` identities only handle equality of top and ambient index; they do not establish this two-scale composition. Define the $K$ counterterm either as explicit new rooted terms with controlled supports, or absorb its local pieces into (2), and define the new running $\beta_K,a_K,\nu_K,E_K$ by the step equations. The abstract (c)–(e) expansions and numerical `StepRatesDirect.stepRates_direct_witness` do not construct these objects. `OUTPUT-OLD-COMPOSE`, the finite-torus counterterm allocation, and the RG map (`RGFREE.md`, in preparation) are **OPEN**.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OUTPUT-DENSITY-IDENTITY.** `StepNonvanishBound.prop26_complex_class_of_activity` proves, from `ClassRegular` and `StepActivityBound`, the exact exponent
$$S.Tstep\,C\,g=\exp\{-\beta Anew+EcompAt(C.Tm,S.psiKd)-Eppp\,n_U+FcompAt(C.Tm,S.psiKd,g)+E0(C)+F0(C,g)\}.\tag{3}$$
`StepLocal.sum_E0loc` (and `StepComplexLocal.sum_E0locC`) adds the rooted local energies to $E0$ under its regularity/nonvanishing premises. `Def6v2.expo2` is the *target* class exponent. `OUTPUT-DENSITY-IDENTITY` must equate (3), after the $K$-term sums, counterterms and unit/source rescaling, with that target exponent for every realizing datum and $\|g\|_1<1$, and match the output characteristic function and real vacuum normalization. `Def6v2Inhabited.isSF2_rho2` packages already constructed clauses and a real exponent; it is not this identity. The final equality is **OPEN**.

## 4. First three independent Lean packages

1. `OutputTermSkeleton` (target ≤300 lines). Inputs: `SmallField.Def6.Terms`, `Fluctuation.StepClass`, `StepObjects.composedPairAt/EcompAt/FcompAt`, `GsSpec.levelGS_strong`; accept abstract new `E_K,M_K` and an `OldLift` parameter (identity copy when the old terms already have ambient index $K$). Define the `Terms … K …` record by cases $j\le k$, $j=K$, and other $j$; prove the two definitional old-copy equations, `gs_spec`, `gsStrong`, and paving invariance for the chosen global generator. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON supplied abstract coefficients and OldLift**; no PhysicalSharpLoc is used.
2. `OutputFixedCoeff` (target ≤300 lines). Inputs: a fixed `StepActivityBound S C g`, `Thm31a.E1Marks`, `Thm31a.DetExpansion`, `Thm31Avg.Gsum`, `Thm31cde.interpAvg_expansion_of_input`, `Thm31a.thm31a_of_input`, and, separately, `Thm31b.ObsMarks`. Define the canonical fixed-datum formulas (1) and $m_{y,i,g}(T)$, prove their sum, bounds and the observable's `bx` support by unfolding the landed proofs. Retain $z,T$ and the datum as explicit parameters; make no root or external analyticity claim. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON E1Marks, DetExpansion, ObsMarks** in addition to `StepActivityBound`; these mark objects are *additional* hypotheses, not fields of `StepActivityBound`.
3. `OutputRootRate` (target ≤300 lines). Inputs: package 2, `CubeSystem.sitesOf/sitesOf_injective/dExt_eq_toReal`, `SizeModG1.dcovMod_of_subset`, `StepRatesDirect.stepRates_direct_witness`, and an explicit `RootCertificate` containing `OUTPUT-ROOT-EMBED`, rooted polymer support, $dExt$-to-`sizeJ` cost, fiber entropy and prefactor hypotheses. Define (2) for abstract all-field coefficient families; prove the finite-sum reindexing and `boundE` at $\kappa_{out}$, leaving analytic/local/symmetry fields to later packages. **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON RootCertificate and OUTPUT-PARAM-COEFF**; neither is landed from the physical step. The proof package may begin now with these hypotheses, independently of PhysicalSharpLoc.

**OPEN after package 3:** construct the parameter-dependent marked/determinant families from a general physical `StepActivityBound`, prove the root certificate uniformly, then prove analytic/local/symmetric output clauses, observable terms, old-term lift, counterterms and (3) to `expo2`. The first three packages give reusable conditional statements, not output-class preservation.
