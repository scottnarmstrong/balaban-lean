## Findings

### V4-01 — BLOCKER — Lemma L's global path claim is false

**Location.** §3.8, Lemma L(c), lines 1426–1439, and the proof at lines 1460–1466. The assertion is for *every* $x\in\mathbb B_q(W_q)\cap U_q^{\sim3}$, with $U,W$ explicitly called the **global** sets built from $Z_q$ at lines 1427–1428. The proof says, “$x$ lies in $(U^B)^{\sim3}$ for exactly one core box $B$.” This does not follow: $B$ is required to be a box in the lineage of the selected ripe component $Z$, while the global set also contains other components of $Z_q$.

**Explicit geometric history.** Work in $d=3$ at a core scale $q$ on a torus much larger than the sets below. Let $a=[R_{q-1}]+1$ and $b=a+3$. Put one certified box $B$ in the lineage of a component that will be ripe at $k=q+n_q(q)$. Far from $B$, put
$$Y=\{(si,sj,s\ell):-N\le i,j,\ell\le N\},\qquad s=a+1,$$
viewed as single $\pi_q$-cubes. Choose the distance between $B$ and $Y$ at least $\omega_q$ and choose $N$ so that $Ns>3MR_q+8M$. Adjacent grid cubes of $Y$ have distance $s<\omega_q$, so $Y$ is one $\omega_q$-component. Its diameter exceeds $\mathrm{crit}_q$, so the hull rule does not replace it by a box. It can be a Pre component created by a (T2) new-cube event: the abstract (G1) permits singleton new-cube seed sets, and (H-T) permits an arbitrarily larger torus. The box $B$ and $Y$ remain different components. Place them sufficiently far apart that subsequent shadow steps do not contact $B$ before its ripeness time.

The global $U_q^{\sim3}=Z_q^{\sim b}$ contains the entire cubical region spanned by the grid, because $s\le2b+1$. A central grid cube $x\in Y$ belongs to $\mathbb B_q(W_q)$: a neighbouring cube is in $Z_q^c$, so its distance to $Z_q^c$ is $1\le2a$. Yet every path from $x$ to $(U_q^{\sim3})^c$ has length at least $Ns$ in $\pi_q$-cube steps (and at least $MNs$ in scale-$q$ sites). This exceeds Lemma L(c)'s $3MR_q+8M$ bound. In particular, $x$ lies in no $(U^B)^{\sim3}$ for a box $B$ of the ripe lineage. This refutes the printed geometric conclusion under the abstract geometry used by §4 and §8; it does not assert that the exact P5–P6 region calculus produces this particular grid.

**Refutation check and repair.** The distance calculation of §3.1 does *not* save the global assertion: it separates the cluster around $B$ from the cluster around $Y$ but places no diameter bound on the latter. P7 p. 498 explicitly says that its §2 works with one component $Z$ and suppresses intersections $Z_j\cap Z$; P7 (3.11), p. 522, is made in that localized convention. Replace Lemma L(c)'s quantifier by $x$ in the localized support of the ripe lineage, equivalently in the union of the $(U^B)^{\sim3}$ for its core boxes, and state $U,W$ there as localized sets. Then prove that the characteristic-function and gradient bounds of P7 (3.7) restrict to that local domain, using Lemma F or a direct locality lemma. For such an $x$, the stated $2a+(a+3)+O(1)$ straight path and the $\omega_q$ margin do work. The global version of (3.11)$'$ must not be claimed.
