## 1. What the landed decay actually bounds

**PROVED (metric and rates).** `Multiscale/PathMetric.lean:54,121` defines the cost of a nearest-neighbour bond as $1/\max(\ell(x),\ell(y))$, where $\ell(x)=L^{\operatorname{layerIndex}(x)}$ (`GeneratingSet.lean:145`), and `G.pathDist` is the path infimum inside $G.\Omega_1$. `Multiscale/Counting.lean:270` defines $\operatorname{decay}(\gamma,D)=e^{-\gamma D}$ for finite $D$ and zero at $D=\infty$. Thus this is a **scaled multiscale path distance**, neither unscaled fine $\ell^1$ nor $\ell^\infty$ distance. `Localization/DecoupledCube.lean:63` gives $\gamma_w(d)=\operatorname{agmonRate}(d)/8$, with $\operatorname{agmonRate}(d)=\operatorname{poincareConst}(d)/(16d)$ (`Multiscale/Agmon.lean:79`). On a *single* layer $j$, `Green.oneScale_pathDist_ge` (`OneScaleDecay.lean:150`) gives $D\ge \operatorname{torusDist}(x,y)/L^j$; a gap $a_K$ at layer 1 would then give $D\ge a_K/L$, far stronger than a polynomial count. This single-layer bound does **not** apply automatically to the mixed P6 operator.

**PROVED (Green statements; no marked derivative).** `Localization/DecoupledMain.lean:380`, `dg3`, says for a source $f$ supported in one $j'$ block,
$$|[G_s(A)f](x)|\le B_G e^{N_0(d,L)\kappa}\,e^{-\gamma_w D_G(x,y')}\,L^{2j'}M_f,$$
with the same right side for the spacing-weighted first and second differences. The constants $c_*,B_G\ge0$ are **existential** in this theorem; there is no numerical $B_G$ in its statement. `Decoupled2Gs.lean:555`, `Dec2.dg2_sup`, supplies the mixed-cover counterpart as a weighted `SupBdW` estimate with $B_G e^{N_0\kappa}C_b$ under $c_*e^{N_0\kappa}\le L^{m_1}$; choosing distance weights yields entry decay, but no separately landed `Dec2.dg3` theorem was found. `Decoupled2Solve.lean:46` makes `Dec2.decG` an alias of `Dec2.Gs` with parameters extended by 1. `Decoupled2Gs.lean:29`, `Gstar_apply`, is a pointwise sum over centres. `Decoupled2Dg.lean:182`, `cube_star`, bounds one interior cube Green solution in the weighted `StarBd` norm by $B_w C_b e^{2\gamma_w d^2}/w(x)$, with existential $B_w$; `Decoupled2Dg.lean:374`, `dg1`, gives the weighted Neumann contraction $1/4$. None bounds $\partial_{s_P}G_s$. `Decoupled2Geometry.lean:116,140,189`, `dg5_support`/`dg5_local`/`dg5'`, give exact support and component locality, not a quantitative tail. `Decoupled2Analytic.lean:96,121` gives analytic entries and Lipschitz dependence in the **potential $A$**, not in one marked $s_P$.

**PROVED (background).** `Decoupled2FieldClose.lean:508`, `Dec2.i4a_close`, compares a solution at $s$ with the one at $s\equiv1$: on $H.\Omega_1$, $\ell(x)^2|\alpha_s-\alpha_1|(x)\le C_{\rm close}\delta_1 e^{-\gamma_w d_H(x,W)}$, and $|\phi_s-\phi_1|(y)\le2C_{\rm close}\delta_1 e^{-\gamma_w d_H(y,W)}$ (also weighted derivatives). Here $W$ must contain **every** interaction box with `sProd(s) ≠ 1`. `Decoupled2Background.lean:248`, `i4b_close`, has the same rate for `decBgMult`/`decBgField` with $C_{\rm close}=\texttt{closeConst}\,d\,L\,N\,B_G\,\kappa\,(\texttt{lemma53Const}\,d\,L\cdot\delta)$. `Decoupled2BackgroundLocal.lean:39`, `i4b_local`, is equality on a component under agreement; it has no exponential rate. `DecoupledBackground.lean:70` defines $K_4=\texttt{lemma53Const}\,[1+L+2dL^2+(N+1)c'_{K,2}]$, a **size constant**. `Fluctuation/HatEngineDom.lean:37` defines `decDomMargin` as $c+2\delta+c[c+2(1+3\delta)]$ with $c=K_4\delta$; neither is a spatial decay theorem.

**PROVED (square root, with a narrower scope).** `Localization/DecoupledCovWeighted.lean:770`, `I4c_close_sqrt_entry`, bounds one matrix entry of $C^{1/2}(s)-C^{1/2}(1)$ at output unit block $b$ by
$$\texttt{covWSqrt}\,d\,L\,a_k\,\kappa\,\gamma_x\,(2d)\ e^{-\gamma_x D},\qquad
\gamma_x=\texttt{xAgmonRate}\,d\,L=\frac{\texttt{xPoincareConst}\,d}{16dL},$$
if that block is at `siteSetDist` at least $D$ from a set $W$ containing **all** non-one parameter interaction boxes. The input block is unweighted. `DecoupledSqrt.lean:330`, `decCovSqrt_bound`, is only the global squared norm bound $\|C^{1/2}(s)f\|_2^2\le\texttt{covB5}^2\|f\|_2^2$. The cited entry theorem is for the **single-generating-set** covariance and fixes its $\alpha,\phi$ inputs; no `Dec2` mixed-cover square-root decay or $\partial_{s_P}$ theorem is landed; call this gap **OPEN-P6-MIXED-COV-SQRT-DECAY**. Its rate $\gamma_x$ cannot be silently replaced by $\gamma_w$.

**PROVED (distance qualification).** `DecoupledFieldX0.lean:60` only gives $D_H(x,p)\ge\min(\operatorname{torusDist}(x,p),\varrho L^{j+1}-1)/L^{j+1}$ for $x$ in layer $j$; it saturates at order $\varrho$. A fine separation $a_K/p_1=L^{K-1}$ therefore does **not** by itself imply path distance $cL^{K-1}$. Conversely `Multiscale/PathMetricBounds.lean:448,482`, `pathDist_separation_le`/`pathDist_separation`, give $D_H(y,y')\ge(\varrho-1)(|j-j'|-1)_+$ for sites in layers $j,j'$. This may give decay exponential in the **number of layers**. For the P6 old operator, `P6PairHalo.lean:233,247` gives $H^-=p6Hold$ with deepest occupied layer $k$ and empty layer $K=k+1$; `MinimalGenSet.lean:176` gives $\varrho=5LM_1$.

## 2. The proposed coefficient product and its failure

**PROVED read-set definition; OPEN geometry inclusion.** Let $A_X$ be the sites of $B(3k+1)\cap X$ at which P6 (4.32)'s **$s$-dependent output** $\psi_{\rm loc}^{(k)}+\beta_k^{-1/2}C_{\rm loc}^{(k)1/2}\psi$ is fed to $B^{(k)}(X,A_k)$. The old-spin arguments on $X\cap\Omega_{k+1}^c$ mentioned immediately after (4.32) are independent inputs in the new fluctuation expectation; do not call them $s$-dependent reads. For a cover patch $P$, the direct operator dependence begins at centres $z$ with $P\in C.Qset(z)$ (`Decoupled2Geometry.lean:110`, `sProd_ne_zero_iff`); set $D_P=\bigcup_{z:P\in C.Qset(z)}(\operatorname{hatI}_H z)$, intersected with $H^-.\Omega_1$. The relevant distance is $d_*(P,A_X)=\inf_{u\in D_P,v\in A_X}H^-.pathDist(u,v)$, not distance to the whole hole or to the whole old operator domain $W_k$. Showing $A_X\subset H^-.\Omega_k$ (hence in layer $k$ because $H^-.\Omega_{k+1}=\varnothing$), and keeping the direct $s_P$ dependence inside $D_P$, is **OPEN-P6-READ-LAYER-AND-SUPPORT** for the actual old-boundary composite.

**REFUTED Cauchy inference, even if every first derivative decays.** Put $R=e^\kappa$, $w_i=e^{-\delta D_i}\ll1$, and $F(s_1,s_2)=R^{-2}\min(w_1,w_2)s_1s_2$. On $|s_i|\le R$, each $\partial_{s_i}F$ is bounded by $R^{-1}w_i$, but the marked coefficient is $a_{\{1,2\}}=R^{-2}\min(w_1,w_2)$, not bounded by a uniform constant times $R^{-2}w_1w_2$ as both weights tend to zero. One-variable Cauchy estimates yield at most **one** spatial weight (or the smallest of several), not a product over disconnected patch components. A product needs **OPEN-P6-MIXED-DIFFERENCE-DECAY**: a uniform mixed finite-difference/derivative estimate for the actual composite with one spatial factor per component, on a common strict H7 polydisc. The all-one comparisons `i4b_close` and `I4c_close_sqrt_entry` do not supply this: with other marks set to 0, their $W$ includes those marks too, and the latter also freezes $\alpha,\phi$. The landed analytic `i4a_analytic` (`Decoupled2SolveAnalytic.lean:64`) supplies no weighted mixed derivative. Hence the requested physical estimate $\|a_{X,C}\|\le\text{(4.34)}\prod w(C_0)$ is **OPEN**, not proved by the displayed Cauchy argument.

## 3. Exact conditional weighted count

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `OPEN-P6-MIXED-DIFFERENCE-DECAY` and `OPEN-PATCH-WEIGHTED-COUNT`.** Let $b=3(L+4)^d$ from `MooreDegree.lean:271` (the sharp direct index estimate is $3(L+2)^d$ at line 223), $B=b+1$, $t=e^{-\kappa_2}$, $z=B^2t<1$, and $\eta=z/(1-z)$. The animal count `Polymer.card_animals_le` (`Animals.lean:188`), underlying `sum_rooted_le` (`RootedAnimals.lean:303`), bounds nonempty connected patch sets of size $n$ containing one fixed patch by $B^{2n}$. Assign to each hole-only component $C_0$ a canonical patch minimizing $d_*(P,A_X)$; then $w(C_0)=e^{-\delta d_*(P,A_X)}$. Assign an active-rooted component a canonical active patch with weight 1. Dropping the disjointness restriction and multiplying the one-root series gives
$$\sum_{C\text{ at fixed }S}t^{|C|}\prod_{C_0\ {\rm hole}}w(C_0)
\le\prod_{P\in R_A\cup R_h}\left(1+\eta u_P\right)
\le\exp\!\left(\eta\left[|R_A|+\sum_{P\in R_h}e^{-\delta d_*(P,A_X)}\right]\right),\tag{3.1}$$
where $u_P=1$ on active roots and $u_P=e^{-\delta d_*(P,A_X)}$ otherwise. This is a **mathematical** graph bound; its Lean packaging is open. The landed `sum_rooted_le` itself has unweighted finite root sets and does not imply the weighted last step. If $|R_A|\le b_Ac_X(1+\Delta_X)$, $b_A=3^d(L^{m_0})^d$, $c_X=L^dc_G$, and the missing count gives $\sum_{R_h}e^{-\delta d_*}\le c_h(1+\Delta_X)$, then (3.1) gives
$$H'=\exp(\eta[b_Ac_X+c_h]),\quad q'=\eta[b_Ac_X+c_h],\quad
\sum_C\cdots\le H'e^{q'\Delta_X}.\tag{3.2}$$
The active-root count is the VMOD-24/26 geometry; the weighted hole-root count for the **actual** candidate set is **OPEN-PATCH-WEIGHTED-COUNT**. A useful exact shell form, separately at every cover layer $j$, is $N_{j,r}=\#\{P\in R_h:P.1=j,\ r\le d_*(P,A_X)<r+1\}$ and
$$\sum_{P\in R_h}e^{-\delta d_*(P,A_X)}
\le\sum_{j=1}^{K}\sum_{r\ge0}N_{j,r}e^{-\delta r}.\tag{3.3}$$
If $N_{j,r}\le c_{j,A}(r+1)^{d-1}$, (3.3) is at most $(d-1)!(1-e^{-\delta})^{-d}\sum_j c_{j,A}$, by $(r+1)^{d-1}\le(d-1)!\binom{r+d-1}{d-1}$. A uniform bound on **the sum over layers** is still needed; no such candidate-patch theorem was found landed.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the read-layer/support and patch-packing hypotheses (flat-wall test).** For a chosen row of $N=L^{k(d-2)}$ layer-1 exterior patches from `VMOD-26.md` (4.1a), assume every site of each dependency region is within path-cost $c_0$ of some site in layer 1, while $A_X\subset H^-.\Omega_k$. Layer separation gives $d_*(P,A_X)\ge[(\varrho-1)(k-2)-c_0]_+$. Put $\delta=\gamma_w/2=\operatorname{agmonRate}(d)/16$ **hypothetically for the coefficient**. The landed P6 pair premise `hsep` (`P6PairOp.lean:147`) says $\delta(\varrho-1)\ge2d\log L$. Hence this selected row contributes at most
$$N e^{-\delta d_*}\le e^{\delta c_0}L^{k(d-2)-2d(k-2)}\le e^{\delta c_0}L^{4d}\quad(k\ge2),\tag{3.4}$$
and at most $L^{d-2}$ for $k=1$. This explicitly pays the polynomial row **if** the proposed decay and read geometry hold; (4.1a) is a lower count, so (3.4) is not an upper bound for all hole patches. The square-root rate $\gamma_x$ may be lower than $\gamma_w/2$; using a common $\delta\le\min(\gamma_w,\gamma_x)$ also needs **OPEN-P6-RATE-BUDGET**: $\delta(\varrho-1)>d\log L$.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `OPEN-P6-ROOT-REDUCTION` and `OPEN-P6-LAYER-PACKING` (one sufficient all-layer sum).** Suppose each candidate root can be charged to a top source-cover cell $B$, $|B|\le c_X(1+\Delta_X)$, and the layer-$j\le k$ roots charged to one cell number at most $c_{\rm pack}L^{d(K-j)}$, with at most $c_{\rm pack}$ top-layer roots. Suppose also $d_*(P,A_X)\ge[(\varrho-1)(k-j-1)-c_0]_+$ for $j\le k$. Set $\lambda=L^de^{-\delta(\varrho-1)}<1$. Summing **each** layer gives
$$\sum_{P\in R_h}e^{-\delta d_*}\le c_{\rm pack}c_X(1+\Delta_X)
\left[1+e^{\delta c_0}L^d\left(1+L^d+\frac{L^d\lambda}{1-\lambda}\right)\right].\tag{3.5}$$
The displayed bracket is a uniform candidate constant $c_h/(c_{\rm pack}c_X)$, with $K=k+1$; insert that $c_h$ in (3.2). At $\delta=\gamma_w/2$, `hsep` gives $\lambda\le L^{-d}$. These packing and root-reduction statements are **OPEN** for the physical support: a component attached to $B$ can have its closest-to-$A_X$ patch away from $B$, so charging its weight to the touching patch requires an additional graph-distance estimate and may consume decay.

## 4. Verdict and author question

**OPEN: propagation decay does not yet close VMOD-26 (4.3).** The exact failure is the step from the landed all-one field/square-root comparisons, or individual parameter derivatives, to a **product of spatial factors over marked components of the actual old-boundary coefficient**. `OPEN-P6-MIXED-COV-SQRT-DECAY`, the read-layer identification, and the uniform weighted patch count are also absent.
