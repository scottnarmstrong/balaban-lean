## 1. What P2, P3, and P6 prove

**PROVED (source reading, checked against P3 PDF images).** P2, *The variational problems for classical $N$-vector models*, §3 (3.22), constructs the potential Green operator by a Neumann series; it has no localization parameters. P3, *Localization expansions. I*, §2 pp. 36–42, introduces them into a generalized random-walk expansion. Its (2.8)–(2.11) carry a factor $e^{-\gamma_0 d(y;\omega)/2}$ for each walk after summation of local Green factors; (2.16) multiplies a walk by the product of $s_\square$ over the large cubes met by that walk, and (2.17) retains an explicit walk-distance factor. Thus there is spatial information **inside** the construction. P3 §3 p. 60 (3.4) writes $C^{1/2}=\pi^{-1}\int_0^\infty t^{-1/2}(C^{-1}+t)^{-1}\,dt$ and constructs a random-walk expansion for its resolvent. These are not stated there as a mixed coefficient estimate with one spatial factor for every marked component.

**PROVED (the coefficient actually used).** P3 Lemma 4.2, p. 77, gives a uniform bound for the analytic composite on (4.33). In (4.41) the unselected $s_\square$ are set to zero. By component locality, if $Y_1=X_1\cup\bigcup_{\square\in\mathcal C}\square$ has a component away from $X$, its differentiated term is exactly zero (p. 77 after (4.42)). For a surviving term, Cauchy on $|s_\square|=e^{\kappa_1}$ gives **only**
$$|\mathcal E'(X,Y_1)|\le E e^{-(\kappa_1-1)|\mathcal C|}
                    e^{-(\kappa-1)d_j(X)}\qquad\text{(P3 (4.47), p. 79).}$$
P3 (4.48)–(4.50), pp. 80–81, are used there to convert $|\mathcal C|$ plus $d_j(X)$ into the size of $Y$ and sum the admissible $Y_1$; Proposition 4.3, p. 81, gives (4.52). There is no marked-to-read distance in (4.47) or (4.52). The separate all-cover inference in P3's proof of (4.48) is refuted in notes/phase1/CHECKS1-astra.md §1; this round does not use it. So the answer to the proposed (i)/(ii) dichotomy is: **(i) for the final localization coefficient, (ii) for its underlying Green walks**; the paper does not carry (ii) into a per-component coefficient bound.

**PROVED (P6 seed).** P6, *Renormalization and localization expansions. II*, p. 34, the paragraph after (4.32), says the old boundary term $B^{(k)}(X,A_k;\cdot)$ depends on spin variables corresponding to the multi-index $A_k$, on $X\cap\Omega_{k+1}$, and on $h,\psi$ restricted to $X$. It explicitly starts the localization with the **full** $X\cap\Omega_{k+1}$ and says every component of $Y_1$ contains a component of that set. P6 invokes P3 Proposition 4.3 and (4.48)–(4.50), allowing disconnected $Y_1$; it records (4.33)–(4.34), not a propagation-weighted coefficient. Thus full-support connectivity permits a patch adjacent to an old $X$ component even when it is far from the sites where the new $s$-dependent output is read. Reducing the seed to that active read set is **OPEN-P6-ACTIVE-SEED-LOCALITY**. With the full seed alone, VMOD-26 (4.1a)'s $L^{k(d-2)}$ row is not uniformly counted by a fixed factor $e^{-\kappa_2}$ per patch.

## 2. A mixed Green estimate from the landed per-centre bounds

**PROVED (abstract marked-walk lemma).** Let $V$ be finite with an extended path metric $D$, $\ell:V\to(0,\infty)$, finite centres $z$, mark sets $Q_z$ with $|Q_z|\le N_0$, and kernels $A_z,B_z$. Put $A_s=\sum_z(\prod_{P\in Q_z}s_P)A_z$, $B_s=\sum_z(\prod_{P\in Q_z}s_P)B_z$, $G_s=A_s(1-B_s)^{-1}$. Suppose the output site of every nonzero entry of $A_z$ or $B_z$ lies in a region $D_P$ for every $P\in Q_z$. For some $R>1,\gamma>0,B_0<\infty,0<q<1$, assume the **absolute per-centre row bounds**
$$\sup_x\sum_{z,y}R^{|Q_z|}e^{\gamma D(x,y)}
       {|A_z(x,y)|\over\ell(y)^2}\le B_0,\qquad
 \sup_x\sum_{z,y}R^{|Q_z|}e^{\gamma D(x,y)}
       {\ell(x)^2|B_z(x,y)|\over\ell(y)^2}\le q. \tag{2.1}$$
At $D(x,y)=\infty$, a summand in (2.1) is zero only when its kernel entry is zero, and otherwise is infinite. For $C$ a finite marked family, let $\Delta_CG=\sum_{J\subset C}(-1)^{|C\setminus J|}G_{s_P=1\ (P\in J),\,s_P=0\ (P\in C\setminus J)}$, with other parameters fixed in the unit disc. Let $T_D(x,y;C)$ be the shortest $D$-length walk from $x$ to $y$ visiting every $D_P$, $P\in C$ (infinity if none exists). Then
$$|\Delta_CG(x,y)|\le \ell(y)^2 B_0 R^{-|C|}
 e^{-\gamma T_D(x,y;C)}
 {q^{\max(0,\lceil |C|/N_0\rceil-1)}\over1-q}. \tag{2.2}$$
Here $\Delta_CG$ is also $\prod_{P\in C}\int_0^1ds_P\,\partial_{s_P}G$ when all other selected variables are held at zero as in P3 (4.41).

**PROVED (proof of (2.2)).** The row bounds make the Neumann series $G_s=\sum_{n\ge0}A_sB_s^n$ absolutely convergent entrywise for $|s_P|\le1$. Expand each product into centre words $z_0,\ldots,z_n$ and intermediate sites. Inclusion–exclusion kills a word unless every $P\in C$ occurs in at least one $Q_{z_i}$. Its coefficient at unit parameters has modulus at most $R^{-|C|}R^{\sum_i|Q_{z_i}|}$ times the product of absolute kernel entries; the same holds if unmarked parameters have modulus at most one. Since $|Q_{z_i}|\le N_0$, such a word has $n+1\ge\lceil|C|/N_0\rceil$. The sequence of its sites visits every $D_P$ by the output-support hypothesis, so the sum of its edge distances is at least $T_D(x,y;C)$. Multiply every edge by $e^{\gamma D}$, extract $e^{-\gamma T}$, and sum sites successively with (2.1): a word of length $n+1$ costs at most $\ell(y)^2B_0q^nR^{-|C|}e^{-\gamma T}$. The remaining geometric series proves (2.2). This proof uses positive **centre-by-centre** bounds; a norm bound for the signed sum $B_s$ alone would not justify it.

**PROVED (application to frozen-potential Dec2.Gs).** In Localization/Decoupled2.lean:56–67, take $A_z=\chi_z\,\mathrm{cubeG}_z$ and $B_z=K_z\,\mathrm{cubeG}_z$; then $G_s=A_s(1-B_s)^{-1}$ and $Q_z=C.Qset(z)$. Let $D=H.pathDist$, $\ell=H.spacing$, and
$D_P=\bigcup_{z:P\in C.Qset(z)}(\operatorname{intBox}z\cap H.\Omega_1)$.
The support claim for $B_z$ is Decoupled2Dg.lean:153, inner_of_Kz_ne_zero; that for $A_z$ follows from Decoupled2.lean:163, inner_of_chi_ne_zero, and :181, cubeG_supp. Decoupled2.lean:141 gives at most $3\cdot5^d$ centres at an output site and $|Q_z|\le N_0$. For witnesses $B_w,B_k$ in Decoupled2Dg.lean:182, cube_star, and :299, Kz_cube_weighted, their proofs give (2.1) with
$$B_0=3\cdot5^d B_w e^{2\gamma d^2}R^{N_0},\qquad
q_*={3\cdot5^d B_kR^{N_0}\over M_1}\le\tfrac14,\quad q=\tfrac14. \tag{2.3}$$
provided $12\cdot5^d B_kR^{N_0}\le M_1$ (the chosen $c_*$ in Decoupled2Dg.lean:374, dg1). To extract *absolute* matrix rows from each “for every input $f$” estimate, fix $(z,x)$ and choose $f(y)$ with modulus $[w(y)\ell(y)^2]^{-1}$ and phase aligning that centre's matrix entries in row $x$. Use the admissible capped weight
$w_{x,M}(u)=\exp\{\gamma[M-\min(D(x,u),M)]\}\ge1$; it satisfies Localization/DecoupledWeighted.lean:33, IsWeight, by the triangle inequality. Let $M$ exceed every finite distance and then tend to infinity. The pointwise proof of Decoupled2Gs.lean:334, dg2_all, supplies the corresponding $A_z$ sum. Thus (2.2) is a **mathematical mixed Green estimate from landed per-centre hypotheses**, including a spatial factor for a walk visiting *all individual marks*. It is not a landed Lean declaration. With $q\le1/4$, a simpler constant is $(16/3)B_0e^{-\kappa|C|}4^{-|C|/N_0}$.

**OPEN-P6-MIXED-BACKGROUND-TREE.** The preceding proof freezes $\alpha$. The actual background has $\alpha=\alpha(s)$ and a nonlinear fixed-point equation. P3 §2 (2.13)–(2.17) suggests a rooted-walk/tree expansion, but landed Dec2.i4b_analytic (Decoupled2Background.lean:308) and Dec2.i4b_close (:248) give analyticity and an all-one comparison, respectively, not a centrewise mixed derivative or a convergent marked-tree majorant. The needed statement is: on the common H7 polydisc, each mixed difference of $\phi_s,\alpha_s$ at a read site is bounded by $C_{\rm bg}^{|C|}R^{-|C|}e^{-\delta T(A;C)}$ with uniform tree constants and $D=H.pathDist$.

**OPEN-P6-MIXED-COV-SQRT-DECAY.** P3 (3.4) would transfer (2.2) to a covariance square root if the *mixed-cover, $s$-dependent* resolvent $(C_s^{-1}+t)^{-1}$ had a marked kernel majorant uniform in $t$ whose constant is integrable against $t^{-1/2}dt$. Its $s$-dependent background inputs require OPEN-P6-MIXED-BACKGROUND-TREE. The landed I4c_close_sqrt_entry (DecoupledCovWeighted.lean:770) compares one fixed-input, single-cover square-root entry with its all-one value; it does not supply this resolvent family. Its rate $\gamma_x=\operatorname{xAgmonRate}(d,L)$ may be less than $\gamma$, so no common P6 rate is yet proved.

## 3. Coefficient transfer and the flat-wall count

**OPEN-P6-H7-MIXED-COMPOSITION.** For the P6 (4.32) old term, let $A_X$ be exactly the sites of $B(3k+1)\cap X$ where the $s$-dependent output field is inserted; old-spin arguments outside $\Omega_{k+1}$ are independent inputs. If every Green, background and square-root factor admits a uniform mixed marked **forest** bound, and the local analytic functional $B^{(k)}$ has compatible higher Fréchet bounds on H7, the chain rule attaches each marked propagation tree to one read in $A_X$. This would give $|a_{X,C}|\le E_Xc^{|C|}R^{-|C|}e^{-\delta T(A_X;C)}$, with $A_X$ contracted to a zero-cost root. P3 Lemma 4.2 gives the analytic $E_X$ bound but no spatially weighted higher derivatives; therefore this transfer remains open. OPEN-P6-READ-LAYER-AND-SUPPORT from VMOD-29 is also needed to place $A_X$ in old layer $k$ and $D_P$ near its patch layer.

**REFUTED (tree weight does not yield VMOD-29 (3.1)).** The bound $e^{-\delta T(A_X;C)}$ does **not** imply $\prod_{C_0}e^{-\delta d(C_0,A_X)}$ over marked patch components. Put the root at $0$ and a fixed $m\ge3$ of mutually nonadjacent marked patches in a cluster of diameter $O(m)$ around distance $D$ on a line. A single route costs $T=D+O(m)$ (or $2D+O(m)$ for equal endpoints), whereas the product costs $e^{-\delta mD+O(m)}$. The ratio diverges as $D\to\infty$. Hence VMOD-29 (3.1), (3.2), and the insertion of its $c_h$ from (3.5) cannot be deduced from (2.2) or its proposed tree analogue. One needs **OPEN-P6-TREE-ANIMAL-SUM**, a direct sum of marked sets together with their shared propagation connectors, or a genuinely stronger per-component estimate. The latter is not supplied by a single walk and should not be presumed. This logical counterexample does not rule out extra exact zeros for the physical coefficient when unmarked parameters are set to zero; proving such zeros with the active read set is OPEN-P6-ACTIVE-SEED-LOCALITY.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON OPEN-P6-READ-LAYER-AND-SUPPORT and the rate budget (single-patch flat-wall arithmetic).** For the *selected* $N=L^{k(d-2)}$ layer-1 patches of VMOD-26 (4.1a), suppose $d_*(P,A_X)\ge[(\varrho-1)(k-2)-c_0]_+$ and take $\delta=\gamma/2=\operatorname{agmonRate}(d)/16$. The landed P6PairOp.lean:147 separation premise gives $\delta(\varrho-1)\ge2d\log L$. Their singleton weighted sum is
$$\sum_{\text{selected }P}e^{-\delta T(A_X;\{P\})}
 \le e^{\delta c_0}L^{k(d-2)-2d(k-2)}
 \le e^{\delta c_0}L^{4d}\quad(k\ge2), \tag{3.1}$$
and at most $L^{d-2}$ for $k=1$. This is scaled $H.pathDist$ decay, **not** a fine-site $e^{-\delta L^{k-1}}$ factor. VMOD-26 supplies a lower count $N$, not an upper count for the full row.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON VMOD-29's OPEN-P6-ROOT-REDUCTION, OPEN-P6-LAYER-PACKING, and the same rate budget (only singleton/root arithmetic).** Its (3.3)–(3.5) still bound $\sum_P e^{-\delta d_*(P,A_X)}$ uniformly in $k$: $\lambda=L^de^{-\delta(\varrho-1)}\le L^{-d}$, and the explicit bracket in VMOD-29 (3.5) is finite. This controls the first marked patch of a shared tree; without OPEN-P6-TREE-ANIMAL-SUM it does not control all marked families. If the actual common rate is limited by $\gamma_x$, the inequality $\delta(\varrho-1)>d\log L$ is still **OPEN-P6-RATE-BUDGET**.

## 4. Verdict and Lean packages

1. **PROVED:** P3's final coefficient uses Cauchy decay plus exact component vanishing; its underlying walks contain spatial decay.
2. **PROVED:** the frozen-potential Dec2.Green operator satisfies (2.2), with (2.3), by the landed per-centre weighted estimates and a positive marked-walk argument.
3. **OPEN:** the nonlinear background needs a mixed marked-tree bound, not merely Dec2.i4b_close.
4. **OPEN:** the mixed-cover covariance square root needs a uniformly integrable marked resolvent bound.
5. **OPEN:** the actual P6 H7 composite needs spatially weighted higher-derivative control and an identified active read set.
6. **REFUTED:** tree-length decay alone implies the product of component weights used in VMOD-29 (3.1).
7. **PROVED CONDITIONAL:** with read-layer placement and $\delta=\gamma/2$, each selected flat-wall singleton row is uniformly paid by (3.1).
8. **OPEN:** the full P6 hole-patch sum requires OPEN-P6-TREE-ANIMAL-SUM and rate/packing bounds; VMOD-26 is not closed.
