## T1. The point at which the fibre enters

**PROVED.** `Fluctuation/StepObjects.lean:StepSetting` stores a real vector `h₀`, a block datum `D'`, and `hh₀ : ∀ x i, D'.h x i = (h₀ i : ℂ)`. `Fluctuation/StepComplexAnalytic.lean:StepSetting.withDatum` replaces `D'` only with another datum satisfying the same equality. This is a restriction in the campaign's *step type*, before the later analytic or coefficient theorems. `Inhabited/StepIdentityFibreFamilies.lean:stepIdentity_zeroClass_fibre_fromTower` retains `hDh` for each `D'`; its `XiCData` hypothesis varies the spin field but does not remove `hDh`. The name “fibre” is literal.

**PROVED.** The dependence is substantive. `StepObjects.psiKd`, `covSqrt`, and `covA` use `D'` in the background and Hessian; `Anew` uses `D'`; `Aold`, `rhoStep`, and `Tstep` use `dataU`, which constructs the old datum with `S.h₀`. The fine-unit action has the term $(\nu/2)\sum_x|\phi(x)-D.h(x)|^2$ (`Background/Data.lean:action`); its complex continuation uses the corresponding **bilinear** square (`Background/MinimizerExpansion.lean:actionC`). `StepComplex.lean:dataUC` also fixes old $h=S.h₀$, and `dataUC_h` proves this. `InteractionHatV.lean:hatLift` and the background `bgFieldC` accept a general datum, but `StepComplexJoint.rawOf_dataUC_psiHat` identifies its old $h$ with `S.D'.h` by `S.hh₀`. `StepComplexProp26` uses the same rewrite in its chart and action identifications. Thus changing only the binder of `withDatum` would leave the old and new actions with different external fields.

**PROVED.** `StepObjects.FcompAt` has the bare term $\sum_{x,i}g(x,i)\phi_D(x,i)$ and evaluates old `Tm.M` at `composedPairAt … D`, whose second component is the composed datum's `h`. Its complex counterpart `FcompAtC` occurs in `StepComplexProp26.prop26C_class`. The variable $g$ is a separate observable source, not the external field $h$ or a declared shift of it. `Inhabited/BgDataStructures.lean:unitBg` is a special constant datum used by physical witnesses; it is not a general-field constructor. `Fluctuation/RenDom.lean:RenDom` adds clipped equality to a fixed `h₀`; `renDom_iff_clip` and `renDom_iff_of_forall_mem` identify that restriction, the latter globally on the whole torus. `Fluctuation/RenormThetaDatum.lean:renThetaDatum_h` says the renormalization variation $\xi$ rescales $\Psi$ and leaves $h$ unchanged; it does not turn an arbitrary $h$ into a constant one.

**PROVED (paper comparison).** P4, printed pp. 677–679, (H.4), (H.7), checked against the PDF images, starts from a constant physical $h$ and asks for local analytic extensions at $(\psi+\psi',h+h')$, with $h'$ allowed to vary over sites, and a separate complex $\ell^1$ ball for $g$. P4 p. 679 writes $\mathcal F_k=\langle g,\phi_k\rangle+\sum_j\mathcal F_k^{(j)}(\psi_k^{(j)},h,g)$, not a replacement $h\mapsto h+g/\beta$. `notes/phase1/FLUCTUATION.md` §3.6 distinguishes P5's fixed-real-$h$ H7 estimates from a possible joint complex-$h$ extension. Hence the physical external field is spatially constant, whereas the P4 small-field *term class* has space-dependent complex $h$ directions. `notes/phase1/CLASS-RULINGS-2.md` §3 identifies the physical source $b_j=\nu_jh$ and keeps $g$ distinct.

## T2. What consumes the extra directions

**PROVED.** `SmallField/Def6Terms.lean:XiDom` permits $h=h_0+h''$ for real constant $h_0$ and complex space-dependent $h''$. `SmallField/Def6v2.lean:SFTermClauses.analyticE/analyticM` require ambient `AnalyticOnNhd ℂ` in the product coordinates $(\Psi,h)$ (and $g$ for `M`). `IsSF2.density` quantifies every real $h_0\in\mathrm{Dom.phys}`. The principal `xiFieldDom` has `phys = Set.univ`; `pinFieldDom` has one real unit vector as `phys` but still admits complex $h''$ in `Pin`. These are draft class declarations pending the stated Q13 decision, not a landed preservation theorem. Importantly, `ParamActivitySubspace.lean:physParam/physV`, `PhysicalTerms.lean:physSΛ`, `Inhabited/E0CoeffFamily.lean:e0CoeffFamily_value_fromTower`, and `Inhabited/ObsFamilyJoint.lean:obsFamily_full_fromTower` already give *candidate coefficient functions* analytic on an open raw-data space which includes variable $h$. What remains unproved is their class-domain inclusion and full physical value identity off the fibre; the coefficient analytic clauses must not be dismissed as absent.

**PROVED.** In a physical step with `S : StepSetting`, `EcompAt` and `FcompAt` evaluate old terms on `composedPairAt … (S.dataU ψ)` or the complex `S.dataUC ψ`, so the current step passes the constant $S.h₀$ to every old term. But the *generic* definitions `composedPairAt`, `EcompAt`, and `FcompAt` accept arbitrary `BgData` and pass its possibly varying `h` through composition. `renThetaDatum_h` preserves whatever $h$ was supplied. Thus the present fibre iteration only samples constant $h$; the approved wide term-class clauses and a future generic chart still test $h''\ne0$. `GENCLASS.md` §T3 (scratch report) correctly lists density beyond the one fibre as OPEN.

**PROVED.** The large-field interface also has $h$ domains: `LargeField/Def7.lean:hDomR` and `hSetB` are respectively unrestricted and a singleton; the draft `LargeField/Def7v2.lean:hDomR2`, `hSetB2` are bounded complex balls around the physical constant field, and `Def7v2Class.lean` states boundary analytic/bound clauses there. A fibre value does not establish those clauses. `Correlations/Identity.lean` and `Correlations/KF0Cauchy.lean` differentiate the observable generating function in the separate $g$ source. The frozen Root-F and Root-C targets quantify each real unit constant $h$ (`notes/phase1/SCOPE.md` §1; `Correlations/RootCStatement.lean`); they do not ask for a physically space-dependent external field. Transport from the coordinate vector to all unit $h$ remains OPEN (`SCOPE.md` §2, unit-field transport).

**OPEN (consumer audit).** An implementation-level theorem that *every* later small-field and large-field bound can be closed from fixed-$h$ values alone has not been supplied. In particular, birth-domain composition, Cauchy estimates, renormalization reference jets, and the draft Def-7 boundary balls explicitly refer to neighbourhoods in $h$; `CLASS-RULINGS-2.md` and `Q13-ANSWER-astra.md` §3 name their missing pinned-chart inputs. The root's fixed physical $h$ does not discharge these intermediate analytic premises.

## T3. Three routes

**OPEN (A, recommended).** Make the general theorem a jointly holomorphic chart theorem for raw $(\Psi,h)$ in a bounded pinned domain, with `S.h₀` retained as the real physical reference for restriction and comparison. Let the complex old datum use the *incoming datum's* $h$, so the mass action, `EcompAtC`, `FcompAtC`, and `hatLift` all see the same $h$. Prove the step identity for this chart directly, then obtain each existing fibre theorem by setting $h''=0$. The first restricting layer is `StepSetting.hh₀` together with `dataU/dataUC`, `rawU`, and the `S.hh₀` comparisons in `StepComplexJoint`/`StepComplexProp26`; it is not the background solver or the abstract `XiCRaw` localization layer. `StepComplexJoint.exists_oldSpin_of_hat` already returns old data with `D₀.h=D'.h`; `SharpENu.lean` has raw-pair analytic/localized interfaces; and the landed `e0`/observable families already have open-domain analytic bounds. Reuse these families, but prove their off-fibre value identities and inclusion in the desired class domain with the actual physical activity. A real-only identity on the fibre cannot be analytically continued uniquely in the transverse $h$ variables.

**REFUTED (B as an automatic repair).** `FieldDom` can syntactically set `dom j G Ψ h := XiDom … Ψ h ∧ h\equiv h_*`, and can set `phys={h_*}`. This changes the class domain and the density quantifier. Fibre *values alone* do not satisfy `SFTermClauses.analyticE/M`: `AnalyticOnNhd` demands analyticity in the full ambient product space at each point, even if the predicate has empty interior in $h$. For instance, $h\mapsto\overline{h(x,i)}$ is constant on a fixed fibre but is not complex analytic at its points. The landed raw-data coefficient functions offer genuine holomorphic extensions, conditional on the needed domain inclusion, so this objection does not refute those particular functions. Replacing the approved wide/pinned class by a merely relative fibre-analytic class would require an author decision and new preservation/large-field interfaces.

**REFUTED (C as a canonical *h-independent* physical extension).** Assigning off-fibre coefficients their values at $h=h_*$ makes them holomorphic in $h$, but equality of values on the fibre does not determine the transverse germ. The main density has the explicit $(\nu/2)|\phi-h|^2$ term, and the background, source pairing, and transported old terms also depend on $h$. For `Dom.phys` containing another real field, freezing the coefficients generally loses the density identity and rotation covariance. Even with `phys={h_*}`, the resulting term family has no proved agreement with the prescribed off-fibre fluctuation coefficients or the pinned-chart bounds and reference jets. The existing *nonconstant raw-data* coefficient functions are a better extension candidate, but their off-fibre step identity is still OPEN. Zero *input* terms are h-independent; this gives no such property for the output.

**OPEN (verdict).** Choose A on the bounded pinned chart of `Def6v2Domain.Pin`/`pinAllFieldDom`, with the source-form dependence on $b_j=\nu_jh$ required by `Def6v2Source.lean`, then transport the real fibre result to all unit vectors. This is a change to the current step implementation, not to the approved two-field class. It is several analytic and physical leaves, plausibly over a thousand Lean lines in total; the estimates below are planning bounds, not a proof-size guarantee.
