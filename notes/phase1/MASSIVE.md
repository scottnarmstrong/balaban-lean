## 1. The two indices and the paper's last integral

**PROVED (P5 §1, (1.9), p. 490).** The switch $k_0$ is determined by $\beta_k\nu_k\le1$: below it the background variational problems omit $\nu_k$; above it they use the complete, positive-mass problems. P5 says at the $k_0$-th transformation the “incomplete” backgrounds change into “complete” ones. P6 §1, pp. 1–3, Theorem 4, applies the ordinary $S^{(k)}T^{(k)}$ procedure above the switch with $R$ the identity; the resulting densities satisfy (H.1)–(H.5), (H.7) while $\nu_k\le1$. This is a claim about the produced density range, not a command to take another ordinary step after the final threshold.

**PROVED (P4 §5, pp. 713–718, especially (5.20)–(5.31)).** This section solves the renormalization conditions for the output of an ordinary $k\to k+1$ step; its fixed-point equations choose the running constants. It does not state a replacement for the later final integral. The positive-mass variational problem at the end is treated separately in P4 §6 and P7 §4.

**PROVED (P4 §6, p. 719, first paragraph; P5 §1, p. 500; P7 §4, p. 529).** The different index $k_f$ is the **first** index with $\nu_{k_f}>\frac78L^{-2}$. P4 states $\nu_{k_f-1}\le\frac78L^{-2}$, $\nu_{k_f,u}=L^2\nu_{k_f-1}\le\frac78$, and $\nu_{k_f}<1$. P5 says the remaining ordinary transformations are performed only until this first crossing, in fewer than $\log\beta_{k_0}$ steps. P7 §1, Theorem 5, describes densities up to this point; P7 §4 then performs the **last integration**, with no further renormalization transformation. In the exact P4 statement the threshold is $\frac78L^{-2}$, not $L^{-2}$; it leaves a positive margin below $L^{-2}$ for every preceding ordinary input.

**PROVED (P4 §6, (6.1)–(6.5), pp. 719–720; P7 §4, (4.1)–(4.9), pp. 529–531).** The final characteristic function confines $\psi_{k_f}$ near the unit vector $h$: P4 (6.2) gives $|\psi_{k_f}-h|<(5L+3)\delta_{k_f}$. P4 expands at $\psi=h$, $\phi(h,h)=h$, $\alpha=0$; its displayed (6.5) is
$$\tfrac12\langle\psi',\Delta^{(k_f)}\psi'\rangle\ge\tfrac{\gamma_0}{2}\langle\psi',(-\Delta+\nu_{k_f})\psi'\rangle\ge\tfrac{\gamma_0}{2}\langle\psi',(-\Delta+\tfrac78L^{-2})\psi'\rangle.\tag{M1}$$
Thus a massive Gaussian controls the integral of the **whole remaining scale-$k_f$ field**, in the rescaled unit-lattice units of that scale. P7 (4.3)–(4.6) poses a new boundary variational problem whose main action already has mass; P7 says the averaging term is not needed to create it. P7 then repeats fluctuation localization for the small-field region, retains boundary and large-field operators, and expands their final gas. The final measure is not merely a free Gaussian over a full torus: it also contains the chart interaction, inherited effective terms, the large-field sum, and sources.

**PROVED (P9 §4.1, p. 515).** P9 uses the same smallest $k(\nu)$ with $\nu_k>\frac78L^{-2}$, describes the last covariance as “massive,” and says that after the last integral “all the integrations down to 0-momentum” are done. Its correlation argument therefore needs the final covariance and its observable derivatives; stopping at $\nu_k\le L^{-2}$ without integrating $\psi_{k_f}$ cannot supply P9's conclusion.

**REFUTED (attempted alternative reading).** $\beta_k\nu_k\sim1$ is not the last-step criterion: it defines $k_0$. The final index is selected by $\nu_k$ of order $L^{-2}$, after which the last covariance has a fixed mass gap on the rescaled lattice. Nor does P5's “as long as $\nu_k\le1$” imply an additional ordinary transformation starting at every $\nu_k\in(L^{-2},1)$: P4 and P7 specify a separate last operation at the earlier threshold.

## 3. Chosen route and bounded work packages

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON the P4/P7 stopping inequalities for the physical running sequence (`STOP-INDEX`).** Define $k_f=\min\{k:\nu_k>\frac78L^{-2}\}$. For every ordinary step with input $k<k_f$, $\nu_k\le\frac78L^{-2}<L^{-2}$, so `ScaleUniform.massLe_iff` admits the level-$(k+1)$ solver. At $k_f$, use the distinct P7 §4 final integral of $\psi_{k_f}$ with covariance controlled by (M1). There is no demand for an ordinary block-spin step with $\nu_{k_f}>L^{-2}$. The all-scale physical assertion `STOP-INDEX` must include existence/uniqueness of $k_f$, $\nu_{k_f}<1$, the background switch, and the physical-to-unit mass identity. It is **OPEN** as an integrated Lean flow theorem.

**OPEN (mathematics of the last step).** A Lean provider must derive the final small-field cutoff $|\psi_{k_f}-h|<(5L+3)\delta_{k_f}$ and (M1); build the massive covariance, complex square-root chart, exponential decay and determinant bounds; integrate the chart interaction plus inherited energy and observables; carry P7's boundary and large-field terms through the final gas and log branch; and prove torus agreement. `Flow/RGInfty.lean` gives conditional coupling recursion, not a density or last integral. `Correlations/KernelLarge.lean` supplies a Gaussian-kernel interface at larger masses, not the physical last measure. Abstract polymer gas/PE lemmas can consume activities after they are constructed. The P7 construction cited in `LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` (LF-8 (a)) has these as analytic inputs; its $k_f=0$ proposal is explicitly a sketch without a source proof (`LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` IV.4.2). No integrated `LargeField/Last` theorem was found at this snapshot.

**OPEN (Lean packages, each at most 300 new lines; larger results split into successive modules).** `StopIndexBridge`: formalize `STOP-INDEX`, including $k_f=0$ and $\nu_{k_f-1}$ only when $k_f>0$, using `ScaleUniform.massLe_iff` and running-mass inequalities. `FinalMassCoercivity`: derive (M1), final cutoff and an explicit gap $\gamma_0\frac78L^{-2}$ with the exact last quadratic operator. `FinalMassGreen`: construct its finite-volume inverse, decay, square root and uniform complex chart; compare P4 (6.5), P7 (4.3)–(4.6), and the landed `KernelLarge` interface. `FinalFluctuation`: perform the single-scale analytic fluctuation integral and localize inherited terms with scale-$k_f$ support. `FinalLargeFieldGas`: prove P7 (4.9)–(4.17), activity bounds, exponentiation, principal log, and torus transport from the physical last density. `FinalBaseHighMass`: treat $k_f=0$ directly at the original unit lattice. **REFUTED (unselected route).** To take an ordinary step at input $\nu_k\in(L^{-2},1)$ one would need $m_{\max}=L^2$ at the level-$(k+1)$ solver, replace `BgHyp`, `DGSetting` and `OpSetting`, parameterize Green/pointwise/cut and contraction constants, and replace `InUnionDomainC` by a nonempty massive chart centered near $h$ with new composition and analyticity proofs. The existing domain is algebraically empty for $m>1$. This would re-prove substantial parts of the background and decoupling theory and still leave P7's last integral and large-field gas. It is not the economical path to Root-F.
