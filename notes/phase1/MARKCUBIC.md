## 1. Function, tangent, and exact derivative

**PROVED.** In the zero class let $K_\Theta$ be the complex covariance square root, $c_c=\beta_k^{-1/2}$, $x_\Phi=\texttt{hatOf}_\rho(\Phi)$, and $y_\Phi^U=c_cK_\Theta x_\Phi$ on the **unit lattice**. Put $v_t^U=u y_\Phi^U+t\,\texttt{maskUC}\ z\,y_\Phi^U$. Its fine-lattice lift is $v_t=\texttt{liftC}(v_t^U)$; set $y_\Phi=\texttt{liftC}(y_\Phi^U)$ and $M_zy_\Phi=\texttt{liftC}(\texttt{maskUC}\ z\,y_\Phi^U)$. Thus $v_t=u y_\Phi+tM_zy_\Phi$, with the mask applied **after** $K_\Theta$. Define
$$\Delta_z(\Theta,u,t,\Phi)=\Phi_1^\mathbb C(\Theta,v_t^U)-\Phi_1^\mathbb C(\Theta,v_0^U),\qquad \Phi_1^\mathbb C=-\beta V_k^\mathbb C.$$
On the admitted chart, $\partial_t\Delta_z|_{t=0}=D\Phi_1^\mathbb C(\Theta,u y_\Phi^U)[\texttt{maskUC}\ z\,y_\Phi^U]$. This is exactly the function in `Fluctuation/StepComplexThm31.lean`, `E1MarksC`, after `StepSetting.hatOf_restr` identifies $x_\Phi$ with the physical hat field. It holds also at $u=0$; no division by $u$ occurs. The alternate identity $D_xW_1(u,x)[K_\Theta^{-1}\texttt{maskUC}\ z\,K_\Theta x]=u f_z(u,x)$ needs the complex inverse and gives no formula by division at $u=0$ (`MARKS.md`, §2).

**PROVED.** At a realizable datum and fine tangent, `InteractionVPart.interactionOne_eq` and `InteractionBridge.interactionV_eq_energy` give, with $Q_\Theta$ the landed `deltaForm`,
$$V_k^\mathbb C(\Theta,v)=\sum_b E_b(\Theta,v)-\tfrac12Q_\Theta(v),\qquad
\Delta_z=-\beta\left\{\sum_b[E_b(\Theta,v_t)-E_b(\Theta,v_0)]-\tfrac12[Q_\Theta(v_t)-Q_\Theta(v_0)]\right\}.\tag{1}$$
Here $E_b$ is `blockEnergyOne` / `blockEnergyFun`, with the fluctuation solver applied to its arbitrary lower tangent. **REFUTED:** $\Delta_z$ is simply a difference of unsubtracted block energies. The first derivative of the quadratic term in (1) generally survives; only its third derivative vanishes (`InteractionVPart.dirS3_interactionOne`). Realizability and the chart hypotheses of these declarations must be checked at every marked tangent used in (1).

## 3. Cubic terms and the marked Cauchy estimate

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** `MARK-RADIUS`, realizability of the marked discs (`MARK-REAL`), and the complex Taylor identity for the combined potential on those discs (`MARK-TAYLOR`). Let $\omega(s)=(1-s)^2/2$ and, for every original block label, set
$$P_{b,Y}(\Theta,v)=\int_0^1\omega(s)D_v^3E_{b,Y}(\Theta,sv)[v,v,v],ds.\tag{3}$$
`InteractionDir3.norm_dirL3_le` and the bound in `vblock_expansion_scaled` give $|P_{b,Y}(\Theta,v_t)|\le 3E_k^*r^{-3}e^{-2\kappa_{dec}d(Y)}$ on (2). The same estimate holds for $v_0$. `InteractionVPart.dirS3_interactionOne` says the sum of the third derivatives in (3) equals that of the combined potential, since $D^3Q=0$. If the potential and its first two derivatives vanish at $v=0$ **on every marked complex line**, Taylor's formula gives $V_k^\mathbb C(\Theta,v)=\sum_{b,Y}P_{b,Y}(\Theta,v)$. Then
$$\Delta_z(\Theta,u,t,\Phi)=\sum_{b,Y}R_{z;b,Y}(\Theta,u,t,\Phi),\quad
R_{z;b,Y}=-\beta[P_{b,Y}(\Theta,v_t)-P_{b,Y}(\Theta,v_0)].\tag{4}$$
The finite sums justify changing their order and differentiating. `StepBaseDomain.VkC_ray_vanish` and `VkC_ray_taylor` establish ray identities under their admitted-ray hypotheses; `MARK-TAYLOR` must show that those hypotheses cover the arbitrary marked complex tangents in (4), or prove the equivalent general tangent identity directly.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** (4) and joint analyticity on $|t|<t_1$. On $|t|\le t_0$, each $R_{z;b,Y}$ is bounded by **twice** the individual cubic bound: $6\beta E_k^*r^{-3}e^{-2\kappa_{dec}d(Y)}$. Cauchy's estimate therefore gives
$$|\partial_tR_{z;b,Y}(\Theta,u,0,\Phi)|\le (6\beta E_k^*/(r^3t_0))e^{-2\kappa_{dec}d(Y)}.\tag{5}$$
The factor $2$ cannot be dropped by citing a bound on $P_{b,Y}$ alone. It can be replaced by $1$ only after proving a direct difference bound of the same size. Applying Cauchy to the **raw** energy coefficient instead gives $2\beta E_k^*/t_0$, of order $\beta_k$, and does not prove MARK-CUBIC.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** the hypotheses of the newly landed `Fluctuation/SharpAssemblyScaled.lean`, `physical_directTerms_eta_scaled`, specialized to the zero class. Write $M=L^{m_0+m_1}$, $r=\eta_r\sqrt{\beta_k}/p_1$, $p_1=A_1(\log\beta_k)^p$, and $Q\delta_1=\texttt{cK2'}\,\delta_1$. Set the old and observable amplitudes $\rho_E=\rho_M=0$, $c_\nu=0$, $c_\beta=\beta>0$, and choose the positive source-slice radius $\eta_s=1/\beta$, whose slice contains the zero source. `prop33EpsEtaScaled` and `InteractionEnergyScaled.energyBdScaled_le` then give the **unmarked** direct-term bound

\le C'_*\frac{(\log\beta_k)^{3p}}{\sqrt{\beta_k}},\quad
C'_*=\frac{3c_1M^dL^dC_EA_1^3}{\eta_r^3}+\frac{Q\delta_1A_1}{\eta_r},\tag{6}$$
when $\log\beta_k\ge1$ and $p>0$. The first summand is `PHYSCUBIC.md` (S0); the second follows from $\beta\eta_s=1$ and $(\log\beta_k)^p\le(\log\beta_k)^{3p}$. ## 4. Root support before and after covariance assembly

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** definition (4) and its tangent locality. By the exact tangent-locality clause of `vblock_expansion_scaled`, $R_{z;b,Y}=0$ if `psiThick Y` misses the fine lift $\widetilde B(z)$ of the marked unit block, provided all data are the same on both evaluations. Since `psiThick Y = Y` plus nearest neighbours, this gives proximity to the root but does **not** imply `rootCube z ∈ blocksOf Y`. A domain $Y$ may end at the boundary of $\widetilde B(z)$ while its nearest-neighbour read set enters it. Thus the proposed direct inference “read set meets $B(z)$, so the original label contains the root” is **REFUTED**. `MARKS.md` §2 proves the root-cube conclusion only if $Y$ itself meets the fine lift.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** the elementary aligned-cube construction `ROOT-BRIDGE` for the periodic paving. If `psiThick Y ∩ \widetilde B(z)\ne\varnothing`, choose adjacent $x\in Y$ and $y\in\widetilde B(z)$ (or $x=y$). There is an aligned $\pi'$ cube $P$ of side $La$, $a=L^{k+1+m_0+m_1}$, containing both their $a$-cells when $L\ge3$; choose one by a fixed total order. The $a$-cell of $x$ is contained in $Y\cap P$ because $Y$ is an `IsDj` union of aligned $\pi'$ cubes. Thus `SizeModUnion.G3_dcov` applies to $Y\cup P$ (with $\Omega=\texttt{Set.univ}$): it is `IsDj` and
$$d(Y\cup P)\le d(Y)+d(P)+c_G.\tag{7}$$
The $a$-cell of $y$ is the root cube, so `rootCube z ∈ blocksOf(Y∪P)`. A single $P$ contains $L^d$ $a$-cells and has bounded normalized covering cost $D_P$ independent of $k,N_1$; the last bound and periodic edge cases are part of `ROOT-BRIDGE` until proved. If $Y$ already meets $\widetilde B(z)$ one can retain $Y$ and pay zero.

**PROVED CONDITIONAL ON** `ROOT-BRIDGE` and its finite-fibre count (`ROOT-FIBRE`). Put $Y'=Y\cup P$. There are at most $N_P(d,L)$ bridge placements near the root. For fixed $Y'$ and $P$, $Y$ differs from $Y'$ only inside the at most $L^d$ $a$-cells of $P$; hence at most $2^{L^d}$ possible original unions, even before imposing `IsDj`. Consequently terms sent to one rooted $Y'$ have total bound at most
$$C_{\rm root}\frac{2\varepsilon_{\rm sc}}{t_0}e^{-a d(Y')},\qquad
C_{\rm root}=N_P(d,L)2^{L^d}e^{a(D_P+c_G)}.\tag{8}$$
This is a uniform constant, with no further rate loss. To use (8) as an activity one must create one fixed finite family $\mathcal Y_z$ of all possible $Y'$ and add zero activities for absent labels; simply changing the support field of the old `DirectTerms` is not a landed operation. `ROOT-FIBRE` is **OPEN** in Lean, though its finite-cell proof is given here.

**OPEN (`MARK-ROWS` and `MARK-ASSEMBLY`).** In the decoupled hat construction the marked tangent must be $v_t(s,\Theta,x)=u\,\texttt{hatPsiF}(s,\Theta,x)+tM_z\texttt{hatPsiF}(s,\Theta,x)$; masking the hat input before `hatPsiF` gives a different field. `RowsCompose.hatPsiF_rows` proves agreement of the decoupled physical tangent at sites of a component from datum agreement and hat-variable agreement on the component's unit blocks. To conclude sharp locality for every $R_{z,Y'}$, use that theorem on the original component and on the extra bridge component, with the cut parameters at the marked block included. The required paired-agreement lemma for this *masked, decoupled* field, and its passage through the connected covariance assembly, are not stated by `RowsCompose` or `PhysicalSharpLocEta`. They are the exact `MARK-ROWS` / `MARK-ASSEMBLY` inputs. They must keep the same $Y'$ in the value identity and in the locality statement. The bridge alone proves geometric anchoring of a prehat difference; it does not prove the hat expansion.
