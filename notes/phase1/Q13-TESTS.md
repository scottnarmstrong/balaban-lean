### Rotation form and the size of the $h$ germ

**PROVED for the proposed indicator algebra:** with $\mu_{\rm cut}=0$, the background-dependent sharp tests do not read $h$, and massless solutions are equivariant. Acting on all spin variables by a real $R\in O(N)$ with $h$ fixed preserves every such set. This is the fixed-$h$ invariance required by (S5)(i); it avoids the complete-cutoff counterexample in Q13-HYBRID §5. Gaussian factors are likewise invariant. The complete action exponent is jointly covariant, $a(Rx;Rh)=a(x;h)$, by the Euler–Lagrange equation and uniqueness, **conditional on** constructing the proposed kernel on the actual support.

The analytic assertion needs more than the word “germ.” LF-RECONSTRUCTION Definition II.5.7 (S5)(ii) asks for analytic continuation at displacements $|h'-h|<1$ for every real unit $h$, simultaneously with the stated complex spin displacements. `Background/Prop51Mass.lean:prop51_mass_analytic_uniform` gives one analytic solution in data and mass on an **open strict-data set**; `Background/Theorem52Analytic.lean:theorem52_analytic` gives the complete solution analytic in the raw data on its open union domain. Neither theorem supplies a uniform radius $1$ in $h$ on every LF support datum. A sufficient missing quantitative hypothesis, call it `hybridS5Tube`, is: all actual localized data at real $h\in S^{N-1}$ and the required complex spin displacements have strict-domain slack $\rho_j>0$; changing $h$ to $h'$ moves the normalized data by at most $C_h\nu_j|h'-h|$ in each strict-data test; and

$$C_h\nu_j(1+\tau)\le\rho_j\qquad(\tau>0)\tag{H4}$$

uniformly over the support and other complex variables. Then the complete solution is analytic for $|h'-h|<1+\tau$. The explicit germ radius certified by these inputs is $r_{h,j}=\rho_j/(C_h\nu_j)$ (restricted further by any chart radius), and (S5) requires $r_{h,j}>1$. `cutoffDataRegularity` below would give a real margin, but its extension to **all** variables of the (S5) displacement neighbourhood and (H4) is **OPEN**.

Assume `hybridS5Tube`, the complex same-datum mass response $\sup_{|h'-h|\le1+\tau}|\phi^m(h')-\phi^0|\le K_mB_*\nu_j$, and an action/other-exponent field derivative bound $|D_\phi a|\le C_A\beta_j|Z|_j$ on this tube. ## A. One inherited-scale regional energy test

Fix an inherited $j\le k_0$, a new level $q>j$, one new cube $X$, $s_j=L^{-(q-j)}$, and a physical region $Y$ with $R_j=Y\cap T^{(j)}$, $B_j=Y^\approx\cap T^{(j)}$. On a **common complex domain** for the two same-datum composed fields $\psi_j^m,\psi_j^0$, the exact P5 (1.8) term is `Def7v2Form.lean:EresJ2`:

$$E_j^Y(\psi)=\sum_{y\in R_j}\sum_{P\ni y,\,P\subset B_j}\mathscr E_j(y,P;\psi,h)-\operatorname{Corr}_j(R_j,B_j;\psi,h).\tag{A1}$$

`Def7v2Corr.lean:corrPair`/`corrSum` include **both** $v_1$ and $v_2\nu_jh$ terms. Add and subtract the unrestricted polymer sum and use the I6 decomposition $\mathscr E_j(y)=I_j(y)+A_j(y)$. With $T_j^Y$ the omitted-polymer tail and $\mathcal B_j=\sum_{R_j}A_j-\operatorname{Corr}_j(R_j,B_j)$,

$$E_j^Y(\psi_j^m)-E_j^Y(\psi_j^0)=\Delta\sum_{y\in R_j}I_j(y)+\Delta\mathcal B_j+\Delta T_j^Y.\tag{A2}$$

This is **PROVED algebraically** under absolute summability and a common domain; its identification with the *actual* step's new-cube assignment is **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `regionalEnergyIdentity`**, still open. For even $a_j$, `R/RegionalFlux.lean:regional_marginal_flux_finset` gives

$$\sum_{y\in R_j}A_j(y)=\tfrac12\sum_{y\in R_j,x\notin R_j}a_j(x-y)\bigl(\psi(x)\cdot\psi(x)-\psi(y)\cdot\psi(y)\bigr).\tag{A3}$$

The finite computation `crossFlux_nn_example` takes $T=\mathbb Z/5$, $R=\{0\}$, $a(\pm1)=1$, all other coefficients zero, and $s=\mathbf1_{\{1\}}$; it gives crossing flux $1/2$, while `AtermRegion_univ` gives zero on the entire torus. Scaling $s$ by any $u>0$ gives $u/2$. Reuse this same local algebraic datum for each $q>j$ with $j,u$ fixed: its crossing contribution is $u/2$ independently of $s_j\to0$. For any fixed $C,\alpha_3>0$, $u/2>C s_j^{d+\alpha_3}$ for sufficiently large $q-j$. Thus **PROVED:** whole-torus antisymmetry or the landed flux bound alone cannot imply the desired irrelevant one-cube estimate. This is a model of the isolated marginal channel; it is **not** an admissible full RG example, because it does not impose the I6 relation of $a_j$ to the actual $v_1,v_2$, the omitted tail, background response, or the geometric rescaling of the actual cube. It therefore does **not** prove that variant A's complete interface is nonsummable.

For the full (A2), REGIONAL.md names the missing `regionalCommonTube` and `regionalMarginalRemainder`. The latter must bound each *grouped and localized* term by $C_iE_0s_j^{d+\alpha_3}$ per relevant $j$ site, including the P5 correction and omitted tail; multiplicity is at most $\Gamma_Es_j^{-d}$. Then Cauchy's estimate in the common tube, with interpolation radius $r\varepsilon_q/(C_{\rm cmp}\nu_j)$, gives per $q$ cube

$$|\Delta E_{j,X}|\le\Gamma_E(C_I+C_B+C_T)E_0(C_{\rm cmp}/r)(\nu_j/\varepsilon_q)s_j^{\alpha_3}.\tag{A4}$$

Summing $q-j\ge n_1$ gives the explicit factor $L^{-n_1\alpha_3}/(1-L^{-\alpha_3})$ in REGIONAL.md (1.4), plus its finite near-scale term. This proof is **PROVED CONDITIONAL ON `regionalEnergyIdentity`, `regionalCommonTube`, `regionalMarginalRemainder`, and the cube multiplicity estimate**. The hypotheses are precisely what the test was meant to establish; they are **OPEN** for actual inherited factors. The landed `regional_flux_diff_le` instead gives an $O(U_j|R_j|)$ bound with no $s_j^{\alpha_3}$, and P5's whole-torus cancellation supplies no bound for the restricted $R_j\times B_j$ sum. Hence no unconditional summable $C_E^{\rm loc}$ follows.
