# Errata ledger for the Balaban O(N)/Heisenberg formalization

## Purpose

One row per distinct item found in the review corpus for the ten-paper series P0-P9 (Balaban; Balaban-O'Carroll), the eleven earlier or companion papers E1-E11, and the three Dimock papers D1-D3. Rows are deduplicated across the inputs, grouped by paper, and tie each item to the roadmap item it affects. Source keys are those of `review/SOURCES.md`. The ledger records what the reviews say; it does not re-check the PDFs. Where two inputs disagree, both readings are in the row.

## Inputs

Git commit of `/home/scott/Balaban` at the time of writing (`git log -1 --format=%h`): `7be9d11`.

- `review/BIG_PICTURE.md` section 7 (items 1-34)
- `review/FORMALIZATION_ROADMAP.md` section 12 (corrections and unconfirmed points) and section 10.2 (gaps no source closes)
- `review/QC_LOG.md` (Phase 1 spot-checks, Phase 3 table and "Phase 3 summary of settled citations and new gaps", "Phase 4 orchestrator check")
- Section 7 ("Weak spots and open points") of each review in `review/papers/`: P0-P9, E1-E11, D1-D3
- `review/BIG_PICTURE.md` section 1.2, where BIG section 7 item 14 points for the P9 Theorem 1.1 defects

## Status vocabulary

- VERIFIED-BY-ORCHESTRATOR: `QC_LOG.md` records an orchestrator check of the item (page image or text layer).
- REPORTED-BY-REVIEWER: recorded in a review, in BIG section 7 or in the roadmap, with no `QC_LOG.md` check. BIG section 7 items 1-14 are headed "checked against the PDFs" by the synthesis, but have no QC_LOG entry, so they carry this status.
- SETTLED by X: a later source (a later paper in the library, or, where stated, a synthesis argument) settles it. A row can be both VERIFIED-BY-ORCHESTRATOR and SETTLED.

Kinds: misprint (including misreadings in a review, marked "error in the review"), wrong citation, gap in proof, false as printed, unconfirmed (the reviewers' UNCERTAIN items and the questions BIG section 7 could not settle), notation clash.

Not entered as rows: remarks that a paper is a survey or a sketch with no further detail, scope remarks (for example that D2 has no large-field estimate, which is in D3), reviewer notes of the form "checked, no error found", and design notes that are not errata (BIG section 7 item 30). Generic remarks that proofs are "similar" are entered only where a specific load-bearing step is named.
