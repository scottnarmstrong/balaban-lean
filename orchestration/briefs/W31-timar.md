**Goal.** Formalize the discrete-geometry fact used by Lemma S of
`notes/phase1/LF-RECONSTRUCTION.md` Part I (Lemma I.3.13; open item O-1): for a finite set
`A ⊂ ℤ^d` that is connected in the nearest-neighbour graph, the outer (exterior) vertex boundary
`∂_ext A = {x ∉ A : x adjacent to A and x connected to ∞ in ℤ^d ∖ A}` is `*`-connected (adjacency
`‖x − y‖_∞ = 1`). Reference: Á. Timár, "Boundary-connectivity via graph theory", Proc. Amer. Math.
Soc. 141 (2013) 475–480, Lemma 2 (arXiv:0711.1713 v2 p. 4); also the torus version needed by Lemma
I.3.13 (read its statement in Part I: the lift from the cube torus to ℤ^d for sets that do not wind).

exactly as stated there.
