## The spine: what remains, as a list

Since 18 October 2026 the proof of Root-F is organized around a *spine*: a Lean theorem,
`rootF_of_spine` (`Balaban/Spine/RootF.lean`), fully proved, that derives Root-F's exact statement
(for every d ≥ 3 and N ≥ 2) from seven named inputs (`Balaban/Spine/RootFInputs.lean`). Each input is
a statement of the papers; the flow from scale to scale, the stopping scale, the conversion to
Root-F's terms, the logarithm and the transport between tori are proved inside the spine. Root-C
follows from the same inputs and two small additions (`rootC_of_spine`). Remaining work is measured
by the inputs still open; details, landed coverage and known mismatches are in
[`notes/phase1/SPINE.md`](../notes/phase1/SPINE.md). Two independent audits
([`SPINE-AUDIT.md`](../notes/phase1/SPINE-AUDIT.md), [`SPINE-AUDIT-2.md`](../notes/phase1/SPINE-AUDIT-2.md))
confirmed the proof of the spine and found that four inputs, as first stated, asked more than the papers
prove. The inputs are now corrected: the classes carry the torus coherence of the large-field data and the
tie of the running coupling to β, and the constants carry every condition a class member needs. The
classes are shown non-empty only at the smallest configuration so far; at the configurations the flow
uses, this is open.

**Remaining work, estimated from the spine** ([`notes/phase1/SPINE-PLAN.md`](../notes/phase1/SPINE-PLAN.md),
19 October 2026). Every open input has been split into named statements of the papers. Summing estimates over
them gives about 48,000 lines of Lean still to write (range 40,000–75,000), of which about 10,000 are new
mathematics: the first renormalization step (blockers 1, 2), old boundary terms across a hole (blocker 3), the
shrinking source radius (blocker 2), the small factor of the removed large-field operators (blocker 4), exact
agreement on two tori at the last scale (blocker 7), the coupling across large-field cells at the last scale
(blocker 5), and the case with no renormalization step (blocker 1). The rest is engineering, led by extending the
step chain from d = L = 3, N = 2 to general d and N. At the present pace this is roughly 30 rounds of work. Re-estimated on 19 October (evening): about 14,000 lines landed that day, most of it the extension to general d and N; the part that recomputes the new terms on the local generating set of each polymer turned out about 5,700 lines larger than planned, because the step chain proves that its coefficients exist without giving them by a formula. The net estimate is about 47,000 lines still to write.

## How the estimates are made

After each batch of results is merged into `main`, every part is compared with its plan in
`notes/phase1/` and with the papers. A part counts as proved only when the exact statement has been
built in Lean. Results that hold only under explicit extra hypotheses are counted at the level of
what they actually prove. The design notes in `notes/phase1/` give, for each part, the remaining
steps as bounded tasks with an estimated size.
